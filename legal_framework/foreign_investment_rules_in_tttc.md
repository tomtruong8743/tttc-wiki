# Foreign Investment Rules in TTTC

## Summary

Foreign investment rules within the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview]] are governed by a series of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). These decrees collectively form the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]], covering areas relevant to foreign investors such as [[Decree 326 Land And Environment]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange]], all operating under the foundational framework established by [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]].

## Key Points

- The decrees governing TTTC were issued by the Văn Phòng Chính Phủ (Government Office), indicating that foreign investment rules carry the authority of the central government. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] forms the foundational legal basis upon which other investment-related regulations in TTTC are built. [5]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment]] is among the regulations relevant to foreign investors operating within TTTC, governing land and environmental conditions. [3]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security]] addresses labor, employment, and social security matters applicable within TTTC, which are key considerations for foreign investors. [6]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] sets out immigration and entry/exit policies relevant to foreign nationals investing or working in TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration framework within TTTC, providing dispute resolution mechanisms for foreign investors. [4]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange]] governs the establishment and operation of a commodities exchange within TTTC, relevant to foreign investment in commodity markets. [1]

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview]] — The financial center (TTTC) established under Vietnamese government decree, subject to a specific legal and regulatory framework for investment and operations. [5]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] — The foundational decree establishing the TTTC financial center, issued by the Văn Phòng Chính Phủ. [5]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — The Government Office of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all TTTC-related decrees cited in the source documents. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] — A decree establishing the international arbitration center within TTTC, providing legal dispute resolution for investors. [4]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange]] — A decree governing the establishment and operations of a commodities exchange within the TTTC financial center. [1]
