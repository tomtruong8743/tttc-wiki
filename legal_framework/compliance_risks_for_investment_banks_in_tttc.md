# Compliance Risks for Investment Banks in TTTC

## Summary
Investment banks operating within the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview]] face a range of compliance obligations arising from the legal and regulatory framework established by the Vietnamese Government. The framework governing the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] is set out across multiple decrees issued by the Office of the Government, including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]]. Understanding and adhering to these decrees is central to managing [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] for any investment bank seeking to operate in TTTC.

## Key Points
- The [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] is established through a series of decrees issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Office of the Government of Vietnam). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] forms the foundational legal basis for the establishment of TTTC, which investment banks must understand as the starting point for their compliance obligations. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] governs financial policies within TTTC, which is directly relevant to the operations of investment banks and their [[Reporting Obligations For Financial Institutions In Tttc]]. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] introduces specific labor and social security rules that investment banks must comply with when hiring staff, including obligations related to [[Foreign Worker Regulations]]. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] sets out land and environmental regulations that may affect investment banks establishing a physical presence within TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] establishes immigration policies applicable within TTTC, relevant for investment banks employing foreign nationals. Compliance responsibilities under this decree extend to Ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, provincial and municipal People's Committees, and all relevant organisations and individuals. [2][5]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] also provides specific procedures, including a formal template (Mẫu NT-01) for applications seeking permanent residency for foreign nationals working at the Vietnam International Financial Centre. Investment banks sponsoring foreign employees must be aware of these procedural requirements. [4]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] provides the framework for dispute resolution within TTTC, which investment banks should factor into their risk and compliance assessments alongside [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]]. [4]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] governs banking licensing, establishment, and foreign exchange management within TTTC. Notably, it references international credit rating agencies (Standard & Poor's, Moody's, Fitch Ratings) in connection with licensing requirements, indicating that credit ratings may form part of compliance criteria for banks seeking to operate in TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] also contains detailed rules on permitted activities for domestic commercial banks (ngân hàng thương mại 100% vốn trong nước), including restrictions on the purchase and sale of government bonds, government-guaranteed bonds, and local government bonds. Specifically, banks are prohibited from selling Vietnamese Government bonds, government-guaranteed bonds, and local government bonds denominated in foreign currency. Investment banks must carefully assess these restrictions when structuring [[Debt Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] transactions. [6]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] establishes the framework for the establishment and operation of commodities exchanges (Sở giao dịch hàng hóa) within TTTC, including licensing requirements and the approval process for clearing centres (Trung tâm thanh toán bù trừ). Investment banks involved in commodities-related activities or advising clients in this sector must understand the licensing and approval obligations under this decree. [1][2]
- All decrees are issued by the same issuing authority (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ), indicating a centralised regulatory structure that investment banks must engage with for [[Licenses And Approvals For Financial Institutions In Tttc]]. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] — The foundational government decree establishing the legal basis and structure of TTTC. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] — A government decree setting out the financial policies applicable within TTTC, relevant to investment banking operations and [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]]. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] — A government decree governing labor, employment, and social security obligations for entities operating within TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] — A government decree addressing land use and environmental compliance requirements within TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] — A government decree establishing immigration and entry/exit policies applicable to TTTC participants, including formal procedures for permanent residency applications by foreign workers. [2][4]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] — A government decree establishing the International Arbitration Center within TTTC and its governing rules. [4]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] — A government decree governing banking licensing, establishment, and foreign exchange management within TTTC, including permitted and restricted activities for domestic and foreign banks. [3][6]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] — A government decree governing the establishment and operation of commodities exchanges within TTTC, including licensing of exchanges and approval of clearing centres. [1][2]
- **Mẫu NT-01** — A standardised application template under [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] used to request permanent residency for foreign nationals working at the Vietnam International Financial Centre. [4]
- **Mẫu số 01.GP** — A standardised licence template for the establishment of a commodities exchange (Sở giao dịch hàng hóa) under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]]. [2]
- **Mẫu số 02.CT.TTBT** — A standardised approval certificate template for organisations seeking to qualify as a clearing centre (Trung tâm thanh toán bù trừ) within a commodities exchange under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]]. [1][2]
- **Ngân hàng thương mại 100% vốn trong nước** — A domestic commercial bank with 100% domestic capital; subject to specific activity restrictions under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], including limitations on foreign currency bond transactions. [6]
- **Trung tâm thanh toán bù trừ** — Clearing centre; an entity that must satisfy specified conditions and obtain formal approval under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] before operating within a TTTC commodities exchange. [1][2]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all major TTTC decrees. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Additional Compliance Considerations

### Banking Activity Restrictions
[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] introduces granular restrictions on permitted banking activities that investment banks must carefully review. Domestic commercial banks operating within TTTC are prohibited from selling Vietnamese Government bonds, government-guaranteed bonds, and local government bonds that are denominated in foreign currency, whether on the primary or secondary market. This restriction has direct implications for investment banks structuring [[Bond Issuance Opportunities In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] or [[Debt Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] transactions involving domestic counterparties. [6]

### Commodities Exchange Licensing and Clearing
Under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]], entities wishing to operate a commodities exchange or serve as a clearing centre must obtain specific licences and approvals from the relevant regulatory authority (Cơ quan điều hành). The approval process involves formal documentation requirements, including use of standardised templates (Mẫu số 01.GP for exchange licences and Mẫu số 02.CT.TTBT for clearing centre approvals).
