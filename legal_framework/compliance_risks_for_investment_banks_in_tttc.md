# Compliance Risks for Investment Banks in TTTC

## Summary
Investment banks operating within the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview]] face a range of compliance obligations arising from the legal and regulatory framework established by the Vietnamese Government. The framework governing the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] is set out across multiple decrees issued by the Office of the Government, including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]]. Understanding and adhering to these decrees is central to managing [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] for any investment bank seeking to operate in TTTC.

## Key Points
- The [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] is established through a series of decrees issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Office of the Government of Vietnam). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] forms the foundational legal basis for the establishment of TTTC, which investment banks must understand as the starting point for their compliance obligations. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] governs financial policies within TTTC, which is directly relevant to the operations of investment banks and their [[Reporting Obligations For Financial Institutions In Tttc]]. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] introduces specific labor and social security rules that investment banks must comply with when hiring staff, including obligations related to [[Foreign Worker Regulations]]. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] sets out land and environmental regulations that may affect investment banks establishing a physical presence within TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] establishes immigration policies applicable within TTTC, relevant for investment banks employing foreign nationals. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] provides the framework for dispute resolution within TTTC, which investment banks should factor into their risk and compliance assessments alongside [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]]. [4]
- All decrees are issued by the same issuing authority (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ), indicating a centralised regulatory structure that investment banks must engage with for [[Licenses And Approvals For Financial Institutions In Tttc]]. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] — The foundational government decree establishing the legal basis and structure of TTTC. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] — A government decree setting out the financial policies applicable within TTTC, relevant to investment banking operations and [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]]. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] — A government decree governing labor, employment, and social security obligations for entities operating within TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] — A government decree addressing land use and environmental compliance requirements within TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] — A government decree establishing immigration and entry/exit policies applicable to TTTC participants. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] — A government decree establishing the International Arbitration Center within TTTC and its governing rules. [4]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all major TTTC decrees. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
