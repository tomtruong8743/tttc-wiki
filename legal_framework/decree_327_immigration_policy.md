# Decree 327 Immigration Policy

## Summary
Decree 327 is a decree concerning immigration (entry and exit) policy within the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC Financial Center]], issued by the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Vietnamese Government Office]] (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). It forms part of a broader series of regulatory instruments governing the TTTC, alongside decrees covering establishment, financial policy, land and environment, and other matters. This decree is part of the [[Financial Center Decrees]] framework that collectively establishes the legal and operational foundation of the TTTC.

## Key Points
- Decree 327 specifically addresses immigration (entry and exit) policy within the TTTC Financial Center. [3]
- The decree was issued by the Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [3]
- Decree 327 is one of a series of decrees regulating the TTTC, which also includes [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment]], and others. [3][4][6]
- A related decree, Decree 324, covers financial policies within the TTTC. [2]
- Decree 328 addresses international arbitration within the TTTC (uncertain). [5]
- Decree 329 covers banking licensing, establishment, and foreign exchange management within the TTTC (uncertain). [1]

## Definitions
- **[[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]]** — A government decree regulating entry and exit (immigration) policy within the TTTC Financial Center. [3]
- **[[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]]** — The financial center subject to a series of government decrees, including Decree 327. [3][6]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — The Government Office of Vietnam, the issuing authority for Decree 327 and related decrees. [3]
- **Xuất nhập cảnh** — Vietnamese term for entry and exit (immigration), the subject matter of Decree 327. [3]
