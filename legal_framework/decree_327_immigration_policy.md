# Decree 327 Immigration Policy

## Summary
Decree 327 is a decree concerning immigration (entry and exit) policy within the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC Financial Center]], issued by the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Vietnamese Government Office]] (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). It forms part of a broader series of regulatory instruments governing the TTTC, alongside decrees covering establishment, financial policy, land and environment, and other matters. This decree is part of the [[Financial Center Decrees]] framework that collectively establishes the legal and operational foundation of the TTTC.

## Key Points
- Decree 327 specifically addresses immigration (entry and exit) policy within the TTTC Financial Center. [3]
- The decree was issued by the Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [3]
- Decree 327 is one of a series of decrees regulating the TTTC, which also includes [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment]], and others. [3][4][6]
- A related decree, [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], covers financial policies within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] addresses international arbitration within the TTTC (uncertain). [5]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] covers banking licensing, establishment, and foreign exchange management within the TTTC (uncertain). [2]

## Permanent Residency Procedures for Foreigners
Decree 327 sets out procedures for granting permanent residency (**thường trú**) to foreign nationals within the TTTC. Key provisions include: [3][10]

- The **Cơ quan điều hành** (Management Authority) is responsible for initiating permanent residency applications on behalf of foreign nationals at the immigration management authority (cơ quan quản lý xuất nhập cảnh). [3]
- Applications for permanent residency must be recommended by a competent ministry or government agency, or by the Chairman of the Ho Chi Minh City People's Committee, or the Chairman of the Da Nang People's Committee. [3]
- This reflects the involvement of both [[Ho Chi Minh City Vs Da Nang In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Ho Chi Minh City and Da Nang]] as the cities hosting the TTTC.

### Required Documents for Permanent Residency Application
The application dossier for permanent residency must include: [3]
- The foreign national's permanent residency application form;
- A judicial record issued by the competent authority of the applicant's country of citizenship;
- A diplomatic note from the representative agency of the applicant's country of citizenship requesting Vietnam to grant permanent residency;
- A certified copy of the passport, or a photocopy of the passport presented alongside the original for verification;
- A written request using Form NT-01 (issued together with Decree 327), along with supporting documents demonstrating eligibility for permanent residency as stipulated in Article 5 of the decree.

## Visa and Temporary Residency Provisions
- Foreign nationals who are spouses or children under 18 years of age of qualifying foreign workers at the TTTC may be granted a visa or temporary residency card bearing the symbol **UD2**, upon the request of the Cơ quan điều hành (Management Authority). The validity of this UD2 visa or card aligns with the validity of the **UD1** visa or temporary residency card issued to the principal foreign national. [10]
- Procedures for inviting, sponsoring entry, and applying for visas and temporary residency cards for foreign nationals follow Articles 16, 19, and 37 of the Law on Entry, Exit, Transit, and Residence of Foreigners in Vietnam. The processing time must not exceed 03 working days. [10]
- These provisions are directly relevant to [[Attracting International Financial Talent To Tttc]] and [[Policies For Foreign Experts In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].

## Definitions
- **[[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]]** — A government decree regulating entry and exit (immigration) policy within the TTTC Financial Center, including visa categories, temporary residency, and permanent residency procedures for foreign nationals. [3][10]
- **[[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]]** — The financial center subject to a series of government decrees, including Decree 327. [3][6]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — The Government Office of Vietnam, the issuing authority for Decree 327 and related decrees. [3]
- **Xuất nhập cảnh** — Vietnamese term for entry and exit (immigration), the subject matter of Decree 327. [3]
- **Thường trú** — Vietnamese term for permanent residency; a status that may be granted to qualifying foreign nationals at the TTTC under Decree 327. [3]
- **Cơ quan điều hành** — The Management Authority of the TTTC, responsible for initiating immigration applications and procedures on behalf of foreign nationals. [3][10]
- **UD1 / UD2** — Visa and temporary residency card symbols used under Decree 327: UD1 is issued to qualifying foreign workers at the TTTC; UD2 is issued to their eligible family members (spouses and children under 18). [10]
- **Form NT-01** — A standardized application form issued under Decree 327, required as part of the permanent residency application dossier. [3]
