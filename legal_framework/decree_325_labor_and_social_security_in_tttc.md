# Decree 325 Labor and Social Security in TTTC

## Summary
Decree 325 is one of the foundational legal instruments governing the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview]], specifically addressing labor, employment, and social security matters within the Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC). Issued by the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ)]], it forms part of a broader suite of decrees — including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] — that collectively establish the regulatory framework for the TTTC. The decree sets out rules governing employment conditions, worker protections, and social insurance arrangements applicable within the financial centre, including specific provisions for foreign workers participating in compulsory social insurance.

## Key Points
- Decree 325 was issued by the Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), the same issuing authority responsible for all other [[Financial Center Decrees]] related to the TTTC. [6]
- The decree specifically covers labor (lao động), employment (việc làm), and social security (an sinh xã hội) within the TTTC. [6]
- Decree 325 is one of at least six decrees forming the [[Legal Documents Establishing The Vietnam International Financial Centre|legal foundation]] of the TTTC, alongside Decrees 323–330. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- The decree's scope — labor and social security — suggests it interacts closely with [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], which governs entry and residency of [[Foreign Worker Regulations|foreign workers]] in the TTTC. (uncertain) [6][2]
- Foreign workers employed at the TTTC who participate in compulsory social insurance are subject to a contribution base with a minimum equal to the reference level (mức tham chiếu) and a maximum of 20 times the reference level at the time of contribution, as stipulated under the Social Insurance Law No. 41/2024/QH15. [5]
- Foreign workers participating in compulsory social insurance at the TTTC may opt to stop participating after at least 12 months of contributions from the time they requested enrollment, provided they submit a request to cease participation. [5]
- Foreign workers may adjust the salary base used for compulsory social insurance contributions after having contributed at the chosen salary level for at least 12 months, provided they submit a request for adjustment. [5]
- Foreign workers may stop participating in compulsory social insurance to receive a lump-sum social insurance payment (bảo hiểm xã hội một lần) in cases where they have participated for at least 12 months and request to stop, or where they no longer work at the TTTC. [5]

## Definitions

- **[[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security]]** — The specific government decree regulating labor, employment, and social security conditions within the TTTC, issued by the Government Office. [6]
- **Lao động (Labor)** — Refers to the workforce and employment relations governed within the TTTC under this decree. [6]
- **Việc làm (Employment)** — The conditions and regulations relating to job creation and work arrangements within the TTTC. [6]
- **An sinh xã hội (Social Security)** — The system of social protections, including social insurance and welfare provisions, applicable to workers in the TTTC. [6]
- **Bảo hiểm xã hội bắt buộc (Compulsory Social Insurance)** — The mandatory social insurance scheme applicable to certain workers at the TTTC, including foreign workers under specified conditions. [5]
- **Mức tham chiếu (Reference Level)** — The benchmark salary level used as the minimum contribution base for compulsory social insurance calculations under the decree. [5]
- **Bảo hiểm xã hội một lần (Lump-Sum Social Insurance Payment)** — A one-time payout of accumulated social insurance entitlements available to eligible foreign workers who cease participation under qualifying circumstances. [5]
- **Luật Bảo hiểm xã hội số 41/2024/QH15 (Social Insurance Law No. 41/2024/QH15)** — The primary social insurance legislation referenced in Decree 325 as the legal basis for contribution rules applicable within the TTTC. [5]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ (Government Office)** — The issuing authority for Decree 325 and all related TTTC decrees. [6]

## Foreign Worker Social Insurance Provisions

Decree 325 contains specific and notably flexible provisions for [[Foreign Worker Regulations|foreign workers]] employed at the TTTC who are enrolled in compulsory social insurance. Key features include: [5]

- **Contribution ceiling**: The maximum salary base for contributions is capped at 20 times the reference level (mức tham chiếu), consistent with the Social Insurance Law No. 41/2024/QH15.
- **Voluntary exit after 12 months**: Foreign workers may request to cease compulsory social insurance participation after a minimum of 12 months of enrollment, offering flexibility not typically available under standard Vietnamese labor law. This provision is likely designed to accommodate the international mobility of [[Attracting International Financial Talent To Tttc|high-quality foreign talent]] at the TTTC. (uncertain)
- **Salary base adjustments**: Foreign workers may revise their chosen contribution salary base after 12 months, allowing for recalibration as remuneration structures evolve.
- **Lump-sum exit benefit**: Workers who stop participating — whether voluntarily or because they leave the TTTC — may claim accumulated entitlements as a lump-sum payment, reducing the risk of stranded social insurance benefits for internationally mobile professionals.

These provisions align with the TTTC's broader strategy of [[Incentives For High Quality Workers In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|incentivizing high-quality workers]] and [[Policies For Foreign Experts In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|attracting foreign experts]] to the financial centre.
