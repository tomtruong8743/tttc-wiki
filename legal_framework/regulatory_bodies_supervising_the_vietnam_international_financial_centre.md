# Regulatory Bodies Supervising the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary
The regulatory framework governing the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) is established and overseen through a series of decrees issued by the Vietnamese government. The primary issuing authority across all known regulatory instruments — spanning establishment, [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policy]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|banking and foreign exchange]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|commodities exchange]] — is the **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** (Office of the Government of Vietnam). This body serves as the central governmental authority responsible for promulgating the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal framework]] of the TTTC. Beyond this central issuing authority, several specialised ministries and bodies hold defined supervisory and implementation roles under the decrees.

---

## Key Points
- All decrees related to the [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment]] and operation of the TTTC are issued by the **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** (Office of the Government of Vietnam). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- The [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policies decree]] governing the TTTC was issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ. [1]
- The [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy decree]] for the TTTC was issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ, with Ministers, Heads of ministerial-level agencies, Government agencies, and Chairmen of provincial People's Committees responsible for its implementation. [2][6]
- The [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment decree]] for the TTTC was issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ. [3]
- The [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration centre decree]] for the TTTC was issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ. [4]
- The [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment decree]] (Decree 323) for the TTTC was issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ. [5]
- The [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour, employment, and social security decree]] for the TTTC was issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ. [6]
- The [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|banking and foreign exchange decree]] (Decree 329) governs foreign exchange management for TTTC members, including rules on borrowing and lending across borders; issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ. [7]
- Under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Decree 330]], the **Bộ Công Thương** (Ministry of Industry and Trade) holds a defined supervisory role, cooperating with the **Cơ quan giám sát** (Supervisory Authority) to inspect the operations of the [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Commodities Exchange]] and related trading activities. [4a]
- Under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Decree 330]], the **Cơ quan điều hành tại các thành phố** (City-level Management Authority) is responsible for reviewing establishment applications, issuing and revoking licences for the Commodities Exchange, approving lists of tradeable goods, and approving lists of foreign exchange contracts for linked trading. [4a]
- Implementation of TTTC decrees extends across a wide range of government bodies, including the Prime Minister, Deputy Prime Ministers, all Ministries and ministerial-level agencies, provincial People's Councils and Committees, the National Assembly, the Supreme Court, the Supreme People's Procuracy, and the State Audit Office. [6]
- All [[Main Decrees Related To The Vietnam International Financial Centre|major decrees]] regulating the TTTC share a single issuing authority, indicating centralised governmental oversight. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- **[[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]]** — Abbreviation for *Trung tâm Tài chính Quốc tế* (Vietnam International Financial Centre), the subject entity being regulated. [5]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the central executive body responsible for issuing the decrees that establish and regulate the TTTC. See also [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]]. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- **[[Financial Center Decrees|Cơ quan phát hành]]** — Vietnamese term meaning "issuing authority"; designates the body responsible for enacting a given regulatory decree. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- **Bộ Công Thương** — Ministry of Industry and Trade; holds supervisory and coordination responsibilities over [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|commodities exchange]] operations within the TTTC. [4a]
- **Cơ quan giám sát** — The Supervisory Authority; referenced in [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Decree 330]] as the body that cooperates with the Ministry of Industry and Trade to inspect commodities exchange activities. [4a]
- **Cơ quan điều hành tại các thành phố** — City-level Management Authority; responsible under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Decree 330]] for licensing, approving, and overseeing the [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Commodities Exchange]] at the local level. Relevant to the [[Cities Involved In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|cities involved]] in the TTTC. [4a]
- **[[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|Quản lý ngoại hối]]** — Foreign exchange management; the regulatory framework under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] governing cross-border borrowing and lending by TTTC members. [7]
