# Financial Supervision and Governance

## Summary
Financial supervision and governance within the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview]] is established through a series of decrees issued by [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|the Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ)]]. These decrees collectively form the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]], covering areas such as [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|banking licensing and foreign exchange management]], [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange|commodities exchange operations]], and broader [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]] applicable within the TTTC financial center.

## Key Points
- All decrees governing the TTTC financial center are issued by the **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** (Government Office), indicating centralised governmental authority over the center's regulatory framework. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] governs the licensing, establishment of banks, and foreign exchange management within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange]] governs the establishment and operation of the Commodities Exchange (Sở Giao Dịch Hàng Hóa) within the TTTC. [2]
- [[Financial Center Decrees|Financial policies]] within the TTTC are regulated under a dedicated decree (Decree 324), separate from other operational decrees. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Immigration policy]] within the TTTC is also governed by a dedicated decree issued by the same central authority. [4]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment|Land and environmental matters]] within the TTTC are subject to their own dedicated decree. [5]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] forms the foundational legal instrument for the establishment of the TTTC financial center itself. [6]

## Definitions

- **[[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]]** — The financial center established and governed by a suite of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office. [6]
- **[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Cấp phép và thành lập ngân hàng]]** — The licensing and establishment of banks operating within the TTTC, as regulated under Decree 329. [1]
- **[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Quản lý ngoại hối]]** — Foreign exchange management within the TTTC, governed under Decree 329. [1]
- **[[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange|Sở Giao Dịch Hàng Hóa]]** — The Commodities Exchange established and operating within the TTTC under Decree 330. [2]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — The Government Office of Vietnam, identified as the issuing authority for all TTTC-related decrees. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- **[[Financial Center Decrees|Chính sách tài chính]]** — Financial policies applicable within the TTTC, regulated under Decree 324. [3]
