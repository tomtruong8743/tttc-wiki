# Arbitration in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Arbitration in the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) is governed by a dedicated legal instrument issued by the Government of Vietnam. [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the framework for an international arbitration centre operating within the TTTC, forming part of a broader suite of foundational decrees — including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] — all issued by the [[Regulatory Bodies Supervising The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Office of the Government of Vietnam]] to regulate activities within the centre. The existence of a dedicated arbitration decree reflects the TTTC's emphasis on robust [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|dispute resolution mechanisms]] as a core component of the centre's [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal framework]].

---

## Key Points

- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] was issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) as the primary legal instrument governing the international arbitration centre within the TTTC. [4]
- The arbitration decree forms part of a coordinated set of foundational decrees establishing the legal and operational framework of the TTTC, alongside decrees on establishment, financial policy, labour, land, immigration, banking and foreign exchange, and commodities exchange. [4][5][6][1][3][2]
- All decrees related to the TTTC, including the arbitration decree, share the same issuing authority: the Office of the Government of Vietnam. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- The existence of a standalone decree dedicated to international arbitration signals that [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution]] is treated as a distinct and important pillar of the TTTC's [[Special Legal Mechanisms In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|special legal mechanisms]]. [4]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] governs the establishment and operation of the Commodities Exchange (Sở giao dịch hàng hóa) within the TTTC. That decree provides that tax and customs incentives applicable to commodities traded on the exchange are to be implemented in accordance with the decree on [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policy within the TTTC]], illustrating the interconnected nature of the TTTC's legal instruments. [1]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] includes governance provisions for member commercial banks operating within the TTTC, including rules on the concurrent holding of positions by members of the Board of Members and the Supervisory Board, reflecting the broader regulatory architecture within which the arbitration framework sits. [4][6]

## Definitions

- **[[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]]** — the governmental decree issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam establishing and regulating the International Arbitration Centre within the TTTC. [4]
- **[[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]]** — the Vietnam International Financial Centre (Trung tâm tài chính quốc tế), established under [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] and governed by a series of coordinated government decrees. [5]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — the Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all foundational TTTC decrees, including the arbitration decree. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- **[[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|International Arbitration Centre]]** — the dedicated body established within the TTTC to handle dispute resolution, governed by Decree 328. [4]
- **Sở giao dịch hàng hóa** — the Commodities Exchange established within the TTTC under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]], which lists and trades commodity contracts, including spot commodity contracts and derivative commodity contracts. [3]
- **Cơ quan điều hành tại các thành phố** — the municipal-level governing authority within the [[Cities Involved In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|cities]] hosting the TTTC, responsible for approving and licensing the Commodities Exchange and related activities under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]]. [1]

## The TTTC Decree Framework and Arbitration's Role Within It

The arbitration framework established by [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] does not operate in isolation. It is one component of a tightly coordinated set of legal instruments that together constitute the [[Legal Documents Establishing The Vietnam International Financial Centre|legal foundation]] of the TTTC. The [[Main Decrees Related To The Vietnam International Financial Centre|main decrees]] cover:

- **[[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]]** — establishment of the TTTC itself
- **[[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]** — [[Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|financial and tax policy]], including incentives applicable across the TTTC [2][5]
- **[[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]]** — [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|labour, employment, and social security]]
- **[[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]]** — [[Land Use Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|land use and environmental]] matters
- **[[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]]** — [[Immigration And Residency Policies For Tttc Workers|immigration and entry/exit policy]]
- **[[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]]** — international arbitration
- **[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]** — [[Banking Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|banking licensing]] and [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]]
- **[[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]]** — establishment and operation of the [[Financial Products And Instruments In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|commodities exchange]]

The cross-referencing between decrees — for example, [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] directing parties to [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] for tax and customs incentive procedures [1] — demonstrates that [[How Disputes Are Handled In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|dispute resolution]] under Decree 328 is likely intended to apply across this full ecosystem of financial, banking, commodities, and related activities conducted within the TTTC.
