# Arbitration in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Arbitration in the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) is governed by a dedicated legal instrument issued by the Government of Vietnam. [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the framework for an international arbitration centre operating within the TTTC, forming part of a broader suite of foundational decrees — including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], and [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] — all issued by the [[Regulatory Bodies Supervising The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Office of the Government of Vietnam]] to regulate activities within the centre. The existence of a dedicated arbitration decree reflects the TTTC's emphasis on robust [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|dispute resolution mechanisms]] as a core component of the centre's [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal framework]].

---

## Key Points

- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] was issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) as the primary legal instrument governing the international arbitration centre within the TTTC. [4]
- The arbitration decree forms part of a coordinated set of foundational decrees establishing the legal and operational framework of the TTTC, alongside decrees on establishment, financial policy, labour, land, and immigration. [4][5][6][1][3][2]
- All decrees related to the TTTC, including the arbitration decree, share the same issuing authority: the Office of the Government of Vietnam. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- The existence of a standalone decree dedicated to international arbitration signals that [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution]] is treated as a distinct and important pillar of the TTTC's [[Special Legal Mechanisms In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|special legal mechanisms]]. [4]

---

## Definitions

- **[[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]]** — the governmental decree issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam establishing and regulating the International Arbitration Centre within the TTTC. [4]
- **[[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]]** — the Vietnam International Financial Centre, established under [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] and governed by a series of coordinated government decrees. [5]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — the Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all foundational TTTC decrees, including the arbitration decree. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- **[[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|International Arbitration Centre]]** — the dedicated body established within the TTTC to handle dispute resolution, governed by Decree 328. [4]
