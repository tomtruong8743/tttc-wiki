# Tax Incentives and Exemptions

## Summary
Tax incentives and exemptions are financial policy mechanisms commonly used within special economic and financial zones to attract investment and encourage business activity. In the context of the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview]], such policies are expected to form a core component of the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]], particularly as governed by instruments issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ) across the suite of [[Financial Center Decrees]], including provisions potentially addressed under [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] and related regulatory frameworks.

## Key Points
- The source documents covering the TTTC financial center were all issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Government Office), indicating centralised governmental authority over all regulatory matters in the zone, which would include any [[Tax Incentives and Exemptions]] framework. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- A dedicated decree on financial policies within the TTTC, Nghi_dinh_324 (Decree 324), exists and is issued by the same governmental authority, suggesting that tax and financial incentive policies are formally regulated at the decree level. [2]
- Multiple decrees cover distinct policy domains — including [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] — indicating a comprehensive and segmented regulatory approach to the TTTC. [1][3][4][6]

## Definitions
- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview]] — the overarching financial center established under the Vietnamese Government's regulatory framework, governed by a series of decrees. [5]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — the Government Office of Vietnam, the issuing authority for all TTTC-related decrees documented in the source materials. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- **Nghi dinh (Decree)** — a form of Vietnamese governmental regulatory instrument used to establish and govern specific policy areas within the TTTC. [2][5]
