# Legal Protections for Investors in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) is supported by a suite of government decrees issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) that together form the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal framework]] protecting investors operating within the centre. These decrees address key areas of investor concern, including [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environmental rights]], [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|international arbitration]], [[Foreign Worker Regulations|labour conditions]], and [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]], collectively establishing a structured legal environment designed to attract and protect both domestic and foreign participants in the TTTC. Additional decrees govern [[Banking Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|banking licensing and foreign exchange management]], [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|commodities exchange operations]], and prudential requirements for member banks, further reinforcing investor protections across a broad range of financial activities.

---

## Key Points

- The [[Legal Documents Establishing The Vietnam International Financial Centre|legal framework for the TTTC]] is established through a series of decrees issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [1][2][3][4][5][6][7][8]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] provides the foundational legal basis for the establishment of the TTTC, forming the basis upon which investor protections rest. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] governs financial policies within the TTTC, which may include provisions relevant to investor rights and financial operations. [1]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]] addresses land and environmental regulations within the TTTC, which are relevant to the rights and obligations of investors holding or using land. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] establishes an International Arbitration Centre within the TTTC, providing investors with access to [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|formal dispute resolution mechanisms]]. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]] covers labour, employment, and social security policies within the TTTC, which affect investor obligations and worker protections. Implementation responsibility is assigned to Ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, Chairmen of the People's Committees of Ho Chi Minh City and Da Nang, and all relevant organisations and individuals. [6]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] regulates immigration policy within the TTTC, relevant to [[Foreign Worker Regulations|foreign investors and workers]] seeking to operate within the centre. [2]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] governs banking licensing, establishment of member banks, and foreign exchange management within the TTTC. It includes detailed requirements for the qualifications and fitness of senior management of member commercial banks, as well as [[Risk Management Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|prudential safety ratios]] that member banks must observe. [7]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Decree 330]] governs the establishment and operation of the [[Capital Market Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Commodities Exchange]] within the TTTC. It sets out eligibility conditions for preferential treatment, including import and export tax incentives, and assigns oversight responsibilities to the Ministry of Industry and Trade (Bộ Công Thương) and the city-level management authority. [8]
- Member banks operating under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] are subject to international prudential standards including the Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR), Leverage Ratio (LEV), Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR), and Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR), providing structural protections for depositors and counterparties. [7]
- Banks applying international accounting standards within the TTTC are exempt from requirements to hold Vietnamese Government bonds or government-guaranteed bonds at prescribed ratios under Vietnamese law, and are not required to comply with the maximum foreign currency position relative to equity capital under Vietnamese law. [7]
- Governance protections for member banks include restrictions on concurrent office-holding: the Chairman of the Members' Council of a member commercial bank may not simultaneously serve as an executive, a member of the Supervisory Board of that bank, or a manager of another enterprise in Vietnam. [7]
- Spousal and close family members of Members' Council members, the General Director (Director), and equivalent officers of a member bank are prohibited from serving as Chief Accountant or head of finance of that same bank. [7]
- Senior management appointments at member commercial banks — including Deputy General Directors, Chief Accountants, and equivalent roles — must meet qualification standards including relevant university-level education or at least three years of direct professional experience in finance, banking, or a relevant specialist field. [7]
- All decrees are issued by the same issuing authority — the Office of the Government — indicating a unified and centralised [[Financial Center Decrees|regulatory approach]] to investor protections. [1][2][3][4][5][6][7][8]
- The city-level management authority (Cơ quan điều hành tại các thành phố) under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Decree 330]] is empowered to grant, reissue, suspend, and revoke establishment licences for the Commodities Exchange, and to approve the list of domestically produced commodities permitted for trading as well as the list of foreign exchange commodity contracts permitted for linked trading. [8]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|**Decree 323**]] — The foundational decree establishing the Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC), issued by the Office of the Government. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|**Decree 324**]] — A decree governing financial policies within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|**Decree 325**]] — A decree covering labour, employment, and social security within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|**Decree 326**]] — A decree addressing land and environmental regulations within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|**Decree 327**]] — A decree regulating immigration and entry/exit policies within the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|**Decree 328**]] — A decree establishing the International Arbitration Centre within the TTTC, providing a formal mechanism for resolving investor disputes. [4]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|**Decree 329**]] — A decree governing banking licensing, establishment, and foreign exchange management within the TTTC, including prudential safety requirements and corporate governance standards for member banks. [7]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|**Decree 330**]] — A decree governing the establishment and operation of the Commodities Exchange within the TTTC, including conditions for preferential treatment and the regulatory responsibilities of the Ministry of Industry and Trade and city-level management authorities. [8]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all TTTC-related decrees referenced in these sources. [1][2][3][4][5][6][7][8]
- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|**TTTC**]] — Abbreviation for "Trung Tâm Tài Chính," referring to the Vietnam International Financial Centre. [5]
- **CAR / LEV / LCR / NSFR** — International prudential banking ratios (Capital Adequacy Ratio, Leverage Ratio, Liquidity Coverage Ratio, Net Stable Funding Ratio) that member banks in the TTTC are required to observe under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]], providing structural safeguards for the financial system. [7]
- **Cơ quan điều hành tại các thành phố** — The city-level management authority responsible for licensing, supervision, and regulatory approvals within the TTTC, including for the [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Commodities Exchange]]. [8]
