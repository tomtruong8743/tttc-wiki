# Decree 330 Commodities Exchange

## Summary
Decree 330 pertains to the regulatory framework governing a Commodities Exchange within the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC Financial Center]], issued as part of a broader series of decrees by the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Vietnamese Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ)]]. It is one of several decrees — alongside instruments such as [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], and others — that together form the [[Financial Center Decrees|comprehensive legal and regulatory architecture of the TTTC]].

## Key Points
- Decree 330 is part of a series of government decrees issued by Văn Phòng Chính Phủ (the Government Office) relating to the TTTC Financial Center. [1]
- The decree series covering TTTC includes instruments on establishment, financial policy, land, labor, international arbitration, and banking — suggesting Decree 330 addresses the commodities exchange dimension of the center (uncertain). [2][3][4][5][6]
- Like companion decrees, Decree 330 was issued by the same issuing authority: [[Văn Phòng Chính Phủ]] (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ). [1]
