# Reporting Obligations for Financial Institutions in TTTC

## Summary
Reporting obligations for financial institutions operating within the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview]] are governed by a series of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office ([[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]], and related instruments). These obligations form part of the broader [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Financial Supervision And Governance]] framework, which aims to ensure transparency, accountability, and regulatory integrity across all entities participating in the centre.

## Key Points
- The decrees governing TTTC were all issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Government Office of Vietnam), indicating that reporting obligations originate from the highest level of executive authority. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] is a primary source document relevant to financial policies, which would encompass reporting requirements for financial institutions within the centre, including insurance enterprises, reinsurance enterprises, and insurance brokerage firms. [1][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis for the TTTC, under which all institutional obligations — including reporting — are established. [5]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] and [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] suggest that reporting obligations extend beyond purely financial matters, encompassing environmental and labour compliance as well. [3][6]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] indicates that dispute resolution mechanisms exist alongside reporting frameworks, suggesting that non-compliance with reporting obligations may be subject to formal arbitration or enforcement processes. [4]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] implies that reporting obligations may also apply to the status and movement of foreign personnel employed by financial institutions within TTTC, including procedures for permanent residence applications for foreign workers. [2][5]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] establishes specific prudential ratios that member banks must monitor and report against, including the Leverage Ratio (LEV), Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR), and Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR). Member banks are required to track and manage compliance with the liquidity coverage ratio on an ongoing basis. [4]
- Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], the supervisory authority ([[Regulatory Bodies Supervising The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]) may, based on the results of its supervision, inspection, and examination of member banks, require individual member banks to maintain more stringent safety ratios than the general standards prescribed, depending on the nature and degree of risk. [4]
- Member banks applying International Accounting Standards are exempt from compliance with the maximum foreign currency position relative to equity capital as otherwise required under Vietnamese law. [4]
- Member banks applying International Accounting Standards are also exempt from requirements to purchase, hold, or invest in Vietnamese Government bonds or Government-guaranteed bonds as otherwise required under Vietnamese law. [4]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] introduces formal licensing and approval documentation requirements, including a template for the approval of a Clearing Centre (Trung tâm thanh toán bù trừ) operating within a Commodities Exchange in the TTTC. Approved entities must comply with all provisions of the relevant decree and related legal instruments. [1][3]
- Licensing documentation under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] includes detailed templates for the establishment and operation of insurance enterprises, reinsurance enterprises, and insurance brokerage firms within the TTTC, requiring disclosure of investor details, capital contributions, and operational scope. [6]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] references internationally recognised credit rating agencies — Standard & Poor's, Moody's, and Fitch Ratings — in the context of bank eligibility or assessment criteria, suggesting that external credit ratings may form part of the reporting or qualification framework for member banks. [2]

## Definitions
- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview]] **TTTC** — the Vietnam International Financial Centre, established by government decree as a special economic and financial zone subject to its own regulatory framework. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] **Financial Policy Decree** — the decree specifically addressing financial policies within TTTC, including the licensing and establishment of insurance and reinsurance entities, and likely the primary instrument governing reporting obligations for financial institutions. [1][6]
- [[Financial Supervision And Governance]] **Reporting Obligations** — the legal duties imposed on financial institutions to disclose operational, financial, and compliance-related information to the relevant regulatory authorities within TTTC. (uncertain) [1][5]
- [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] **Compliance Requirements** — the set of rules and standards that financial institutions must adhere to, including reporting, as a condition of operating within TTTC. (uncertain) [1][5]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] **International Arbitration Centre** — a body established within TTTC to handle disputes, potentially including those arising from failures to meet reporting obligations. [4]
- **Trung tâm thanh toán bù trừ** — Clearing Centre; an entity approved to perform clearing and settlement functions within a [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Commodities Exchange]] in the TTTC, subject to its own approval documentation and compliance obligations. [1][3]
- **Tỷ lệ khả năng chi trả (LCR)** — Liquidity Coverage Ratio; a prudential metric that member banks under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] must monitor, manage, and where necessary report upon to the supervisory authority. [4]
- **Tỷ lệ nguồn vốn ổn định ròng (NSFR)** — Net Stable Funding Ratio; another prudential safety ratio prescribed under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] that member banks are required to maintain and track. [4]
