# AML and Compliance Summary for Investment Banks in TTTC

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) is governed by a suite of decrees issued by the Government Office, establishing the legal and regulatory framework within which investment banks must operate. [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] for investment banks include adherence to [[Anti Money Laundering Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], [[Kyc Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], and broader [[Financial Supervision And Governance]] obligations. These requirements intersect with the financial policies set out under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], the establishment framework under [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], and related decrees covering banking, foreign exchange, and institutional oversight within the TTTC. The framework also extends to commodities exchange operations under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]], banking licensing under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], and insurance-related activities regulated under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]].

## Key Points

- The decrees governing TTTC were issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Government Office of Vietnam), establishing the overarching legal authority for all compliance obligations within the centre. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis for the establishment of the TTTC, within which all [[Anti Money Laundering Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and compliance frameworks for investment banks are situated. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out the financial policy environment relevant to investment banks, including those related to [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]. It also regulates insurance agency activities and ancillary insurance services within the TTTC, imposing conditions on the legal capacity and certification of personnel involved. [1][6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] and [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] form part of the broader regulatory environment that investment banks operating in the TTTC must be aware of for full [[Compliance Risks For Investment Banks In Tttc]]. [3][6]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] governs the movement of personnel, including procedures for granting permanent residency to foreign nationals working within the TTTC, which is relevant to [[Foreign Worker Regulations]] and compliance staffing within investment banks. [2][4]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes mechanisms for [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]], which may be relevant when compliance or AML-related disputes arise. [4]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] sets conditions for banking licensing within the TTTC, including references to international credit rating agencies such as Standard & Poor's, Moody's, and Fitch Ratings as part of eligibility or assessment criteria for institutions operating in the centre. [2]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] establishes the legal framework for the formation and operation of commodities exchanges within the TTTC, including approval procedures for clearing centre (Trung tâm thanh toán bù trừ) status and associated compliance obligations. Investment banks with commodities-related activities should be aware of these requirements. [1][5]
- The approval process for a clearing centre under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] requires submission of documentation and satisfaction of conditions set out in the relevant decree, with formal approval issued by the governing authority of the TTTC. [1]
- All six core decrees were issued by the same issuing authority (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ), indicating a unified government approach to the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]]. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] **Legal Framework of TTTC** — The collective body of decrees and regulations issued by the Vietnamese Government Office establishing and governing the Vietnam International Financial Centre. [5]
- [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] **Compliance Requirements** — The obligations imposed on financial institutions, including investment banks, to adhere to applicable laws, regulations, and standards within the TTTC. [1][5]
- [[Anti Money Laundering Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] **AML Rules** — Regulations requiring investment banks and other financial institutions to detect, prevent, and report money laundering activities within the TTTC. [5]
- [[Kyc Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] **KYC (Know Your Customer) Requirements** — Due diligence obligations requiring financial institutions to verify the identity and assess the risk profile of their clients. [5]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Government Office of Vietnam; the issuing authority for the foundational decrees establishing and regulating the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] **Dispute Resolution** — Mechanisms, including the International Arbitration Centre established under [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], available for resolving legal and compliance disputes in the TTTC. [4]
- **Trung tâm thanh toán bù trừ** — Clearing centre; an entity approved under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] to perform clearing and settlement functions for a commodities exchange (Sở giao dịch hàng hóa) within the TTTC. [1][5]
- **Sở giao dịch hàng hóa** — Commodities exchange; a regulated marketplace established within the TTTC under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] for the trading of goods and related services. [1][5]
- **Standard & Poor's / Moody's / Fitch Ratings** — International credit rating agencies referenced in [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] as part of eligibility or assessment criteria for banking institutions seeking to operate within the TTTC. [2]
