# Currency and Capital Controls

## Summary
Currency and capital controls within the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview]] are governed primarily by [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], which addresses the licensing and establishment of banks and foreign exchange management within the [[Financial Center Decrees]] framework. These regulations are issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) and form part of the broader [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] that oversees financial operations, foreign currency transactions, and capital flows in the TTTC financial center.

## Key Points
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] covers the licensing and establishment of banks, as well as foreign exchange management (quản lý ngoại hối) within the TTTC financial center. [1]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange]] regulates the establishment and operation of a Commodities Exchange within TTTC, which may intersect with currency and capital flow matters. [2]
- Financial policies within TTTC, which likely include provisions relevant to capital controls, are addressed under a dedicated financial policy decree (Nghị định 324 về chính sách tài chính trong TTTC). [3]
- All decrees relating to currency and capital controls are issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), indicating central government authority over these matters. [1][2][3]
- Additional regulatory decrees covering land, environment, immigration, and international arbitration also originate from the same issuing authority, suggesting a unified government framework for TTTC governance. [4][5][6]

## Definitions
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] **Quản lý ngoại hối (Foreign Exchange Management)** — the regulatory oversight of foreign currency transactions and exchange operations within the TTTC financial center, as addressed in Decree 329. [1]
- **Nghị định (Decree)** — a regulatory instrument issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) establishing rules and policies for specific domains within TTTC. [1][2][3]
- **TTTC** — the financial center subject to these regulatory decrees, governed by a series of government-issued instruments covering banking, foreign exchange, commodities, and financial policy. [1][2][3]
- **Cơ quan phát hành (Issuing Authority)** — the body responsible for promulgating a decree; in all cases here, this is the Văn Phòng Chính Phủ (Office of the Government). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
