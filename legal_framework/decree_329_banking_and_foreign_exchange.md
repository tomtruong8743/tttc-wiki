# Decree 329 Banking and Foreign Exchange

## Summary

Decree 329 is a decree issued by the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|Vietnamese Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ)]] concerning the licensing, establishment of banks, and foreign exchange management within the [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|TTTC Financial Center]]. It forms part of the broader [[Financial Center Decrees|series of decrees]] governing the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal and regulatory framework of the TTTC]], alongside related decrees covering financial policy, land, labor, and immigration.

## Key Points

- Decree 329 was issued by the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Văn Phòng Chính Phủ]] (Government Office) [1].
- The decree specifically addresses [[Banking Licensing and Establishment]] and [[Foreign Exchange Management]] within the TTTC Financial Center [1].
- It is one of a series of decrees regulating the TTTC, including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment]], and [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] [1][5][6][4][3].
- The full title references licensing (cấp phép), establishment (thành lập) of banks, and foreign exchange administration (quản lý ngoại hối) within the TTTC [1].

## Definitions

- **[[Banking Licensing and Establishment]]** — The formal process of granting permissions and authorising the founding of banking institutions operating within the TTTC Financial Center [1].
- **[[Foreign Exchange Management]]** — Regulatory oversight and administration of foreign currency transactions and exchange operations within the TTTC [1].
- **[[Tttc Financial Center Overview|Văn Phòng Chính Phủ]]** — The Government Office of Vietnam, the issuing authority for Decree 329 and the related series of TTTC decrees [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- **[[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]]** — The financial center subject to the regulatory framework established by the series of government decrees, including Decree 329 [1][5].
