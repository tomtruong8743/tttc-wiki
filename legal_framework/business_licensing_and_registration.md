# Business Licensing and Registration

## Summary
Business licensing and registration within the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview]] framework is governed by a series of decrees issued by the Government Office (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ). These decrees collectively establish the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]], covering areas such as financial policy under [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], land and environment under [[Decree 326 Land And Environment]], labor and social security under [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security]], immigration policy under [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and international arbitration under [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]]. Together, they form the regulatory backbone for businesses seeking to operate within the TTTC financial center.

## Key Points
- All decrees governing business operations within the TTTC were issued by the VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (Government Office), indicating centralized regulatory authority over licensing and registration matters. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] appears to be the foundational decree relevant to the establishment and registration of entities within the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview]]. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies]] sets out financial policies applicable within the TTTC, which are likely relevant to businesses during the registration and licensing process. [1]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment]] governs land and environmental regulations within the TTTC, which may form part of the requirements for business establishment. [3]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security]] addresses labor, employment, and social security matters, forming part of the compliance framework for registered businesses. [6]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] covers immigration policies relevant to foreign businesses and personnel operating within the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the international arbitration framework, which may be relevant to dispute resolution for registered businesses. [4]

## Definitions
- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview]] **TTTC** — the financial center established under Vietnamese government decree, subject to a dedicated regulatory framework across multiple policy areas. [5]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — the Government Office of Vietnam, the issuing authority for all decrees governing the TTTC framework. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] **Legal Framework** — the body of decrees and regulations issued by the Government Office that collectively govern business, financial, labor, land, immigration, and arbitration matters within the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
