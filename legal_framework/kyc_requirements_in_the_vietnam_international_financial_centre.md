# KYC Requirements in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Know Your Customer (KYC) requirements are a critical component of [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], forming part of the broader regulatory framework governing entities operating within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]]. KYC obligations are closely linked to [[Anti Money Laundering Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Financial Supervision And Governance]] standards applied to financial institutions, including [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Asset Managers In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]. The establishment of the TTTC through a series of decrees issued by the Government Office sets the legal context within which compliance obligations, including KYC, would be expected to apply. [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] provides detailed provisions on the licensing, establishment, and governance of member banks within the TTTC, including fit-and-proper requirements for key personnel that intersect with identity verification and KYC-related standards.

## Key Points

- The TTTC was established through a series of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ), which collectively form the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] within which all compliance requirements, including KYC, would operate. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] is the foundational legal instrument establishing the TTTC, setting the overarching governance structure under which KYC and other [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] would apply. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] governs financial policies within the TTTC, including the financial regime applicable to member banks. It permits member commercial banks and foreign bank branches to apply the financial policies of their parent institution or owner, or alternatively to comply with Decree 135/2025/NĐ-CP. It also permits members to adopt [[International Accounting Standards (IFRS)|International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS)]] or generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP), with a requirement to declare compliance in financial statement notes — which supports transparency and auditability relevant to KYC processes. [3][6]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] sets out detailed fit-and-proper standards for senior personnel of member commercial banks, including Deputy General Directors, Chief Accountants, and equivalent roles. These standards require relevant qualifications in finance, banking, economics, business administration, law, accounting, or auditing, as well as residency in Vietnam during their term of office. [1][2]
- Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], individuals who have been found responsible for violations leading to administrative penalties against Vietnamese credit institutions or member banks — particularly in areas such as licensing, governance, capital contributions, share ownership, credit extension, corporate bond purchases, or prudential ratios — may be assessed as lacking professional ethics, which is a relevant consideration for KYC and fit-and-proper assessments of counterparties and institution personnel. [2]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] also clarifies that individuals who have not previously worked in Vietnam are exempt from certain disqualification provisions under Article 33, accommodating the international character of the TTTC workforce while still applying baseline governance standards. [2]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] relates to immigration policies within the TTTC, including a standard form (Mẫu NT-01) for applications for permanent residency by foreigners working at the TTTC. This intersects with KYC requirements for verifying the identity and residency status of foreign participants and workers. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] includes a physical infrastructure declaration form (Mẫu số 05) required of organisations registering within the TTTC, covering office space, equipment, software systems, and systems for customer asset management — indicating that operational infrastructure relevant to KYC and client onboarding processes is a formal registration requirement. [6]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration mechanism that could be relevant for resolving disputes arising from KYC compliance failures (uncertain). [4]
- All decrees identified in the source excerpts were issued by the same authority — the Vietnamese Government Office — indicating a centralised regulatory approach to the TTTC, including oversight of [[Anti Money Laundering Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and KYC obligations. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- For member banks applying international accounting standards under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], where IFRS or GAAP and Vietnamese accounting law address the same matter, the international standard takes precedence — an important consideration for institutions designing KYC and client data management systems that must satisfy both domestic and international audit requirements. [3]

## Definitions

- [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|KYC (Know Your Customer)]] — A regulatory obligation requiring financial institutions to verify the identity of their clients, assess their risk profiles, and monitor transactions to prevent financial crime. [5]
- [[Anti Money Laundering Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|AML (Anti-Money Laundering)]] — A set of laws, regulations, and procedures designed to prevent the generation of income through illegal actions; closely linked to KYC requirements in financial centres. [5]
- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Decree]] — A legally binding instrument issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ) establishing rules and policies applicable within the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Financial Supervision And Governance|Regulatory Oversight]] — The supervision and enforcement of compliance obligations, including KYC, by designated authorities within the TTTC framework (uncertain). [5]
- **Fit-and-Proper Requirements** — Standards set under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] requiring that senior personnel of member banks meet defined qualifications, residency conditions, and professional ethics standards, including the absence of disqualifying prior violations. These requirements are directly relevant to KYC processes for vetting institutional counterparties and their key management. [1][2]
- **Professional Ethics Assessment (Đánh giá có đạo đức nghề nghiệp)** — A formal evaluation under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] determining whether a person is disqualified from holding a senior role at a member bank due to prior regulatory violations or findings of misconduct; a component of the broader KYC and governance framework. [2]
- **Mẫu NT-01** — A standard form under [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] used to apply for permanent residency for foreign nationals working at the TTTC, supporting identity documentation and verification processes relevant to KYC. [5]
