# Decree 324 Financial Policies of TTTC

## Summary
Decree 324 (số 324/2025/NĐ-CP) concerns the financial policies governing the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC (Trung tâm Tài chính)]], Vietnam's International Financial Centre. It is one of a series of decrees issued by the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Vietnamese Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ)]] to regulate various aspects of the financial centre, alongside related instruments such as [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange]]. The decree forms part of the broader [[Financial Center Decrees|legal and regulatory framework]] underpinning the TTTC.

Decree 324 implements and provides detailed guidance on Articles 10, 11, 12, 18, 19, 24, 26, 27, and 31 of National Assembly Resolution No. 222/2025/QH15 on the International Financial Centre in Vietnam, which is the primary legislative basis for the TTTC. [5] The decree was issued on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance (Bộ trưởng Bộ Tài chính). [5]

## Key Points

### General Scope and Authority
- Decree 324 was issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), the official body responsible for promulgating government decrees. [1 (original)]
- It implements Resolution No. 222/2025/QH15 of the National Assembly on the International Financial Centre in Vietnam, specifically Articles 10, 11, 12, 18, 19, 24, 26, 27, and 31. [5]
- It is specifically dedicated to [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]] within the TTTC. [1 (original)]
- The decree is part of a coordinated series of government instruments establishing and regulating the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]]. [1 (original)]
- All decrees in this series, including Decree 324, share the same issuing authority: VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Government Office). [1 (original)]

### Subjects of Application
The decree applies to the following categories of persons and organisations: [5]
- Members (Thành viên) of the International Financial Centre in Vietnam
- Investors (Nhà đầu tư)
- Agencies and organisations belonging to the International Financial Centre
- Other agencies, organisations, and individuals related to the activities of the International Financial Centre as defined in Resolution No. 222/2025/QH15

### Securities Business Organisations
- Decree 324 includes provisions and application forms relating to [[Securities Activities In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|securities business organisations]] that are Members (Thành viên) of the TTTC, including licensing and establishment procedures. [2 (original)]
- The [[Regulatory Bodies Supervising The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Cơ quan điều hành]] (Executive Authority) holds the authority to issue, amend, supplement, and revoke licences for securities business organisations that are Members. [3]
- Securities business organisations that are Members must follow accounting policies in accordance with point (e), clause 1, Article 11 of Resolution No. 222/2025/QH15. [3]
- Securities business organisations that are Members are **not** required to consolidate their financial statements with those of a domestic securities business investor. [3]
- The activities of a securities business organisation that is a Member are **excluded** when calculating the financial safety ratio of a domestic securities business investor. [3]
- The decree specifies documentation requirements for investors seeking a [[Licenses And Approvals For Financial Institutions In Tttc|licence to establish and operate]] a securities business organisation as a Member, including audited financial statements for the two most recent years. [3 (original)]
- Where the investor is a parent company, consolidated audited financial statements for the two most recent years must be submitted. [3 (original)]
- A draft charter (Điều lệ) for the securities business organisation consistent with relevant laws must be included in the licence application. [3 (original)]
- A list of proposed personnel at the securities business organisation must be submitted, including copies of passports for foreign personnel and copies of securities practice certificates in Vietnam or equivalent documents proving authorisation to practise securities business abroad. [3 (original)]
- Licence applications may be submitted directly at the headquarters of the Cơ quan điều hành, by post, or via an online system. [3 (original)]
- The Cơ quan điều hành has a processing window of 05 working days from receipt of a complete application before issuing a response. [3 (original)]

### Insurance Enterprises
- Decree 324 also regulates the licensing, operation, restructuring, dissolution, and bankruptcy of [[Financial Services In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|insurance enterprises]], reinsurance enterprises, and insurance broker enterprises within the TTTC. [1][2][4][6][7][9]
- The Cơ quan điều hành holds the authority to issue, amend, supplement, and revoke operating licences for insurance enterprises, reinsurance enterprises, and insurance broker enterprises. [7]
- The licence to establish and operate an insurance enterprise, reinsurance enterprise, or insurance broker enterprise simultaneously constitutes the enterprise registration certificate and the Member registration certificate. [1]
- Insurance enterprises, reinsurance enterprises, and insurance broker enterprises must be organised as limited liability companies (công ty trách nhiệm hữu hạn). [1]

#### Licensing Conditions
- Investors contributing capital to establish an insurance enterprise, reinsurance enterprise, or insurance broker enterprise must be legal entities. [1]
- For domestic investors (organisations established under Vietnamese law), the following conditions apply: [1]
  - The investor must already be an insurance enterprise, reinsurance enterprise, or insurance broker enterprise established and operating in Vietnam under the Law on Insurance Business;
  - The investor must have been directly operating in the relevant field for a minimum of 05 consecutive years immediately preceding the year of the licence application;
  - The investor must ensure that the proposed charter capital contributed in cash meets the minimum level prescribed by Vietnamese law on insurance business, and must place the funds in escrow at a licensed commercial bank or foreign bank branch operating in Vietnam.
- Members and their employees may participate in insurance at insurance enterprises established in the TTTC, insurance enterprises established in the rest of Vietnam, and insurance enterprises abroad. [7]

#### Insurance Business Activities
- Non-life insurance enterprises (doanh nghiệp bảo hiểm phi nhân thọ) may conduct non-life insurance business and health insurance products with a term of one year or less. [4]
- Life insurance enterprises (doanh nghiệp bảo hiểm nhân thọ) may conduct life insurance and health insurance business. [4]
- Health insurance enterprises (doanh nghiệp bảo hiểm sức khoẻ) may conduct health insurance business. [4]
- Insurance contracts are governed by Vietnamese law on insurance business; however, dispute resolution relating to insurance contracts follows the [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|dispute resolution rules of the International Financial Centre]]. [4]
- Insurance enterprises are autonomous and self-responsible in designing and developing insurance products (uncertain, excerpt truncated). [4]

#### Corporate Changes Requiring Approval
- Insurance enterprises, reinsurance enterprises, and insurance broker enterprises must obtain prior written approval from the Cơ quan điều hành before making changes to any of the following: [2]
  - Name or registered office address
  - Charter capital level
  - Scope, content, and duration of operations
  - Transfer of contributed capital leading to a change of owner
  - Division, separation, merger, consolidation, or dissolution
  - Change of Chairman of the Members' Council, General Director or Director, or Actuary (the actuary requirement does not apply to insurance broker enterprises)
- Applications for approval of such changes must follow procedures prescribed by Vietnamese law on insurance business. [2]

#### Restructuring of Insurance Enterprises
- Division, separation, merger, and consolidation of insurance enterprises, reinsurance enterprises, and insurance broker enterprises must satisfy conditions including: not causing harm to the lawful rights and interests of policyholders, employees, and the State; compliance with applicable law; and the investing party meeting the licensing conditions under the decree. [9]
