# Decree 324 Financial Policies of TTTC

## Summary
Decree 324 (số 324/2025/NĐ-CP) concerns the financial policies governing the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC (Trung tâm Tài chính)]], Vietnam's International Financial Centre. It is one of a series of decrees issued by the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Vietnamese Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ)]] to regulate various aspects of the financial centre, alongside related instruments such as [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange]]. The decree forms part of the broader [[Financial Center Decrees|legal and regulatory framework]] underpinning the TTTC.

## Key Points
- Decree 324 was issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), the official body responsible for promulgating government decrees. [1]
- It is specifically dedicated to [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]] within the TTTC. [1]
- The decree is part of a coordinated series of government instruments establishing and regulating the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]]. [1]
- All decrees in this series, including Decree 324, share the same issuing authority: VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Government Office). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- Decree 324 includes provisions and application forms relating to [[Securities Activities In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|securities business organisations]] that are Members (Thành viên) of the TTTC, including licensing and establishment procedures. [2][3]
- The decree specifies documentation requirements for investors seeking a [[Licenses And Approvals For Financial Institutions In Tttc|licence to establish and operate]] a securities business organisation as a Member, including audited financial statements for the two most recent years. [3]
- Where the investor is a parent company, consolidated audited financial statements for the two most recent years must be submitted. [3]
- A draft charter (Điều lệ) for the securities business organisation consistent with relevant laws must be included in the licence application. [3]
- A list of proposed personnel at the securities business organisation must be submitted, including copies of passports for foreign personnel and copies of securities practice certificates in Vietnam or equivalent documents proving authorisation to practise securities business abroad. [3]
- Licence applications may be submitted directly at the headquarters of the [[Regulatory Bodies Supervising The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Cơ quan điều hành]] (Executive Authority), by post, or via an online system. [3]
- The Cơ quan điều hành has a processing window of 05 working days from receipt of a complete application before issuing a response. [3]
- Decree 324 includes standardised form templates (Mẫu biểu) covering a range of [[Financial Products And Instruments In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|financial activities]], including: [2]
  - Mẫu số 01: Application for a licence to establish and operate a securities business organisation as a Member
  - Mẫu số 02: Personnel list for a securities business organisation
  - Mẫu số 03: Application for a licence to provide a [[Capital Raising In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|crowdfunding platform]] (nền tảng huy động vốn cộng đồng)
  - Mẫu số 04: Crowdfunding registration form
  - Mẫu số 05: Physical infrastructure description
  - Mẫu số 06: Licence to provide a crowdfunding platform
  - Mẫu số 07: Application to revoke a crowdfunding platform licence
  - Mẫu số 08: Application for a licence to establish and operate an [[Financial Services In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|insurance enterprise]], reinsurance enterprise, or insurance broker
  - Mẫu số 09: Licence to establish and operate an insurance enterprise, reinsurance enterprise, or insurance broker

## Definitions
- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] — Trung tâm Tài chính, the Vietnamese abbreviation for the International Financial Centre established under this series of decrees. [1]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Government Office of Vietnam; the issuing authority for Decree 324 and the related series of financial centre decrees. [1]
- [[Financial Center Decrees|Chính sách tài chính]] — "Financial policies"; the subject matter regulated by Decree 324 within the TTTC. [1]
- **Tổ chức kinh doanh chứng khoán là Thành viên** — Securities business organisation that is a Member of the TTTC; a key category of licensed institution under Decree 324. [3]
- **Nền tảng huy động vốn cộng đồng** — Crowdfunding platform; a financial activity covered by Decree 324's licensing regime. [2]
- **Cơ quan điều hành** — The Executive Authority responsible for receiving licence applications and administering the financial centre at the city level. [3]
