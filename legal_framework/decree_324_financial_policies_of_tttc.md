# Decree 324 Financial Policies of TTTC

## Summary
Decree 324 concerns the financial policies governing the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC (Trung tâm Tài chính)]], Vietnam's International Financial Centre. It is one of a series of decrees issued by the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Vietnamese Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ)]] to regulate various aspects of the financial centre, alongside related instruments such as [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]]. The decree forms part of the broader [[Financial Center Decrees|legal and regulatory framework]] underpinning the TTTC.

## Key Points
- Decree 324 was issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), the official body responsible for promulgating government decrees. [1]
- It is specifically dedicated to [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]] within the TTTC. [1]
- The decree is part of a coordinated series of government instruments establishing and regulating the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]]. [1]
- All decrees in this series, including Decree 324, share the same issuing authority: VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Government Office). [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions
- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] — Trung tâm Tài chính, the Vietnamese abbreviation for the International Financial Centre established under this series of decrees. [5]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Government Office of Vietnam; the issuing authority for Decree 324 and the related series of financial centre decrees. [1]
- [[Financial Center Decrees|Chính sách tài chính]] — "Financial policies"; the subject matter regulated by Decree 324 within the TTTC. [1]
