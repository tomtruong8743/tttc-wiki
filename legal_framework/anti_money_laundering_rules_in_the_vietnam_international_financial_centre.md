# Anti Money Laundering Rules in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The [[Vietnam International Financial Centre For Investment Banking Interns|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]] is established and governed by a suite of decrees issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam, including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange]]. These decrees collectively form the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] for the TTTC.

The source documents do not contain a dedicated AML chapter or explicit anti-money laundering provisions. However, [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] (Nghị định 329 on licensing, establishment of banks, and [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|foreign exchange management]] in the TTTC) contains several provisions that are directly relevant to AML compliance in practice, particularly around [[Reporting Obligations For Financial Institutions In Tttc|reporting obligations]], information disclosure, and controls on cross-border capital flows. These provisions form part of the broader [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|compliance framework]] applicable to member institutions.

## Key Points

### Reporting and Disclosure Obligations for Member Institutions

Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], member institutions operating in the TTTC are subject to mandatory information declaration (*khai báo thông tin*) and reporting regime (*chế độ báo cáo*) requirements in connection with cross-border borrowing and lending activities.[4]

Specifically:

- **Borrowing from overseas**: Members are permitted to borrow foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, but must declare information and comply with reporting requirements.[4]
- **Lending abroad (100% foreign-owned members)**: Members wholly owned by foreign investors and lending to overseas parties must also declare information and comply with the reporting regime.[4]
- **Lending abroad (non-100% foreign-owned members)**: Members not wholly owned by foreign investors must comply with the conditions for overseas lending set out in Article 89 of Decree 329, and must similarly declare information and comply with reporting requirements.[4]

These disclosure and reporting obligations are consistent with standard [[Kyc Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|KYC]] and AML-adjacent controls, as they create a paper trail for all cross-border financial flows involving TTTC members.

### Conditions on Overseas Lending

[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] sets out specific eligibility conditions for lending abroad by TTTC member institutions that are not wholly foreign-owned:[4]

- A 100% domestically-funded commercial bank (*ngân hàng thương mại 100% vốn trong nước*) may lend in foreign currency to:
  - Organisations established and operating outside Vietnamese territory in which a Vietnamese enterprise (member or non-member) holds more than 50% of shares or contributed capital; or
  - Organisations established outside Vietnam that are themselves majority-owned (>50%) by such entities.[4]
- A member enterprise may lend to organisations established outside Vietnam in which the lending member or its parent company holds more than 50% of shares or contributed capital.[4]

These conditions limit the scope of permissible overseas lending and reduce the risk of funds being channelled to unrelated or opaque offshore entities, which is relevant to [[Compliance Risks For Investment Banks In Tttc|AML and compliance risk management]].

### Permitted Banking Activities Relevant to AML Monitoring

[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] enumerates a range of permitted activities for TTTC member banks, several of which carry AML monitoring implications:[2][5]

- Buying and selling Vietnamese Government bonds, government-guaranteed bonds, and local government bonds.[2]
- Buying and selling corporate bonds issued on Vietnamese territory (excluding bonds issued by Vietnamese credit institutions).[5]
- Buying and selling corporate bonds denominated in foreign currency issued overseas.[5]
- Buying and selling Government bonds denominated in foreign currency issued overseas.[5]
- Issuing bonds in accordance with securities law and Article 87 of Decree 329.[5]
- Buying and selling debt (*mua, bán nợ*).[2]
- Trustee and entrusted activities (*ủy thác, nhận ủy thác*) in banking, subject to owner and parent bank policies and the principles set out in Article 51, Clause 21 of Decree 329.[2]
- Providing monetary brokerage services (*dịch vụ môi giới tiền tệ*) for foreign credit institutions.[5]
- Providing banking advisory services and other business advisory services within the scope of the licence issued by the City Management Authority (*Cơ quan điều hành tại thành phố*).[2]
- Providing treasury services, asset custody services, and safe deposit box rental.[2]

Each of these activities is subject to the broader Vietnamese legal framework on [[Phong Chong Rua Tien In The Context Of Tttc|phòng chống rửa tiền]] (anti-money laundering), even where Decree 329 does not restate those obligations explicitly.

### Infrastructure Requirements for Compliance Systems

[[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] includes a standard form (Form 05 — *Bản thuyết minh cơ sở vật chất*) requiring registering organisations to describe their physical infrastructure, including:[6]

- Office space and layout;
- IT systems and equipment, including systems serving crowdfunding platform operations (*nền tảng huy động vốn cộng đồng*) and client asset management activities (*hoạt động quản lý tài sản khách hàng*).[6]

While not an explicit AML requirement, the documentation of technology systems for client asset management is directly relevant to the technical infrastructure required for AML transaction monitoring, [[Kyc Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|KYC]] systems, and [[Reporting Obligations For Financial Institutions In Tttc|suspicious transaction reporting]].

## Definitions

- **Khai báo thông tin** — Information declaration; the obligation to disclose details of a transaction or relationship to the relevant authority. Required for cross-border borrowing and lending by TTTC members under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]].[4]
- **Chế độ báo cáo** — Reporting regime; the system of periodic and event-driven reporting required of financial institutions. Applies to all TTTC members engaged in cross-border borrowing and lending.[4]
- **Phòng chống rửa tiền** — [[Phong Chong Rua Tien In The Context Of Tttc|Anti-money laundering]]; the legal and regulatory framework for preventing money laundering under Vietnamese law.
- **Ủy thác / Nhận ủy thác** — Trustee / entrusted activities in banking; activities where a bank acts as agent or trustee for another party, subject to AML-relevant disclosure principles.[2]
- **Môi giới tiền tệ** — Monetary brokerage; intermediation services between financial institutions, a category of activity that carries AML monitoring obligations.[5]
- **Ngân hàng thương mại 100% vốn nước ngoài** — 100% foreign-owned commercial bank; a bank licensed in the TTTC that is wholly owned by foreign investors. See [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].[2]
- **Cơ quan điều hành tại thành phố** — City Management Authority; the body responsible for issuing licences to TTTC member institutions. See [[Regulatory Bodies Supervising The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].[2]

## Gaps and Limitations

The source documents reviewed for this page do not contain:

- A standalone AML chapter or dedicated AML provisions within any of the TTTC decrees;
- Explicit references to suspicious transaction reporting (*báo cáo giao dịch đáng ngờ*) requirements;
- Customer due diligence (CDD) or enhanced due diligence (EDD) standards specific to the TTTC;
- References to Vietnam's AML Law (*Luật Phòng, chống rửa tiền*) or FATF standards as they apply within the TTTC;
- Designated compliance officer or AML officer requirements for TTTC members.
