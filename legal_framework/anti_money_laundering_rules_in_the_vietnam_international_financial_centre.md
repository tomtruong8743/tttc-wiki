# Anti Money Laundering Rules in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The [[Vietnam International Financial Centre For Investment Banking Interns|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]] is established and governed by a suite of decrees issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam, including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]]. These decrees collectively form the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] for the TTTC. However, the specific anti-money laundering (AML) rules applicable within the TTTC are not covered in the source documents available for this page.
