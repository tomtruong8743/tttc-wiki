# Supervision Mechanisms in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The supervision mechanisms governing the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) are established through a suite of government decrees issued by the [[Legal Documents Establishing The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Office of the Government of Vietnam]]. These decrees collectively set out the regulatory and supervisory framework across multiple domains, including [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policy]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], and the [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment of the TTTC]] itself. Together, these instruments form the core [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal and supervisory framework]] of the centre.

## Key Points

- The supervision and governance framework of the TTTC is underpinned by a series of decrees all issued by the [[Financial Center Decrees|Office of the Government of Vietnam]] (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational basis for the establishment and, by extension, the oversight structure of the TTTC. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] covers [[Financial Supervision And Governance|financial policies]] applicable within the TTTC, forming a key pillar of financial supervision. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] governs labour, employment, and social security matters within the TTTC, contributing to the supervisory regime for workforce-related matters. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] addresses land and environmental regulation within the TTTC, forming part of the broader supervisory structure. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] sets out immigration policies applicable to the TTTC, governing the entry and stay of foreign workers and experts. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the framework for international arbitration within the TTTC, providing a [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution]] and oversight mechanism. [4]
- All decrees are issued by the same central authority — the Office of the Government of Vietnam — indicating a unified and centralised supervisory approach. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- [[Financial Center Decrees|Decree]] — a binding legal instrument issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) that establishes rules, policies, and supervisory mechanisms applicable within the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|TTTC]] — the Vietnam International Financial Centre, the subject of all six identified decrees and the entity whose activities are governed and supervised by these instruments. [5]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|International Arbitration Centre]] — a mechanism established within the TTTC framework to resolve disputes, forming part of the supervisory and governance architecture. [4]
- [[Regulatory Bodies Supervising The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Cơ quan phát hành]] — the issuing authority; in all cases identified in the source documents, this is the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
