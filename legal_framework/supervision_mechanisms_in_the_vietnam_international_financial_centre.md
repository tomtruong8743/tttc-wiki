# Supervision Mechanisms in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The supervision mechanisms governing the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) are established through a suite of government decrees issued by the [[Legal Documents Establishing The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Office of the Government of Vietnam]]. These decrees collectively set out the regulatory and supervisory framework across multiple domains, including [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policy]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|banking and foreign exchange]], [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|commodities exchange]], and the [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment of the TTTC]] itself. Together, these instruments form the core [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal and supervisory framework]] of the centre.

Two principal supervisory bodies are referenced across the decrees: the **Cơ quan điều hành** (Managing Authority, operating at the city level) and the **Cơ quan giám sát** (Supervisory Authority). These bodies exercise distinct but complementary functions across licensing, operational oversight, enforcement, and suspension of [[Financial Services In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|financial institutions]] operating within the TTTC.

## Key Points

- The supervision and governance framework of the TTTC is underpinned by a series of decrees all issued by the [[Financial Center Decrees|Office of the Government of Vietnam]] (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational basis for the establishment and, by extension, the oversight structure of the TTTC. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] covers [[Financial Supervision And Governance|financial policies]] applicable within the TTTC, forming a key pillar of financial supervision. It also sets out detailed supervisory procedures for [[Securities Activities In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|securities businesses]] operating as Members of the TTTC. [3][5][6]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] governs labour, employment, and social security matters within the TTTC, contributing to the supervisory regime for workforce-related matters. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] addresses land and environmental regulation within the TTTC, forming part of the broader supervisory structure. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] sets out immigration policies applicable to the TTTC, governing the entry and stay of [[Policies For Foreign Experts In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign workers and experts]]. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the framework for international arbitration within the TTTC, providing a [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution]] and oversight mechanism. [4]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] establishes qualification and fitness requirements for senior management of member commercial banks, including Deputy General Directors, Chief Accountants, and equivalent roles, as part of the supervisory framework for [[Banking Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|banking activities]] within the TTTC. [4]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] establishes a dedicated state management framework (**Chương IV: Quản lý Nhà nước**) for the [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange|Commodities Exchange]] within the TTTC, with responsibilities distributed between the Ministry of Industry and Trade (Bộ Công Thương) and the Cơ quan điều hành at the city level. [1][2]
- All decrees are issued by the same central authority — the Office of the Government of Vietnam — indicating a unified and centralised supervisory approach. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## The Two Principal Supervisory Bodies

### Cơ quan điều hành (Managing Authority)

The Cơ quan điều hành operates at the city level and exercises the following supervisory functions across financial institutions and exchanges within the TTTC:

- **Licensing**: Issues, reissues, suspends, and revokes establishment licences for entities such as [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Commodities Exchanges]] and [[Securities Activities In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|securities businesses]] that are Members of the TTTC. [1][6]
- **Pre-launch oversight**: Reviews and approves or rejects operational readiness at least 30 days before a [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Member securities business]] commences operations, covering capital adequacy, premises, staffing, IT systems, and internal procedures including [[Anti Money Laundering Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|anti-money laundering]], counter-terrorist financing, and counter-proliferation financing frameworks. [5]
- **Suspension of launch**: May suspend the opening of a Member securities business if pre-launch conditions under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] are not met. [6]
- **Licence expiry**: Publicly announces on its electronic portal when an establishment and operating licence has lapsed, including where a Member securities business has failed to commence operations within 12 months of licence issuance. [6]
- **Temporary suspension decisions**: Approves or rejects requests from Member securities businesses to temporarily suspend operations within 7 working days of receiving documentation, provided the suspension does not harm client interests. [3]
- **Revocation for prolonged suspension**: Revokes a Member's establishment and operating licence if temporary suspension exceeds 90 days. [3]
- **Commodities Exchange approvals**: Approves the list of Vietnam-produced commodities permitted for trading on the Commodities Exchange, and approves the list of foreign Commodities Exchange contracts eligible for linked trading. [1]
- **Commodity list amendments**: Reviews and decides on additions, amendments, suspensions, or terminations of commodity trading. [1]

### Cơ quan giám sát (Supervisory Authority)

The Cơ quan giám sát operates as a distinct supervisory body with the following functions:

- **Joint inspection**: Coordinates with the Ministry of Industry and Trade to inspect the activities of Commodities Exchanges and commodity trading through those exchanges within the TTTC. [1]
- **State management of securities activities**: Together with the Cơ quan điều hành, exercises state management over the activities of Member securities businesses in the securities sector within the TTTC, within the scope of each body's respective authority. [6]
- **Notification rights**: Member securities businesses must report to the Cơ quan giám sát within 24 hours of resuming operations following a temporary suspension. [3]

### Ministry of Industry and Trade (Bộ Công Thương)

Under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]], the Ministry of Industry and Trade plays a coordinating supervisory role specifically for the [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange|Commodities Exchange]], including:

- Coordinating with the Cơ quan giám sát to inspect Commodities Exchange activities and commodity trading through those exchanges. [1]
- Coordinating with relevant agencies to implement preferential treatment rules and handle violations relating to import-export activities and commodity trading under the decree. [1]

## Supervisory Procedures for Securities Businesses (Members)

[[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] contains detailed procedural provisions governing the lifecycle of Member [[Securities Activities In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|securities businesses]] from a supervisory perspective:

### Pre-Launch Requirements
At least 30 days before commencing operations, a Member securities business must demonstrate: [5]
- Sufficient charter capital, premises, and equipment.
- Adequate qualified personnel across operational divisions.
- Appropriate securities trading software (if required) and IT systems consistent with its approved operational plan.
- Issued internal procedures covering operational workflows, [[Risk Management Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|internal controls]], risk management, and [[Anti Money Laundering Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|AML]]/counter-terrorist financing/counter-proliferation frameworks in accordance with Vietnamese law.
- Public disclosure of its launch on one TTTC media outlet and one Vietnamese online newspaper, covering its name, address, licence details, and intended launch date.

### Temporary Suspension
A Member securities business may only suspend operations temporarily where: [3]
- The suspension does not adversely affect client interests.
- A formal written request is submitted stating the suspension
