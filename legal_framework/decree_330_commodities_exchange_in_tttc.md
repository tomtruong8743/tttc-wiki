# Decree 330 Commodities Exchange in TTTC

## Summary

Decree 330 pertains to the regulation of commodities exchange operations within the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC (Vietnam International Financial Centre)]]. It is one of a series of foundational decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government to establish and govern the legal and operational framework of the TTTC, alongside related instruments such as [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], and [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. The decree sits within the broader [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] governing financial and commercial activities in the centre.

## Key Points

- Decree 330 is identified as part of the series of decrees forming the [[Financial Center Decrees]] that collectively govern the TTTC. [1]
- The decree is issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), consistent with the issuing authority for all other TTTC-related decrees. [1]
- Decree 330 specifically addresses the [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange]] framework within the TTTC, distinguishing it from banking and foreign exchange regulations covered under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. [1]
- The commodities exchange regime operates within the broader financial and investment environment established by [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], which governs financial policies across the TTTC. [1]
