# Decree 330 Commodities Exchange in TTTC

## Summary

Decree 330 pertains to the regulation of commodities exchange operations within the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC (Vietnam International Financial Centre)]]. It is one of a series of foundational decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government to establish and govern the legal and operational framework of the TTTC, alongside related instruments such as [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], and [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. The decree sits within the broader [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] governing financial and commercial activities in the centre.

> **Note:** The source excerpts currently available do not contain the specific text of Decree 330. The content of this page is drawn from contextual references in the broader TTTC decree series. Information on related decrees has been updated with newly available source material as described below.

## Key Points

- Decree 330 is identified as part of the series of decrees forming the [[Financial Center Decrees]] that collectively govern the TTTC. [1]
- The decree is issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), consistent with the issuing authority for all other TTTC-related decrees. [1]
- Decree 330 specifically addresses the [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange]] framework within the TTTC, distinguishing it from banking and [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange]] regulations covered under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], and from [[Capital Market Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital market and securities activities]] covered under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]. [1]
- The commodities exchange regime operates within the broader financial and investment environment established by [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], which governs [[Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policies]] across the TTTC. [1]

## Related Decree Provisions (from Available Sources)

While the text of Decree 330 itself is not yet available, the newly available source excerpts provide additional context on the regulatory environment within which the TTTC commodities exchange framework sits:

### Securities Member Organisations (Decree 324)

- Investors applying for a licence to establish and operate a securities business organisation as a Member (Thành viên) of the TTTC must submit audited annual financial statements for the two most recent years. Where the investor is a parent company, consolidated audited financial statements for the two most recent years are required. [1]
- Applications must include a draft charter (*Điều lệ*) for the securities business organisation consistent with applicable law. [1]
- A list of proposed personnel must be submitted, including copies of passports for foreign personnel and copies of Vietnamese securities practice certificates or equivalent foreign authorisations. [1]
- The [[Business Licensing And Registration|licensing procedure]] for securities member organisations (Article 19 of Decree 324) requires submission of application documents to the Management Authority (*Cơ quan điều hành*) — either in person at the Authority's headquarters, by post, or via an online system. The Authority has 05 working days from receipt to process the application. [1]
- Private placement of shares within the TTTC is subject to transfer restrictions: strategic investors face a minimum lock-up of 03 years, and institutional investors face a minimum lock-up of 01 year from the date the offering is completed. Transfers back to Vietnamese investors who are not Members are not permitted. [6]
- Private placement share offerings by innovative start-up enterprises (*doanh nghiệp khởi nghiệp sáng tạo*) within the TTTC must be approved by the competent authority in accordance with the enterprise's charter. [6]

### Foreign Exchange and Lending (Decree 329)

- Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], TTTC Members may borrow foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to information disclosure and reporting obligations. [3]
- Members that are 100% foreign-investor-owned may lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to reporting requirements. [3]
- Members that are not 100% foreign-investor-owned must comply with the conditions for cross-border lending set out in Article 89 of Decree 329, including requirements around foreign currency lending to offshore entities with majority Vietnamese shareholding. [3]
- Member banks are subject to prudential ratios including the Leverage Ratio (LEV), Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR), and Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR). The [[Supervision Mechanisms In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|supervisory authority]] may impose stricter ratios where necessary. [4]
- Member banks applying International Accounting Standards are exempt from requirements to purchase and hold Vietnamese Government bonds or Government-guaranteed bonds under Vietnamese law. [4]
- Member banks are not required to comply with the maximum foreign currency position relative to equity capital under Vietnamese law. [4]

### Labour and Social Security (Decree 325)

- Under [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], foreign workers at the TTTC who participate in compulsory social insurance may cease participation after a minimum of 12 months of contributions, upon request. [2]
- Foreign workers may adjust the salary basis for compulsory social insurance contributions after at least 12 months at the chosen contribution level, upon request. [2]
- Foreign workers may cease compulsory social insurance participation to receive a lump-sum social insurance payment in specified circumstances, including having contributed for at least 12 months or no longer working at the TTTC. [2]
- The contribution ceiling for compulsory social insurance is set at a maximum of 20 times the reference level at the time of contribution, as provided under the Social Insurance Law No. 41/2024/QH15. [2]

### Governance of Member Banks (Decree 329)

- The Supervisory Board (*Ban kiểm soát*) of a member commercial bank must have a minimum of 03 members, with the number determined by the bank's charter. [5]
- The Supervisory Board must include an internal audit unit and a support unit. [5]
- The term of the Supervisory Board must not exceed 05 years. If membership falls below the minimum, the bank must restore minimum membership within 90 days. [5]
- The Supervisory Board must promptly report to the City Management Authority (*Cơ quan điều hành tại thành phố*) and the Supervisory Authority (*Cơ quan giám sát*) as required under Article 52(13) of the Law on Credit Institutions. [5]

## Definitions

- **Thành viên** — "Member"; refers to financial institutions and business organisations that are admitted members of the TTTC and subject to its regulatory framework.
- **Cơ quan điều hành** — "Management Authority"; the body responsible for administering licensing and operational matters within the TTTC.
- **Cơ quan giám sát** — "Supervisory Authority"; the body responsible for prudential oversight of member institutions within the TTTC.
- **Doanh nghiệp khởi nghiệp sáng tạo** — "Innovative start-up enterprise"; a category of enterprise with specific capital-raising permissions under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]].
- **Ban kiểm soát** — "Supervisory Board"; the governance body within a member bank responsible for oversight and internal audit functions.
