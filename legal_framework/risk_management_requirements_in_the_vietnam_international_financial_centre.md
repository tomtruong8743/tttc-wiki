# Risk Management Requirements in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) is governed by a suite of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office ([[Financial Center Decrees|VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ]]), including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]]. These decrees collectively form the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] within which [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|compliance]] and risk management obligations for institutions operating in the TTTC are situated.

## Key Points

- The decrees governing the TTTC were issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ), establishing the official regulatory authority behind the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]]. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational establishment framework within which risk management obligations for institutions in the TTTC are embedded. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] addresses financial policies applicable within the TTTC, which may include requirements relevant to [[Financial Supervision And Governance]]. Registration documentation under this decree includes a physical infrastructure statement (Bản thuyết minh cơ sở vật chất) requiring registering organisations to disclose details of their office premises, technical equipment, software systems, and platforms used for capital-raising and asset management activities — representing an operational risk disclosure requirement. [1][5]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] covers labour, employment, and social security matters within the TTTC, representing a distinct area of operational risk management for participating entities.
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] governs land and environmental matters within the TTTC, relevant to [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|environmental risk]] considerations.
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] establishes immigration policies applicable to the TTTC, forming part of the broader [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|compliance]] environment. The decree includes prescribed forms — notably Form NT-01 — for managing the permanent residency of foreign nationals working at the TTTC, requiring institutions to identify whether individuals are important investors, experts, senior managers, scientists, or persons with special talent. This categorisation has implications for [[Attracting International Financial Talent To Tttc|talent risk management]] and [[Immigration And Residency Policies For Tttc Workers|immigration compliance]]. [4][6]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, providing a [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution]] mechanism relevant to legal and counterparty risk management.
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] contains personnel qualification requirements for [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|member commercial banks]] operating within the TTTC. Deputy Directors, Chief Accountants, and equivalent positions must hold at least a university-level qualification in finance, banking, economics, business administration, law, accounting, or auditing — or a university degree in another field combined with at least three years of direct experience in finance or banking. This constitutes a governance and personnel risk management requirement. [3]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] introduces specific risk management considerations for the [[Capital Market Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|commodities exchange]] operating within the TTTC. Goods subject to specialised inspection — including food safety, medical, environmental, agricultural, scientific-technological, national defence, and security categories — must comply with the procedures and oversight of the relevant specialised regulatory authority. The Ministry of Industry and Trade (Bộ Công Thương) is responsible for coordinating with the supervisory authority to inspect the operations of the commodities exchange and to handle violations. The local Operating Authority (Cơ quan điều hành) at each city has powers to grant, reissue, suspend, and revoke establishment licences for commodities exchanges, and to approve the list of eligible commodities for trading. [2]

## Definitions

- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ]] — The Vietnamese Government Office; the issuing authority for the suite of decrees governing the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|TTTC]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, established under the relevant government decrees as a special financial and regulatory zone. [5]
- [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|International Arbitration Centre]] — A body established under [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] to handle dispute resolution within the TTTC, relevant to legal risk management.
- **Bản thuyết minh cơ sở vật chất** — Physical infrastructure disclosure statement required under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], documenting office space, technical equipment, software systems, and operational platforms; relevant to operational risk assessment for registering entities. [1][5]
- **Form NT-01** — A prescribed form under [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] used by organisations to apply for permanent residency for foreign nationals working at the TTTC; requires categorisation of the individual's role and duration of work at the centre. [4][6]
- **Cơ quan điều hành** — The Operating Authority at each participating city, responsible under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] for licensing and supervising commodities exchange activities, including the power to suspend or revoke licences. [2]
- **Bộ Công Thương** — The Ministry of Industry and Trade, responsible for coordinating inspection of commodities exchange operations and enforcement of trading rules under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]]. [2]
