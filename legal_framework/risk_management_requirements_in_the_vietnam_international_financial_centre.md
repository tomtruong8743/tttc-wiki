# Risk Management Requirements in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) is governed by a suite of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office ([[Financial Center Decrees|VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ]]), including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]]. These decrees collectively form the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] within which [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|compliance]] and risk management obligations for institutions operating in the TTTC are situated.

## Key Points

- The decrees governing the TTTC were issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ), establishing the official regulatory authority behind the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]]. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational establishment framework within which risk management obligations for institutions in the TTTC are embedded. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] addresses financial policies applicable within the TTTC, which may include requirements relevant to [[Financial Supervision And Governance]]. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] covers labour, employment, and social security matters within the TTTC, representing a distinct area of operational risk management for participating entities. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] governs land and environmental matters within the TTTC, relevant to [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|environmental risk]] considerations. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] establishes immigration policies applicable to the TTTC, forming part of the broader [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|compliance]] environment. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, providing a [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution]] mechanism relevant to legal and counterparty risk management. [4]

## Definitions

- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ]] — The Vietnamese Government Office; the issuing authority for the suite of decrees governing the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|TTTC]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, established under the relevant government decrees as a special financial and regulatory zone. [5]
- [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|International Arbitration Centre]] — A body established under [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] to handle dispute resolution within the TTTC, relevant to legal risk management. [4]
