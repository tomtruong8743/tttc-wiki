# Dispute Resolution in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Dispute resolution in the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) is governed by a dedicated legal framework established by the Vietnamese Government Office. A key instrument in this framework is [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], which specifically addresses the establishment and operation of an international arbitration centre within the TTTC. This decree sits alongside a broader suite of founding legislation — including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], and [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] — that collectively form the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] for the financial centre.

## Key Points

- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] was issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ) and specifically concerns the international arbitration centre operating within the TTTC. [4]
- The dispute resolution framework is part of a broader set of [[Financial Center Decrees|decrees]] establishing and regulating the TTTC, all issued by the same issuing authority — the Vietnamese Government Office. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis upon which the TTTC, including its [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]], operates. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], and [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] form complementary pillars of regulation alongside the dispute resolution provisions. [1][2][3][6]
- The existence of a dedicated international arbitration decree ([[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]]) suggests that the TTTC offers [[Special Legal Mechanisms In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] for resolving commercial and financial disputes. [4]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] **Trung tâm trọng tài quốc tế (International Arbitration Centre)** — the dedicated arbitration body established within the TTTC framework, governed by Decree 328, intended to resolve international disputes arising within the financial centre. [4]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (Vietnamese Government Office)** — the issuing authority responsible for promulgating all foundational decrees related to the TTTC, including those covering dispute resolution. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] **TTTC Legal Framework** — the collective body of decrees (323–328 and beyond) issued by the Vietnamese Government Office that establishes and regulates all aspects of the Vietnam International Financial Centre. [5]
