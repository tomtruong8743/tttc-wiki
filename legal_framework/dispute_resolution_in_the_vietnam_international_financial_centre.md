# Dispute Resolution in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Dispute resolution in the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) is governed by a dedicated legal framework established by the Vietnamese Government Office. A key instrument in this framework is [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], which specifically addresses the establishment and operation of an international arbitration centre within the TTTC. This decree sits alongside a broader suite of founding legislation — including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange]] — that collectively form the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] for the financial centre.

## Key Points

- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] was issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ) and specifically concerns the international arbitration centre operating within the TTTC. [4]
- The dispute resolution framework is part of a broader set of [[Financial Center Decrees|decrees]] establishing and regulating the TTTC, all issued by the same issuing authority — the Vietnamese Government Office. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis upon which the TTTC, including its [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]], operates. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], and [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] form complementary pillars of regulation alongside the dispute resolution provisions. [1][2][3][6]
- The existence of a dedicated international arbitration decree ([[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]]) suggests that the TTTC offers [[Special Legal Mechanisms In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] for resolving commercial and financial disputes. [4]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange]] governs the establishment and operation of the [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Commodities Exchange]] within the TTTC, including enforcement of violations related to import/export activities and commodities trading — areas where dispute resolution mechanisms may be relevant. [1]
- Under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange]], the Ministry of Industry and Trade (Bộ Công Thương) is responsible for cooperating with the [[Supervision Mechanisms In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|supervisory authority]] to inspect and oversee the operations of the Commodities Exchange, including handling violations. [1]
- The [[Regulatory Bodies Supervising The Vietnam International Financial Centre|operating authority]] at the city level holds powers to grant, renew, suspend, and revoke establishment licences for the Commodities Exchange — a form of administrative dispute and enforcement mechanism within the TTTC. [1]
- Conditions for enjoying tax and customs incentives under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange]] are linked to compliance with [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], indicating that financial policy and dispute resolution frameworks are interconnected across the TTTC's regulatory structure. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] assigns implementation responsibilities to Ministers, heads of ministerial-level agencies, and the Chairmen of the People's Committees of [[Ho Chi Minh City Vs Da Nang In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Ho Chi Minh City and Da Nang]] — both cities involved in the TTTC — with oversight extending to the Supreme People's Court and Supreme People's Procuracy, underscoring the role of Vietnamese judicial institutions alongside arbitration. [3]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] contains provisions related to accounting standards and regulatory compliance for banking institutions in the TTTC, breaches of which may give rise to disputes requiring resolution under the applicable frameworks. [2]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] **Trung tâm trọng tài quốc tế (International Arbitration Centre)** — the dedicated arbitration body established within the TTTC framework, governed by Decree 328, intended to resolve international disputes arising within the financial centre. [4]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (Vietnamese Government Office)** — the issuing authority responsible for promulgating all foundational decrees related to the TTTC, including those covering dispute resolution. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] **TTTC Legal Framework** — the collective body of decrees (323–330 and beyond) issued by the Vietnamese Government Office that establishes and regulates all aspects of the Vietnam International Financial Centre. [5]
- **Bộ Công Thương (Ministry of Industry and Trade)** — the ministry responsible for cooperating with the supervisory authority to inspect and handle violations related to commodities exchange activities within the TTTC. [1]
- **Cơ quan điều hành tại các thành phố (Operating Authority at the Cities)** — the city-level regulatory body with powers to grant, revoke, or suspend licences for entities such as the Commodities Exchange, functioning as an administrative enforcement and dispute mechanism within the TTTC. [1]
