# Compliance Requirements in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) operates under a framework of government-issued decrees that collectively establish the compliance requirements for institutions and individuals operating within it. These decrees, all issued by the [[Legal Documents Establishing The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Office of the Government]] (Văn phòng Chính phủ), cover areas including [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policy]], [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|banking and foreign exchange]], [[Foreign Worker Regulations|labour and social security]], [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration]], and [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|international arbitration]]. Together, they form the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal and regulatory backbone]] of the TTTC, defining what participants must adhere to in order to operate lawfully within the centre.

## Key Points

- The compliance framework of the TTTC is established through a series of government decrees, all issued by the Office of the Government (Văn phòng Chính phủ). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] governs the establishment of the TTTC itself, forming the foundational compliance instrument. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out the [[Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|financial policies]] applicable within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] addresses [[Foreign Worker Regulations|labour, employment, and social security]] obligations for entities operating in the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] covers [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|land use and environmental]] compliance requirements. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] establishes the rules governing [[Foreign Worker Regulations|immigration and entry/exit]] policies within the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] provides the framework for the [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|international arbitration centre]] associated with the TTTC. [4]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] — the foundational decree establishing the Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC), issued by the Office of the Government. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] — the decree governing financial policies within the TTTC, issued by the Office of the Government. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]] — the decree covering labour, employment, and social security matters within the TTTC, issued by the Office of the Government. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]] — the decree addressing land and environmental regulations within the TTTC, issued by the Office of the Government. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] — the decree establishing immigration and entry/exit policies for the TTTC, issued by the Office of the Government. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] — the decree establishing the international arbitration centre within the TTTC, issued by the Office of the Government. [4]
- **Văn phòng Chính phủ** — the Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all TTTC-related decrees listed in the source documents. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
