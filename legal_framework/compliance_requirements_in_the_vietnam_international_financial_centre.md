# Compliance Requirements in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) operates under a framework of government-issued decrees that collectively establish the compliance requirements for institutions and individuals operating within it. These decrees, all issued by the [[Legal Documents Establishing The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Office of the Government]] (Văn phòng Chính phủ), cover areas including [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policy]], [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|banking and foreign exchange]], [[Foreign Worker Regulations|labour and social security]], [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration]], [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|international arbitration]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|commodities exchange]]. Together, they form the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal and regulatory backbone]] of the TTTC, defining what participants must adhere to in order to operate lawfully within the centre.

## Key Points

- The compliance framework of the TTTC is established through a series of government decrees, all issued by the Office of the Government (Văn phòng Chính phủ). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] governs the establishment of the TTTC itself, forming the foundational compliance instrument. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out the [[Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|financial policies]] applicable within the TTTC, including tax, accounting, and sector-specific financial rules. [6][8]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] addresses [[Foreign Worker Regulations|labour, employment, and social security]] obligations for entities operating in the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] covers [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|land use and environmental]] compliance requirements. [4]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] establishes the rules governing [[Immigration And Residency Policies For Tttc Workers|immigration and entry/exit]] policies within the TTTC, including a standardised application form (Form NT-01) for granting permanent residency to foreign nationals working in the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] provides the framework for the [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|international arbitration centre]] associated with the TTTC. [5]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] governs [[Banking Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|banking licensing]], establishment, and [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]] within the TTTC, including qualification requirements for senior bank executives and rules on cross-border lending and borrowing. [2][4]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] establishes compliance conditions for the [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange|commodities exchange]] within the TTTC, including conditions for preferential treatment, customs procedures, and sector-specific inspection requirements. [1]
- Members investing in restricted or conditional sectors under Vietnamese law must comply with applicable investment legislation; relaxation of market access conditions requires approval from the relevant sectoral ministry. [6]
- Members must obtain written approval from the Operating Authority (Cơ quan điều hành) before undertaking outbound investment activities, unless sector-specific decrees provide otherwise. [6]
- Investment from the rest of Vietnam into the TTTC is governed by [[Financial Center Decrees|Resolution 222/2025/QH15]] and its implementing decrees. [6]
- [[Reporting Obligations For Financial Institutions In Tttc|Reporting and disclosure obligations]] apply to members borrowing foreign currency from individuals or organisations outside Vietnamese territory, as well as to members engaged in cross-border lending. [4]
- Insolvency and dissolution procedures for insurance enterprises, reinsurance enterprises, and insurance brokers within the TTTC follow a prescribed priority order for settling debts, including wages, insurance payouts, tax liabilities, and other obligations, before formal dissolution is conducted under the guidance of the Operating Authority. [7]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] — the foundational decree establishing the Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC), issued by the Office of the Government. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] — the decree governing financial policies within the TTTC, including [[Corporate Income Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|corporate income tax]], [[Personal Income Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|personal income tax]], accounting, and sector-specific financial rules, issued by the Office of the Government. [6][7][8]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]] — the decree covering labour, employment, and social security matters within the TTTC, issued by the Office of the Government. [3]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]] — the decree addressing land and environmental regulations within the TTTC, issued by the Office of the Government. [4]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] — the decree establishing immigration and entry/exit policies for the TTTC, issued by the Office of the Government on 18 December 2025; includes Form NT-01 for permanent residency applications by foreign nationals working in the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] — the decree establishing the international arbitration centre within the TTTC, issued by the Office of the Government. [5]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] — the decree governing banking licensing, establishment, and foreign exchange management within the TTTC, including qualification standards for senior executives of member commercial banks and rules on cross-border borrowing and lending. [2][4]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Decree 330]] — the decree governing the establishment and operation of the commodities exchange (Sở giao dịch hàng hóa) within the TTTC, including conditions for preferential treatment and state management responsibilities. [1]
- **Văn phòng Chính phủ** — the Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all TTTC-related decrees listed in the source documents. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- **Cơ quan điều hành** — the Operating Authority; the body responsible for approving establishment applications, issuing and revoking licences, approving commodity and contract lists for the commodities exchange, and overseeing dissolution procedures within the TTTC. [1][7]
- **Form NT-01** — the standardised application form under [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] for requesting permanent residency status for foreign nationals working in the TTTC. [3]
- **Thành viên** — "Member"; the term used in the decrees to refer to institutions and individuals participating in the TTTC who are subject to its compliance requirements. [4][6]

## Compliance Areas

### Financial and Tax Compliance
Members operating within the TTTC are subject to the [[Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|tax and financial policies]] set out in [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]. This includes a [[Corporate Income Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|corporate income tax rate of 10% for 30 years]], with a maximum tax exemption period of 4 years and a 50% tax reduction for up to 9 subsequent years. [8] Members investing in sectors on the restricted or conditional investment list under general Vietnamese law must comply with applicable investment regulations, and any relaxation of market access conditions requires approval by the relevant ministry. [6]

### Banking and Foreign Exchange Compliance
[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] imposes qualification requirements on senior executives of member commercial banks, including deputy directors-general, chief accountants, and equivalent positions. These individuals must hold at least a bachelor's degree in finance, banking, economics, business administration, law, accounting, auditing, or a relevant specialist field — or a degree in another field combined with at least three years of direct experience in finance, banking, or the relevant specialism. [2] They must also not fall within categories of persons prohibited from holding such positions under the decree. [2]
