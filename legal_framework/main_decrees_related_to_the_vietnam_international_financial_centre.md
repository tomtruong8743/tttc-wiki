# Main Decrees Related to the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary
The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) is governed by a series of decrees issued by the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Vietnamese Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ)]]. These decrees collectively establish the [[Legal Documents Establishing The Vietnam International Financial Centre|legal foundation]] for the TTTC, covering key areas such as its [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policies]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment|land and environment]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|labour and social security]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|banking and foreign exchange]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange|commodities exchange]]. Together, they form the comprehensive [[Financial Center Decrees|regulatory framework]] underpinning the TTTC's operations.

---

## Key Points
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] concerns the establishment of the TTTC and was issued by the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ)]] [5].
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] addresses [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]] within the TTTC, also issued by the Government Office [1].
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|Decree 325]] addresses [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|labour, employment, and social security]] policies within the TTTC, and was issued by the Government Office. Responsible officials include the Chairmen of the People's Committees of [[Ho Chi Minh City Vs Da Nang In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Ho Chi Minh City and Da Nang]] [6].
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment|Decree 326]] covers [[Land Use Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|land]] and [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|environmental]] regulations applicable to the TTTC, issued by the Government Office [3].
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] sets out [[Immigration And Residency Policies For Tttc Workers|immigration (entry and exit) policies]] for the TTTC, issued by the Government Office [2].
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] establishes and governs the [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|International Arbitration Centre]] within the TTTC. The Centre has jurisdiction to resolve disputes related to investment and business activities at the TTTC by agreement between parties, but does not resolve disputes involving administrative decisions, labour matters, personal rights, or matters already decided by a competent Vietnamese state authority or court [5].
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] covers licensing, establishment of banks, and [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]] within the TTTC. It sets out detailed standards and conditions for the appointment of key management personnel, including board members, supervisory board members, directors, deputy directors, and chief accountants of member commercial banks [2][3].
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Decree 330]] governs the establishment and operation of the [[Capital Market Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Commodities Exchange]] (Sở giao dịch hàng hóa) within the TTTC. It includes conditions for tax incentives on export and import duties, cross-referencing the financial policies decree, and provisions on specialised inspection of goods. State management responsibilities are shared between the Ministry of Industry and Trade (Bộ Công Thương) and the operating authority in each city [1][4].
- All known decrees related to the TTTC share a common issuing authority: **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** (the Government Office of Vietnam) [1][2][3][4][5][6].

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|**Decree 323**]] — The decree concerning the establishment (thành lập) of the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) [5].
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|**Decree 324**]] — The decree addressing [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]] (chính sách tài chính) within the TTTC [1].
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|**Decree 325**]] — The decree covering [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|labour, employment, and social welfare]] (lao động, việc làm và an sinh xã hội) within the TTTC. Implementation responsibility is placed on the Chairmen of the People's Committees of [[Cities Involved In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Ho Chi Minh City and Da Nang]], among other officials [6].
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|**Decree 326**]] — The decree governing [[Land Use Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|land]] and [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|environmental matters]] (đất đai và môi trường) within the TTTC [3].
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|**Decree 327**]] — The decree setting out [[Immigration And Residency Policies For Tttc Workers|entry and exit]] (xuất nhập cảnh) policies for the TTTC [2].
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|**Decree 328**]] — The decree establishing the [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|International Arbitration Centre]] (Trung tâm trọng tài quốc tế) within the TTTC. The Centre may not resolve disputes involving administrative acts, labour matters, personal rights of individuals, or matters already resolved by a competent state or court authority in Vietnam [5].
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|**Decree 329**]] — The decree on licensing, establishment of banks, and [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|foreign exchange management]] (quản lý ngoại hối) within the TTTC. Establishes detailed [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|professional ethics and qualification standards]] for key management personnel of member commercial banks [2][3].
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|**Decree 330**]] — The decree on the establishment and operation of the Commodities Exchange (Sở giao dịch hàng hóa) within the TTTC. Includes provisions on [[Conditions For Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|conditions for tax incentives]], specialised goods inspection, and state management responsibilities [1][4].
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Government Office of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all TTTC-related decrees listed here [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|**TTTC**]] — Abbreviation for Trung Tâm Tài Chính, the Vietnam International Financial Centre [5].
- **Sở giao dịch hàng hóa** — Commodities Exchange; the entity established and governed under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Decree 330]] within the TTTC [4].
- **Trung tâm trọng tài quốc tế** — International Arbitration Centre; established under [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] to resolve [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|investment and business disputes]] within the TTTC [5].
