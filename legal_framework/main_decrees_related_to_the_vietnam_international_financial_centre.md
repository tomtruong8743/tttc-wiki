# Main Decrees Related to the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary
The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) is governed by a series of decrees issued by the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Vietnamese Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ)]]. These decrees collectively establish the [[Legal Documents Establishing The Vietnam International Financial Centre|legal foundation]] for the TTTC, covering key areas such as its [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment]], [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment|land and environment]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|labour and social security]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]]. Together, they form the comprehensive [[Financial Center Decrees|regulatory framework]] underpinning the TTTC's operations.

---

## Key Points
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] concerns the establishment of the TTTC and was issued by the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ)]] [5].
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|Decree 325]] addresses labour, employment, and social security policies within the TTTC, and was issued by the Government Office [6].
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment|Decree 326]] covers land and environmental regulations applicable to the TTTC, issued by the Government Office [3].
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] sets out immigration (entry and exit) policies for the TTTC, issued by the Government Office [2].
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] establishes and governs the International Arbitration Centre within the TTTC, issued by the Government Office [4].
- A further decree, referenced as Decree 324, addresses [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]] within the TTTC, also issued by the Government Office [1].
- All known decrees related to the TTTC share a common issuing authority: **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** (the Government Office of Vietnam) [1][2][3][4][5][6].

---

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|**Decree 323**]] — The decree concerning the establishment (thành lập) of the Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) [5].
- [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|**Decree 324**]] — The decree addressing financial policies (chính sách tài chính) within the TTTC [1].
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|**Decree 325**]] — The decree covering labour, employment, and social welfare (lao động, việc làm và an sinh xã hội) within the TTTC [6].
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment|**Decree 326**]] — The decree governing land and environmental matters (đất đai và môi trường) within the TTTC [3].
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|**Decree 327**]] — The decree setting out entry and exit (xuất nhập cảnh) policies for the TTTC [2].
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|**Decree 328**]] — The decree establishing the International Arbitration Centre (Trung tâm trọng tài quốc tế) within the TTTC [4].
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Government Office of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all TTTC-related decrees listed here [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|**TTTC**]] — Abbreviation for Trung Tâm Tài Chính, the Vietnam International Financial Centre [5].
