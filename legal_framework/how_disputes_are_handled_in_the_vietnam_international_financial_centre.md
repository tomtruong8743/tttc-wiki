# How Disputes Are Handled in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre ([[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|TTTC]]) has dedicated mechanisms for resolving disputes that arise within its jurisdiction. A key element of this framework is the establishment of a specialised [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|international arbitration centre]], governed by [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. This centre forms part of the broader [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal framework]] underpinning the TTTC, alongside other foundational decrees covering areas such as [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policies]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|banking and foreign exchange]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange|commodities exchange]].

Beyond arbitration, the TTTC framework also contains provisions for handling violations and enforcement actions within specific sectors. [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out procedures for the suspension and cessation of activities by member securities businesses, while [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] establishes state management responsibilities — including the handling of violations — related to commodities exchange operations. [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] includes governance and compliance requirements for member commercial banks that are relevant to avoiding and resolving disputes.

## Key Points

- The primary legal instrument governing dispute resolution within the TTTC is [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ). [4-src]
- An [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|international arbitration centre]] has been specifically established within the TTTC framework to handle disputes. [4-src]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] is one of a suite of decrees forming the [[Legal Documents Establishing The Vietnam International Financial Centre|legal foundation]] of the TTTC, alongside [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange]]. [4-src][5-src][1-src][6-src][3-src][2-src]
- All founding decrees, including those related to dispute resolution, were issued by the same issuing authority: the Office of the Government of Vietnam (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ). [1-src][2-src][3-src][4-src][5-src][6-src]
- Under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], member [[Securities Activities In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|securities businesses]] may only suspend operations if client interests are not affected, and must follow a formal process including notifying the [[Regulatory Bodies Supervising The Vietnam International Financial Centre|supervisory authority]] within 24 hours of resuming operations. Suspensions are capped at 90 days, after which the operating licence may be revoked. [5]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] assigns the Ministry of Industry and Trade (Bộ Công Thương) a role in inspecting commodities exchange activities and cooperating with relevant authorities to handle violations related to import/export and commodities trading. [1]
- The operating authority ([[Cơ quan điều hành]]) in each city under the TTTC framework holds powers to grant, suspend, and revoke establishment licences for [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|commodities exchanges]], and to review and decide on modifications or terminations of trading activities. [1]
- Tax and customs incentive conditions under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] — including those subject to specialist inspection (food safety, health, environment, agriculture, science and technology, defence, and security) — are subject to compliance with the procedures and oversight of the relevant specialist regulatory authority. [1]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] contains governance restrictions on members of the Board of Directors and Supervisory Board of member commercial banks, including rules on concurrent positions, which help prevent conflicts of interest that could give rise to disputes. [3][4-src]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] — The specific government decree governing the establishment and operation of the international arbitration centre within the TTTC, issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. [4-src]
- [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|International Arbitration Centre]] — A specialised dispute resolution body established within the TTTC to handle conflicts arising in the financial centre's jurisdiction. [4-src]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all TTTC-related decrees, including those on dispute resolution. [4-src]
- [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Dispute Resolution]] — The formal process by which conflicts between parties operating within the TTTC are adjudicated or settled, primarily through the international arbitration mechanism. [4-src]
- **Cơ quan điều hành** — The operating/administrative authority at each TTTC city, which holds powers over licensing, suspension, and revocation relevant to enforcement and dispute-adjacent decisions. [1]
- **Cơ quan giám sát** — The supervisory authority within the TTTC framework, which cooperates with the Ministry of Industry and Trade and the operating authority on inspections and enforcement. [1][5]
- **Đình chỉ hoạt động** — Suspension of operations; a formal enforcement action that may be imposed on member securities businesses or other entities within the TTTC in cases of non-compliance. [5]

## Enforcement and Violation Handling

In addition to the [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|international arbitration mechanism]], the TTTC framework contains sector-specific enforcement provisions:

### Securities Businesses
Under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], a member [[Securities Activities In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|securities business]] may voluntarily suspend operations for up to 90 days, subject to strict conditions protecting client interests. These include:
- Demonstrating that the suspension does not harm clients
- Submitting a suspension plan and a plan for handling existing client contracts
- Providing documentation confirming the transfer of asset management responsibilities to a replacement member entity
- The operating authority (Cơ quan điều hành) must respond within 7 working days; if refused, reasons must be provided in writing
- The entity must notify both the operating authority and [[Supervision Mechanisms In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|supervisory authority]] within 24 hours of resuming activity
- Exceeding the 90-day limit results in revocation of the establishment and operating licence [5]

### Commodities Exchange
Under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]]:
- The Ministry of Industry and Trade (Bộ Công Thương) cooperates with the supervisory authority to inspect [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|commodities exchange]] activities and handles violations related to import/export and commodities trading
- The operating authority in each TTTC city reviews and approves establishment applications, issues and revokes licences, approves lists of tradeable goods and linked foreign exchange contracts, and decides on additions, amendments, suspensions, or terminations of trading activities [1]

### Banking Governance
[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] imposes restrictions on concurrent positions for members of the Board of Directors, Members' Council, and Supervisory Board of member commercial banks. These restrictions help reduce conflicts of interest and governance failures that could lead to disputes. [3][4-src]
