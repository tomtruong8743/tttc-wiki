# How Disputes Are Handled in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre ([[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|TTTC]]) has dedicated mechanisms for resolving disputes that arise within its jurisdiction. A key element of this framework is the establishment of a specialised [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|international arbitration centre]], governed by [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. This centre forms part of the broader [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal framework]] underpinning the TTTC, alongside other foundational decrees covering areas such as [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policies]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], and [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]].

## Key Points

- The primary legal instrument governing dispute resolution within the TTTC is [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ). [4]
- An [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|international arbitration centre]] has been specifically established within the TTTC framework to handle disputes. [4]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] is one of a suite of decrees forming the [[Legal Documents Establishing The Vietnam International Financial Centre|legal foundation]] of the TTTC, alongside [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], and [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]]. [4][5][1][6][3][2]
- All founding decrees, including those related to dispute resolution, were issued by the same issuing authority: the Office of the Government of Vietnam (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ). [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] — The specific government decree governing the establishment and operation of the international arbitration centre within the TTTC, issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. [4]
- [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|International Arbitration Centre]] — A specialised dispute resolution body established within the TTTC to handle conflicts arising in the financial centre's jurisdiction. [4]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all TTTC-related decrees, including those on dispute resolution. [4]
- [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Dispute Resolution]] — The formal process by which conflicts between parties operating within the TTTC are adjudicated or settled, primarily through the international arbitration mechanism. [4]
