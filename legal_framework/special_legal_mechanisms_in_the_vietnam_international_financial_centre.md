# Special Legal Mechanisms in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) operates under a suite of special legal mechanisms established through a series of government decrees issued by the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Office of the Government of Vietnam]]. These mechanisms cover a broad range of policy areas — including [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]], [[Foreign Investment Rules In Tttc]], [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment policy]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], and [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]] — each governed by a dedicated decree under the [[Legal Documents Establishing The Vietnam International Financial Centre|TTTC legal framework]]. Together, these instruments create a distinct and specialised legal environment intended to attract international financial activity to Vietnam.

A tenth decree, [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]], establishes the framework for a commodities exchange (Sở giao dịch hàng hóa) within the TTTC, adding a further specialised mechanism. [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] provides detailed rules on banking licensing and [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]], including rules on cross-border borrowing and lending by TTTC members.

## Key Points

- The [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] was issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) and serves as the foundational legal instrument establishing the TTTC. [5-orig]
- The [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out the financial policy framework applicable within the TTTC, and was issued by the same authority. Tax and customs incentive conditions for goods traded through the commodities exchange are linked directly to this decree. [1-orig][3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] establishes special immigration and entry/exit (xuất nhập cảnh) policies applicable within the TTTC. [2-orig]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] governs [[Land Use Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|land use]] and [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|environmental regulations]] specific to the TTTC. [3-orig]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the framework for an international [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|arbitration centre]] operating within the TTTC, providing a dedicated [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution mechanism]]. [4-orig]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] addresses [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|labour, employment]], and social security policies within the TTTC. [6-orig]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] governs the establishment and operation of the commodities exchange (Sở giao dịch hàng hóa) within the TTTC. Conditions for enjoying preferential treatment — including export and import tax exemptions and customs procedure benefits — are set out in Article 18 of this decree, which cross-references [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] for the implementation of those incentives. [3]
- Under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]], the Ministry of Industry and Trade (Bộ Công Thương) is responsible for coordinating inspection of commodities exchange activities, while the local Operating Authority (Cơ quan điều hành) in each city is responsible for issuing, suspending, and revoking establishment licences, and for approving the list of domestically produced goods eligible for trading and lists of foreign exchange-linked contracts. [3]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] contains detailed [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|foreign exchange (ngoại hối)]] management rules for TTTC members, including rules governing cross-border borrowing and lending. Members may borrow foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to disclosure and reporting obligations. [4][5]
- Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], members that are 100% foreign-investor-owned are permitted to lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to reporting requirements. Members not fully foreign-owned must satisfy additional conditions set out in Article 89 of the decree before lending abroad. [4]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] also sets out qualification and conduct standards for senior personnel of member commercial banks, including Deputy General Directors, Chief Accountants, and equivalent positions, requiring relevant educational qualifications or direct experience in [[Banking Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|finance, banking]], accounting, or auditing. [5][6]
- Personnel of member banks who have been found responsible for violations leading to the highest-tier administrative penalties in the monetary and banking sector are assessed as failing the professional ethics standard required under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. [5]
- All decrees identified in the source documents were issued by the same issuing authority: the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [1-orig][2-orig][3-orig][4-orig][5-orig][6-orig]

## Definitions

- **[[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]]** — The government decree establishing the Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC), serving as the primary legal foundation for the centre's existence. [5-orig]
- **[[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]** — A decree outlining the specific financial policies (chính sách tài chính) that apply within the TTTC, including the financial incentive regime cross-referenced by other decrees such as [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]]. [1-orig][3]
- **[[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]]** — A decree governing entry and exit (xuất nhập cảnh) policies for individuals operating within or travelling to the TTTC. [2-orig]
- **[[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]]** — A decree covering land use rights and environmental standards (đất đai và môi trường) within the TTTC. [3-orig]
- **[[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]]** — A decree establishing an international arbitration centre (Trung tâm Trọng tài Quốc tế) as part of the TTTC's [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|legal infrastructure]]. [4-orig]
- **[[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]]** — A decree regulating labour, employment, and social security (lao động, việc làm và an sinh xã hội) within the TTTC. [6-orig]
- **[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]** — A decree governing the licensing and establishment of banks and the management of [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange (ngoại hối)]] within the TTTC, including rules on cross-border borrowing and lending by TTTC members, and qualification requirements for senior bank personnel. [4][5][6]
- **[[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]]** — A decree governing the establishment and operation of the commodities exchange (Sở giao dịch hàng hóa) within the TTTC, including conditions for preferential tax and customs treatment and governance responsibilities of the Ministry of Industry and Trade and the local Operating Authority. [2][3]
- **Sở giao dịch hàng hóa** — The commodities exchange established under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] as part of the TTTC's [[Financial Products And Instruments In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|financial market infrastructure]]. [2][3]
- **Cơ quan điều hành** — The Operating Authority in each city within the TTTC, responsible under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] for licensing, approving tradeable goods lists, and approving foreign-linked contract lists. [3]
- **Bộ Công Thương** — The Ministry of Industry and Trade; the national authority responsible under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] for coordinating inspection of commodities exchange activities. [3]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all identified TTTC decrees. [1-orig][2-orig][3-orig][4-
