# Special Legal Mechanisms in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) operates under a suite of special legal mechanisms established through a series of government decrees issued by the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Office of the Government of Vietnam]]. These mechanisms cover a broad range of policy areas — including [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]], [[Foreign Investment Rules In Tttc]], [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment policy]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], and [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]] — each governed by a dedicated decree under the [[Legal Documents Establishing The Vietnam International Financial Centre|TTTC legal framework]]. Together, these instruments create a distinct and specialised legal environment intended to attract international financial activity to Vietnam.

---

## Key Points

- The [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] was issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) and serves as the foundational legal instrument establishing the TTTC. [5]
- The [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out the financial policy framework applicable within the TTTC, and was issued by the same authority. [1]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] establishes special immigration and entry/exit (xuất nhập cảnh) policies applicable within the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] governs land use and environmental regulations specific to the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the framework for an international arbitration centre operating within the TTTC, providing a dedicated [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution mechanism]]. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] addresses labour, employment, and social security policies within the TTTC. [6]
- All decrees identified in the source documents were issued by the same issuing authority: the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [1][2][3][4][5][6]

---

## Definitions

- **[[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]]** — The government decree establishing the Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC), serving as the primary legal foundation for the centre's existence. [5]
- **[[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]** — A decree outlining the specific financial policies (chính sách tài chính) that apply within the TTTC. [1]
- **[[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]]** — A decree governing entry and exit (xuất nhập cảnh) policies for individuals operating within or travelling to the TTTC. [2]
- **[[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]]** — A decree covering land use rights and environmental standards (đất đai và môi trường) within the TTTC. [3]
- **[[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]]** — A decree establishing an international arbitration centre (Trung tâm Trọng tài Quốc tế) as part of the TTTC's legal infrastructure. [4]
- **[[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]]** — A decree regulating labour, employment, and social security (lao động, việc làm và an sinh xã hội) within the TTTC. [6]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all identified TTTC decrees. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
