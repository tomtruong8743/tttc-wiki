# Legal Protections for Foreign Financial Institutions in TTTC

## Summary

The [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]] operates under a framework of government decrees issued by the Office of the Government (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ), which together establish the legal environment governing financial institutions operating within the centre. These decrees — including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] — collectively form the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] that underpins protections and obligations for [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign financial institutions]] participating in the TTTC.

## Key Points

- The legal framework for the TTTC is established through a series of decrees issued by the Office of the Government (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] forms the foundational legal instrument for the establishment of the TTTC and its governing structure. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] governs financial policies within the TTTC, which are relevant to [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign financial institutions]] operating there. It also includes standardised application forms for licensing and establishment of securities trading organisations, crowdfunding platform providers, and insurance enterprises operating within the TTTC. [1][6]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes provisions relating to international arbitration within the TTTC, forming part of the [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] available to foreign institutions. [4]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] addresses land and environmental policy within the TTTC, which may affect the operational rights of institutions established there. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] sets out immigration policies relevant to foreign personnel working within the TTTC, including provisions for permanent residency applications for foreign nationals working at the TTTC (as set out in Form NT-01). [2][3]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] governs labour, employment, and social security conditions within the TTTC, applicable to institutions employing staff there. [6]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] establishes detailed corporate governance requirements for member commercial banks operating within the TTTC. These include requirements for Risk Management Committees and Personnel Committees, each of which must have a minimum of three members, with more than half of voting members being non-executive members. [1][4]
- Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], member commercial banks must establish a Supervisory Board (Ban kiểm soát) with a minimum of three members, an internal audit unit, and supporting staff. The Supervisory Board's term of office may not exceed five years, and the bank must replenish members within 90 days if the number falls below the minimum. [4]
- The Supervisory Board of member commercial banks is responsible for monitoring and evaluating compliance with legal regulations, internal rules, the bank's charter, and resolutions and decisions of the owner and Members' Council. It is required to report promptly to the city's executive authority and the supervisory authority. [1][4]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] establishes the legal basis for the establishment and operation of a Commodities Exchange within the TTTC, including the approval process for clearing centres (Trung tâm thanh toán bù trừ) within the exchange. Approved entities must comply with the decree and all related legal instruments. [2]
- [[Licenses And Approvals For Financial Institutions In Tttc|Licensing]] of financial institutions within the TTTC, including securities trading organisations, crowdfunding platforms, and insurance enterprises, follows standardised application and approval procedures set out in the annexes to [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]. [6]
- All decrees were issued by the same issuing authority — VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (Office of the Government) — indicating a unified governmental approach to the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]]. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, established and governed by a series of government decrees. [5]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] — The foundational decree establishing the TTTC. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] — The decree governing financial policies within the TTTC, including licensing forms for securities, crowdfunding, and insurance entities. [1][6]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]] — The decree governing labour, employment, and social security within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]] — The decree addressing land and environmental regulations within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] — The decree governing immigration and entry/exit policies within the TTTC, including permanent residency provisions for qualifying foreign nationals (Form NT-01). [2][3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] — The decree establishing the international arbitration centre within the TTTC. [4]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] — The decree governing banking licensing, establishment, and foreign exchange management within the TTTC, including detailed corporate governance requirements for member commercial banks. [1][4]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Decree 330]] — The decree governing the establishment and operation of the Commodities Exchange and its clearing centre within the TTTC. [2]
- **Ban kiểm soát** — The Supervisory Board of a member commercial bank within the TTTC, responsible for monitoring regulatory and charter compliance, with a minimum of three members and a maximum term of five years. [4]
- **Ủy ban quản lý rủi ro** — Risk Management Committee of a member commercial bank, required to have at least three members with a majority of non-executive voting members. [1]
- **Ủy ban nhân sự** — Personnel Committee of a member commercial bank, subject to the same structural requirements as the Risk Management Committee. [1]
- **Trung tâm thanh toán bù trừ** — Clearing centre of the Commodities Exchange, subject to approval and compliance requirements under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]]. [2]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam, the issuing authority for all TTTC-related decrees. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
