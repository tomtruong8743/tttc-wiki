# Legal Framework of TTTC

## Summary
The legal framework of TTTC (Trung tâm Tài chính — Financial Centre) is established through a series of decrees issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn phòng Chính phủ). These decrees collectively govern the establishment, financial policies, land and environment, immigration, banking licensing, foreign exchange management, and international arbitration within the TTTC.

## Key Points
- A dedicated decree governs the establishment of the TTTC [6].
- Financial policies applicable within the TTTC are regulated by a separate government decree [2].
- Immigration policies (entry and exit) within the TTTC are addressed under their own decree [3].
- Land use and environmental matters within the TTTC are covered by a specific decree [4].
- The licensing and establishment of banks, as well as foreign exchange management within the TTTC, are subject to a dedicated decree [1].
- An International Arbitration Centre operating within the TTTC is governed by its own decree [5].
- All decrees identified in the sources were issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn phòng Chính phủ) [1][2][3][4][5][6].

## Definitions
- **TTTC** — Trung tâm Tài chính; a Financial Centre established and regulated under Vietnamese government decrees [6].
- **Văn phòng Chính phủ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all decrees in the TTTC legal framework [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- **Trung tâm trọng tài quốc tế** — International Arbitration Centre; an institution operating within the TTTC under a specific regulatory decree [5].
- **Nghị định** — Decree; the form of legal instrument used to establish and regulate all aspects of the TTTC [1][2][3][4][5][6].
