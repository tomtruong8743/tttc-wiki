# Foreign Worker Regulations

## Summary
Foreign Worker Regulations within the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview]] context fall under the broader [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] established by the Vietnamese Government's Office. The source documents available relate primarily to the establishment and operational decrees governing the [[Financial Center Decrees]], including areas such as banking, land, commodities exchange, and international arbitration. Specific provisions regarding foreign worker regulations may be addressed within the overarching policy framework issued by [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security]] and [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]].

## Key Points
- All source documents were issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), indicating that foreign worker regulations, like other TTTC policies, originate from central government authority. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- The regulatory framework for the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview]] spans multiple decrees covering finance ([[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]), land ([[Decree 326 Land And Environment]]), commodities ([[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange]]), arbitration ([[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]]), and establishment ([[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]]), suggesting that foreign worker rules likely interact with several of these domains. [1][2][4][5][6]
- Financial policy within the TTTC is governed separately under its own decree, which may include provisions relevant to foreign worker employment and compensation. [3]

## Definitions
- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview]] **TTTC (Trung Tâm Tài Chính)** — The Financial Center established under Vietnamese government decree, subject to a suite of specialized regulations covering multiple operational areas. [6]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all decrees governing the TTTC framework, including any labor and foreign worker provisions. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
