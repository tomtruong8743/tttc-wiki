# Legal Documents Establishing the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary
The [[Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]] is established and governed by a series of decrees issued by the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Vietnamese Government]] through the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). These decrees collectively form the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal framework]] of the TTTC, covering its [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment]], [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|labour and social security]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment|land and environment]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]].

---

## Key Points
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] concerns the establishment of the TTTC and was issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [5]
- [[Financial Center Decrees|Decree 324]] concerns [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]] within the TTTC and was issued by the Office of the Government. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|Decree 325]] covers labour, employment, and [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|social security]] within the TTTC and was issued by the Office of the Government. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment|Decree 326]] addresses land and environment matters within the TTTC and was issued by the Office of the Government. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] governs immigration and entry/exit policies within the TTTC and was issued by the Office of the Government. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] establishes the [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|International Arbitration Centre]] within the TTTC and was issued by the Office of the Government. [4]
- All six decrees share the same issuing authority: **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** (Office of the Government of Vietnam). [1][2][3][4][5][6]

---

## Definitions

- **[[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]]** — The decree concerning the establishment (thành lập) of the Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC). [5]
- **[[Financial Center Decrees|Decree 324]]** — The decree concerning [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]] (chính sách tài chính) within the TTTC. [1]
- **[[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|Decree 325]]** — The decree concerning labour, employment, and social security (lao động, việc làm và an sinh xã hội) within the TTTC. [6]
- **[[Decree 326 Land And Environment|Decree 326]]** — The decree concerning land and environment (đất đai và môi trường) within the TTTC. [3]
- **[[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]]** — The decree concerning immigration and entry/exit policy (xuất nhập cảnh) within the TTTC. [2]
- **[[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]]** — The decree concerning the [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|International Arbitration Centre]] (Trung tâm trọng tài quốc tế) within the TTTC. [4]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all six foundational decrees of the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- **[[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|TTTC]]** — Abbreviation for Trung tâm Tài chính Quốc tế, the Vietnam International Financial Centre. [5]
