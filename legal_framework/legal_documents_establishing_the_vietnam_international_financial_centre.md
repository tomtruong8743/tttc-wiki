# Legal Documents Establishing the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary
The [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]] is established and governed by a series of decrees issued by the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Vietnamese Government]] through the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). These decrees collectively form the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal framework]] of the TTTC, covering its [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policies]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|labour and social security]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment|land and environment]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|banking and foreign exchange]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange|commodities exchange]].

---

## Key Points
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] concerns the establishment of the TTTC and was issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] concerns [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]] within the TTTC, including [[Corporate Income Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|corporate income tax]] and [[Personal Income Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|personal income tax]] incentives, and was issued by the Office of the Government. [1][5new]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]] covers [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|labour, employment]], and [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|social security]] within the TTTC and was issued by the Office of the Government. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]] addresses [[Land Use Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|land]] and [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|environment]] matters within the TTTC and was issued by the Office of the Government. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] governs [[Immigration And Residency Policies For Tttc Workers|immigration and entry/exit policies]] within the TTTC and was issued by the Office of the Government. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] establishes the [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|International Arbitration Centre]] within the TTTC and was issued by the Office of the Government. [4]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] governs [[Banking Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|banking licensing and establishment]], as well as [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]] (quản lý ngoại hối), within the TTTC. It includes detailed rules on [[Capital Movement Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|cross-border borrowing and lending]] by TTTC members and [[Rules For Banks Operating In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|conduct standards for bank personnel]]. [2new][3new][6new]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Decree 330]] concerns the establishment and operation of the [[Financial Products And Instruments In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Commodities Exchange]] within the TTTC, including pre-agreed settlement arrangements. [1new]
- All eight decrees share the same issuing authority: **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** (Office of the Government of Vietnam). [1][2][3][4][5][6][1new][2new]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] references **Nghị quyết số 222/2025/QH15** as the overarching National Assembly resolution guiding investment activities into and within the TTTC. [5new]
- Members wishing to invest in [[Foreign Investment Rules In Tttc|restricted or conditional sectors]] must comply with general Vietnamese investment law, with any relaxation of conditions requiring ministerial approval. [5new]

## Definitions

- **[[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]]** — The decree concerning the establishment (thành lập) of the Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC). [5]
- **[[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]]** — The decree concerning [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]] (chính sách tài chính) within the TTTC, including tax, accounting, and financial sector regulations. [[Corporate Income Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Corporate income tax]] is set at 10% for 30 years, with exemptions of up to 4 years and 50% reductions for up to 9 subsequent years. [1][5new]
- **[[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]]** — The decree concerning [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|labour, employment]], and social security (lao động, việc làm và an sinh xã hội) within the TTTC. [6]
- **[[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]]** — The decree concerning [[Land Use Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|land]] and [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|environment]] (đất đai và môi trường) within the TTTC. [3]
- **[[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]]** — The decree concerning [[Immigration And Residency Policies For Tttc Workers|immigration and entry/exit policy]] (xuất nhập cảnh) within the TTTC. [2]
- **[[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]]** — The decree concerning the [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|International Arbitration Centre]] (Trung tâm trọng tài quốc tế) within the TTTC. [4]
- **[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]]** — The decree concerning [[Licenses And Approvals For Financial Institutions In Tttc|licensing and establishment of banks]] and [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|foreign exchange management]] (quản lý ngoại hối) within the TTTC. Covers [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign bank branches]], [[Cross Border Payments In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|cross-border borrowing and lending]], and [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|professional conduct standards]] for bank executives and key personnel. [2new][3new][6new]
- **[[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Decree 330]]** — The decree concerning the establishment and operation of the Commodities Exchange (Sở giao dịch hàng hóa) within the TTTC, including rules on pre-agreed settlement terms. [1new]
- **Nghị quyết số 222/2025/QH15** — The National Assembly resolution that provides the overarching legal authority for the TTTC framework; investment activities into the TTTC are governed by this resolution and its implementing decrees. [5new]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all foundational decrees of the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- **[[Trung Tam Tai Chinh Quoc Te Definition And Importance|TTTC]]** — Abbreviation for Trung tâm Tài chính Quốc tế, the Vietnam International Financial Centre. [5]
- **Thành viên** — "Member"; the term used in the decrees to refer to licensed entities operating within the TTTC. Members may carry out investment and business activities with persons and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to written approval from the regulatory authority (Cơ quan điều hành). [5new]
