# Decree 326 Land and Environment in TTTC

## Summary
Decree 326 is one of a series of foundational decrees issued by the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Vietnamese Government]] establishing the regulatory framework for the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]]. Specifically, this decree addresses land use and environmental policies applicable within the TTTC, complementing related instruments such as [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], and [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]]. It was issued by the [[Main Decrees Related To The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Office of the Government]] (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) as part of a coordinated legislative effort to govern the TTTC's operations. The broader set of decrees in this series — numbered 323 through 330 — came into force on 18 December 2025, as confirmed by the effective date provisions in related decrees. [4]

## Key Points
- Decree 326 covers the topics of land (*đất đai*) and environment (*môi trường*) within the TTTC framework. [3]
- The decree was issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), consistent with the other [[Financial Center Decrees]] in this series. [3]
- It forms part of a broader set of decrees (323–330) collectively establishing the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] for the TTTC, alongside decrees on establishment [3], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policies]] [2][6], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]] [4][5], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration]] [3], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|banking and foreign exchange]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange|the commodities exchange]] [1].
- The subject matter of [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|land and environment regulation]] is a distinct policy area within the TTTC, warranting its own dedicated decree. [3]
- The series of decrees as a whole took effect on 18 December 2025, as confirmed by the effectiveness provisions in [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]]. [4]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] (số 327/NĐ-CP, dated 18 December 2025) confirms the coordinated issuance date for decrees in this series, further situating Decree 326 within the same legislative package. [3]

## Land Use Procedures for TTTC Members

Decree 326 establishes specific procedures governing how TTTC members obtain, use, and manage land rights within the [[Facilities And Zones In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|financial centre zone]]. Key provisions drawn directly from the decree include: [6]

- **Land allocation and leasing:** The Chairperson of the People's Committee (*Chủ tịch Ủy ban nhân dân*), together with the competent land management authority, tax authority, and finance authority in [[Ho Chi Minh City Vs Da Nang In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Ho Chi Minh City and Da Nang]], are responsible for carrying out procedures for land allocation (*giao đất*), land leasing (*cho thuê đất*), permitting changes in land use purpose (*chuyển mục đích sử dụng đất*), extension of land use terms (*gia hạn sử dụng đất*), adjustment of land use terms (*điều chỉnh thời hạn sử dụng đất*), and issuance of Certificates of Land Use Rights and ownership of assets attached to land (*Giấy chứng nhận quyền sử dụng đất*) in accordance with land law. [6]
- **Physical handover of land:** The competent land management authority in Ho Chi Minh City and Da Nang is responsible for physically handing over land plots (*bàn giao đất trên thực địa*) to TTTC members. [6]
- **Investment registration certificate as basis for land lease:** The Investment Registration Certificate (*Giấy chứng nhận đăng ký đầu tư*) constitutes one of the legal bases for leasing land or permitting a change in land use purpose. [6]
- **Transfer of land use rights involving foreign credit institutions:** Where land use rights and assets attached to land are mortgaged by economic organisations or foreign-invested enterprises at foreign credit institutions, the recipient of any transfer of those rights upon enforcement of the mortgage security must fall within the categories of eligible recipients set out in Article 28 of the Land Law (*Luật Đất đai*). [6]
- **Supporting documentation:** The relevant authorities are responsible for providing TTTC members with documents relating to land use change, extension, adjustment of land use periods, and other relevant materials (*các tài liệu khác nếu có*). [6]

## Provincial Responsibilities for Land Management

[[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] confirms that the provincial People's Committees of the cities hosting the TTTC — namely Ho Chi Minh City and Da Nang — bear specific state management responsibilities in relation to land within the financial centre. These include: [3]

- Issuing coordination regulations (*quy chế phối hợp*) to handle issues related to land (*đất đai*), construction (*xây dựng*), infrastructure (*hạ tầng*), public services (*dịch vụ công*), and public order (*an ninh trật tự*) within the TTTC in a unified manner. [3]
- Proactively resolving difficulties and bottlenecks in the construction and development of the TTTC, including proposing specific or superior mechanisms and policies as required. [3]
- Directing local departments, agencies, and sectors to perform state management functions in accordance with applicable regulations. [3]

These provincial obligations reflect the [[Cities Involved In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|dual-city structure]] of the TTTC and the decentralised approach to land administration within the framework.

## Definitions

- **[[Decree 326 Land And Environment]]** — the specific government decree governing land use rights and environmental obligations within the TTTC. [3]
- **Đất đai (Land)** — refers to land allocation, use rights, leasing, extension, adjustment, and related procedures applicable to TTTC members. [3][6]
- **Môi trường (Environment)** — refers to environmental standards, obligations, and protections applicable within the TTTC zone. [3]
- **Giao đất** — land allocation by the state to a user, one of the core land procedures administered by Ho Chi Minh City and Da Nang authorities for TTTC members. [6]
- **Cho thuê đất** — land leasing, a primary mechanism by which TTTC members obtain the right to use land within the financial centre. [6]
- **Giấy chứng nhận quyền sử dụng đất** — Certificate of Land Use Rights (and ownership of assets attached to land), issued by competent authorities to TTTC members following land allocation or leasing procedures. [6]
- **Giấy chứng nhận đăng ký đầu tư** — Investment Registration Certificate, which serves as a legal basis for land leasing and changes in land use purpose under Decree 326. [6]
- **Chuyển mục đích sử dụng đất** — change of land use purpose, a procedure administered by provincial authorities in the TTTC cities. [6]
- **Luật Đất đai** — the Land Law of Vietnam, Article 28 of which governs the eligible recipients of transferred land use rights in the context of mortgage enforcement by foreign credit institutions. [6]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — the Office of the Government of Vietnam, the issuing authority for this and the related TTTC decrees. [3]
- **Trung tâm tài chính quốc tế tại Việt Nam** — the formal Vietnamese name for the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]], referenced across all decrees in this series. [1][3][4]
