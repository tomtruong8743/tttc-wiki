# Decree 326 Land and Environment in TTTC

## Summary
Decree 326 is one of a series of foundational decrees issued by the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Vietnamese Government]] establishing the regulatory framework for the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]]. Specifically, this decree addresses land use and environmental policies applicable within the TTTC, complementing related instruments such as [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], and [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]]. It was issued by the [[Main Decrees Related To The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Office of the Government]] (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) as part of a coordinated legislative effort to govern the TTTC's operations.

## Key Points
- Decree 326 covers the topics of land (*đất đai*) and environment (*môi trường*) within the TTTC framework. [3]
- The decree was issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), consistent with the other [[Financial Center Decrees]] in this series. [3]
- It forms part of a broader set of six decrees (323–330) collectively establishing the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] for the TTTC, alongside decrees on establishment [5], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policies]] [1], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]] [6], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration]] [2], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]] [4].
- The subject matter of [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|land and environment regulation]] is a distinct policy area within the TTTC, warranting its own dedicated decree. [3]

## Definitions

- **[[Decree 326 Land And Environment]]** — the specific government decree governing land use rights and environmental obligations within the TTTC. [3]
- **Đất đai (Land)** — refers to land allocation, use rights, and related policies applicable to entities operating within the TTTC. [3]
- **Môi trường (Environment)** — refers to environmental standards, obligations, and protections applicable within the TTTC zone. [3]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — the Office of the Government of Vietnam, the issuing authority for this and the related TTTC decrees. [3]
