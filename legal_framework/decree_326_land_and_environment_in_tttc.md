# Decree 326 Land and Environment in TTTC

## Summary
Decree 326 is one of a series of foundational decrees issued by the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Vietnamese Government]] establishing the regulatory framework for the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]]. Specifically, this decree addresses land use and environmental policies applicable within the TTTC, complementing related instruments such as [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], and [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]]. It was issued by the [[Main Decrees Related To The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Office of the Government]] (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) as part of a coordinated legislative effort to govern the TTTC's operations. The broader set of decrees in this series — numbered 323 through 330 — came into force on 18 December 2025, as confirmed by the effective date provisions in related decrees. [4]

## Key Points
- Decree 326 covers the topics of land (*đất đai*) and environment (*môi trường*) within the TTTC framework. [3]
- The decree was issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), consistent with the other [[Financial Center Decrees]] in this series. [3]
- It forms part of a broader set of decrees (323–330) collectively establishing the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] for the TTTC, alongside decrees on establishment [3], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policies]] [2][6], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]] [4][5], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration]] [3], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|banking and foreign exchange]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange|the commodities exchange]] [1].
- The subject matter of [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|land and environment regulation]] is a distinct policy area within the TTTC, warranting its own dedicated decree. [3]
- The series of decrees as a whole took effect on 18 December 2025, as confirmed by the effectiveness provisions in [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]]. [4]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] (số 327/NĐ-CP, dated 18 December 2025) confirms the coordinated issuance date for decrees in this series, further situating Decree 326 within the same legislative package. [3]

## Definitions

- **[[Decree 326 Land And Environment]]** — the specific government decree governing land use rights and environmental obligations within the TTTC. [3]
- **Đất đai (Land)** — refers to land allocation, use rights, and related policies applicable to entities operating within the TTTC. [3]
- **Môi trường (Environment)** — refers to environmental standards, obligations, and protections applicable within the TTTC zone. [3]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — the Office of the Government of Vietnam, the issuing authority for this and the related TTTC decrees. [3]
- **Trung tâm tài chính quốc tế tại Việt Nam** — the formal Vietnamese name for the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]], referenced across all decrees in this series. [1][3][4]

## Context Within the Decree Series

Decree 326 sits within a tightly coordinated package of [[Financial Center Decrees]] issued simultaneously on 18 December 2025. The other decrees in the series address complementary subject matters:

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] — establishment of the TTTC
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] — financial policies, including securities licensing, capital raising, and investment rules [2][6]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] — labour, employment, and social security (including social insurance, unemployment insurance, and health insurance for Vietnamese and foreign workers) [4][5]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] — immigration and residency policies, including permanent residency procedures for foreign workers [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] — international arbitration centre
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] — banking and foreign exchange
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] — establishment and operation of the commodities exchange, including clearing house approval procedures [1]

Together, these decrees establish the full [[Legal Documents Establishing The Vietnam International Financial Centre|legal and regulatory foundation]] for the TTTC across its key operating domains.
