# Dispute Resolution Mechanisms

## Summary
Dispute resolution mechanisms within the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview]] are governed by a framework of government decrees issued by the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]]. Most notably, [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes a dedicated international arbitration centre operating within the TTTC, providing a specialised institutional structure for resolving commercial and financial disputes. This framework sits alongside related regulations covering [[Foreign Investment Rules In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], and [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security]], all of which may give rise to disputes requiring formal resolution mechanisms.

## Key Points
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] was issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) and establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC. [4]
- All decrees relating to the TTTC, including those covering dispute-prone areas such as land, labour, banking, and immigration, were issued by the same issuing authority: the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment]] governs land and environmental matters within the TTTC, areas commonly subject to dispute. [3]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security]] covers labour, employment, and social security within the TTTC, which may require dedicated dispute resolution procedures. [6]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] regulates banking licensing, establishment, and foreign exchange management within the TTTC, areas that may generate financial disputes. [1]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] governs immigration policy within the TTTC, a further regulatory domain that may involve dispute or appeals processes. [2]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational establishment framework for the TTTC within which all dispute mechanisms operate. [5]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] **International Arbitration Centre (Trung tâm trọng tài quốc tế)** — A specialised institution established within the TTTC to handle arbitration of disputes, governed by Decree 328. [4]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ (Office of the Government)** — The issuing authority responsible for all TTTC-related decrees, including those establishing dispute resolution structures. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- **TTTC** — The financial centre established under [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], within which dispute resolution mechanisms and other regulatory frameworks operate. [5]
