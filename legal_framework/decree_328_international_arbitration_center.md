# Decree 328 International Arbitration Center

## Summary

Decree 328 pertains to the International Arbitration Center within the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC Financial Center]], forming part of the broader [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal framework]] governing the center. It is one of several decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) under the [[Financial Center Decrees|series of decrees]] regulating the establishment and operation of the TTTC, alongside related instruments such as [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment]], and [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]].
