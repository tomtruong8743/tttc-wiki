# How Investment Banks Can Participate in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) is governed by a series of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), which collectively establish the legal, financial, labour, land, immigration, arbitration, banking, and commodities frameworks that enable institutions such as investment banks to operate within the centre. Key instruments include [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]], all of which form part of the broader [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] governing participation in the TTTC.

---

## Key Points

- The TTTC is established and governed through a set of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), providing the foundational legal basis for institutional participation, including by investment banks [1][2][3][4][5][6][7][8].
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the primary legal instrument for the establishment of the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|TTTC]] [5].
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out the financial policy framework relevant to participants such as investment banks operating within the TTTC, including rules on insurance licensing and participation by TTTC members [4][8].
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] governs labour, employment, and social security conditions within the TTTC, which would apply to investment banks hiring staff there [6].
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] addresses land and environmental regulations that may affect investment banks seeking to establish a physical presence in the TTTC [3].
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] regulates immigration and entry/exit policies within the TTTC, relevant to investment banks employing or seconding foreign staff [2].
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC with jurisdiction over investment and commercial disputes related to TTTC activities, subject to defined exclusions. Courts may not refuse to hear applications to set aside arbitral awards in certain circumstances [6].
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] governs banking licensing, establishment, and [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]] for TTTC members, including rules on cross-border borrowing, offshore lending, and the activities of foreign-owned banks operating in the TTTC [1][3][7].
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] establishes the framework for the establishment and operation of a commodities exchange within the TTTC, with the local operating authority responsible for licensing, approving tradeable commodity lists, and approving linkages with foreign exchanges [2].
- Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], TTTC members may borrow in foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside Vietnam, subject to disclosure and reporting obligations [1].
- Fully foreign-owned TTTC members may lend to persons and organisations outside Vietnam, subject to reporting requirements; members not wholly foreign-owned are subject to additional conditions set out in Decree 329 [1].
- 100% domestically-funded commercial banks that are TTTC members may lend in foreign currency to offshore entities in which Vietnamese companies or members hold more than 50% of shares or contributed capital [1].
- 100% foreign-owned commercial banks and branches of foreign banks operating as TTTC members may conduct a defined range of activities with organisations in Vietnam that are not Vietnamese credit institutions, including: lending, discounting, bank guarantees, factoring, letters of credit, buying and selling corporate bonds issued in Vietnam (excluding bonds issued by Vietnamese credit institutions), and trading in Vietnamese government bonds including on the primary market [3].
- Senior management of member commercial banks, including deputy directors and chief accountants, must meet qualification and residency requirements, including relevant university-level education and, in certain cases, direct experience in finance, banking, accounting, or auditing [7].
- Insurance enterprises established and operating in the TTTC may provide coverage to TTTC members and their employees; members may also participate in insurance with enterprises outside the TTTC or abroad [8].
- All decrees are issued by the same issuing authority — the Vietnamese Government Office — indicating a coordinated and unified regulatory approach [1][2][3][4][5][6][7][8].

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] **Decree 323** — the foundational decree establishing the Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC), issued by the Vietnamese Government Office [5].
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] **Decree 324** — the decree setting out financial policies applicable within the TTTC, including provisions on insurance licensing and participation [4][8].
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] **Decree 325** — the decree governing labour, employment, and social security within the TTTC [6].
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] **Decree 326** — the decree addressing land use and environmental regulations within the TTTC [3].
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] **Decree 327** — the decree governing immigration and entry/exit policies applicable within the TTTC [2].
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] **Decree 328** — the decree establishing an international arbitration centre as part of the TTTC framework, with jurisdiction over investment and business disputes connected to TTTC activities [6].
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] **Decree 329** — the decree governing banking licensing, establishment, and foreign exchange management for TTTC members, including rules on cross-border lending and borrowing [1][3][7].
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] **Decree 330** — the decree establishing the framework for the establishment and operation of a commodities exchange within the TTTC [2].
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — the Vietnamese Government Office; the issuing authority for all decrees cited here [1][2][3][4][5][6][7][8].
- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview]] **TTTC** — abbreviation for Trung Tâm Tài Chính, referring to the Vietnam International Financial Centre [5].
- [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc]] **Ngoại hối** — foreign exchange; the subject of the foreign exchange management provisions in [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] [1][3].
- **Thành viên** — "member"; referring to entities that are registered participants in the TTTC and subject to its regulatory framework [1][3][7][8].
- **Cơ quan điều hành** — the operating/management authority at the city level within the TTTC, responsible for licensing and supervisory functions including for banks, insurance companies, and the commodities exchange [2][8].
