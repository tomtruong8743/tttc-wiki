# How Investment Banks Can Participate in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) is governed by a series of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), which collectively establish the legal, financial, labour, land, immigration, and arbitration frameworks that would enable institutions such as investment banks to operate within the centre. Key instruments include [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], all of which form part of the broader [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] governing participation in the TTTC.

---

## Key Points

- The TTTC is established and governed through a set of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), providing the foundational legal basis for institutional participation, including by investment banks [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the primary legal instrument for the establishment of the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|TTTC]] [5].
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out the financial policy framework relevant to participants such as investment banks operating within the TTTC [1].
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] governs labour, employment, and social security conditions within the TTTC, which would apply to investment banks hiring staff there [6].
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] addresses land and environmental regulations that may affect investment banks seeking to establish a physical presence in the TTTC [3].
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] regulates immigration and entry/exit policies within the TTTC, relevant to investment banks employing or seconding foreign staff [2].
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, providing [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] available to investment banks operating there [4].
- All decrees are issued by the same issuing authority — the Vietnamese Government Office — indicating a coordinated and unified regulatory approach [1][2][3][4][5][6].

---

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] **Decree 323** — the foundational decree establishing the Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC), issued by the Vietnamese Government Office [5].
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] **Decree 324** — the decree setting out financial policies applicable within the TTTC [1].
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] **Decree 325** — the decree governing labour, employment, and social security within the TTTC [6].
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] **Decree 326** — the decree addressing land use and environmental regulations within the TTTC [3].
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] **Decree 327** — the decree governing immigration and entry/exit policies applicable within the TTTC [2].
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] **Decree 328** — the decree establishing an international arbitration centre as part of the TTTC framework [4].
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — the Vietnamese Government Office; the issuing authority for all decrees cited here [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview]] **TTTC** — abbreviation for Trung Tâm Tài Chính, referring to the Vietnam International Financial Centre [5].
