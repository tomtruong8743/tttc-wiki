# Ho Chi Minh City vs Da Nang in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The [[Vietnam International Financial Centre Overview|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) is established through a series of decrees issued by the [[Legal Documents Establishing The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnamese Government Office]], covering areas such as financial policy, land and environment, labour, immigration, and international arbitration. The [[Cities Involved In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|cities involved]] in the TTTC framework — notably Ho Chi Minh City and Da Nang — are referenced as host locations for these centres, with each governed by the same suite of foundational decrees including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]].

## Key Points

- The TTTC is established under decrees issued by the VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (Government Office of Vietnam). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] governs the formal establishment of the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]]. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out the [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial and tax policies]] applicable within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] covers [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|labour, employment, and social security]] arrangements within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] addresses [[Land Use Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|land use and environmental regulations]] for the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] governs [[Immigration And Residency Policies For Tttc Workers|immigration and entry/exit policies]] for workers and participants in the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the framework for [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|international arbitration]] within the TTTC. [4]
- All decrees are issued by the same issuing authority, VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ, suggesting a unified regulatory framework applicable across [[Cities Involved In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|the cities involved]]. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- **[[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|TTTC]]** — Trung tâm tài chính quốc tế; the Vietnam International Financial Centre, established by the Vietnamese Government. [5]
- **[[Legal Documents Establishing The Vietnam International Financial Centre|VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ]]** — The Government Office of Vietnam; the issuing authority for the foundational decrees establishing and governing the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- **[[Cities Involved In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Host Cities]]** — The cities designated to host the TTTC, referenced in the context of these decrees (uncertain as to the precise roles of Ho Chi Minh City vs Da Nang based on source excerpts alone). [5]
- **[[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Nghị định 323]]** — The decree governing the establishment of the TTTC. [5]
- **[[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Trung tâm trọng tài quốc tế]]** — International Arbitration Centre; a body established under [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] within the TTTC framework. [4]
