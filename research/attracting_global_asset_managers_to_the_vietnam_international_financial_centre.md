# Attracting Global Asset Managers to the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) is supported by a suite of government decrees aimed at creating a competitive environment for global financial participants, including [[Asset Managers In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|asset managers]]. These decrees, issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), cover areas ranging from [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial and tax policies]] to [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment|land and environment]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|labour and social security]], and the [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment of the centre itself]]. Together, these regulatory frameworks form the legal foundation intended to attract global asset managers and other financial institutions to operate within the TTTC.

## Key Points

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] was issued by the Office of the Government and establishes the legal basis for the Vietnam International Financial Centre. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] covers financial policies within the TTTC, which are directly relevant to [[Fund Management In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|fund management]] and [[Asset Managers In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|asset management]] activities. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]] addresses labour, employment, and social security within the TTTC, providing a framework for hiring both domestic and international professionals relevant to [[Rules For Fund Managers In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|fund manager operations]]. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]] governs land and environmental regulations within the TTTC, covering the physical and operational premises available to financial institutions. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] establishes immigration policies within the TTTC, facilitating the entry and residence of foreign experts and professionals, which is essential for global [[Asset Managers In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|asset managers]]. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] establishes an International Arbitration Centre within the TTTC, providing [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution mechanisms]] that offer legal confidence to foreign financial participants. [4]
- All six decrees were issued by the same authority, Văn Phòng Chính Phủ (the Office of the Government of Vietnam), indicating a coordinated policy approach to developing the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]]. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- **[[Asset Managers In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Asset Manager]]** — A financial institution or professional entity that manages investment portfolios on behalf of clients within the TTTC framework. (uncertain — specific definition not provided in source documents) [1]
- **[[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]]** — The foundational government decree establishing the Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC). [5]
- **[[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]]** — A government decree outlining financial policies applicable within the TTTC. [1]
- **[[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]]** — A government decree governing labour, employment, and social security within the TTTC. [6]
- **[[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]]** — A government decree covering land use and environmental regulations within the TTTC. [3]
- **[[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]]** — A government decree establishing immigration and entry/exit policies for participants in the TTTC. [2]
- **[[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]]** — A government decree establishing an International Arbitration Centre as part of the TTTC. [4]
- **[[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]]** — Abbreviation for Trung Tâm Tài Chính (Vietnam International Financial Centre). [5]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam, the issuing authority for all six TTTC-related decrees. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
