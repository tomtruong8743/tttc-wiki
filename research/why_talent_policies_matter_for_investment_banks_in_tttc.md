# Why Talent Policies Matter for Investment Banks in TTTC

## Summary

Talent policies are a critical component of the regulatory framework governing the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]]. The [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] establishes the rules around labor, employment, and social security within TTTC, while the [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] governs the entry, residency, and movement of foreign nationals working in the centre. Together, these frameworks shape the ability of [[How Investment Banks Can Participate In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|investment banks]] to recruit, retain, and deploy international and domestic talent — a fundamental requirement for any competitive financial institution operating in a specialized financial zone.

## Key Points

- The [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] was issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) and forms the primary legal basis for labor and employment rules within TTTC. [6]
- The [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] was also issued by the Office of the Government and governs immigration and entry/exit policies relevant to foreign workers in TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], the foundational decree establishing TTTC, was issued by the same authority, indicating a coordinated, whole-of-government approach to building the centre's regulatory ecosystem. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] covers financial policies within TTTC and was issued by the Office of the Government, suggesting that talent and financial policies are developed in parallel as part of an integrated framework. [1]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] were similarly issued by the Office of the Government, reflecting a comprehensive suite of decrees designed to support the full operational needs of institutions — including investment banks — in TTTC. [3][4]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] — The decree issued by the Vietnamese Government regulating labor, employment, and social security conditions for workers within TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] — The decree governing immigration, entry, and exit policies for foreign nationals within the TTTC framework. [2]
- [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] — The body of rules derived from government decrees that determine how investment banks and other financial institutions may hire, manage, and retain staff within TTTC. [6]
- [[Immigration And Residency Policies For Tttc Workers]] — Policies controlling how foreign professionals may legally reside and work within TTTC, directly impacting an investment bank's ability to staff international teams. [2]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ (Office of the Government)** — The issuing authority for all core TTTC decrees, indicating that the talent and labor framework carries the full weight of the Vietnamese central government. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
