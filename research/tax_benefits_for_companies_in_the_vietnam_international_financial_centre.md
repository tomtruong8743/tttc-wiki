# Tax Benefits for Companies in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) offers a range of tax benefits designed to attract domestic and foreign companies to operate within its jurisdiction. These incentives are primarily governed by [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam, which sets out the financial policy framework applicable to entities established or operating within the TTTC. These benefits form a core part of the broader [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]] regime designed to make the TTTC competitive with other international financial hubs.

## Key Points

- The financial policies applicable to companies in the TTTC, including tax benefits, are governed by [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], issued by the Office of the Government (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ). [1]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal framework within which tax and financial incentive policies for companies in the TTTC operate. [5]
- Related policy areas, such as [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], complement the tax benefit framework by addressing other aspects of the business environment in the TTTC. [2][3][4][6]
- All decrees establishing the TTTC's policy framework were issued by the same authority, VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (Office of the Government), indicating a coordinated, government-wide approach to incentivising activity in the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] — The primary decree governing financial policies, including tax benefits, for companies operating within the Vietnam International Financial Centre, issued by the Office of the Government. [1]
- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre; a specially designated financial zone subject to unique regulatory and tax policy frameworks established by a series of government decrees. [5]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for the decrees establishing the TTTC and its associated policy frameworks. [1]
- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] — The collective body of decrees and regulations, including financial, labour, land, immigration, and arbitration policies, that govern operations within the TTTC. [2][3][4][5][6]
