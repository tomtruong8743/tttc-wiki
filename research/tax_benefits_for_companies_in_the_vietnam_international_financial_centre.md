# Tax Benefits for Companies in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) offers a range of tax benefits designed to attract domestic and foreign companies to operate within its jurisdiction. These incentives are primarily governed by [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam, which sets out the financial policy framework applicable to entities established or operating within the TTTC. These benefits form a core part of the broader [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]] regime designed to make the TTTC competitive with other international financial hubs.

[[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] also establishes accounting standards frameworks for TTTC members, permitting the use of International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) or other generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP) in place of Vietnamese Accounting Standards (VAS) for the preparation and presentation of financial statements and consolidated financial statements. Financial statements prepared under international standards are treated as the legal financial records of the entity and must be submitted to the relevant Vietnamese authorities. [6]

## Key Points

- The financial policies applicable to companies in the TTTC, including tax benefits, are governed by [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], issued by the Office of the Government (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ). [1]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal framework within which tax and financial incentive policies for companies in the TTTC operate. [5 (existing)]
- Related policy areas, such as [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], complement the tax benefit framework by addressing other aspects of the business environment in the TTTC. - All decrees establishing the TTTC's policy framework were issued by the same authority, VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (Office of the Government), indicating a coordinated, government-wide approach to incentivising activity in the TTTC. [1][2 (existing)][3 (existing)][4 (existing)][5 (existing)][6 (existing)]
- TTTC members that choose to apply international accounting standards are legally responsible for that choice and must submit financial statements prepared under those standards to the governing authority ([[Regulatory Bodies Supervising The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Cơ quan điều hành]]), the supervisory authority (Cơ quan giám sát), and other competent Vietnamese authorities. [6]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] establishes [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management rules]] for TTTC members, including rules governing borrowing from and lending to individuals and organisations outside Vietnam. Members that are 100% foreign-investor-owned may lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnam and must comply with information disclosure and reporting obligations. Members not 100% foreign-investor-owned are subject to additional conditions for cross-border lending set out in that decree. [4][5]
- Foreign investors making capital transfers related to foreign investment into the TTTC must do so through a designated foreign investor capital account opened at a member bank as specified in [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. [5]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] covers the establishment and operation of the commodities exchange within the TTTC, which may interact with the broader financial and tax policy environment for members. [1]

## Accounting Standards for TTTC Members

Under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], TTTC members have the option to apply International Accounting Standards (IAS/IFRS) or other generally accepted accounting principles, rather than Vietnamese Accounting Standards (VAS), when preparing financial statements and consolidated financial statements. Key provisions include: [6]

- If a member applies international accounting standards or GAAP, it is **not required** to also prepare financial statements under VAS. [6]
- Financial statements prepared under IAS/IFRS or GAAP constitute the **legal financial records** of the entity for regulatory submission and public disclosure purposes. [6]
- Members must submit financial statements and consolidated financial statements to the TTTC governing authority, the supervisory authority, and other competent Vietnamese state authorities. [6]
- Reporting periods and submission deadlines follow applicable Vietnamese accounting law. [6]
- Members bear full legal responsibility for their choice to apply international accounting standards or GAAP. [6]

This flexibility in accounting standards is a notable operational benefit for international financial institutions operating in the TTTC, reducing the compliance burden associated with parallel reporting under domestic and international frameworks. See also [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Reporting Obligations For Financial Institutions In Tttc]].

## Foreign Exchange and Cross-Border Lending Rules for Members

[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] sets out specific [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management rules]] applicable to TTTC members in connection with borrowing and lending activities: [4][5]

- **Borrowing from abroad:** All TTTC members may borrow in foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside Vietnam, subject to mandatory information disclosure and reporting compliance. [4]
- **Lending abroad (100% foreign-owned members):** Members wholly owned by foreign investors may lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnam, subject to information disclosure and reporting obligations. [4]
- **Lending abroad (non-100% foreign-owned members):** Members not wholly owned by foreign investors must meet the conditions for cross-border lending specified in [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], in addition to disclosure and reporting requirements. [4]
- **Domestic borrower limits:** Where the domestic borrower is a Vietnamese credit institution, short-term borrowings from TTTC members are subject to caps on outstanding principal as a percentage of the borrower's standalone equity — 30% for commercial banks that are not members, and 150% for foreign bank branches and other credit institutions that are not members. [5]
- **Long-term loans:** Loans with a term exceeding 12 months must serve the domestic borrower's credit activities, with at least 50% of the loan value used for on-lending to investment projects in sectors eligible for investment incentives under current Vietnamese investment law. [5]

These rules are relevant to understanding the full financial operating environment for companies in the TTTC and interact with the broader [[Capital Movement Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Cross Border Payments In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] frameworks.

## Definitions

- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] — The primary decree governing financial policies, including tax benefits and accounting standards, for companies operating within the Vietnam International Financial Centre, issued by the Office of the Government. [1 (existing)][6]
- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre; a specially designated financial zone subject to unique regulatory and tax policy frameworks established by a series of government decrees. [5 (existing)]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for the decrees establishing the TTTC and its associated policy frameworks. [1 (existing)]
- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] — The collective body of decrees and regulations, including financial, labour, land, immigration, and arbitration policies, that govern operations within the TTTC. [2 (existing)][3 (existing)][4 (existing)][5 (existing)][6 (existing)]
- **Cơ quan điều hành** — The governing/managing authority of the TTTC at the city level, responsible for issuing licences to member institutions and receiving their financial reports. [6]
- **Chuẩn mực kế toán quốc tế / IFRS** — International Accounting Standards or International Financial Reporting Standards; permitted under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] as an alternative to Vietnamese Accounting Standards for TTTC members. [6]
- [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|Ngoại hối]] — Foreign exchange; the subject of detailed management rules under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] applicable to TTTC member institutions, including rules on cross-border borrowing and lending. [4][5]
