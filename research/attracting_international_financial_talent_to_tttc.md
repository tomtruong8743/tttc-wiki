# Attracting International Financial Talent to TTTC

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre ([[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]]) was established through a series of government decrees issued by the Vietnamese Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). Among the key regulatory frameworks governing TTTC are [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|Decree 325 on Labour, Employment and Social Security]] and [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327 on Immigration Policy]], both of which are directly relevant to attracting and retaining international financial talent. Supporting frameworks such as [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324 on Financial Policies]] and [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323 on the Establishment of TTTC]] provide the broader institutional context within which talent attraction policies operate.

## Key Points

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] establishing TTTC was issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324 on Financial Policies]] within TTTC was issued by the Office of the Government and is relevant to the financial environment that international talent would operate within. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325 on Labour, Employment and Social Security]] was issued by the Office of the Government and directly governs [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|labour and employment rules]] applicable to workers, including international professionals, within TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327 on Immigration Policy]] was issued by the Office of the Government and provides the immigration and entry/exit framework relevant to [[Immigration And Residency Policies For Tttc Workers|international workers entering TTTC]]. [2]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326 on Land and Environment]] was issued by the Office of the Government, forming part of the broader regulatory environment that supports TTTC's operations. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328 on the International Arbitration Centre]] was issued by the Office of the Government, establishing [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|dispute resolution mechanisms]] that may provide confidence to international professionals and institutions operating in TTTC. [4]

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|**TTTC**]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, established by a series of government decrees issued by Văn Phòng Chính Phủ (the Office of the Government). [5]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|**Decree 327**]] — A decree issued by the Office of the Government governing immigration policy (xuất nhập cảnh) within TTTC, directly relevant to the entry and residency of international financial talent. [2]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|**Decree 325**]] — A decree issued by the Office of the Government governing labour, employment, and social security (lao động, việc làm và an sinh xã hội) within TTTC. [6]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam, the issuing authority for all decrees establishing and governing TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
