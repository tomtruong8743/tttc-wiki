# Private Equity in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Private equity represents one of the investment activities anticipated within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]], a financial hub established under a suite of government decrees issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. The legal and policy framework governing the TTTC — including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] — collectively creates the enabling environment within which private equity and related [[Investment Funds In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|investment fund]] activities may operate.

## Key Points

- The legal framework for the TTTC is established through a series of decrees issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational basis for the establishment of the TTTC, within which [[Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital market]] and private equity activities may be conducted. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out the financial policies applicable within the TTTC, which would govern financial structures relevant to [[Fund Management In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|fund management]] and private equity. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] addresses labour and employment matters within the TTTC, relevant to staffing [[Asset Managers In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|asset managers]] and private equity firms. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] covers land and environmental regulations applicable to entities operating within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] governs immigration and entry/exit policies, relevant to foreign professionals working in private equity within the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, providing [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution]] mechanisms relevant to private equity transactions and investor protections. [4]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|TTTC]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, established by the Vietnamese Government as a specialised financial hub. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Financial Policies]] — The regulatory and fiscal rules governing financial activities, including investment and fund management, within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|International Arbitration Centre]] — A dispute resolution body established within the TTTC to handle commercial and investment disputes, including those arising from private equity transactions. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Labour and Social Security Policies]] — Regulations governing employment conditions, social insurance, and workforce matters for entities and employees operating within the TTTC. [6]
