# Private Equity in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Private equity represents one of the investment activities anticipated within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]], a financial hub established under a suite of government decrees issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. The legal and policy framework governing the TTTC — including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], and [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] — collectively creates the enabling environment within which private equity and related [[Investment Funds In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|investment fund]] activities may operate.

## Key Points

- The legal framework for the TTTC is established through a series of decrees issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational basis for the establishment of the TTTC, within which [[Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital market]] and private equity activities may be conducted. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out the financial policies applicable within the TTTC, which would govern financial structures relevant to [[Fund Management In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|fund management]] and private equity. It also establishes a priority waterfall for debt repayment upon dissolution of financial entities, covering employee wages and benefits, insurance obligations, tax debts, and other liabilities — relevant to private equity investors assessing wind-down risk. [1][5]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] addresses labour and employment matters within the TTTC, relevant to staffing [[Asset Managers In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|asset managers]] and private equity firms. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] covers land and environmental regulations applicable to entities operating within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] governs immigration and entry/exit policies, relevant to foreign professionals working in private equity within the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, providing [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution]] mechanisms relevant to private equity transactions and investor protections. [4]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] establishes the [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange]] rules applicable to TTTC members, with direct relevance to private equity capital flows, cross-border lending, and foreign investor capital transfers. [1][2][3][6]

### Foreign Exchange and Capital Flow Rules Relevant to Private Equity

- Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], TTTC members may borrow foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside Vietnam, subject to information declaration and reporting requirements. [1]
- Members 100% owned by foreign investors are permitted to lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnam, subject to information declaration and [[Reporting Obligations For Financial Institutions In Tttc|reporting obligations]]. Members not 100% foreign-owned must satisfy additional lending conditions set out in the decree. [1]
- Domestic-capital commercial banks that are TTTC members may lend in foreign currency to overseas entities where more than 50% of shares or capital contributions are held by Vietnamese enterprises (member or non-member), or to entities controlled by such qualifying organisations. [1]
- Member enterprises may lend to overseas entities in which the lending member or its parent company holds more than 50% of shares or capital contributions — a structure potentially relevant to private equity portfolio management and cross-border investment. [1]
- [[Foreign Investor Participation In Vietnam Financial Markets Via Tttc|Foreign investors]] must channel capital transfers related to foreign investment into the TTTC through a foreign investor capital account opened at a member bank, as prescribed under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. [2]
- Short-term borrowing from TTTC members by domestic credit institutions is subject to limits: the total outstanding principal of short-term loans from members must not exceed 30% of standalone equity for commercial banks that are non-members, and 150% for foreign bank branches and other non-member credit institutions that are non-members. [2]
- Loans with a term exceeding 12 months must serve the credit activities of the domestic borrower, with at least 50% of the loan value used for on-lending to investment projects in preferential investment sectors under applicable law. [2]
- Borrowing entities must have maintained required safety ratios and financial stability ratios (if applicable) for the three consecutive months prior to signing the loan agreement or any amendment increasing loan value. [2]

### Banking Activities Relevant to Private Equity Financing

- Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], 100% foreign-owned commercial banks and foreign bank branches that are TTTC members may conduct a range of activities with non-credit institution organisations on Vietnamese territory, including lending, discounting, bank guarantees, factoring, letter of credit operations, and the purchase and sale of corporate bonds issued in Vietnam (excluding bonds issued by Vietnamese credit institutions) and government bonds. [3]
- 100% domestically-capitalised commercial banks that are TTTC members may participate in international payment systems of which their owners are already members. [6]
- Banks that do not apply [[International Capital Flows In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|International Accounting Standards]] are prohibited from extending credit to certain categories of persons and organisations, as specified in [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. [6]

### Governance and Staffing Requirements

- Senior management of member commercial banks — including Deputy General Directors, Chief Accountants, and equivalent roles — must meet qualification and experience requirements, including a university degree in finance, banking, economics, business administration, law, accounting, auditing, or a relevant professional field, or alternatively a degree in another field combined with at least three years of direct experience in finance, banking, or the relevant specialisation. [4]
- Senior executives of member banks must reside in Vietnam during their tenure. [4]
- These requirements are relevant to private equity firms and [[Asset Managers In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|asset managers]] establishing or acquiring stakes in member banks or financial institutions within the TTTC. [4]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|TTTC]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, established by the Vietnamese Government as a specialised financial hub. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Financial Policies]] — The regulatory and fiscal rules governing financial activities, including investment and fund management, within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|International Arbitration Centre]] — A dispute resolution body established within the TTTC to handle commercial and investment disputes, including those arising from private equity transactions. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Labour and Social Security Policies]] — Regulations governing employment conditions, social insurance, and workforce matters for entities and employees operating within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|Ngoại hối]] — Foreign exchange; the rules governing foreign currency transactions, borrowing, lending, and capital transfers within the TTTC, as set out in [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. [1][2]
- **Tài khoản vốn của nhà đầu tư nước ngoài** — Foreign investor capital account; the designated account through which foreign investors must channel investment capital transfers into the TTTC. [2]
