# How Investment Banks Can Advise Clients on TTTC

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre ([[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]]) was established through a series of government decrees issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. Investment banks seeking to advise clients on opportunities within the TTTC should familiarise themselves with the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]], which is defined by a suite of foundational decrees covering areas such as financial policies, land and environment, labour, immigration, and international arbitration. Understanding this regulatory architecture is essential for providing accurate, actionable advisory services to clients considering participation in the TTTC.

## Key Points

- The TTTC was established and is governed by a series of decrees issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Office of the Government of Vietnam). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis for the TTTC's establishment, which investment banks must understand when advising clients on entry and participation. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out the financial policies applicable within the TTTC, a critical reference for advising clients on [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]] and related financial structuring. [1]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] covers land and environmental regulations, relevant for clients considering physical establishment or real estate investment within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] addresses labour, employment, and social security matters, which investment banks should communicate to clients planning to hire staff within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] governs immigration and entry/exit policies, important for advising foreign clients and expatriate staff on operational requirements. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the international arbitration centre within the TTTC, relevant for advising clients on [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] and legal risk management. [4]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] **TTTC (Trung tâm Tài chính Quốc tế)** — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, established by government decree as a special financial zone. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] **Financial Policies Decree** — The decree governing financial policies within the TTTC, including potential [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]] and other fiscal frameworks applicable to participants. [1]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] **International Arbitration Centre** — A dispute resolution body established within the TTTC to handle international commercial disputes. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] **Labour and Social Security Decree** — The decree regulating employment conditions and social security obligations for entities and workers operating within the TTTC. [6]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam, the issuing authority for all TTTC-related decrees referenced in the source documents. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
