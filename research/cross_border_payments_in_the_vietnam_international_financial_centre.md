# Cross Border Payments in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Cross border payments are a key component of the operational framework of the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre (VIFC)]]. The regulatory environment governing cross border transactions is established through a series of government decrees, including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], and [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], which together shape how funds may move into and out of the centre. These rules intersect with broader frameworks covering [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], [[Capital Movement Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], and [[Currency And Capital Controls]].

[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] contains dedicated provisions on foreign exchange management for borrowing and lending activities by VIFC members, including detailed rules on borrowing from overseas individuals and organisations and on lending to parties outside Vietnam. These provisions represent some of the most operationally specific cross border payment rules documented for the VIFC. [1][6]

## Key Points

- The source documents are all issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), confirming the official government origin of the regulatory framework governing the VIFC, which includes cross border payment rules. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] contains a dedicated section (Section 4) on foreign exchange management rules applicable to borrowing and lending activities of VIFC members (*thành viên*). [1]
- Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], VIFC members are permitted to borrow foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to mandatory information disclosure and compliance with reporting requirements. [1]
- Members that are 100% foreign investor-owned are permitted to lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to information disclosure and reporting obligations. [1]
- Members that are **not** 100% foreign investor-owned must comply with additional conditions for overseas lending, as set out in Article 89 of [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. [1]
- A 100% domestically-funded commercial bank (*ngân hàng thương mại 100% vốn trong nước*) that is a VIFC member may lend in foreign currency overseas to: (a) organisations established and operating outside Vietnam in which Vietnamese enterprises or non-member Vietnamese entities hold more than 50% of shares or contributed capital; and (b) organisations established and operating outside Vietnam that are majority-owned by entities falling under category (a). [1]
- A corporate member (*doanh nghiệp thành viên*) may lend to organisations established and operating outside Vietnam in which the lending member or its parent company holds more than 50% of shares or contributed capital. [1]
- Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], member banks are **exempt** from the maximum foreign currency position limit relative to equity capital (*trạng thái ngoại tệ tối đa so với vốn tự có*) that applies under general Vietnamese law. This has direct implications for cross border foreign currency operations. [6]
- Member banks applying [[International Accounting Standards]] are also exempt from requirements to purchase, hold, or invest in Vietnamese Government bonds or government-guaranteed bonds under Vietnamese law. [6]
- The supervisory authority (*Cơ quan giám sát*) may, based on inspection and supervision results, require member banks to maintain stricter prudential ratios — including the Leverage Ratio (LEV), Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR), and Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR) — where necessary to ensure operational safety. [6]
- The regulatory framework for cross border payments sits alongside rules on [[Foreign Currency Transactions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Transferring Capital Into And Out Of The Vietnam International Capital Centre]]. (uncertain) [1]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] requires members to prepare and submit financial statements and consolidated financial statements to the Operating Authority (*Cơ quan điều hành*), supervisory authority, and competent Vietnamese authorities, which supports the transparency obligations relevant to cross border transactions. [3]
- Organisations and bodies within the VIFC may receive non-refundable monetary contributions and sponsorships from domestic and foreign individuals, organisations, and enterprises, with such funds managed as lawful revenue of the VIFC and not subject to state budget procedures. [5]

## Definitions

- **Cross Border Payments** — financial transactions involving the movement of funds between parties located in different countries, governed within the VIFC by the applicable government decrees, particularly [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. (uncertain) [1]
- **[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]** — a government decree issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam governing banking licensing, establishment, and foreign exchange management (*quản lý ngoại hối*) within the Vietnam International Financial Centre. [1][6]
- **[[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]** — a government decree setting out financial policies applicable within the Vietnam International Financial Centre, including accounting and financial reporting requirements. [3]
- **[[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]]** — the foundational decree establishing the legal and institutional framework of the Vietnam International Financial Centre. [2][5]
- **[[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc]]** (*Ngoại hối*) — foreign exchange; the management of ngoại hối for borrowing and lending by VIFC members is a central feature of the cross border payments framework under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. [1]
- **Thành viên** — a member of the VIFC; the borrowing, lending, and reporting obligations for cross border transactions are framed around thành viên under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. [1]
- **Trạng thái ngoại tệ tối đa** — maximum foreign currency position; member banks are exempt from the general Vietnamese law cap on this ratio relative to equity capital. [6]
- **[[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]** — the regulations governing the conversion and transfer of foreign currencies within the VIFC context, of which the borrowing and lending provisions in [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] are a core component. [1][6]
- **Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) / Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR) / Leverage Ratio (LEV)** — prudential ratios applicable to member banks under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], relevant to the liquidity and solvency dimensions of cross border payment capacity. [6]
