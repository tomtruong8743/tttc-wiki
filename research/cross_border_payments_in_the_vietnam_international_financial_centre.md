# Cross Border Payments in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Cross border payments are a key component of the operational framework of the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre (VIFC)]]. The regulatory environment governing cross border transactions is established through a series of government decrees, including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], and [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], which together shape how funds may move into and out of the centre. These rules intersect with broader frameworks covering [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], [[Capital Movement Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], and [[Currency And Capital Controls]].

## Key Points

- The source documents provided are all issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), confirming the official government origin of the regulatory framework governing the VIFC, which would include cross border payment rules. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] is among the key decrees relevant to financial policies within the VIFC, which would encompass cross border payment mechanisms. [1]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis for the VIFC, under which cross border payment activities are structured. [5]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] is identified as a related decree relevant to banking and foreign exchange activities, both of which are central to cross border payments. (uncertain) [1][5]
- The regulatory framework for cross border payments sits alongside rules on [[Foreign Currency Transactions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Transferring Capital Into And Out Of The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]. (uncertain) [1][5]

## Definitions

- **[[Cross Border Payments]]** — financial transactions that involve the movement of funds between parties located in different countries, governed within the VIFC by the applicable government decrees. (uncertain) [1][5]
- **[[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]** — a government decree issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam setting out financial policies applicable within the Vietnam International Financial Centre. [1]
- **[[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]]** — the foundational decree establishing the legal and institutional framework of the Vietnam International Financial Centre. [5]
- **[[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]** — the regulations governing the conversion and transfer of foreign currencies within the VIFC context. (uncertain) [1]
