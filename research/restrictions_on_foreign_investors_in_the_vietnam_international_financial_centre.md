# Restrictions on Foreign Investors in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

This page addresses the restrictions placed on foreign investors operating within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC). The TTTC is governed by a suite of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office, including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], and [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], which together form the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] for all participants, including [[Foreign Investment Rules In Tttc|foreign investors]].

## Key Points

- The decrees governing the TTTC were issued by the VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (Government Office of Vietnam), establishing the overarching regulatory authority over all participants, including foreign investors. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] forms the foundational legal instrument for the establishment of the TTTC, within which any restrictions on foreign investors would be grounded. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] governs financial policies within the TTTC, which may include rules affecting [[Foreign Investment Rules In Tttc|foreign investor]] activity. Applicants are required to submit detailed documentation including physical infrastructure declarations (Mẫu số 05 — Bản thuyết minh cơ sở vật chất), covering office floor plans, equipment lists, and technology systems for activities such as [[Capital Raising In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|crowdfunding platforms]] and [[Asset Managers In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|asset management systems]]. [1][3]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] regulates labour and employment within the TTTC, potentially imposing conditions on foreign entities employing staff. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] governs land use and environmental regulations within the TTTC, which may restrict or condition foreign investors' access to land and property. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] sets out immigration policies relevant to foreign individuals associated with investor entities operating in the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the framework for international arbitration within the TTTC, relevant to [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] available to and imposed upon foreign investors. [4]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] sets out detailed activity-by-activity restrictions distinguishing between [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|100% foreign-owned commercial banks and foreign bank branches]] on one hand, and [[Domestic Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|100% domestically-owned commercial banks]] on the other, with specific permitted and restricted activities for each category. [2][4][6]

## Restrictions on Foreign Banks and Foreign Bank Branches

[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] contains specific provisions restricting and delimiting the activities of [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|100% foreign-owned commercial banks (ngân hàng thương mại 100% vốn nước ngoài) and foreign bank branches (chi nhánh ngân hàng nước ngoài)]] operating as members within the TTTC. Key restrictions identified in the decree include:

- **Scope of advisory services**: Advisory services provided by a 100% foreign-owned commercial bank member or foreign bank branch must be performed by that member bank itself (or another appropriately licensed member), and must be consistent with the scope of activity set out in the licence issued by the city's governing authority (Cơ quan điều hành tại thành phố). This limits the ability of foreign bank members to provide advice through affiliated entities without appropriate licensing. [4]
- **Restrictions on bond trading**: Foreign bank members may buy and sell corporate bonds issued on Vietnamese territory, **excluding** bonds issued by Vietnamese credit institutions. They may separately trade corporate bonds denominated in foreign currency issued overseas, and government bonds denominated in foreign currency issued overseas. [2]
- **Restriction on Vietnamese-currency government bonds**: Foreign bank members are restricted from selling Vietnamese Government bonds (trái phiếu Chính phủ Việt Nam) denominated in foreign currency, government-guaranteed bonds denominated in foreign currency, and local government bonds denominated in foreign currency. (uncertain — the relevant provision appears to restrict the selling of these instruments in certain contexts.) [6]
- **International payment systems**: 100% foreign-owned commercial banks and foreign bank branches may only participate in international payment systems of which their parent bank or owner is already a member, limiting independent access to new payment infrastructure. [6]
- **Personnel fitness requirements**: Key management personnel (including members of the Members' Council, Supervisory Board, General Director, Deputy General Directors, and Chief Accountant) of foreign bank members are subject to professional ethics assessments. Persons responsible for violations that led to the highest-tier administrative penalties for a Vietnamese credit institution or member bank — covering areas including licensing, governance, equity, credit extension, corporate bond purchases, and safety ratios — are deemed not to meet professional ethics standards. Persons holding positions in Vietnam for the first time are subject to conditions excluding provisions relating to certain disqualifying circumstances set out in Article 33 of the decree. [5]

## Restrictions Specific to Domestically-Owned Banks (Contrast)

For comparison, [[Domestic Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|100% domestically-owned commercial banks (ngân hàng thương mại 100% vốn trong nước)]] operating in the TTTC with individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory are also subject to notable restrictions, including:

- **Prohibition on VND/foreign currency exchange**: Domestically-owned banks may conduct foreign currency-to-foreign currency transactions consistent with their parent/owner's international market foreign exchange licence, but are **explicitly prohibited** from conducting transactions between Vietnamese Dong (VNĐ) and foreign currencies in this context. [6]
- **Restrictions on interest rate derivatives**: Domestic banks may trade and supply interest rate derivative products, but this **excludes** derivative transactions involving Vietnamese Dong. [6]
- **Lending restrictions**: Lending activities are limited to conditions specified in particular articles of the decree (Articles 88 and 89, Chapter VIII). [6]

## Infrastructure and Registration Requirements

Under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], all organisations registering to operate within the TTTC — including foreign investors — must submit a physical infrastructure declaration (Bản thuyết minh cơ sở vật chất) as part of their application. This includes:

- Total office floor area and layout plans
- A detailed inventory of equipment and software, including systems supporting [[Capital Raising In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|community capital mobilisation (crowdfunding) platforms]] and [[Fund Management In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|client asset management systems]]

This requirement applies equally to foreign-registered entities and creates a baseline compliance burden for any foreign investor seeking to establish operations. [1][3]

## Definitions

- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ]] — The Government Office of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all major decrees governing the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|TTTC]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre; the special financial zone subject to these regulatory decrees. [5]
- [[Foreign Investment Rules In Tttc|Foreign Investor]] — An overseas individual or entity seeking to participate in or operate within the TTTC, subject to the restrictions and conditions set out in the governing decrees. (uncertain) [1][5]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Land and Environment Regulations]] — Rules governing access to and use of land within the TTTC, as established under Decree 326, which may impose conditions on foreign-owned entities. [3]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Labour and Social Security Rules]] — Regulations under Decree 325 governing employment conditions within the TTTC, applicable to all employers including foreign investors. [6]
- **Ngân hàng thương mại 100% vốn nước ngoài** — A 100% foreign-owned commercial bank; a distinct category of [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign bank]] member under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], subject to specific activity permissions and restrictions.
