# Restrictions on Foreign Investors in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

This page addresses the restrictions placed on foreign investors operating within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC). The TTTC is governed by a suite of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office, including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], which together form the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] for all participants, including [[Foreign Investment Rules In Tttc|foreign investors]].

## Key Points

- The decrees governing the TTTC were issued by the VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (Government Office of Vietnam), establishing the overarching regulatory authority over all participants, including foreign investors. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] forms the foundational legal instrument for the establishment of the TTTC, within which any restrictions on foreign investors would be grounded. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] governs financial policies within the TTTC, which may include rules affecting [[Foreign Investment Rules In Tttc|foreign investor]] activity. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] regulates labour and employment within the TTTC, potentially imposing conditions on foreign entities employing staff. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] governs land use and environmental regulations within the TTTC, which may restrict or condition foreign investors' access to land and property. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] sets out immigration policies relevant to foreign individuals associated with investor entities operating in the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the framework for international arbitration within the TTTC, relevant to [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] available to and imposed upon foreign investors. [4]

## Definitions

- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ]] — The Government Office of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all major decrees governing the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|TTTC]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre; the special financial zone subject to these regulatory decrees. [5]
- [[Foreign Investment Rules In Tttc|Foreign Investor]] — An overseas individual or entity seeking to participate in or operate within the TTTC, subject to the restrictions and conditions set out in the governing decrees. (uncertain) [1][5]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Land and Environment Regulations]] — Rules governing access to and use of land within the TTTC, as established under Decree 326, which may impose conditions on foreign-owned entities. [3]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Labour and Social Security Rules]] — Regulations under Decree 325 governing employment conditions within the TTTC, applicable to all employers including foreign investors. [6]
