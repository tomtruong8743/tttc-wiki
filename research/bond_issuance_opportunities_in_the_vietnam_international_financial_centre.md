# Bond Issuance Opportunities in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) presents potential opportunities for bond issuance as part of its broader [[Debt Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|debt capital markets]] framework. The centre is established and governed through a series of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office, including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], and related instruments that collectively shape the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal and financial environment]] within which bond issuance and other [[Capital Market Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital market activities]] may take place.

## Key Points

- The TTTC is governed by a suite of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ), which collectively form the regulatory basis for activities including bond issuance. [1][5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] is the primary instrument addressing financial policies within the TTTC, and is likely the key regulatory reference for bond issuance opportunities (uncertain). [1]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] establishes the foundational legal structure of the TTTC within which [[Capital Raising In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital raising]] activities, including bond issuance, may be conducted. [5]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] contains detailed prudential and banking safety requirements applicable to member banks in the TTTC, including rules on capital adequacy ratios such as the Leverage Ratio (LEV), [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign currency position limits]], Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR), and Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR). [1]
- Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], member banks applying international accounting standards ([[Accounting Standards In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|IFRS]] or generally accepted accounting principles) are exempt from the requirement to comply with Vietnamese-law ratios for purchasing, holding, and investing in Vietnamese Government bonds or Government-guaranteed bonds. [1]
- Member banks in the TTTC are also exempt from maximum foreign currency position limits relative to equity capital as prescribed under Vietnamese law, pursuant to [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. [1]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] addresses accounting policy, including the conditions under which TTTC members may elect to apply international accounting standards or generally accepted accounting principles, subject to full and unconditional compliance with those standards. [4]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] provides for import duty exemptions on certain goods including raw materials, supplies, and components, subject to notification procedures for exempt goods lists in accordance with Vietnamese export and import tax law. [4]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] and [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] form part of the broader regulatory environment governing operations within the TTTC. [3][6]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] provide supporting frameworks related to personnel and [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution]] relevant to bond market participants. [2]
- The management authority of the TTTC is responsible for coordinating with relevant agencies to receive and process feedback, complaints, and disputes arising within the International Financial Centre. [5]
- All decrees are issued by the same issuing authority — the Vietnamese Government Office — suggesting a coordinated and unified regulatory approach to the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Financial Policies Decree]] — the decree governing financial policies within the TTTC, issued by the Vietnamese Government Office, covering matters including [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|tax and duty incentives]], accounting policy elections, and related [[Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|financial incentives]] applicable to TTTC members. [1][4]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Establishment Decree]] — the foundational decree creating the TTTC as a legal and institutional entity within Vietnam, including the governance powers of the TTTC management authority. [5]
- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] — the Vietnam International Financial Centre (Trung tâm Tài chính Quốc tế), a specially established financial centre in Vietnam governed by a dedicated set of government decrees. [5]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|International Arbitration Centre]] — a dispute resolution body established within the TTTC framework, relevant to bond issuance participants seeking legal recourse. [2]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Banking and Foreign Exchange Decree]] — the decree governing licensing, establishment of banks, and foreign exchange management within the TTTC, including prudential ratios applicable to member banks and exemptions from certain Vietnamese-law requirements. [1]
- **Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR)** — a prudential metric measuring a bank's ability to meet short-term obligations, applicable to member banks in the TTTC under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], with differentiated requirements depending on whether the bank applies international accounting standards. [1]
- **Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR)** — a prudential metric measuring a bank's stable funding relative to its assets and off-balance-sheet activities, applicable to member banks in the TTTC under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. [1]
- **Government Bond Holding Ratios** — under Vietnamese law, banks are typically required to hold certain ratios of Vietnamese Government bonds or Government-guaranteed bonds; TTTC member banks applying international accounting standards are exempt from this requirement under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. [1]

## Accounting Policy and Bond-Relevant Exemptions

A notable feature of the TTTC regulatory framework with direct relevance to bond issuance and investment is the accounting policy flexibility provided under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]. TTTC members may elect to apply International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) or other generally accepted accounting principles, subject to full compliance without modification. [4]

This election has material consequences for bond market activity. Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], member banks that apply international accounting standards are:

- **Exempt from Vietnamese Government bond holding ratios** — not required to comply with rules on purchasing, holding, or investing in Vietnamese Government bonds or Government-guaranteed bonds as prescribed under Vietnamese law. [1]
- **Exempt from maximum foreign currency position limits** — not required to comply with Vietnamese-law restrictions on foreign currency positions relative to equity capital, which may be relevant to [[Foreign Currency Transactions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign currency bond]] issuance and hedging activities. [1]

These exemptions may enhance the operational flexibility of international banks operating in the TTTC in managing bond portfolios and participating in bond issuance transactions (uncertain). [1]

## Prudential Requirements for Member Banks

[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] establishes a prudential framework for member banks operating within the TTTC. Key elements include:

- **Safety ratios** — member banks must comply with prudential ratios including the Leverage Ratio (LEV), Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR), and Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR). [1]
- **Enhanced requirements** — the [[Regulatory Bodies Supervising The Vietnam International Financial Centre|supervisory authority]] may, based on the results of supervision, inspection, or examination, require individual member banks to comply with stricter prudential ratios than the standard policy where necessary to ensure operational safety, taking into account the nature and degree of risk. [1]
- **Liquidity management** — member banks must monitor and manage their positions to ensure continuous compliance with liquidity coverage requirements. Where a bank's 30-day LCR calculation falls below the required threshold, specific remedial procedures apply. [1]

These requirements are relevant to bond issuance participants, as they affect the capacity of member banks to underwrite, hold, and distribute bonds within the TTTC framework. [1]
