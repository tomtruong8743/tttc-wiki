# Labor and Employment Rules in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Labor and employment rules in the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) are governed primarily by [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], issued by the Office of the Government (Văn phòng Chính phủ). This decree sits alongside a broader suite of [[Financial Center Decrees]] — including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] — which together establish the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] for the TTTC. The labor and social security framework is designed to support the TTTC's goals of attracting international talent and financial institutions to operate within the centre.

## Key Points

- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] is the primary decree governing labor, employment, and social security within the TTTC, issued by the Office of the Government (Văn phòng Chính phủ). [1]
- The decree forms part of a coordinated set of [[Main Decrees Related To The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] that collectively regulate all aspects of the TTTC's operation. [1]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] is a related instrument that may govern the entry and stay of [[Foreign Worker Regulations|foreign workers]] operating within the TTTC, complementing the labor rules under Decree 325. [2]
- All founding decrees of the TTTC, including the labor and social security decree, were issued by the same issuing authority — the Office of the Government (Văn phòng Chính phủ) — indicating a unified governmental approach to the centre's regulatory framework. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] — The specific government decree (Nghị định 325) addressing labor, employment, and social security (an sinh xã hội) matters within the Vietnam International Financial Centre. [6]
- **Văn phòng Chính phủ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all foundational TTTC decrees, including the labor and social security decree. [6]
- **An sinh xã hội** — Social security; one of the subject areas covered alongside labor and employment within Decree 325. [6]
- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] — The collective set of decrees (323–330) that establish and regulate the TTTC, within which the labor rules operate. [5][6]
