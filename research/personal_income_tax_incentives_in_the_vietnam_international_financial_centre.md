# Personal Income Tax Incentives in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Personal income tax incentives form part of the broader [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]] framework established for participants operating within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC). These incentives are governed by the financial policies set out under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], which was issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. The incentives are designed to support the [[Main Goals Of The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] of attracting skilled professionals, foreign experts, and financial sector participants to operate within the centre.

## Key Points

- The source documents related to personal income tax incentives in the TTTC were issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [1]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] is the primary decree covering financial policies within the TTTC, which is expected to include provisions on [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]] such as personal income tax. [1] (uncertain)
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] also establishes accounting and financial reporting obligations for TTTC members, including the option to apply International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) or Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP) rather than Vietnamese Accounting Standards. Members choosing international standards must submit financial reports to the governing authority (Cơ quan điều hành), the supervisory authority (Cơ quan giám sát), and other competent Vietnamese authorities. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] may also be relevant to personal income tax matters insofar as they relate to labour, employment, and social security contributions within the TTTC. [6] (uncertain)
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] governing entry and exit policies may interact with personal income tax incentives as they apply to foreign experts and workers operating in the TTTC. [2] (uncertain)
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] governs foreign exchange management within the TTTC, including rules on borrowing from individuals and organisations outside Vietnam, lending abroad, and the transfer of foreign investment capital into and out of the financial centre. These foreign exchange arrangements interact with the broader tax and financial incentive framework applicable to TTTC members. [5][2]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] — A decree issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam establishing financial policies applicable within the Vietnam International Financial Centre, including accounting standards and potentially personal income tax incentives. Members may apply IFRS or GAAP instead of Vietnamese Accounting Standards, with reports submitted to regulatory and supervisory authorities. [1][4]
- [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]] — Preferential tax treatment offered to individuals or entities operating within a designated zone such as the TTTC, which may include reduced rates or exemptions on personal income tax. [1] (uncertain)
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] — A decree covering labour, employment, and social security arrangements within the TTTC, which may intersect with personal income tax obligations for workers in the centre. [6] (uncertain)
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] — A decree governing banking licensing, establishment, and foreign exchange management within the TTTC, including rules on cross-border lending, borrowing, and capital transfers by TTTC members. [5][2]
- **Cơ quan điều hành** — The governing authority of the TTTC, responsible among other things for receiving financial and accounting reports submitted by TTTC members. [4]
- **Cơ quan giám sát** — The supervisory authority of the TTTC, which also receives financial and accounting reports from TTTC members. [4]
