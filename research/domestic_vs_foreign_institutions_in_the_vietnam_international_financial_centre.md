# Domestic vs Foreign Institutions in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The [[Vietnam International Financial Centre Overview|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) operates under a framework established by a series of government decrees issued by [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Vietnam's Office of the Government]], covering areas such as [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policies]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], and the establishment of the centre itself. The distinction between domestic and foreign institutions operating within the TTTC is shaped by this overarching [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal framework]], which sets out the conditions, rights, and obligations for different categories of participants in the centre.

## Key Points

- The TTTC is governed by a set of decrees all issued by the same authority — VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (Office of the Government of Vietnam) — establishing a unified regulatory framework applicable to both domestic and foreign institutions. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis for the establishment of the TTTC, within which rules differentiating domestic and foreign participants would be anchored. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out the financial policies governing institutions operating within the TTTC, which may apply differently to domestic versus foreign entities. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] addresses labour, employment, and social security matters within the TTTC, which has relevance to both domestic and foreign institutions hiring staff in the centre. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] covers land and environmental regulations, relevant to how both domestic and foreign institutions may occupy or use physical facilities within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] establishes immigration and entry/exit policies, which are particularly relevant to [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign institutions]] bringing in international staff and experts. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, providing a [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|dispute resolution]] mechanism applicable to both domestic and foreign institutions. [4]

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] — Trung tâm tài chính quốc tế (Vietnam International Financial Centre); the special financial zone established under Vietnamese government decree. [5]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all foundational decrees governing the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Domestic Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Domestic institutions]] — Vietnamese-incorporated financial entities operating within the TTTC framework (uncertain, as specific distinctions are not detailed in source excerpts). [5]
- [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Foreign institutions]] — Internationally incorporated financial entities participating in the TTTC, subject to specific immigration, financial, and operational rules. [2][1]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|International Arbitration Centre]] — A body established within the TTTC to resolve disputes, accessible to both domestic and foreign institutions. [4]
