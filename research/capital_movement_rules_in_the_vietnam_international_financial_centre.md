# Capital Movement Rules in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Capital movement rules govern how funds may be transferred into and out of the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC). These rules are part of the broader [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] established through a series of government decrees issued by the [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Office of the Government of Vietnam]]. Related policy areas include [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], [[Currency And Capital Controls]], and [[Foreign Investment Rules In Tttc]], all of which intersect with how capital may flow through the TTTC's financial ecosystem.

## Key Points

- The decrees governing the TTTC were issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), indicating they carry official government authority over financial centre policies, including capital movement. [1][5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] is the primary decree addressing financial policies within the TTTC, which would be expected to include capital movement provisions. Tax and customs incentive conditions for commodity exchange participants within the TTTC are also governed by reference to this decree. [1][5b]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] is a key decree governing foreign exchange management and the licensing and establishment of banks within the TTTC, and contains detailed provisions on the permitted activities of foreign and domestic member banks with respect to capital flows and financial transactions. [2][6]
- The establishment decree, [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], provides the foundational legal basis under which all capital movement rules operate. [5]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] and [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] are companion decrees issued alongside the financial policy framework, indicating a comprehensive regulatory structure surrounding the TTTC. [3][6]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] governs entry and exit policies, which may intersect with the movement of human capital and related financial flows within the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes a dispute resolution mechanism that applies to disputes arising from investment and business activities within the TTTC, which may include capital movement disputes. The International Arbitration Centre has jurisdiction over disputes agreed upon by the parties relating to investment and business activities within the TTTC, but explicitly does not cover disputes involving administrative decisions or actions of state management authorities, labour disputes, personal rights disputes, or matters already resolved by competent Vietnamese courts or state management agencies. [4]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] governs the establishment and operation of commodities exchanges within the TTTC, and provides that export and import tax incentives and customs procedure benefits for eligible goods are implemented in accordance with [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|the financial policies decree]]. This creates a link between commodities trading capital flows and the broader financial policy framework. [5b]

## Banking Activities and Capital Flows

Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], 100% foreign-owned commercial banks and foreign bank branches that are TTTC members are permitted to conduct a defined range of capital-related activities with organisations on Vietnamese territory that are not Vietnamese credit institutions. These permitted activities include: [6]

- **Lending** — extending credit to eligible counterparties.
- **Discounting** — discounting of financial instruments.
- **Bank guarantees** — providing guarantee services.
- **Factoring** — factoring and related services.
- **Letter of credit operations** — and other related business activities.
- **Corporate bond trading** — buying and selling corporate bonds issued on Vietnamese territory (excluding bonds issued by Vietnamese credit institutions).
- **Government bond trading** — buying and selling Vietnamese Government bonds, government-guaranteed bonds, and local government bonds, including on the primary market, subject to applicable laws.
- **Debt purchase** — purchasing debt obligations.

These permitted activities define the scope of capital movement that [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign member banks]] may engage in with respect to Vietnamese entities, and are directly relevant to [[International Capital Flows In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Cross Border Payments In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].

## Governance Standards for Capital Movement Participants

[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] also sets standards for persons appointed to key management positions in member banks operating within the TTTC. Persons responsible for oversight of banking and monetary activities — including members of the Board of Members, Supervisory Board, General Directors, Deputy General Directors, and Chief Accountants — must meet prescribed professional and ethical standards. [2]

Individuals are assessed as failing professional ethics standards if they bear responsibility (as determined by inspection or audit conclusions) for administrative violations in the monetary and banking sector that led to the highest penalty bracket being imposed, covering violations related to: [2]

- Licensing
- Governance and management
- Shareholding and share capital
- Equity contribution and share acquisition
- Credit extension
- Corporate bond purchases
- Safety ratio requirements under applicable administrative penalty regulations

This governance framework is relevant to the integrity of capital movement processes within the TTTC and intersects with [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], [[Risk Management Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], and [[Anti Money Laundering Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].

## Environmental Registration and the TTTC Operating Authority

Under [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], TTTC members that undertake investment projects must complete environmental registration before discharging waste into the environment, unless exempted under applicable environmental protection law. Investment project owners may elect to submit environmental registration through the TTTC Operating Authority (Cơ quan điều hành Trung tâm tài chính quốc tế) in Ho Chi Minh City or Da Nang, in addition to direct submission to the commune-level People's Committee. Where projects span two or more administrative communes, the project owner may choose which commune-level authority to register with. [1]

While this is primarily an environmental compliance matter rather than a capital movement rule, it is relevant to [[Foreign Investors In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] undertaking capital deployment through investment projects, and demonstrates the administrative role of the TTTC Operating Authorities across both [[Ho Chi Minh City Vs Da Nang In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Ho Chi Minh City and Da Nang]]. [1]

## Commodities Exchange and Capital Settlement

[[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] provides that goods trading through the TTTC's commodities exchange involving pre-agreed settlement terms are subject to the decree's framework. The Operating Authority at each city has responsibility for: [5b]

- Reviewing and appraising applications for the establishment of commodities exchanges by TTTC members.
- Issuing, re-issuing, suspending, and revoking establishment licences.
- Approving the list of domestically produced goods permitted for trading.
- Approving the list of contracts from foreign commodities exchanges permitted for linked trading.

The Ministry of Industry and Trade (Bộ Công Thương) is responsible for coordinating with the supervisory authority to inspect commodities exchange activities and for implementing provisions on incentives and penalties related to import/export and commodities trading under the decree. [5b]

Tax and customs incentives for eligible commodities are implemented by reference to [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], reinforcing that decree's central role in governing the financial terms of capital flows through the TTTC. [5b]

## Definitions

- **[[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|TTTC]]** — The Vietnam International Financial Centre (Trung tâm tài chính quốc tế), the special financial zone governed by the suite of government decrees referenced here. [5]
- **[[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Financial Policies Decree]]** — The decree (Nghị định 324) issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam specifically addressing financial policies within the TTTC, including provisions relevant to capital movement and tax and customs incentives. [1]
- **[[Currency And Capital Controls|Capital Movement]]** — The transfer of money and financial assets into, within, and out of the TTTC, subject to applicable Vietnamese government regulations. [1][5]
- **[[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Establishment Decree]]** — The foundational decree (Nghị định 323) that legally created the TTTC and provides the overarching framework under which all other TTTC regulations operate. [5]
- **[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Foreign Exchange Management Decree]]** — The decree (Nghị định 329) governing the licensing and establishment of banks and foreign exchange management within the TTTC, including the scope of permitted capital-related activities for member banks. [2][
