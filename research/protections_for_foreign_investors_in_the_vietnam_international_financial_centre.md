# Protections for Foreign Investors in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) is governed by a suite of government decrees that together establish a comprehensive framework for [[Legal Protections For Investors In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]. These decrees, issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), cover areas including [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], and [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], collectively forming the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] that underpins protections available to foreign investors operating within the Centre.

---

## Key Points

- The foundational legal basis for the TTTC, including any investor protections it contains, is established under [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], issued by the Office of the Government. [1]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] forms part of the regulatory framework governing financial policies within the TTTC, which may include protections relevant to foreign investors. Tax and customs incentives for goods traded through the TTTC — including import/export tax advantages — are governed by this decree, with eligibility conditions including proof of qualifying goods and compliance with applicable customs procedures. [2][5]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], issued by the Office of the Government, is one of the [[Financial Center Decrees]] that forms part of the broader protective framework for participants in the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], issued by the Office of the Government, establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, which is a key [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] tool for foreign investors. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], issued by the Office of the Government, addresses labour and social security matters within the TTTC, forming part of the regulatory environment for foreign investors and their employees. [5]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], issued by the Office of the Government, governs immigration policy within the TTTC, relevant to [[Foreign Worker Regulations]] and the ability of foreign investors to deploy international personnel. A standardised application form (Mẫu NT-01) exists for foreign nationals working at the TTTC to apply for permanent residency in Vietnam. [6]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] establishes detailed governance and qualification standards for senior management of member commercial banks operating in the TTTC, including requirements that senior officers reside in Vietnam during their tenure and hold relevant qualifications in finance, banking, economics, business administration, law, accounting, or auditing. [7][8]
- Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], members of the TTTC that are 100% foreign-investor-owned are permitted to lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to information declaration and reporting obligations. Members not wholly foreign-owned must satisfy additional conditions before lending abroad. [9]
- Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], TTTC members are permitted to borrow foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to information declaration and compliance with reporting requirements. [9]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] governs the establishment and operation of the commodities exchange within the TTTC. Goods qualifying for preferential treatment — including import/export tax benefits and customs procedure advantages — must be documented and comply with applicable sector-specific inspection requirements (e.g. food safety, health, environment, agriculture, science and technology, defence, and security). [10]

## Definitions

- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] — The collection of government decrees and regulations issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) that establish and govern the Vietnam International Financial Centre. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] — The foundational decree establishing the Vietnam International Financial Centre, issued by the Office of the Government. [1]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] — A decree establishing an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, providing a mechanism for [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] for investors. [4]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] — A decree governing immigration and entry/exit policies within the TTTC, issued by the Office of the Government. Includes provisions for permanent residency applications by foreign nationals working at the TTTC (Form Mẫu NT-01). [6]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] — A decree addressing labour, employment, and social security matters within the TTTC. [5]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] — A decree governing land and environmental matters within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] — A decree governing bank licensing, establishment, and [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] within the TTTC, including governance standards for member bank senior management and rules on cross-border lending and borrowing. [7][8][9]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] — A decree governing the establishment and operation of the commodities exchange within the TTTC, including conditions for preferential tax and customs treatment. [10]

## Foreign Exchange and Cross-Border Capital Protections

Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], specific protections and permissions apply to [[Capital Movement Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] for foreign investors:

- TTTC members may borrow foreign currency from parties outside Vietnam, provided they declare relevant information and comply with reporting obligations. [9]
- TTTC members that are 100% foreign-investor-owned may lend to entities and individuals outside Vietnamese territory, subject to information declaration and reporting requirements — a significant operational freedom compared to domestically-owned members. [9]
- Domestically-owned member banks that wish to lend abroad in foreign currency may do so to foreign-incorporated entities in which Vietnamese companies (members or non-members) hold more than 50% of shares or capital contributions, or to entities controlled by such qualifying organisations. [9]

These rules are relevant to the broader topic of [[Foreign Currency Transactions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Profit Repatriation In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].

## Bank Governance and Management Protections

[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] establishes qualification and conduct requirements for senior management of member banks in the TTTC, which serve to protect investors by ensuring sound institutional governance:

- Deputy Directors General, Chief Accountants, and equivalent roles must hold at least an undergraduate degree in finance, banking, economics, business administration, law, accounting, auditing, or a relevant specialist field, or alternatively hold an undergraduate degree in another field combined with at least three years of direct experience in finance, banking, or the relevant specialist area. [7]
- Senior officers must reside in Vietnam during their tenure. [7]
- Certain disqualification provisions apply to senior management roles (per Article 33 of [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]), with modified standards for individuals who have not previously worked in Vietnam. [8]
- Conflict-of-interest restrictions prohibit the Chairman of the Members' Council from simultaneously serving as an executive officer or supervisory board member of the same or another credit institution, member bank, or other enterprise in Vietnam. [8]

These governance standards are relevant to [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Risk Management Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].

## Commodities Exchange Protections and Incentives

Under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]], investors and members participating in the commodities exchange within the TTTC benefit from:

- Access to export and import tax incentives and customs procedure advantages, provided qualifying goods documentation requirements are met and relevant sector-specific inspection regimes are followed. [10]
- A structured state management framework in which the Ministry of Industry and Trade (Bộ Công Thương) cooperates with the supervisory authority (Cơ quan giám sát) to monitor exchange operations and trading activities. [10]
- The city-level operating authority (Cơ quan điều hành) holds powers including licensing, approval of tradeable domestic goods lists, and approval of linked foreign exchange contract lists — providing a regulated and transparent framework for exchange participants. [10]
