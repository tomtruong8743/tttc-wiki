# Protections for Foreign Investors in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) is governed by a suite of government decrees that together establish a comprehensive framework for [[Legal Protections For Investors In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]. These decrees, issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), cover areas including [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], and [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], collectively forming the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] that underpins protections available to foreign investors operating within the Centre.

---

## Key Points

- The foundational legal basis for the TTTC, including any investor protections it contains, is established under [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], issued by the Office of the Government. [1]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] forms part of the regulatory framework governing financial policies within the TTTC, which may include protections relevant to foreign investors. [2]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], issued by the Office of the Government, is one of the [[Financial Center Decrees]] that forms part of the broader protective framework for participants in the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], issued by the Office of the Government, establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, which is a key [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] tool for foreign investors. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], issued by the Office of the Government, addresses labour and social security matters within the TTTC, forming part of the regulatory environment for foreign investors and their employees. [5]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], issued by the Office of the Government, governs immigration policy within the TTTC, relevant to [[Foreign Worker Regulations]] and the ability of foreign investors to deploy international personnel. [6]

---

## Definitions

- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] — The collection of government decrees and regulations issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) that establish and govern the Vietnam International Financial Centre. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] — The foundational decree establishing the Vietnam International Financial Centre, issued by the Office of the Government. [1]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] — A decree establishing an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, providing a mechanism for [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] for investors. [4]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] — A decree governing immigration and entry/exit policies within the TTTC, issued by the Office of the Government. [6]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] — A decree addressing labour, employment, and social security matters within the TTTC. [5]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] — A decree governing land and environmental matters within the TTTC. [3]
