# Developing Vietnam Capital Markets Through TTTC

## Summary
The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) represents a strategic initiative by the Vietnamese government to develop and deepen domestic [[Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]. Established through a series of government decrees issued by the [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Office of the Government]], the TTTC framework encompasses [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environmental policy]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security arrangements]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policies]], and [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|banking licensing and foreign exchange management]], all designed to attract capital market participants and stimulate [[Capital Raising In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].

## Key Points
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] was issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) and establishes the foundational legal basis for the TTTC. [5-orig]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] covers financial policies within the TTTC, forming a core pillar of the centre's [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]]. [1-orig]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]] addresses labour, employment, and social security within the TTTC, providing a regulatory environment for the workforce supporting [[Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]. [6-orig]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]] governs land use and environmental regulations within the TTTC, underpinning the physical and operational infrastructure needed to support capital market development. [3-orig]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] sets out immigration and entry/exit policies for the TTTC, facilitating the movement of international participants in [[Equity Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Debt Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]. It includes provisions for permanent residency applications (Mẫu NT-01) for foreign nationals working at the Vietnam International Financial Centre. [2-orig][3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, providing [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] essential for investor confidence in capital markets. [4-orig]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] governs banking licensing, establishment, and [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]] within the TTTC, and contains detailed rules on cross-border borrowing and lending by member institutions. [1][2][4][5][6]
- All foundational decrees were issued by the same authority — the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) — indicating a coordinated, whole-of-government approach to capital market development. [1-orig][2-orig][3-orig][4-orig][5-orig][6-orig]

### Banking and Foreign Exchange Rules Under Decree 329
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] permits TTTC member institutions to borrow foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to information disclosure and reporting obligations. [1]
- Members wholly owned by foreign investors (100% foreign capital) may lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to disclosure and reporting obligations. [1]
- Members not wholly foreign-owned must satisfy specific lending conditions set out in Article 89 of [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] before extending loans abroad. [1]
- 100% domestically funded commercial banks that are members may lend in foreign currency to foreign organisations in which Vietnamese enterprises or members hold more than 50% of charter capital or contributed capital, or to subsidiaries of such organisations. [1]
- 100% foreign-owned commercial banks and foreign bank branches that are members may conduct a wide range of activities with non-credit institution organisations on Vietnamese territory, including: lending, discounting, bank guarantees, factoring, letters of credit, buying and selling corporate bonds issued in Vietnam (excluding bonds issued by Vietnamese credit institutions), and buying and selling Vietnamese Government bonds, government-guaranteed bonds, and local government bonds (including on the primary market). [2]
- Foreign bank member branches may also open payment accounts in Vietnamese dong and foreign currency at non-member banks, conduct foreign currency transactions, and participate in international payment systems where the parent institution is already a member. [4][5]
- Foreign bank members are permitted to provide banking advisory services, treasury and asset custody services, and safe deposit box services, subject to the scope of their licence issued by the city-level managing authority (Cơ quan điều hành tại thành phố). [6]
- Banks not applying [[International Capital Flows In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|international accounting standards]] are subject to restrictions on credit extension under Chapter V of [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]]. [4]
- Domestically funded commercial banks that are members may participate in international payment systems where the parent owner is already a member. [4]

## Definitions

- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|TTTC]] — Trung Tâm Tài Chính (Vietnam International Financial Centre); the special financial centre established by the Vietnamese government through a suite of dedicated decrees to develop capital markets and attract international financial institutions. [5-orig]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] — The foundational government decree establishing the TTTC, issued by the Office of the Government. [5-orig]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] — The government decree governing financial policies applicable within the TTTC. [1-orig]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]] — The government decree governing labour, employment, and social security arrangements within the TTTC. [6-orig]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]] — The government decree governing land use and environmental matters within the TTTC. [3-orig]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] — The government decree governing immigration and entry/exit policies for participants in the TTTC, issued on 18 December 2025. Includes standardised forms (e.g., Mẫu NT-01) for permanent residency applications by foreign nationals employed at the TTTC. [2-orig][3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] — The government decree establishing the international arbitration centre within the TTTC. [4-orig]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] — The government decree on banking licensing, establishment, and [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|foreign exchange management]] (quản lý ngoại hối) within the TTTC. Covers permissible activities for foreign and domestic bank members, cross-border lending and borrowing rules, and prudential safety restrictions. [1][2][4][5][6]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all foundational TTTC decrees. [1-orig][2-orig][3-orig][4-orig][5-orig][6-orig]
- **Mẫu NT-01** — A standardised form under [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] for submitting a permanent residency application on behalf of foreign nationals working at the Vietnam International Financial Centre. [3]
- **Cơ quan điều hành tại thành phố** — The city-level managing authority responsible for issuing licences (Giấy phép) to member banks operating within the TTTC, and for supervising permissible activities under those licences. [6]
- **[[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|Ngoại hối]]** — Foreign exchange; a key regulatory domain within the TTTC governed by Section 4 of [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]], covering borrowing and lending activities in foreign currency by member institutions. [1]
