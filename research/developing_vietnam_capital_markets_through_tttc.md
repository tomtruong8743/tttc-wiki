# Developing Vietnam Capital Markets Through TTTC

## Summary
The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) represents a strategic initiative by the Vietnamese government to develop and deepen domestic [[Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]. Established through a series of government decrees issued by the [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Office of the Government]], the TTTC framework encompasses [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environmental policy]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security arrangements]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], and [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policies]], all designed to attract capital market participants and stimulate [[Capital Raising In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].

## Key Points
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] was issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) and establishes the foundational legal basis for the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] covers financial policies within the TTTC, forming a core pillar of the centre's [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]]. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]] addresses labour, employment, and social security within the TTTC, providing a regulatory environment for the workforce supporting [[Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]] governs land use and environmental regulations within the TTTC, underpinning the physical and operational infrastructure needed to support capital market development. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] sets out immigration and entry/exit policies for the TTTC, facilitating the movement of international participants in [[Equity Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Debt Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, providing [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] essential for investor confidence in capital markets. [4]
- All six decrees were issued by the same authority — the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) — indicating a coordinated, whole-of-government approach to capital market development. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|TTTC]] — Trung Tâm Tài Chính (Vietnam International Financial Centre); the special financial centre established by the Vietnamese government through a suite of dedicated decrees to develop capital markets and attract international financial institutions. [5]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] — The foundational government decree establishing the TTTC, issued by the Office of the Government. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] — The government decree governing financial policies applicable within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]] — The government decree governing labour, employment, and social security arrangements within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]] — The government decree governing land use and environmental matters within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] — The government decree governing immigration and entry/exit policies for participants in the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] — The government decree establishing the international arbitration centre within the TTTC. [4]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all six foundational TTTC decrees. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
