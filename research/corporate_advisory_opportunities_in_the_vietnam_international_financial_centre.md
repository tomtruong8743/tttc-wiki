# Corporate Advisory Opportunities in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) presents a range of corporate advisory opportunities, operating under a framework of government decrees issued by the [[Legal Documents Establishing The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Office of the Government of Vietnam]]. These decrees — covering areas such as [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policies]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration]], [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|international arbitration]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], and [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment]] — together form the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal and regulatory framework]] within which corporate advisory activities may be conducted at the TTTC.

## Key Points

- The TTTC is governed by a series of decrees all issued by the same issuing authority: VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Office of the Government of Vietnam). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis for the establishment of the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|TTTC]], which is the starting point for any corporate advisory engagement within the centre. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] governs the [[Financial Services In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|financial policies]] applicable within the TTTC, which would be of direct relevance to corporate advisors working on [[Capital Raising In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital raising]], [[Mergers And Acquisitions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|M&A]], and related transactions. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] covers [[Foreign Worker Regulations|labour, employment, and social security]] matters within the TTTC, relevant to corporate advisors advising on workforce structuring and human resources compliance. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] addresses [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|land and environmental]] regulations, which may be relevant to corporate advisory work involving real estate, infrastructure, or environmental compliance. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] sets out immigration policies applicable to the TTTC, relevant to advisors and their international staff seeking to operate within the centre. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|International Arbitration Centre]] within the TTTC, providing a formal mechanism for dispute resolution relevant to corporate advisory engagements. [4]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|**TTTC**]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre (Trung tâm tài chính), established under Decree 323 and governed by a suite of related decrees. [5]
- [[Legal Documents Establishing The Vietnam International Financial Centre|**VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ**]] — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all primary decrees governing the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|**Financial Policies (Chính sách tài chính)**]] — The regulatory framework under Decree 324 governing financial activities within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|**International Arbitration Centre (Trung tâm trọng tài quốc tế)**]] — A dispute resolution body established within the TTTC under Decree 328. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|**Labour and Social Security (Lao động, việc làm và an sinh xã hội)**]] — The framework under Decree 325 regulating employment conditions and social security obligations within the TTTC. [6]
