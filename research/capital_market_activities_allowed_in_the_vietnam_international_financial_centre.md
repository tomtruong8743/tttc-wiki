# Capital Market Activities Allowed in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) is governed by a series of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office, which together establish the legal and regulatory framework for financial and capital market activities within the centre. These decrees cover a broad range of policy areas relevant to capital markets, including [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], and related regulations on land, labour, immigration, and dispute resolution. The specific capital market activities permitted within the TTTC are defined within this overarching [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]].

## Key Points

- The TTTC is established and governed by a suite of government decrees issued by the VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (Government Office of Vietnam). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] is among the key legal instruments addressing financial policies within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] forms the foundational legal basis for the creation and operation of the TTTC. [5]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] governs land and environmental matters relevant to the centre's operations. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] addresses immigration and entry/exit policies for participants in the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the framework for international arbitration within the TTTC. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] covers labour, employment, and social security policies applicable within the TTTC. [6]

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre (Trung tâm tài chính), a special financial zone established by the Vietnamese government under a series of decrees. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] — A government decree specifically addressing financial policies within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] — The foundational government decree establishing the TTTC. [5]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Government Office of Vietnam, the issuing authority for the series of TTTC-related decrees. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
