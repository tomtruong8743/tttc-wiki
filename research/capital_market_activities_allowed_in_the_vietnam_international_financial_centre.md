# Capital Market Activities Allowed in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) is governed by a series of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office, which together establish the legal and regulatory framework for financial and capital market activities within the centre. These decrees cover a broad range of policy areas relevant to capital markets, including [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], and related regulations on land, labour, immigration, banking, foreign exchange, and dispute resolution. The specific capital market activities permitted within the TTTC are defined within this overarching [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]], with [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] providing particularly detailed provisions on permitted financial and capital market transactions for member institutions.

## Key Points

- The TTTC is established and governed by a suite of government decrees issued by the VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (Government Office of Vietnam). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] is among the key legal instruments addressing financial policies within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] forms the foundational legal basis for the creation and operation of the TTTC. [5]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] governs land and environmental matters relevant to the centre's operations, including environmental registration requirements for member investment projects. [6]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] addresses immigration and entry/exit policies for participants in the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the framework for international arbitration within the TTTC, including the jurisdiction of the International Arbitration Centre to resolve disputes related to investment and business activities within the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] covers labour, employment, and social security policies applicable within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] contains detailed rules on permitted capital market activities for member banks operating within the TTTC, including bond trading and cross-border lending. [3][4][5]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] addresses the establishment and operation of a commodities exchange within the TTTC, including pre-agreed settlement arrangements. [1]

## Permitted Capital Market Activities Under Decree 329

[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] sets out a range of capital market and banking activities that member institutions — including 100% foreign-owned commercial banks and foreign bank branches — are permitted to conduct within the TTTC. These include:

### Bond Market Activities

- **Buying and selling Vietnamese corporate bonds** issued within the territory of Vietnam (excluding bonds issued by Vietnamese credit institutions). [4][5]
- **Buying and selling Vietnamese Government bonds**, Government-guaranteed bonds, and local government bonds in accordance with applicable law, including purchasing on the primary market. [4][5]
- **Buying and selling debt** (mua, bán nợ). [4]

These activities are relevant to the broader [[Debt Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Bond Issuance Opportunities In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].

### Cross-Border Lending and Borrowing

- **Borrowing in foreign currency** from individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory is permitted for TTTC members, subject to information declaration and reporting obligations. [3]
- **Lending abroad**: Members wholly owned (100%) by foreign investors may lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to declaration and reporting requirements. [3]
- Members **not** wholly owned by foreign investors must comply with additional conditions for outbound lending set out in Article 89 of Decree 329, including restrictions on which foreign entities they may lend to (e.g., organisations established outside Vietnam in which a Vietnamese enterprise or member holds more than 50% of shares or capital contributions). [3]
- 100% domestically owned commercial banks may lend in foreign currency to organisations established outside Vietnam where a Vietnamese enterprise holds more than 50% of shares or capital contributions, or to organisations held by such qualifying entities. [3]

These provisions relate to [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], [[Capital Movement Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], and [[Cross Border Payments In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].

### Advisory and Other Banking Services

- Member banks (including 100% foreign-owned commercial banks and foreign bank branches) may **provide advisory services** on banking activities and other business activities consistent with the scope of activities specified in their licence issued by the city-level management authority. Advisory solutions must be implemented by the member bank itself or another qualifying member institution. [4]
- Members may also provide **treasury services, asset custody, and safe deposit services**. [4]
- **Factoring and related services**, **letters of credit** and related activities, **discounting**, and **bank guarantees** are also permitted for transactions with non-credit institution organisations on Vietnamese territory. [5]

### Loans from Foreign Organisations

- Members may **borrow in foreign currency** from individuals and organisations outside Vietnam and must comply with information declaration and reporting regimes. [3]

## Arbitration for Capital Market Disputes

Under [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], the International Arbitration Centre within the TTTC has jurisdiction to resolve disputes related to **investment and business activities** at the TTTC, based on agreement between the parties and in accordance with the Centre's own rules and regulations. [2]

However, the International Arbitration Centre does **not** have jurisdiction over:

- Disputes involving administrative decisions or conduct by state management agencies or authorised persons;
- Labour disputes;
- Disputes involving personal rights of individuals;
- Matters already resolved by a competent state management agency or Vietnamese court. [2]

Courts may not refuse to hear applications to annul arbitral awards in cases where the waiver agreement is void under the Law on Commercial Arbitration, or where the waiver was made after one party had already filed an annulment application with the court. [2]

This is relevant to [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], and [[Investor Protection Mechanisms In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].

## Environmental Registration for TTTC Members

Under [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], TTTC members must **register their investment projects with environmental authorities** before discharging waste into the environment, unless exempt under environmental protection law. Environmental registration may be submitted through the TTTC Management Authority in Ho Chi Minh City or Da Nang, as an alternative to direct submission to the relevant commune-level People's Committee. [6]

This procedural pathway reflects the TTTC's role as a one-stop administrative facilitator for member institutions. See also [[Environmental Regulations Tttc]] and [[Land Use Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].

## Commodities Exchange

[[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] governs the establishment and operation of a commodities exchange within the TTTC. Provisions include arrangements for **pre-agreed settlement** of commodity transactions. ## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre (Trung tâm tài chính quốc tế), a special financial zone established by the Vietnamese government under a series of decrees. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] — A government decree specifically addressing financial policies within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] — The foundational government decree establishing the TTTC. [5]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] — A government decree governing banking licensing, establishment, and foreign exchange management within the TTTC, including detailed rules on permitted capital market activities for member institutions. [3][4][5]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] — A government decree establishing the International Arbitration Centre within the TTTC and defining its jurisdiction and operational rules. [2]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Decree 330]] — A government decree governing the establishment and operation of a commodities exchange within the TTTC. [1]
- **Thành viên (Member)** — An institution or entity registered and operating within the TTTC under the applicable decrees. [3][4][5]
- **Trái phiếu Chính phủ** — Vietnamese Government bonds.
