# Land Use Policies in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Land use policies in the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) are governed primarily by [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], one of a suite of foundational decrees issued by the Government Office ([[Financial Center Decrees|VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ]]) to establish and regulate the TTTC. This decree sits alongside related instruments covering [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policies]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], and other aspects of the centre's [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal framework]].

---

## Key Points

- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] is the primary decree addressing land and environmental matters within the TTTC, issued by the Government Office (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ). [3]
- Land use policy forms part of a broader, integrated regulatory framework established through multiple government decrees, all issued by the same issuing authority, VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational establishment context within which land use policies in the TTTC operate. [5]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] covers both land (*đất đai*) and environment (*môi trường*) matters, suggesting that [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|environmental regulations]] are treated alongside land use within the same decree. [3]
- Land use policies are one of several specialised policy areas — alongside [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policy]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour policy]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|arbitration]] — that together constitute the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|complete regulatory framework]] of the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

---

## Definitions

- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] **Decree 326** — The specific government decree governing land use (*đất đai*) and environmental (*môi trường*) policies within the Vietnam International Financial Centre, issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ. [3]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Government Office of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all foundational TTTC decrees, including those covering land use. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- **TTTC** — Abbreviation for *Trung tâm Tài chính*, referring to the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]]. [5]
- [[Environmental Regulations Tttc]] **Môi trường (Environment)** — Environmental matters addressed within [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], treated together with land use under the same regulatory instrument. [3]
