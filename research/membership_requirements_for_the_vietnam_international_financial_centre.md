# Membership Requirements for the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The membership requirements for the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) are governed by a suite of decrees issued by the Government Office of Vietnam. These decrees cover the establishment of the centre, financial policies, land and environment regulations, immigration policy, labour and social security, international arbitration, banking and foreign exchange, and the commodities exchange, all of which together define the conditions under which institutions and individuals may participate in the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]]. The full framework is set out across [[Financial Center Decrees|multiple decrees]], including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]].

## Key Points

- Membership and participation requirements for the TTTC are established through decrees issued by the **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** (Government Office of Vietnam) [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal framework]] for the establishment of the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|TTTC]], which underpins all membership and participation rules [5].
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out the [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]] applicable to participants within the TTTC, including tax incentives and rules governing investment activities [4][5].
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] governs land and environmental conditions relevant to entities seeking to operate within the TTTC [3].
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] establishes [[Foreign Worker Regulations|immigration and entry/exit policies]] that apply to foreign individuals participating in or working within the TTTC [2].
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] defines the [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|labour, employment, and social security]] conditions for participants and workers in the TTTC [6].
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] provides the framework for the [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|international arbitration centre]] associated with the TTTC, which is relevant to participating institutions' legal obligations [4].
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] establishes detailed [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management rules]] for members (Thành viên), including rules on borrowing foreign currency from overseas individuals and organisations, and conditions under which members may lend abroad [2].
- Members (**Thành viên**) that invest in restricted or conditional business sectors applicable to foreign investors in the rest of Vietnam must comply with relevant investment laws; requests for relaxed market access conditions must be submitted by the relevant line ministry to a competent authority for consideration [4].
- Members must obtain written approval from the [[Regulatory Bodies Supervising The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Cơ quan điều hành]] (Management Authority) before conducting overseas investment activities, unless a specific decree provides otherwise [4].
- Investment activities from the rest of Vietnam into the TTTC are governed by National Assembly Resolution No. 222/2025/QH15 and its implementing decrees [4][5].
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] establishes conditions for enjoying preferential treatment (**điều kiện hưởng ưu đãi**) in relation to the [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange|commodities exchange]] within the TTTC, including documentary requirements and compliance with specialised inspection rules for certain categories of goods [1].
- Export and import tax preferences, and other customs procedure incentives for commodities exchange members, are implemented in accordance with [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] [1].
- Goods subject to specialised inspection (food safety, health, environment, agriculture, science and technology, defence, and security) must comply with the regulations and oversight of the relevant specialised management authority [1].
- The [[Regulatory Bodies Supervising The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Cơ quan điều hành]] at each city is responsible for examining applications for establishment of a commodities exchange by TTTC members, issuing and revoking establishment licences, and approving lists of commodities and contract types eligible for trading [1].
- Special investor selection procedures apply for [[Infrastructure Planned For The Vietnam International Financial Centre|TTTC infrastructure projects]], including both PPP and business investment projects, pursuant to Resolution No. 222/2025/QH15 [5].

## Conditions for Enjoying Preferential Treatment (Commodities Exchange)

Under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]], members seeking preferential treatment in connection with the commodities exchange must satisfy the following conditions [1]:

1. **Documentary evidence**: The member must hold documents proving that the relevant goods fall within the categories specified under the applicable article of the decree.
2. **Tax and customs compliance**: Export and import tax preferences and customs procedure incentives are implemented in accordance with [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] on financial policies within the TTTC.
3. **Specialised inspection compliance**: Goods subject to specialised inspection (covering food safety, health, environment, agriculture, science and technology, defence, and security) must comply with the inspection regulations, procedures, and authority of the relevant specialised management agency.

## Foreign Exchange Rules Applicable to Members

Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], the following principles govern [[Foreign Currency Transactions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign currency borrowing and lending]] by TTTC members [2]:

- **Borrowing from overseas**: Members may borrow foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to information declaration and reporting obligations.
- **Lending abroad — 100% foreign-owned members**: Members wholly owned (100%) by foreign investors may lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to information declaration and reporting obligations.
- **Lending abroad — non-100% foreign-owned members**: Members not wholly owned by foreign investors must comply with additional conditions set out in the relevant article of [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], and must also fulfil information declaration and reporting obligations.
- **Domestic commercial banks lending abroad**: A 100% domestically funded commercial bank member may lend in foreign currency to organisations established and operating outside Vietnamese territory where more than 50% of shares or capital contributions are held by Vietnamese members or Vietnamese enterprises that are not members, or to organisations in which such entities hold more than 50% of shares or capital contributions.
- **Member enterprises lending abroad**: A member enterprise may lend to organisations established and operating outside Vietnamese territory where more than 50% of shares or capital contributions are held by the lending member or its parent company.

## Investment Activities and Market Access Conditions

Under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], the following rules apply to members regarding investment activities and market access [4]:

- Members investing in sectors on the **restricted market access list for foreign investors**, or in **conditional business sectors** applicable in the rest of Vietnam, must comply with investment law and related legislation.
- Requests for relaxed market access or investment conditions must be submitted by the relevant sector ministry to the competent authority for review and decision.
- Members are entitled to conduct investment and business activities with organisations and individuals outside Vietnamese territory.
- Before undertaking overseas investment, members must obtain **written approval** from the [[Regulatory Bodies Supervising The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Cơ quan điều hành]], unless a specific provision of a relevant decree provides otherwise.
- Investment from the rest of Vietnam into the TTTC is governed by **Resolution No. 222/2025/QH15** and its implementing decrees.

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|**TTTC**]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, established under Decree 323 by the Government Office of Vietnam [5].
- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|**VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ**]] — The Government Office of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all decrees governing TTTC membership requirements [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|**Chính sách xuất nhập cảnh**]] — Immigration and entry/exit policy, governing the conditions under which foreign nationals may enter and participate in the TTTC [2].
