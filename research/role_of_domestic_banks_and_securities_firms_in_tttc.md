# Role of Domestic Banks and Securities Firms in TTTC

## Summary

Domestic banks and securities firms are anticipated participants in the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]], operating alongside foreign institutions within the special regulatory framework established by the Vietnamese Government. Their roles are governed by the suite of decrees issued by the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ)]], including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], and [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], which collectively define the legal and financial environment in which all market participants — domestic and foreign — may operate.

## Key Points

- The source documents were all issued by the Government Office of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), indicating that the regulatory framework governing domestic banks and securities firms in the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] originates at the highest level of government. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis for the establishment of the TTTC, within which domestic financial institutions would be expected to operate. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] covers financial policies applicable within the TTTC, which would be relevant to the operations of domestic banks and securities firms. [1]
- The broader legal framework includes provisions on land and environment ([[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]]), labour and social security ([[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]]), immigration ([[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]]), and international arbitration ([[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]]), all of which affect how domestic institutions may establish and run operations within the TTTC. [3][6][2][4]

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, a special financial zone established by the Vietnamese Government under a series of decrees. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Financial policies]] — Regulations governing financial activities within the TTTC, issued under Decree 324 by the Government Office. [1]
- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Văn Phòng Chính Phủ]] — The Government Office of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all the foundational TTTC decrees. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
