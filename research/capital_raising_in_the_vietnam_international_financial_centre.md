# Capital Raising in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Capital raising within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) is governed by a suite of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office. The legal and financial framework established through instruments such as [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], and related decrees creates the foundational structure within which capital-raising activities may take place. Supporting policies covering areas such as [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]], [[Currency And Capital Controls]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]] collectively shape the environment for domestic and international capital formation within the TTTC.

[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] provides detailed rules governing [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign and domestic bank members]] operating within the TTTC, including provisions on bond trading, cross-border lending, and [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]] that are directly relevant to capital-raising activities. [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] further extends the capital-raising environment to include commodities exchange activities, with tax and customs incentive conditions linked to the financial policy framework established under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]].

## Key Points

- The legal framework for the TTTC, including capital-raising activities, was established through decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational establishment of the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]], within which [[Investment Banking Opportunities In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital-raising activities]] are situated. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out the [[Financial Services Investment Banks Can Provide In Tttc|financial policies]] applicable within the TTTC, which are directly relevant to capital raising. Tax and customs incentives applicable to [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|commodities exchange]] activities are also implemented by reference to this decree. [2][6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] addresses land and environmental matters within the TTTC, which may affect capital-raising activities tied to physical development. [3]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] covers labour, employment, and social security within the TTTC, forming part of the broader investment environment. [6]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] governs immigration policies within the TTTC, supporting the entry of foreign participants who may be involved in capital-raising transactions. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, providing [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution]] infrastructure relevant to investor confidence and capital raising. [4]
- Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], 100% foreign-owned commercial banks and branches of foreign banks that are TTTC members are permitted to buy and sell corporate bonds issued on Vietnamese territory (excluding bonds issued by Vietnamese credit institutions) and to buy and sell Vietnamese Government bonds, government-guaranteed bonds, and local government bonds — including purchases on the primary market. [1]
- [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Foreign bank members]] under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] are also permitted to underwrite foreign-currency-denominated bonds issued or guaranteed by the Vietnamese Government, as well as foreign-currency-denominated local government bonds, which is directly relevant to [[Debt Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|debt capital markets]] activity. [1]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] permits TTTC members to borrow foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to information disclosure and reporting requirements — a key mechanism for [[International Capital Flows In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|international capital flows]]. [4]
- Members that are 100% foreign-investor-owned are permitted to lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to disclosure and reporting obligations. Members that are not 100% foreign-investor-owned must meet additional lending conditions set out in the decree. [4]
- Domestically capitalised 100% Vietnamese commercial bank members may lend in foreign currency to organisations established and operating outside Vietnamese territory where more than 50% of shares or capital contributions are held by Vietnamese enterprises or by entities controlled by such enterprises. [4]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] includes registration forms for organisations seeking to participate in the TTTC's infrastructure, including a physical premises description form (Bản thuyết minh cơ sở vật chất) that covers systems supporting [[How Companies Raise Capital Through The Vietnam International Financial Centre|crowd-funding capital raising platforms]] (nền tảng huy động vốn cộng đồng) and client asset management activities. [2]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] establishes that tax and other incentives for import and export activities related to the [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange|commodities exchange]] are administered in accordance with the financial policy decree for the TTTC, and that the operating authority in each city is responsible for licensing, approving commodity lists, and approving linkage with foreign commodities exchanges. [6]
- Professional conduct requirements for key personnel at member banks under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] include disqualification criteria related to violations concerning licences, corporate governance, shareholding, credit extension, corporate bond purchases, and safety ratios — matters relevant to the integrity of capital-raising activities within the TTTC. [5]

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre (Trung tâm Tài chính), established by Government decree as a specialised financial hub. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Financial Policies]] — The set of rules and frameworks governing financial activities, including capital raising, within the TTTC, as outlined in Decree 324. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|International Arbitration Centre]] — A body established within the TTTC to resolve disputes, supporting legal certainty for capital markets participants. [4]
- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Legal Framework]] — The collection of decrees issued by the Government Office (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ) that together regulate operations within the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- **Nền tảng huy động vốn cộng đồng** — Crowd-funding capital raising platform; referenced in [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] registration forms as a permitted activity type within the TTTC infrastructure. [2]
- **Bảo lãnh phát hành** — Underwriting; the activity of guaranteeing the issuance of securities or bonds, permitted for qualifying foreign bank members under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. [1]
- **[[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|Ngoại hối]]** — Foreign exchange; governed within the TTTC under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], including rules on cross-border borrowing and lending relevant to capital raising. [4]

## Capital Raising Activities Permitted Under Decree 329

[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] specifies that 100% foreign-owned commercial banks and foreign bank branches that are TTTC members may engage in the following activities relevant to capital raising when transacting with non-credit-institution organisations on Vietnamese territory: [1]

- Lending
- Discounting
- Bank guarantees
- Factoring and related services
- Letters of credit and related business activities
- Buying and selling corporate bonds issued in Vietnam (excluding bonds issued by Vietnamese credit institutions)
- Buying and selling Vietnamese Government bonds, government-guaranteed bonds, and local government bonds — including on the primary market

These permissions are significant for [[Debt Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|debt capital markets]] and [[Bond Issuance Opportunities In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|bond issuance]] activity within the TTTC.
