# Capital Raising in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Capital raising within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) is governed by a suite of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office. The legal and financial framework established through instruments such as [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], and related decrees creates the foundational structure within which capital-raising activities may take place. Supporting policies covering areas such as [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]], [[Currency And Capital Controls]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]] collectively shape the environment for domestic and international capital formation within the TTTC.

## Key Points

- The legal framework for the TTTC, including capital-raising activities, was established through decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational establishment of the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]], within which [[Investment Banking Opportunities In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital-raising activities]] are situated. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out the [[Financial Services Investment Banks Can Provide In Tttc|financial policies]] applicable within the TTTC, which are directly relevant to capital raising. [1]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] addresses land and environmental matters within the TTTC, which may affect capital-raising activities tied to physical development. [3]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] covers labour, employment, and social security within the TTTC, forming part of the broader investment environment. [6]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] governs immigration policies within the TTTC, supporting the entry of foreign participants who may be involved in capital-raising transactions. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, providing [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution]] infrastructure relevant to investor confidence and capital raising. [4]

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre (Trung tâm Tài chính), established by Government decree as a specialised financial hub. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Financial Policies]] — The set of rules and frameworks governing financial activities, including capital raising, within the TTTC, as outlined in Decree 324. [1]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|International Arbitration Centre]] — A body established within the TTTC to resolve disputes, supporting legal certainty for capital markets participants. [4]
- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Legal Framework]] — The collection of decrees issued by the Government Office (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ) that together regulate operations within the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
