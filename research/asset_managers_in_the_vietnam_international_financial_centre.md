# Asset Managers in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Asset managers are among the financial institutions envisaged to operate within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC). The regulatory framework governing the TTTC, including provisions relevant to [[Fund Management In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|fund management]] and asset management activities, is established through a series of government decrees issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], and related instruments covering areas such as [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], and [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]].

## Key Points

- The regulatory framework for the TTTC, which encompasses asset management activities, is established through a suite of decrees issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis for the establishment of the TTTC, within which [[Fund Management In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|asset and fund managers]] would operate. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] governs financial policies within the TTTC, which are directly relevant to [[Types Of Financial Institutions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|asset managers and other financial institutions]] operating there. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] covers labour, employment, and social security matters applicable to workers within the TTTC, including those employed by asset management firms. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] addresses land and environmental regulations relevant to entities, including asset managers, that establish a physical presence in the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] sets out immigration and entry/exit policies that would apply to foreign professionals working for asset managers in the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, providing a [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution mechanism]] relevant to asset managers and their counterparties. [4]

## Definitions

- [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|**Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)**]] — The international financial centre established in Vietnam, governed by a series of government decrees, within which asset managers and other financial institutions are permitted to operate. [5]
- [[Fund Management In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|**Asset/Fund Manager**]] — A financial institution or entity engaged in managing investment funds or portfolios on behalf of clients, operating within the regulatory framework of the TTTC. [1]
- [[Financial Center Decrees|**Government Decrees**]] — Legally binding instruments issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) that establish and regulate the TTTC and its participants. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|**Financial Policies Decree (Decree 324)**]] — The decree governing financial policies within the TTTC, including provisions relevant to asset managers. [1]
- [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|**International Arbitration Centre**]] — A body established under [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] to resolve disputes arising within the TTTC. [4]
