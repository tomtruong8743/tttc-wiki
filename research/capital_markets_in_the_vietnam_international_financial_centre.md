# Capital Markets in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Capital markets within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]] operate under a legal and regulatory framework established by the Vietnamese Government Office ([[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]]). The centre is supported by a suite of related decrees covering [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], all issued by the VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (Government Office of Vietnam).

## Key Points

- The legal framework governing the TTTC, including its capital markets activities, was established through a series of government decrees issued by the VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (Vietnam Government Office). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] provides the foundational establishment of the TTTC. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] covers financial policies within the TTTC, which are relevant to capital markets operations. [1]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, supporting [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution]] for capital markets participants. [4]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]] addresses land and environment policy within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]] covers labour, employment, and social security within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] governs immigration policy within the TTTC, relevant to attracting international capital markets participants. [2]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|**TTTC**]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, established by government decree and governed by a suite of legal instruments issued by the VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ. [5]
- [[Financial Center Decrees|**VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ**]] — The Government Office of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all decrees governing the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|**Financial Policies (Decree 324)**]] — The set of financial regulations and policies applicable within the TTTC, as established by Decree 324. [1]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|**International Arbitration Centre**]] — A dispute resolution body established within the TTTC under Decree 328, relevant to capital markets participants seeking legal recourse. [4]
