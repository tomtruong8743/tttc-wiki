# Capital Markets in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Capital markets within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]] operate under a legal and regulatory framework established by the Vietnamese Government Office ([[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]]). The centre is supported by a suite of related decrees covering [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|banking and foreign exchange]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|commodities exchange]], all issued by the VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (Government Office of Vietnam).

## Key Points

- The legal framework governing the TTTC, including its capital markets activities, was established through a series of government decrees issued by the VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (Vietnam Government Office). [1][2][3][4][5][6][7][8]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] provides the foundational establishment of the TTTC. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] covers financial policies within the TTTC, which are relevant to capital markets operations, including infrastructure and physical requirements for organisations registering to operate within the centre. [1][8]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, supporting [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution]] for capital markets participants. [4]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]] addresses land and environment policy within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]] covers labour, employment, and social security within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] governs immigration policy within the TTTC, relevant to attracting international capital markets participants. [2]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] governs banking licensing, establishment, and [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]] within the TTTC, with direct implications for capital markets participants. [7]
- Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]], TTTC member institutions may borrow in foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to disclosure and reporting obligations. [7]
- [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|100% foreign-owned commercial banks]] and branches of foreign banks that are TTTC members may purchase and sell corporate bonds issued within Vietnam (excluding bonds issued by Vietnamese credit institutions), as well as Vietnamese Government bonds, government-guaranteed bonds, and local government bonds — including purchases on the primary market. [7]
- Member institutions that are 100% foreign-owned may lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to disclosure and [[Reporting Obligations For Financial Institutions In Tttc|reporting requirements]]. [7]
- Member institutions that are not 100% foreign-owned are subject to additional conditions when lending abroad, including restrictions on eligible borrowers based on ownership thresholds. [7]
- 100% domestically-owned commercial banks that are TTTC members may lend in foreign currency to offshore organisations in which Vietnamese enterprises or members hold more than 50% of shares or contributed capital. [7]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Decree 330]] governs the establishment and operation of a [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange|Commodities Exchange]] within the TTTC, providing an additional venue for capital markets-adjacent trading activity. [3]
- Physical infrastructure requirements — including office space, IT systems supporting [[Capital Raising In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|crowdfunding platforms]], and systems for [[Asset Managers In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|asset management]] — are specified in forms under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] for organisations registering to operate in the TTTC. [8]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|**TTTC**]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, established by government decree and governed by a suite of legal instruments issued by the VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ. [5]
- [[Financial Center Decrees|**VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ**]] — The Government Office of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all decrees governing the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6][7][8]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|**Financial Policies (Decree 324)**]] — The set of financial regulations and policies applicable within the TTTC, as established by Decree 324, including physical infrastructure requirements for registered organisations. [1][8]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|**International Arbitration Centre**]] — A dispute resolution body established within the TTTC under Decree 328, relevant to capital markets participants seeking legal recourse. [4]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|**Foreign Exchange Management (Decree 329)**]] — Rules under Decree 329 governing [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|ngoại hối]] (foreign exchange) activities of TTTC member institutions, including borrowing, lending abroad, [[Bond Issuance Opportunities In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|bond trading]], and [[Foreign Currency Transactions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign currency transactions]]. [7]
- **Member Institution (Thành viên)** — An entity that has been admitted to operate within the TTTC framework and is subject to the rights and obligations set out in the relevant decrees, including [[Reporting Obligations For Financial Institutions In Tttc|disclosure and reporting requirements]]. [7]
- **Crowdfunding Platform (Nền tảng huy động vốn cộng đồng)** — A capital-raising mechanism referenced in [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] infrastructure requirements, indicating that [[How Companies Raise Capital Through The Vietnam International Financial Centre|community or crowd-based capital raising]] is a recognised activity within the TTTC. [8]
