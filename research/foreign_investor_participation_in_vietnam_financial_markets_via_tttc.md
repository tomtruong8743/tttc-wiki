# Foreign Investor Participation in Vietnam Financial Markets via TTTC

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre ([[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|TTTC]]) was established through a series of government decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office ([[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]]), creating a dedicated framework designed to attract and facilitate [[Foreign Investment Rules In Tttc|foreign investor participation]] in Vietnam's financial markets. The broader regulatory architecture governing the TTTC spans multiple policy areas, including [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policy]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], and [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|banking licensing and foreign exchange management]], collectively forming the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] intended to support foreign participation in Vietnam's capital and financial markets.

## Key Points

- The TTTC and its associated policies were established through decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ), indicating high-level governmental authority behind the initiative. [1][2][3][4][5][6][7][8]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] forms the foundational legal document for the establishment of the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]]. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] governs the financial policies applicable within the TTTC, relevant to [[Incentives For Foreign Investors In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign investor incentives]] and [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]]. It also includes provisions on the liquidation priority order for insurance enterprises and reinsurance enterprises operating within the TTTC, including wage obligations, insurance claims, tax debts, and other liabilities. [1][2][3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] addresses entry and exit policies within the TTTC, which is directly relevant to [[Foreign Worker Regulations]] and the ability of foreign professionals to operate within the centre. [2]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] covers land and environmental regulations applicable to the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the framework for an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, supporting [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] for foreign investors. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] covers labour, employment, and social security policies within the TTTC, relevant to staffing by [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign financial institutions]]. [6]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] contains detailed [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management rules]] for TTTC member institutions, including rules governing borrowing in foreign currency from overseas individuals and organisations, and conditions for lending abroad. [7][8][9][10]
- All decrees were issued by the same authority (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ — the Government Office of Vietnam), reflecting a coordinated and unified policy approach to the TTTC's establishment. [1][2][3][4][5][6][7][8]

## Foreign Exchange and Lending Rules for TTTC Members

[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] (Nghị định 329) sets out a dedicated section on [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]] as it applies to borrowing and lending activities of TTTC members. Key provisions include:

### Borrowing from Overseas

- TTTC members are permitted to borrow in foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside the territory of Vietnam, subject to information disclosure and reporting obligations. [7]

### Lending Abroad

- Members that are **100% foreign investor-owned** are permitted to lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnam, subject to information disclosure and [[Reporting Obligations For Financial Institutions In Tttc|reporting requirements]]. [7]
- Members **not** 100% foreign-owned must comply with specific conditions set out in [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] (Article 89) for any overseas lending, and are similarly subject to reporting requirements. [7]
- A 100% domestically-funded commercial bank member may lend in foreign currency to:
  - Organisations established and operating outside Vietnam in which a Vietnamese enterprise or non-member Vietnamese company holds more than 50% of shares or capital contributions; or
  - Organisations established and operating outside Vietnam that are majority-owned (>50%) by such qualifying organisations. [7]
- A member enterprise may lend to organisations established and operating outside Vietnam in which the lending member or its parent company holds more than 50% of shares or capital contributions. [7]

### Activities of 100% Foreign-Owned Banks and Foreign Bank Branches

Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], 100% foreign-capital commercial banks and foreign bank branches that are TTTC members are authorised to conduct a range of activities with organisations on Vietnamese territory that are not Vietnamese credit institutions, including: [8][9]

- **Lending** [8]
- **Discounting** [8]
- **Bank guarantees** — subject to conditions, including where the guarantee beneficiary is an individual or organisation in Vietnam, or where 100% of the guarantee value is deposited as collateral [9]
- **Factoring** and related services [8][9]
- **Letter of credit** operations and related commercial activities [8][9]
- **Buying and selling corporate bonds** issued within Vietnamese territory (excluding bonds issued by Vietnamese credit institutions) [8]
- **Buying and selling Vietnamese Government bonds**, government-guaranteed bonds, and local government bonds, including on the primary market [8]
- **Opening payment accounts** in Vietnamese dong and foreign currency at non-member commercial banks or non-member foreign bank branches, in accordance with laws on payment accounts and the State Bank Governor's regulations on dong accounts held by non-residents [9]
- **Foreign currency transactions** — including foreign currency-to-foreign currency transactions, in accordance with the scope defined by the State Bank Governor's regulations on foreign exchange activities [9]
- **Trust and agency services** in banking activities, consistent with parent bank or owner policies [5]
- **Banking advisory services** — advisory solutions must be executed by the 100% foreign-capital bank, the foreign bank branch member, or another member bank holding an appropriate licence issued by the city-level management authority [5]
- **Cash management services**, asset custody, and safe deposit box rental [5]
- **Buying and selling debt** [8]

### Insurance Enterprises within TTTC

Under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], TTTC members and their employees may take out insurance with: [3]

- Insurance enterprises established and operating within the TTTC itself;
- Insurance enterprises established and operating in the rest of Vietnam; or
- Insurance enterprises located overseas.

The management authority (Cơ quan điều hành) is responsible for issuing, modifying, supplementing, and revoking establishment and operating licences for insurance enterprises, reinsurance enterprises, and insurance brokerage enterprises within the TTTC. [3]

Upon dissolution, insurance and reinsurance enterprises within the TTTC must settle liabilities in the following priority order: [2]

1. Wages, severance allowances, social insurance, health insurance, unemployment insurance, and other employee entitlements under signed labour contracts;
2. Payment of indemnities, insurance proceeds, surrender values, contract account values, or premium refunds approved by the insurer or reinsurer;
3. Tax liabilities;
4. Other debts.

Following settlement of these obligations, dissolution proceeds according to the management authority's guidance. [2]
