# Rules for Banks Operating in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The rules governing banks operating in the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) are established through a series of government decrees issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. These decrees collectively form the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]], covering areas such as [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policies]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], and the [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment of the TTTC]] itself.

## Key Points

- The rules for banks operating in the TTTC are governed by multiple decrees issued by the [[Financial Center Decrees|Office of the Government of Vietnam]] (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out the [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]] applicable within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis for the establishment and operation of the TTTC, within which banks operate. [5]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] governs land and environmental matters relevant to institutions, including banks, operating within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] regulates entry and exit policies, which may affect foreign banking personnel working in the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the framework for international arbitration, providing [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] relevant to banks operating in the TTTC. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] covers labour, employment, and social security rules applicable to staff at banks and other institutions in the TTTC. [6]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|TTTC]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, established by government decree as a special financial zone. [5]
- [[Financial Center Decrees|Văn Phòng Chính Phủ]] — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all decrees regulating the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Financial Policies]] — Rules and incentives governing financial activities, including those of banks, within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|International Arbitration Centre]] — A dedicated dispute resolution body established within the TTTC to handle legal disputes, including those involving banks. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Labour and Social Security Rules]] — Regulations governing employment conditions and social security obligations for workers, including bank employees, within the TTTC. [6]
