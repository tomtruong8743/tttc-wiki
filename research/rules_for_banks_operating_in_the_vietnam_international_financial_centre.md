# Rules for Banks Operating in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The rules governing banks operating in the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) are established through a series of government decrees issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. These decrees collectively form the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]], covering areas such as [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policies]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], and the [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment of the TTTC]] itself. Of particular importance to banks is [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], which governs bank licensing, establishment, and [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]] within the TTTC.

## Key Points

- The rules for banks operating in the TTTC are governed by multiple decrees issued by the [[Financial Center Decrees|Office of the Government of Vietnam]] (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out the [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]] applicable within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis for the establishment and operation of the TTTC, within which banks operate. [5]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] governs land and environmental matters relevant to institutions, including banks, operating within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] regulates entry and exit policies, which may affect foreign banking personnel working in the TTTC, including provisions for permanent residency applications (Form NT-01) for foreign nationals working at the TTTC. [2][3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the framework for [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|international arbitration]], providing [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] relevant to banks operating in the TTTC. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] covers labour, employment, and social security rules applicable to staff at banks and other institutions in the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] contains detailed rules on [[Banking Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|banking activities]], licensing, management qualifications, [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|foreign exchange]] (ngoại hối) management, and cross-border lending for member banks in the TTTC. [2][4][6]

## Foreign Exchange and Cross-Border Lending Rules

[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] includes a dedicated section on [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]] for borrowing and lending activities by TTTC member banks. Key provisions include:

- **Borrowing from abroad:** Member banks are permitted to borrow foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to mandatory disclosure and [[Reporting Obligations For Financial Institutions In Tttc|reporting obligations]]. [2]
- **Lending abroad — 100% foreign-owned members:** Members that are 100% foreign-investor-owned are permitted to lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to disclosure and reporting requirements. [2]
- **Lending abroad — non-100% foreign-owned members:** Members not 100% foreign-investor-owned must comply with specific conditions set out in Article 89 of the Decree and must also fulfil disclosure and reporting requirements. [2]
- **Conditions for domestic commercial banks lending abroad (Article 89):** A member that is a 100% domestically funded commercial bank may lend in foreign currency to:
  - Organisations established and operating outside Vietnamese territory in which Vietnamese enterprises (member or non-member) hold more than 50% of shares or contributed capital; and
  - Organisations established and operating outside Vietnamese territory that are themselves majority-owned (more than 50%) by such organisations. [2]
- **Conditions for member enterprises lending abroad:** A member enterprise may lend to organisations established and operating outside Vietnamese territory in which the lending member or its parent company holds more than 50% of shares or contributed capital. [2]

These provisions are relevant to [[Capital Movement Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], [[Cross Border Payments In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], [[International Capital Flows In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], and [[Foreign Currency Transactions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].

## Management and Governance Requirements for Member Banks

[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] sets out qualification and fitness standards for key personnel at member commercial banks. [4][6]

- **Deputy General Directors (Deputy Directors), Chief Accountants, and equivalent roles** must meet the following standards:
  - Must not fall within the prohibited categories specified in Article 33(2) of the Decree; Deputy General Directors must additionally not fall within the prohibited categories in Article 33(1). [4]
  - Must hold at least a university degree in one of the following fields: finance, banking, economics, business administration, law, accounting, auditing, or another field relevant to their designated role; **or** hold a university degree in another field plus at least three years of direct working experience in finance, banking, or their relevant specialisation. [4]
  - Must be resident in Vietnam during their term of office. [4]
  - Chief Accountants must additionally meet the standards and conditions prescribed under Vietnamese accounting law. [6]

- **Personnel not yet having worked in Vietnam:** Candidates for positions including members of the Members' Council, Supervisory Board, General Director (Director), Deputy General Director (Deputy Director), Chief Accountant, and equivalent roles who have not yet accumulated working time in Vietnam are exempt from the disqualification provisions set out in points (a), (b), (c), (d), and (e) of Article 33(1) and Article 33(2) of the Decree. [6]

These requirements are relevant to [[Attracting International Financial Talent To Tttc]], [[Foreign Worker Regulations]], and [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].

## Professional Ethics Standards for Bank Personnel

[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] specifies the circumstances under which individuals are considered to lack professional ethics (đạo đức nghề nghiệp), which would disqualify them from key roles at member banks. These include: [6]

- Persons held responsible by inspection or examination conclusions that led to a Vietnamese credit institution or member bank receiving an administrative penalty in the monetary and banking sector at the highest penalty bracket, for violations involving licensing, governance, management, shares, equity contributions, share purchases, credit extension, corporate bond purchases, or safety ratio requirements. [6]
- Persons held responsible by inspection or examination conclusions that led to a Vietnamese credit institution or member bank receiving an administrative penalty in the monetary and banking sector within a prescribed period (the excerpt does not specify the full duration). [6]

These standards are relevant to [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], [[Risk Management Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], and [[Aml And Compliance Summary For Investment Banks In Tttc]].

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|TTTC]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, established by government decree as a special financial zone. [5]
- [[Financial Center Decrees|Văn Phòng Chính Phủ]] — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all decrees regulating the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Financial Policies]] — Rules and incentives governing financial activities, including those of banks, within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|International Arbitration Centre]] — A dedicated dispute resolution body established within the TTTC to handle legal disputes, including those involving banks. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Labour and Social Security Rules]] — Regulations governing employment conditions and social security obligations for workers, including bank employees, within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|Ngoại hối]] — Foreign exchange; the subject of a dedicated regulatory section in [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] governing borrowing and lending activities of member banks. [2]
- **Thành viên** — Member; refers to TTTC member institutions, including member banks,
