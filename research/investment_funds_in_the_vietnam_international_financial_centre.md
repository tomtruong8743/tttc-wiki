# Investment Funds in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Investment funds operating within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) are part of the broader financial ecosystem established under the legal framework created by the Vietnamese Government. The TTTC, governed by a series of decrees issued by [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] and supported by complementary regulations including [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324 on financial policies]], represents a specialised zone designed to attract and facilitate a range of financial services activities, including [[Fund Management In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|fund management]] and related investment activities. The regulatory environment for investment funds is shaped by policies covering [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|tax incentives]], [[Capital Movement Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital movement]], and [[Foreign Investment Rules In Tttc|foreign investment rules]], all of which are administered through the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). Additional regulatory dimensions relevant to fund operations include [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]] rules under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]], [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|labour regulations]] under [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]], and the [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|commodities exchange framework]] under Decree 330.

## Key Points

- The legal framework governing investment funds and financial activities within the TTTC was issued by the **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** (Office of the Government of Vietnam), indicating high-level governmental authority over the centre's operations. [1]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational establishment decree for the TTTC, under which investment fund activities are situated. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] specifically addresses financial policies within the TTTC, which are expected to cover the regulatory conditions applicable to [[Investment Banking Opportunities In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|investment]] and [[Fund Management In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|fund management]] entities. Application forms and documentation requirements for entities operating under Decree 324 require the signature of the legal representative of the company, with accompanying documents listed and submitted formally. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] governs labour, employment, and social security within the TTTC, which directly affects staffing and operations of [[Asset Managers In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|investment fund managers]] operating in the centre. Notably, foreign workers employed by TTTC member entities may be **exempt from work permit requirements** in certain circumstances, including where they meet professional standards set by the Managing Authority (Cơ quan điều hành) at the relevant cities, or where they fall within exemption categories established under Resolution 22/2025/QH15 or Decree 219/2025/NĐ-CP. Employers remain responsible for applying for confirmation of work permit exemption status on behalf of eligible foreign workers. [3]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] covers land and environmental regulations relevant to the physical establishment of fund management offices and financial institutions within the TTTC. [Source 3 (existing)]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] regulates immigration and entry/exit policies, relevant to foreign fund managers and experts working within the TTTC. [Source 2 (existing)]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the international arbitration framework within the TTTC, which provides [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution mechanisms]] relevant to investment fund operations and [[Investor Protection Mechanisms In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|investor protections]]. [Source 4 (existing)]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] sets out detailed [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]] rules for TTTC members, including rules on **cross-border borrowing and lending** that are relevant to fund financing structures:
  - TTTC members may borrow foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside Vietnam's territory, subject to information declaration and reporting obligations. [4]
  - Members that are **100% foreign investor-owned** may lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnam's territory, subject to the same declaration and reporting obligations. [4]
  - Members that are **not 100% foreign investor-owned** must comply with additional lending conditions set out in Decree 329, including restrictions on which foreign entities they may lend to — generally limited to entities in which the lending member or its parent company holds more than 50% of shares or contributed capital. [4]
  - These cross-border lending and borrowing rules have direct implications for [[Private Equity In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|private equity]] and other investment fund structures that may rely on offshore financing. [4]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] also sets out detailed **qualification and fitness standards** for key management personnel of member commercial banks, including Deputy General Directors, Chief Accountants, and equivalent roles, who must hold at minimum a university-level degree in finance, banking, economics, business administration, law, accounting, auditing, or a related specialist field — or alternatively a university degree in another field combined with at least three years of direct experience in finance, banking, or the relevant specialist area. [2][6]
- Conduct and professional ethics standards are also regulated under Decree 329, with persons found responsible for regulatory violations in monetary and banking sectors — particularly those resulting in the highest tier of administrative penalties — being assessed as failing professional ethics requirements, which may disqualify them from management roles within TTTC member institutions. [6]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Decree 330]] governs the establishment and operation of commodities exchanges (Sở giao dịch hàng hóa) within the TTTC, which may be relevant to investment funds seeking exposure to commodity-linked financial products. Tax incentives for import and export goods traded through the commodities exchange are governed by reference to [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324 on financial policies]], and goods subject to specialist sector inspection (food safety, health, environment, agriculture, science and technology, defence, and security) must comply with the relevant regulatory authority's procedures. [5]
- Under Decree 330, the **Managing Authority (Cơ quan điều hành)** at the relevant cities holds key supervisory responsibilities over the commodities exchange, including reviewing and approving applications for establishment, issuing and revoking licences, approving lists of Vietnam-produced goods permitted for trading, and approving lists of foreign commodities exchange contracts eligible for linked trading. [5]
- All primary decrees are issued by the same issuing authority — the Office of the Government of Vietnam — indicating a unified and coordinated regulatory approach to the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|**TTTC (Trung Tâm Tài Chính)**]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, established by Decree 323, serving as a specialised financial zone in Vietnam. [Source 5 (existing)]
- [[Fund Management In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|**Fund Management**]] — The professional management of investment funds and pooled capital within the TTTC framework, subject to the financial policies set out under Decree 324. [1]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all primary decrees governing the TTTC, including those affecting investment funds. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|**Financial Policies Decree (Decree 324)**]] — The decree establishing the financial policy framework within the TTTC, under which investment fund regulations and [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|tax incentives]] are administered. [1]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|**International Arbitration Centre**]] — An institution established within the TTTC under Decree 328 to provide [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution]] services relevant to investment fund stakeholders. [Source 4 (existing)]
- **Cơ quan điều hành** — The Managing Authority operating at the relevant TTTC cities (Ho Chi Minh City or Da Nang), responsible for key licensing, supervisory, and administrative functions including work permit exemption confirmations and commodities exchange oversight. [3][5]
