# Investment Funds in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Investment funds operating within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) are part of the broader financial ecosystem established under the legal framework created by the Vietnamese Government. The TTTC, governed by a series of decrees issued by [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] and supported by complementary regulations including [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324 on financial policies]], represents a specialised zone designed to attract and facilitate a range of financial services activities, including [[Fund Management In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|fund management]] and related investment activities. The regulatory environment for investment funds is shaped by policies covering [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|tax incentives]], [[Capital Movement Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital movement]], and [[Foreign Investment Rules In Tttc|foreign investment rules]], all of which are administered through the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ).

## Key Points

- The legal framework governing investment funds and financial activities within the TTTC was issued by the **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** (Office of the Government of Vietnam), indicating high-level governmental authority over the centre's operations. [1]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational establishment decree for the TTTC, under which investment fund activities are situated. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] specifically addresses financial policies within the TTTC, which are expected to cover the regulatory conditions applicable to [[Investment Banking Opportunities In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|investment]] and [[Fund Management In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|fund management]] entities. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] governs labour, employment, and social security within the TTTC, which may affect staffing and operations of [[Asset Managers In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|investment fund managers]] operating in the centre. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] covers land and environmental regulations relevant to the physical establishment of fund management offices and financial institutions within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] regulates immigration and entry/exit policies, relevant to foreign fund managers and experts working within the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the international arbitration framework within the TTTC, which provides [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution mechanisms]] relevant to investment fund operations and investor protections. [4]
- All six decrees are issued by the same issuing authority — the Office of the Government of Vietnam — indicating a unified and coordinated regulatory approach to the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|**TTTC (Trung Tâm Tài Chính)**]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, established by Decree 323, serving as a specialised financial zone in Vietnam. [5]
- [[Fund Management In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|**Fund Management**]] — The professional management of investment funds and pooled capital within the TTTC framework, subject to the financial policies set out under Decree 324. [1]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all primary decrees governing the TTTC, including those affecting investment funds. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|**Financial Policies Decree (Decree 324)**]] — The decree establishing the financial policy framework within the TTTC, under which investment fund regulations and [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|tax incentives]] are likely administered. [1]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|**International Arbitration Centre**]] — An institution established within the TTTC under Decree 328 to provide [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution]] services relevant to investment fund stakeholders. [4]
