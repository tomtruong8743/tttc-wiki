# Infrastructure Planned for the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The infrastructure framework for the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) is established through a suite of government decrees issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam ([[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]]). These decrees collectively define the regulatory, physical, legal, and institutional architecture of the TTTC, covering areas such as [[Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|labour and employment]], [[Immigration And Residency Policies For Tttc Workers|immigration policy]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policies]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]]. Together, they form the foundational infrastructure — both regulatory and physical — upon which the TTTC operates.

---

## Key Points

- All core decrees governing the TTTC were issued by the **Office of the Government of Vietnam** (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), indicating a high level of central government authority and commitment to the centre's establishment. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the legal basis for the formal establishment of the TTTC as an institutional entity. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out the [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial and tax policy]] infrastructure applicable within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] governs the physical land and environmental regulatory infrastructure of the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] establishes the labour, employment, and social security framework that underpins the TTTC's workforce infrastructure. [6]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] creates the immigration and entry/exit policy infrastructure to facilitate the movement of international professionals into the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|international arbitration centre]] as part of the TTTC's legal and dispute resolution infrastructure. [4]

---

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|**Decree 323**]] — The foundational decree establishing the Vietnam International Financial Centre as a legal and institutional entity, issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|**Decree 324**]] — The decree governing financial policies, including [[Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|tax incentives]] and related financial regulations, within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|**Decree 325**]] — The decree covering labour, employment, and social security arrangements for workers within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|**Decree 326**]] — The decree regulating land use and environmental matters within the TTTC's physical jurisdiction. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|**Decree 327**]] — The decree establishing immigration and entry/exit policies for personnel operating within the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|**Decree 328**]] — The decree establishing an international arbitration centre as part of the TTTC's legal infrastructure. [4]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all foundational TTTC decrees listed in the source documents. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
