# Tax Incentives in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The [[Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) operates under a framework of financial policies established by the Vietnamese Government, with [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]] forming a core component of its strategy to attract participants. These incentives are primarily governed by [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], issued by the Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), which sets out the financial and tax policy arrangements applicable within the TTTC. The broader regulatory environment is supported by a suite of related decrees covering areas such as [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]].

## Key Points

- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] is the primary decree governing financial policies, including tax incentives, within the TTTC, and was issued by the Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [1]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis upon which the TTTC, and its associated incentive framework, was established. [5]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] was issued alongside the financial policy decree, suggesting that tax and financial incentives operate in conjunction with labour and social security policies within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] was issued by the same authority, indicating that land-related policies and potential land tax incentives are part of the broader TTTC incentive structure. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] complements the financial incentives by addressing immigration policies, which may support the movement of foreign professionals into the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|dispute resolution mechanisms]] that provide legal certainty, reinforcing the value of the tax and financial incentives offered. The International Arbitration Centre has jurisdiction to resolve disputes relating to investment and business activities at the TTTC, but explicitly excludes disputes involving administrative decisions or actions of state management bodies, labour disputes, disputes relating to personal rights, and matters already resolved by a competent Vietnamese court or state authority. [4][5]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] governs [[Banking Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|banking licensing, establishment, and foreign exchange management]] within the TTTC. It sets out the activities that 100% foreign-owned commercial banks and foreign bank branches may conduct, including lending, discounting, bank guarantees, factoring, letters of credit, and trading of corporate bonds, government bonds, and government-guaranteed bonds issued in Vietnam. It also establishes [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange rules]] for borrowing and lending across borders, and requires foreign investors to channel investment capital through designated capital accounts at member banks. [2][3][4][6]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] governs the establishment and operation of the [[Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|commodities exchange]] within the TTTC, including pre-agreed settlement arrangements. [1]
- All foundational decrees were issued by **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** (the Government Office of Vietnam), indicating a coordinated, whole-of-government approach to the TTTC's incentive framework. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Banking and Foreign Exchange Rules Relevant to Tax and Financial Incentives

[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] contains detailed provisions that interact with the broader tax and financial incentive regime:

- **Borrowing from overseas**: TTTC members may borrow foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside Vietnam, subject to information declaration and reporting obligations. [3]
- **Lending overseas**: Members that are 100% foreign-investor-owned may lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnam, subject to declaration and reporting. Members not 100% foreign-owned must satisfy additional conditions before lending abroad, including rules on the share of loan proceeds that must be on-lent to investment projects in preferential investment sectors under Vietnamese investment law. [3][4]
- **Short-term borrowing limits**: Vietnamese credit institutions borrowing short-term from TTTC members are subject to caps on total principal outstanding relative to standalone equity — 30% for commercial banks that are not TTTC members, and 150% for foreign bank branches and other credit institutions that are not TTTC members. [4]
- **Capital accounts for foreign investors**: Foreign investors must transfer capital related to their investment activities into the TTTC through a designated foreign investor capital account opened at a member bank. [4]
- **Activities of 100% foreign-owned banks and foreign bank branches**: These institutions may conduct a broad range of activities with non-credit-institution entities on Vietnamese territory, including lending, discounting, bank guarantees, factoring, letters of credit, purchase and sale of corporate bonds (excluding those issued by Vietnamese credit institutions), and purchase and sale of Vietnamese government bonds (including on the primary market), government-guaranteed bonds, and local government bonds. [6]
- **Advisory services**: Member banks may provide advisory services on banking and other business activities consistent with the scope of their licence, provided that the advice is implemented by the relevant member bank itself. [2]
- **Treasury and custody services**: Member banks may provide treasury services, asset custody, and safe deposit box rental. [2]

## Dispute Resolution and Legal Certainty

The [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|International Arbitration Centre]] established under [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] provides an important underpinning for the TTTC's incentive framework by offering reliable [[How Disputes Are Handled In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|dispute resolution]] to investors. Under Decree 328:

- The Centre has jurisdiction over disputes arising from investment and business activities at the TTTC, based on agreement between the parties. [5]
- Courts may not refuse to hear applications to set aside arbitral awards or decisions of an arbitral tribunal, except where a waiver agreement falls within the categories of void agreements under the Law on Commercial Arbitration, or where the agreement was concluded after a party has already filed an application to set aside the award. [5]
- The Centre's licence may be revoked, resulting in cessation of its operations, in accordance with applicable arbitration law. [5]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] **Decree 324** — the primary Vietnamese Government decree setting out financial policies, including tax-related incentives, applicable to entities operating within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview]] **TTTC (Trung Tâm Tài Chính Quốc Tế)** — the Vietnam International Financial Centre, a special economic and financial zone operating under a dedicated regulatory framework including tax incentive policies. [5]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — the Government Office of Vietnam, the issuing authority for the foundational decrees establishing the TTTC and its associated policy frameworks. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Financial Center Decrees]] **Foundational Decrees** — the suite of decrees (323–330) collectively establishing the legal, financial, labour, land, immigration, arbitration, banking, and commodities exchange framework of the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc]] **Ngoại hối** — foreign exchange, governed within the TTTC by [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], including rules on cross-border borrowing, lending, and capital transfers. [3][4]
- **Tài khoản vốn của nhà đầu tư nước ngoài** — foreign investor capital account, the designated account through which foreign investors must channel investment-related transfers into the TTTC. [4]
