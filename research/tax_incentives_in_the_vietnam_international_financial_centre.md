# Tax Incentives in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The [[Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) operates under a framework of financial policies established by the Vietnamese Government, with [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]] forming a core component of its strategy to attract participants. These incentives are primarily governed by [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], issued by the Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), which sets out the financial and tax policy arrangements applicable within the TTTC. The broader regulatory environment is supported by a suite of related decrees covering areas such as [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]].

## Key Points

- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] is the primary decree governing financial policies, including tax incentives, within the TTTC, and was issued by the Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [1]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis upon which the TTTC, and its associated incentive framework, was established. [5]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] was issued alongside the financial policy decree, suggesting that tax and financial incentives operate in conjunction with labour and social security policies within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] was issued by the same authority, indicating that land-related policies and potential land tax incentives are part of the broader TTTC incentive structure. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] complements the financial incentives by addressing immigration policies, which may support the movement of foreign professionals into the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes dispute resolution mechanisms that provide legal certainty, reinforcing the value of the tax and financial incentives offered. [4]
- All decrees were issued by **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** (the Government Office of Vietnam), indicating a coordinated, whole-of-government approach to the TTTC's incentive framework. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] **Decree 324** — the primary Vietnamese Government decree setting out financial policies, including tax-related incentives, applicable to entities operating within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview]] **TTTC (Trung Tâm Tài Chính Quốc Tế)** — the Vietnam International Financial Centre, a special economic and financial zone operating under a dedicated regulatory framework including tax incentive policies. [5]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — the Government Office of Vietnam, the issuing authority for all six foundational decrees establishing the TTTC and its associated policy frameworks. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Financial Center Decrees]] **Foundational Decrees** — the suite of six decrees (323–328) collectively establishing the legal, financial, labour, land, immigration, and arbitration framework of the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
