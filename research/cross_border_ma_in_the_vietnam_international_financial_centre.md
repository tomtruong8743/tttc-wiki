# Cross Border M&A in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Cross border mergers and acquisitions (M&A) activity within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) is governed by a framework of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office. The TTTC has been established as a special financial zone designed to attract foreign participation in Vietnam's financial markets, with a legal architecture spanning financial policy, land and environment, labour, immigration, and international arbitration. Key governing instruments include [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], all of which collectively shape the environment in which cross border M&A transactions may occur within the centre.

## Key Points

- The TTTC is established and governed by a suite of decrees issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Government Office of Vietnam), forming the foundational [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] for all activities, including cross border M&A. [1][5]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the primary legal basis for the establishment of the TTTC, within which cross border M&A transactions would be structured and recognised. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out the financial policy framework applicable within the TTTC, which is directly relevant to the structuring and execution of cross border M&A deals. [1]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] governs land and environmental matters within the TTTC, which may have relevance to M&A transactions involving real property or physical assets. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] provides immigration policy settings for the TTTC, which may facilitate the movement of foreign professionals and deal teams involved in cross border M&A activity. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, providing a [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution mechanism]] relevant to cross border M&A transactions involving foreign parties. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] addresses labour and social security matters within the TTTC, which may affect workforce-related considerations in M&A transactions. [6]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|TTTC]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, established by decree of the Vietnamese Government Office as a special financial zone. [5]
- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Governing Decrees]] — A suite of government decrees issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ that collectively regulate activities within the TTTC, including financial policy, land, labour, immigration, and dispute resolution. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|International Arbitration Centre]] — A dispute resolution body established within the TTTC under Decree 328, relevant to resolving cross border M&A disputes. [4]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Financial Policy Framework]] — The regulatory framework governing financial activities within the TTTC, established under Decree 324. [1]
