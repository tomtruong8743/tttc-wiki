# Financial Services in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) is established and governed by a series of decrees issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. These decrees collectively define the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]], covering areas such as [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]], forming the foundational regulatory structure for [[Financial Services In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] operating within the centre.

## Key Points

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] was issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam, establishing the legal basis for the TTTC. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] was issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam, setting out financial policies applicable within the centre, including rules governing the winding-up and debt-priority order for [[Insurance]] enterprises, reinsurance enterprises, and insurance brokers operating in the TTTC. [1][6]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] was issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam, addressing labour, employment, and social security matters within the TTTC. [6 (context)]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] was issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam, governing land and environmental regulations within the centre. [3 (context)]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] was issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam, covering entry and exit policies relevant to the TTTC. [2 (context)]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] was issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam, establishing the framework for [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] within the TTTC. [4 (context)]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] governs banking licensing, establishment, and [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] at the TTTC. Key provisions include: [2][5]
  - Members of the TTTC may borrow foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory and must comply with information declaration and [[Reporting Obligations For Financial Institutions In Tttc|reporting obligations]]. [2]
  - Members wholly owned (100%) by foreign investors may lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to information declaration and reporting requirements. [2]
  - Members not wholly owned by foreign investors must satisfy additional conditions under Article 89 of [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] before lending abroad, including restrictions based on shareholding thresholds. [2]
  - Domestic commercial banks (100% domestically owned) may lend in foreign currency to overseas entities in which Vietnamese members or enterprises hold more than 50% of shares or capital contributions. [2]
  - Senior management of member commercial banks — including Deputy General Directors, Chief Accountants, and equivalent roles — must meet qualification requirements including a university-level degree in finance, banking, economics, business administration, law, accounting, auditing, or a related specialisation, or a university degree in another field combined with at least three years of direct experience in finance or banking. [5]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] governs the establishment and operation of the [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange|Commodities Exchange]] within the TTTC. Key provisions include: [3][4]
  - Tax incentives for export and import duties, as well as other customs procedure incentives related to eligible commodities, are implemented in accordance with the financial policies decree ([[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]). [4]
  - Commodities subject to specialised inspection (including food safety, health, environment, agriculture, science and technology, defence, and security) must comply with applicable regulations, inspection procedures, and the authority of the relevant specialised management agencies. [4]
  - The Ministry of Industry and Trade ([[Regulatory Bodies Supervising The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Bộ Công Thương]]) is responsible for coordinating with the supervisory authority to inspect the operations of the Commodities Exchange and trading activities conducted through it. [4]
  - The Operating Authority in each city is responsible for reviewing and processing applications for establishment of the Commodities Exchange by TTTC members, issuing and revoking establishment licences, approving the list of domestically produced commodities permitted for trading, approving the list of contracts from foreign commodities exchanges permitted for linked trading, and deciding on additions, amendments, suspensions, or terminations of transactions. [4]
- All decrees forming the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] share a common issuing authority: the Office of the Government of Vietnam (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- Upon completion of debt repayment obligations during dissolution, insurance enterprises, reinsurance enterprises, and insurance brokers within the TTTC must proceed with dissolution in accordance with guidance from the Operating Authority ([[Regulatory Bodies Supervising The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]). [6]

## Definitions

- **TTTC (Trung tâm Tài chính Quốc tế)** — the Vietnam International Financial Centre, the subject entity established and regulated by the series of government decrees. See [[Trung Tam Tai Chinh Quoc Te Definition And Importance]]. [5]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — the Office of the Government of Vietnam, the issuing authority for all decrees related to the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- **Financial policies** — the regulatory and fiscal rules governing [[Financial Services In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and institutions operating within the TTTC, as set out in [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]. [1]
- **International Arbitration Centre** — a [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|dispute resolution]] body established within the TTTC framework to handle international commercial disputes, governed by [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]]. [4 (context)]
- **Ngoại hối (ngoai hoi)** — foreign exchange; [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|foreign currency]] transactions and management rules applicable to TTTC members, governed primarily by [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. [2]
- **Thành viên (Member)** — a TTTC member institution, subject to the rights and obligations set out across the decrees, including [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange borrowing and lending rules]], reporting requirements, and management qualification standards. [2][5]
- **Sở giao dịch hàng hóa (Commodities Exchange)** — the commodities trading exchange established and operated within the TTTC under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]], subject to oversight by both the Ministry of Industry and Trade and the local Operating Authority. [3][4]
- **Cơ quan điều hành (Operating Authority)** — the management body in each city responsible for licensing, supervision, and regulatory decisions over TTTC institutions and exchanges, including the [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange|Commodities Exchange]] and insurance enterprises undergoing dissolution. [4][6]
- **Debt priority order (upon dissolution)** — under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], when an insurance, reinsurance, or insurance brokerage enterprise dissolves, debts are repaid in the following order: (1) employee wages, severance, social insurance, health insurance, unemployment insurance, and other contractual entitlements; (2) insurance and reinsurance payment obligations already approved, including surrender values, policy account values, and premium refunds; (3) tax debts; (4) other debts. [6]
