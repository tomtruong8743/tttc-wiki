# Financial Services in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) is established and governed by a series of decrees issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. These decrees collectively define the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]], covering areas such as [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], and [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], forming the foundational regulatory structure for financial services operating within the centre.

## Key Points

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] was issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam, establishing the legal basis for the TTTC. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] was issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam, setting out financial policies applicable within the centre. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] was issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam, addressing labour, employment, and social security matters within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] was issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam, governing land and environmental regulations within the centre. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] was issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam, covering entry and exit policies relevant to the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] was issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam, establishing the framework for international arbitration within the TTTC. [4]
- All decrees forming the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] share a common issuing authority: the Office of the Government of Vietnam (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ). [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] **TTTC (Trung tâm Tài chính Quốc tế)** — the Vietnam International Financial Centre, the subject entity established and regulated by the series of government decrees. [5]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — the Office of the Government of Vietnam, the issuing authority for all decrees related to the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] **Financial policies** — the regulatory and fiscal rules governing financial services and institutions operating within the TTTC, as set out in Decree 324. [1]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] **International Arbitration Centre** — a dispute resolution body established within the TTTC framework to handle international commercial disputes. [4]
