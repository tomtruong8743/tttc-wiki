# Tax Incentive Summary for Investment Banks in TTTC

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre ([[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|TTTC]]) is governed by a series of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office, collectively forming the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] for financial, tax, land, labour, immigration, and arbitration matters. [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]] available to [[Investment Banking Opportunities In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|investment banks]] operating in TTTC are established primarily under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], alongside related policies set out in companion decrees. These incentives form a core part of Vietnam's strategy for [[Attracting Foreign Investors To Vietnam Capital Markets Via Tttc]].

The broader regulatory framework also encompasses [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], which governs banking licensing and foreign exchange management within TTTC, and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]], which establishes the commodities exchange and its clearing infrastructure. Both decrees interact with the financial policy environment relevant to investment banks considering operations in TTTC.

## Key Points

- The decrees governing TTTC, including those relevant to tax incentives for investment banks, were issued by the **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** (Office of the Government of Vietnam). [1][5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] is the primary decree covering financial policies within TTTC, which would include [[Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|tax incentive provisions]] applicable to investment banks. It also contains template licensing frameworks for insurance enterprises, reinsurance enterprises, and insurance brokers operating within TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] establishes the legal foundation of TTTC, upon which all subsequent financial and tax policies are built, including provisions addressing prohibited activities such as support for proliferation of weapons of mass destruction. [6]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] addresses labour and employment matters within TTTC, which may interact with [[Personal Income Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] for workers and experts. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] covers land and environmental policy within TTTC, which may be relevant to the physical establishment of investment bank operations. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] governs immigration policies within TTTC, relevant to [[Foreign Worker Regulations]] for international banking staff. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration centre within TTTC, supporting [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] for investment banks. [4]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] governs the licensing and establishment of banks and the management of foreign exchange within TTTC. It specifies permitted activities for both fully foreign-owned commercial banks and 100% domestically owned commercial banks operating in TTTC, including buying and selling government bonds, corporate bonds, lending, discounting, bank guarantees, factoring, letters of credit, trust operations, advisory services, and treasury and asset custody services. [4][5]
- Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], 100% domestically owned commercial banks (*ngân hàng thương mại 100% vốn trong nước*) operating in TTTC are permitted to buy and sell Vietnamese government bonds on both the primary and secondary market, but are prohibited from selling government bonds, government-guaranteed bonds, or local government bonds issued in foreign currency. [4]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] governs the establishment and operation of the commodities exchange (*Sở giao dịch hàng hóa*) within TTTC, including the approval process for clearing centres (*Trung tâm thanh toán bù trừ*). Entities approved as clearing centres must comply with the requirements of the relevant 2025 government decree and all related legal instruments. [1]
- Credit ratings from **Standard & Poor's**, **Moody's**, or **Fitch Ratings** are referenced in [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] in the context of eligibility criteria for banking entities operating within TTTC. [2]

> **Note:** The source excerpts provide procedural, licensing, and operational content from multiple decrees. Detailed content of specific tax incentive rates, durations, or conditions remains **not fully covered** in the source documents provided. Information on exact tax rates or exemption periods should be verified against the full text of [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]].

## Definitions

- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] **Decree 324** — The government decree establishing financial policies within TTTC, understood to be the primary legal instrument governing [[Tax Benefits For Companies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|tax benefits]] and related financial incentives for entities including investment banks. Also contains licensing templates for insurance and reinsurance enterprises. [3]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] **Decree 329** — The government decree governing banking licensing, establishment, and foreign exchange management within TTTC. Sets out permitted activities for foreign and domestic commercial banks and references international credit rating standards. [2][4][5]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] **Decree 330** — The government decree governing the establishment and operation of the commodities exchange (*Sở giao dịch hàng hóa*) within TTTC, including the approval and operation of clearing centres. [1]
- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|**TTTC**]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre; a special financial zone established by the Vietnamese Government. [5]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all TTTC-related decrees. [1][5]
- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|**Legal Framework**]] — The suite of decrees (323–330) collectively establishing the governance, financial, labour, land, immigration, arbitration, banking, and commodities exchange rules of TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- ***Trung tâm thanh toán bù trừ*** — Clearing centre; an entity approved to perform clearing and settlement functions within the TTTC commodities exchange framework under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]]. [1]
- ***Ngân hàng thương mại 100% vốn trong nước*** — A fully domestically owned commercial bank operating within TTTC under the provisions of [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. [4]
- ***Ngân hàng thương mại 100% vốn nước ngoài*** — A fully foreign-owned commercial bank operating within TTTC under the provisions of [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. [5]
