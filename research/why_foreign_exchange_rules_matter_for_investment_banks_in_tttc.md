# Why Foreign Exchange Rules Matter for Investment Banks in TTTC

## Summary

Foreign exchange rules are a critical regulatory dimension for investment banks operating within the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC (Vietnam International Financial Centre)]]. These rules, established through the suite of government decrees issued by the [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ)]], shape how banks manage [[Foreign Currency Transactions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign currency transactions]], move capital across borders, and conduct [[International Capital Flows In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|international capital flows]]. Understanding the [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange rules in TTTC]] is essential for any investment bank seeking to operate effectively within the centre's [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal framework]].

[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] — formally titled the decree on licensing, establishment of banks, and foreign exchange management (*quản lý ngoại hối*) in TTTC — is the primary instrument governing foreign exchange operations within the centre, and contains specific provisions on cross-border borrowing, lending abroad, and [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|ngoại hối]] management for member institutions [1][5].

## Key Points

- The decrees governing TTTC were all issued by the same authority — VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (Office of the Government) — indicating a unified, top-level regulatory framework applicable to investment banks [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] is the specific decree on banking licensing, establishment, and **foreign exchange management** (*quản lý ngoại hối*) at TTTC. It contains a dedicated section (Section 4) on foreign exchange rules governing borrowing and lending activities by member institutions [5].
- Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], member institutions (**thành viên**) are permitted to borrow foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, but must comply with information declaration and reporting obligations [5].
- Fully foreign-owned member institutions (100% foreign capital) are permitted to lend foreign currency to individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to declaration and reporting requirements [5].
- Member institutions that are **not** 100% foreign-owned must satisfy additional conditions for lending abroad, as specified in Article 89 of [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. These conditions include restrictions based on the ownership structure of the overseas borrower (e.g., the borrower must be more than 50% owned by a Vietnamese enterprise or by the lending member) [5].
- Domestically capitalised commercial bank members (100% domestic capital) may lend in foreign currency to overseas entities where a Vietnamese company or non-member Vietnamese enterprise holds more than 50% of shares or contributed capital [5].
- Enterprise members may lend to overseas organisations in which the lending member or its parent company holds more than 50% of shares or contributed capital [5].
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] addresses financial policies within TTTC, which are directly relevant to how investment banks handle currency and capital matters, including licensing provisions distributed to the [[Financial Supervision And Governance|supervisory authority]] [2].
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis upon which all subsequent rules — including foreign exchange rules — are built [5].
- Internal governance of member commercial banks — including risk management committees and supervisory boards — is regulated under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], with requirements for composition, operating charters, and mandatory reporting to both the city-level executive authority (*Cơ quan điều hành tại thành phố*) and the [[Regulatory Bodies Supervising The Vietnam International Financial Centre|supervisory authority]] (*Cơ quan giám sát*) [1].
- Multiple decrees govern overlapping aspects of investment bank operations, including [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]] [3], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]] [6], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration]] [3][6], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]] [4], all of which intersect with the operational needs of foreign-facing financial institutions.

## Foreign Exchange Management Rules for Member Institutions

The following provisions are drawn directly from Section 4 ("Quy định quản lý ngoại hối đối với hoạt động vay, cho vay của thành viên") of [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] [5]:

### Borrowing from Overseas (Article 87)
- Member institutions (**thành viên**) may borrow foreign currency (*vay vốn bằng ngoại tệ*) from individuals or organisations outside Vietnamese territory.
- Such borrowing requires mandatory **information declaration** and compliance with the applicable **reporting regime** (*chế độ báo cáo*).

### Lending Abroad (Articles 88–89)
- **100% foreign-owned members**: May lend to individuals or organisations outside Vietnamese territory and must comply with information declaration and reporting obligations.
- **Members not 100% foreign-owned**: Must satisfy the additional conditions for overseas lending set out in Article 89, in addition to declaration and reporting obligations.
- **100% domestically capitalised commercial bank members**: May lend in foreign currency to:
  - Overseas organisations where a Vietnamese enterprise (that is a member or non-member) holds more than 50% of shares or contributed capital; or
  - Overseas organisations that are themselves held more than 50% by such organisations.
- **Enterprise members**: May lend to overseas organisations in which the lending member or its parent company holds more than 50% of shares or contributed capital.

### Internal Governance and Reporting (Articles 26–27)
- Risk Management Committees (*Ủy ban quản lý rủi ro*) and Personnel Committees (*Ủy ban nhân sự*) of member commercial banks must each have a minimum of **three members**, with the chairperson being a member of the Board of Members (*Hội đồng thành viên*) [1].
- More than half of each committee's voting members must be **non-executive members** [1].
- A Board of Members member may only chair **one** committee [1].
- Within **10 days** of issuing internal operating charters, member banks must submit these to the city-level executive authority and the supervisory authority [1].
- The Supervisory Board (*Ban kiểm soát*) monitors compliance with legal regulations, internal rules, and decisions of the owner and Board of Members [1].

## Definitions

- [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|**Ngoại hối**]] — Foreign exchange; the Vietnamese term used in [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] to describe the subject matter of foreign exchange management rules applicable to TTTC member institutions [5].
- [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|**Foreign exchange rules**]] — Regulations governing the buying, selling, conversion, and transfer of foreign currencies within TTTC, including cross-border borrowing and lending rules for member institutions [5].
- **Thành viên** — "Member"; refers to member institutions of TTTC subject to the foreign exchange rules in [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] [5].
- **Chế độ báo cáo** — Reporting regime; the mandatory reporting obligations that member institutions must comply with when engaging in cross-border borrowing or lending in foreign currency [5].
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|**Decree 324**]] — A decree issued by the Office of the Government (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ) covering financial policies within TTTC, including licensing and supervisory distribution requirements [2].
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|**Decree 323**]] — The foundational decree establishing TTTC, issued by the Office of the Government [5].
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all major TTTC-related decrees [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|**Decree 329**]] — The decree specifically addressing banking licensing, establishment, and foreign exchange management (*quản lý ngoại hối*) within TTTC, containing detailed provisions on cross-border borrowing, lending abroad, internal governance, and reporting obligations for member institutions [1][5].
