# Foreign Ownership Rules in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Foreign ownership rules within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) govern the extent to which foreign investors and institutions may hold stakes in financial entities and participate in capital markets operating within the centre. These rules intersect with broader [[Financial Services In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|financial services]] regulations, [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|tax incentive frameworks]], and [[Currency And Capital Controls|currency and capital control]] policies established under the suite of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office, including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], and related instruments. The specific provisions aim to balance [[Attracting Foreign Investors To Vietnam Capital Markets Via Tttc|attracting foreign capital]] with maintaining appropriate oversight of the domestic financial system.
