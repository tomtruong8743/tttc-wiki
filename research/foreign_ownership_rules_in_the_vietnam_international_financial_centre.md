# Foreign Ownership Rules in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Foreign ownership rules within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) govern the extent to which foreign investors and institutions may hold stakes in financial entities and participate in capital markets operating within the centre. These rules intersect with broader [[Financial Services In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|financial services]] regulations, [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|tax incentive frameworks]], and [[Currency And Capital Controls|currency and capital control]] policies established under the suite of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office, including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], and related instruments. The specific provisions aim to balance [[Attracting Foreign Investors To Vietnam Capital Markets Via Tttc|attracting foreign capital]] with maintaining appropriate oversight of the domestic financial system.

A key structural distinction running through the TTTC framework is whether a member entity is **100% foreign-owned** or only **partially foreign-owned**. This distinction triggers different rights and obligations — particularly in relation to [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]], cross-border lending, and the scope of permitted [[Banking Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|banking activities]].

## Key Points

### 100% Foreign-Owned Member Entities

Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] (Article 88), a TTTC member that is **100% owned by foreign investors** (*nhà đầu tư nước ngoài sở hữu 100% vốn điều lệ*) is permitted to lend funds to individuals and organisations located outside Vietnamese territory, subject to information disclosure and reporting requirements. [1]

This represents a significant [[Incentives For Foreign Investors In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|incentive for fully foreign-owned institutions]], as it grants them broader cross-border lending capacity compared to mixed-ownership or fully domestic members. [1]

Separately, under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], **100% foreign-owned commercial banks** (*ngân hàng thương mại 100% vốn nước ngoài*) and **foreign bank branches** (*chi nhánh ngân hàng nước ngoài*) operating as TTTC members are authorised to provide banking advisory services, provided that those advisory solutions are carried out by the foreign bank member or another member bank within the scope of its licence issued by the city-level management authority (*Cơ quan điều hành tại thành phố*). [6]

### Partially Foreign-Owned or Domestically Owned Member Entities

Members that are **not 100% foreign-owned** face additional conditions when engaging in cross-border lending. Under Article 88 of [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], such members must comply with the specific lending conditions set out in Article 89 of the same decree before they may lend to parties outside Vietnamese territory, and must also fulfil information disclosure and reporting obligations. [1]

Under Article 89, the following conditions apply:

- A **100% domestically owned commercial bank** (*ngân hàng thương mại 100% vốn trong nước*) may lend in foreign currency to:
  - Organisations established and operating outside Vietnamese territory in which Vietnamese enterprises or non-member Vietnamese companies hold more than 50% of shares or contributed capital; and
  - Organisations established and operating outside Vietnamese territory that are themselves majority-owned (>50%) by such qualifying organisations. [1]

- A **member enterprise** (non-bank) may lend to organisations established and operating outside Vietnamese territory in which the lending member or its parent company holds more than 50% of shares or contributed capital. [1]

### Foreign Investor Requirements for Insurance Entities

Under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], foreign investors seeking to establish insurance companies (*doanh nghiệp bảo hiểm*), reinsurance companies (*doanh nghiệp tái bảo hiểm*), or insurance brokerage companies (*doanh nghiệp môi giới bảo hiểm*) within the TTTC must provide documentation that includes, among other items: [3]

- Information on the **ownership ratio** (*tỷ lệ sở hữu*) held by each investor in the prospective entity (this requirement does not apply to insurance brokerage enterprises); [3]
- A **blocked account certificate** from a licensed bank operating within the TTTC or elsewhere in Vietnam, confirming the amount of capital contributed by each investor, the purpose of the block, the block duration, and the conditions for release; [3]
- A **confirmation document from the competent authority** of the country where the foreign investor is headquartered, confirming that the investor is permitted to establish an insurance or reinsurance or brokerage entity within the TTTC. Where such home-country law does not require a formal approval, a confirmation from the relevant competent person or body under that country's law may be substituted. [3]

These requirements apply to the [[Licenses And Approvals For Financial Institutions In Tttc|licensing and establishment]] process for insurance-sector participants.

### Borrowing from Overseas

Under Article 87 of [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], **all TTTC members** — regardless of ownership structure — are permitted to borrow funds in foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory. However, they must comply with information disclosure requirements and the applicable reporting regime. [1]

This provision applies symmetrically to both foreign-owned and domestically owned members and reflects the [[Capital Movement Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital movement rules]] and [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|ngoại hối (foreign exchange)]] management framework of the TTTC. [1]

### Commodities Exchange

Under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]], the management authority (*Cơ quan điều hành*) at the city level is responsible for approving the list of contracts from **foreign commodities exchanges** (*Sở giao dịch hàng hóa nước ngoài*) that are permitted for linked trading (*liên thông giao dịch*) within the TTTC. [4] The decree does not specify ownership thresholds for participation in the commodities exchange but does require compliance with import/export and customs supervision rules. [4]

## Definitions

- **Nhà đầu tư nước ngoài sở hữu 100% vốn điều lệ** — A member entity whose entire charter capital is owned by foreign investors; triggers broader cross-border lending rights under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. [1]
- **Ngân hàng thương mại 100% vốn trong nước** — A commercial bank whose entire capital is domestically owned; subject to more restrictive cross-border lending conditions under Article 89. [1]
- **Ngân hàng thương mại 100% vốn nước ngoài** — A 100% foreign-owned commercial bank; permitted to provide advisory and certain cross-border banking services within the TTTC. [6]
- **Chi nhánh ngân hàng nước ngoài** — A branch of a foreign bank operating as a member of the TTTC; eligible for similar treatment as 100% foreign-owned banks in certain contexts. [6]
- **Tỷ lệ sở hữu** — Ownership ratio; must be disclosed in licensing applications for insurance entities involving foreign capital. [3]
- **Cơ quan điều hành tại thành phố** — The city-level management authority responsible for issuing operating licences to bank members and approving certain foreign-linked trading arrangements. [4][6]
