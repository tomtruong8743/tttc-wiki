# Follow Up Questions for Investment Banks Entering TTTC

## Summary

This page addresses follow-up questions that investment banks may raise when evaluating entry into the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]]. The TTTC is governed by a suite of foundational decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office, covering establishment, [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial and tax policies]], land and environment, labor and social security, immigration, and [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|international arbitration]]. Investment banks conducting due diligence will typically have questions spanning all of these regulatory areas, from [[Licenses And Approvals For Financial Institutions In Tttc|licensing requirements]] to [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange rules]], [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|employment conditions]], and [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|dispute resolution mechanisms]].

## Key Points

- The TTTC is established and governed by a set of government decrees issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Government Office of Vietnam), forming the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal framework]] that investment banks must understand before entering. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] covers the establishment of the TTTC, making it the foundational document for any investment bank assessing the centre's legal basis and [[Main Goals Of The Vietnam International Financial Centre|core mandate]]. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] addresses financial policies within the TTTC, which is directly relevant to questions about [[Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|tax incentives]], [[Capital Movement Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital movement]], and permissible [[Financial Services In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|financial services]]. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]] covers labor, employment, and social security within the TTTC, relevant to investment banks asking about [[Attracting International Financial Talent To Tttc|hiring international talent]] and [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|employment obligations]]. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]] governs land and environmental policies, which is pertinent to investment banks with questions about [[Real Estate And Office Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|office and real estate arrangements]] within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] sets out immigration and entry/exit policies, addressing common questions from investment banks about [[Immigration And Residency Policies For Tttc Workers|visas and residency]] for foreign staff and executives. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] establishes the international arbitration centre within the TTTC, covering [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution mechanisms]] that investment banks frequently ask about when assessing legal risk. [4]
- All six decrees are issued by the same authority — VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ — indicating a coordinated, top-level government commitment to the TTTC regulatory framework. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- **[[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]]** — Trung tâm Tài chính Quốc tế, the Vietnam International Financial Centre; the special financial zone established by the Vietnamese government to attract global financial institutions. [5]
- **[[Legal Framework Of Tttc|VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ]]** — The Government Office of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all foundational TTTC decrees. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- **[[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]]** — The decree establishing the TTTC, setting out its legal basis, purpose, and structure. [5]
- **[[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]]** — The decree governing financial policies applicable within the TTTC, including tax and capital rules. [1]
- **[[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]]** — The decree addressing labor, employment, and social security conditions within the TTTC. [6]
- **[[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]]** — The decree covering land use and environmental regulations within the TTTC. [3]
- **[[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]]** — The decree setting immigration and entry/exit policies for individuals working within or visiting the TTTC. [2]
- **[[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]]** — The decree establishing the international arbitration centre within the TTTC, providing a framework for [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|dispute resolution]]. [4]
