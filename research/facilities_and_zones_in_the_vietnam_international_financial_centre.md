# Facilities and Zones in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary
The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) is established and governed through a series of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). These decrees collectively define the legal, financial, land, labour, immigration, and arbitration frameworks that structure the zones and facilities operating within the TTTC. Key governing instruments include [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], each addressing a distinct operational dimension of the centre.

## Key Points
- The TTTC is established under a dedicated decree issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [1][5]
- Financial policies governing operations within the TTTC are set out in a separate dedicated decree. [1]
- Land and environmental regulations specific to the TTTC are governed by their own decree, indicating that the centre operates under distinct land-use rules. [3]
- Labour, employment, and social security arrangements within the TTTC are covered by a dedicated [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]]. [6]
- Immigration and entry/exit policies for workers and participants in the TTTC are governed by a dedicated [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], suggesting the centre has a distinct [[Immigration And Residency Policies For Tttc Workers|immigration zone or framework]]. [2]
- An international arbitration centre forms part of the TTTC's institutional facilities, governed by [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]]. [4]
- All six decrees were issued by the same issuing authority — the Vietnamese Government Office — indicating centralised governance of the TTTC's facilities and zones. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] — The foundational decree establishing the Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC), issued by the Vietnamese Government Office. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] — The decree governing financial policies within the TTTC, issued by the Vietnamese Government Office. [1]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] — The decree covering land use and environmental regulations applicable within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] — The decree governing labour, employment, and social security arrangements within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] — The decree governing immigration and entry/exit policies for individuals participating in the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] — The decree establishing and governing the international arbitration centre as a facility within the TTTC. [4]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — The Vietnamese Government Office; the issuing authority for all foundational TTTC decrees. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
