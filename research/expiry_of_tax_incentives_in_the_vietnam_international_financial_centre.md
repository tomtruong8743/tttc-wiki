# Expiry of Tax Incentives in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The expiry of [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]] within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] framework is governed by the financial policy regulations established for the centre. These incentives, including [[Corporate Income Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Personal Income Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], are subject to specific conditions and time-bound provisions set out under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. Understanding when and how these incentives expire is essential for entities operating within the centre, including [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], [[Asset Managers In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], and other licensed participants.

## Key Points

- The financial policies governing the Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC), including provisions related to the expiry of [[Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], are contained in [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [1]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal framework within which all incentive schemes, including their duration and expiry, operate. [5]
- Related policy instruments, such as [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], were issued by the same authority, indicating a coordinated legislative approach to the centre's incentive regime. [2][3][4][6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] includes template licensing documentation for insurance enterprises, reinsurance enterprises, and insurance brokerage enterprises operating within the TTTC, indicating that ongoing eligibility for tax incentives is tied to holding a valid licence issued by the competent authority. [3]
- Applicants for establishment licences within the TTTC — including those seeking to benefit from tax incentive regimes — must submit a five-year business plan (phương án kinh doanh 05 năm đầu) as part of their licensing documentation. This suggests that incentive eligibility assessments are linked to ongoing business plan compliance. [4]
- Entities operating under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] — including 100% foreign-owned commercial banks and foreign bank branches — are subject to the scope of activities defined in their licences issued by the city-level governing authority (Cơ quan điều hành tại thành phố), which may affect their eligibility for, and the duration of, applicable tax incentives. [2]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange]] similarly requires that entities operating the commodities exchange (Sở giao dịch hàng hóa) within the TTTC comply with all relevant legal instruments, including those governing the financial centre, which would encompass tax incentive conditions and their expiry. [6]
- The [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] framework under [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] provides for dispute resolution in matters relating to investment and business activities at the TTTC, which may be relevant where disputes arise concerning the application, continuation, or expiry of tax incentives. [5]

## Definitions

- **[[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]]** — Preferential tax treatments granted to qualifying entities operating within the TTTC, subject to conditions and time limits as defined in the relevant decrees. [1]
- **[[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]** — The primary decree issued by the Office of the Government governing financial policies, including tax incentive provisions and licensing templates, applicable within the Vietnam International Financial Centre. [1][3]
- **[[Conditions For Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]** — The qualifying criteria that entities must meet to be eligible for, and to continue receiving, tax incentives within the TTTC, including holding valid licences and complying with approved business plans. [1][4]
- **[[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]]** — The foundational decree establishing the Vietnam International Financial Centre, providing the overarching legal basis for all associated policy measures including incentive schemes. [5]
- **Phương án kinh doanh 05 năm đầu** — The five-year initial business plan required as part of the licensing application process for financial institutions within the TTTC, compliance with which may be linked to ongoing incentive eligibility. [4]
- **Cơ quan điều hành tại thành phố** — The city-level governing authority responsible for issuing operating licences to member institutions within the TTTC; the scope of the licence issued by this authority may determine the extent and duration of applicable tax incentives. [2]
