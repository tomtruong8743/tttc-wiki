# Expiry of Tax Incentives in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The expiry of [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]] within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] framework is governed by the financial policy regulations established for the centre. These incentives, including [[Corporate Income Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Personal Income Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], are subject to specific conditions and time-bound provisions set out under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. Understanding when and how these incentives expire is essential for entities operating within the centre.

## Key Points

- The financial policies governing the Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC), including provisions related to the expiry of [[Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], are contained in [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [1]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal framework within which all incentive schemes, including their duration and expiry, operate. [5]
- Related policy instruments, such as [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], were issued by the same authority, indicating a coordinated legislative approach to the centre's incentive regime. [2][3][4][6]

## Definitions

- **[[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]]** — Preferential tax treatments granted to qualifying entities operating within the TTTC, subject to conditions and time limits as defined in the relevant decrees. [1]
- **[[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]** — The primary decree issued by the Office of the Government governing financial policies, including tax incentive provisions, applicable within the Vietnam International Financial Centre. [1]
- **[[Conditions For Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]** — The qualifying criteria that entities must meet to be eligible for, and to continue receiving, tax incentives within the TTTC. [1]
- **[[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]]** — The foundational decree establishing the Vietnam International Financial Centre, providing the overarching legal basis for all associated policy measures. [5]
