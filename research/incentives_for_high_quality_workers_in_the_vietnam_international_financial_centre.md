# Incentives for High Quality Workers in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) offers a range of incentives designed to attract and retain high-quality workers, including both domestic and foreign experts. These incentives are established through a series of government decrees issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), most notably [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], which governs labour, employment, and social security arrangements within the TTTC. Additional related policies are found across [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and other [[Financial Center Decrees]] that collectively shape the working environment for high-quality professionals operating within the centre.

## Key Points

- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] was issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) and covers labour, employment, and social security policy within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] was issued by the Office of the Government and addresses entry and exit policies relevant to foreign workers and experts operating in the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] was issued by the Office of the Government and includes financial policies that may encompass [[Tax Benefits For Workers And Experts In Tttc|tax benefits for workers and experts]]. [1]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] was issued by the Office of the Government as the foundational legal instrument establishing the TTTC framework. [5]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] was issued by the Office of the Government and covers land and environment policy within the TTTC, which may affect working and living conditions for high-quality workers. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] was issued by the Office of the Government and establishes the international arbitration centre associated with the TTTC. [4]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Nghị định 325]] — The decree governing labour, employment, and social security (lao động, việc làm và an sinh xã hội) within the TTTC, issued by the Office of the Government. [6]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Nghị định 327]] — The decree governing immigration (xuất nhập cảnh) policy within the TTTC, relevant to the entry and residence of foreign high-quality workers. [2]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam, the issuing authority for all decrees establishing and governing the TTTC. [1]
- [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|TTTC]] — Abbreviation for Trung Tâm Tài Chính (International Financial Centre), the special financial zone established by the Vietnamese Government. [5]
