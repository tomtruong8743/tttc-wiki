# Tax vs Regulatory Incentives in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The [[Vietnam International Financial Centre For Investment Banking Interns]] (TTTC) is governed by a suite of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office, each addressing distinct policy domains. Among the most strategically significant distinctions in the TTTC framework is the difference between [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]] — such as [[Corporate Income Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Personal Income Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] — and regulatory incentives, which encompass special rules on licensing, land use, labor, immigration, arbitration, and foreign exchange. Together, these two categories form the core attraction mechanism of the TTTC, as codified across multiple decrees including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]].

## Key Points

- The TTTC's incentive framework is established through a series of decrees all issued by the VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (Government Office of Vietnam), indicating a centralised and coordinated policy approach. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis for the TTTC, upon which both tax and regulatory incentives are built. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] governs [[Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and other financial policies, representing the primary instrument for tax-based incentives within the TTTC. [1]
- Regulatory incentives related to land and the environment are addressed separately under [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], distinguishing them from purely fiscal measures. [3]
- Labor and social security incentives — a form of regulatory incentive — are governed by [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], covering employment conditions distinct from tax benefits. [6]
- Immigration and visa policy incentives, another regulatory (non-tax) mechanism to attract [[Attracting International Financial Talent To Tttc]], are handled under [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]]. [2]
- The establishment of a dedicated [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] framework under [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] represents a regulatory incentive focused on [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] rather than tax relief. [4]
- The separation of tax policy (Decree 324) from regulatory policies (Decrees 325–328) suggests that the TTTC deliberately distinguishes fiscal and non-fiscal incentives as complementary but structurally separate tools. (uncertain) [1][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]] — Fiscal benefits such as reduced or zero tax rates granted to qualifying entities operating within the TTTC, primarily governed by [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]. [1]
- **Regulatory incentives** — Non-fiscal benefits provided through special rules and frameworks governing labor, land, immigration, arbitration, and financial operations within the TTTC, as distinct from tax measures. [2][3][4][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] — The foundational decree establishing the legal existence and structure of the Vietnam International Financial Centre. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] — The decree governing financial and tax policies applicable within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] — The decree setting out labor, employment, and social security rules within the TTTC, constituting a form of regulatory incentive. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] — The decree governing land use and environmental regulations within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] — The decree governing immigration and entry/exit policies for workers and experts in the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] — The decree establishing the international arbitration centre within the TTTC as a regulatory dispute resolution mechanism. [4]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Government Office of Vietnam, the issuing authority for all TTTC-related decrees referenced in this page. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
