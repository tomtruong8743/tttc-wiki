# Securities Activities in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Securities activities within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]] are governed by a suite of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office ([[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]], and related instruments). These decrees collectively establish the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] for [[Capital Market Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital market operations]], financial services, and investment activities conducted within the TTTC, positioning it as a specialised hub for domestic and international securities business.

## Key Points

- The foundational legal documents governing activities in the TTTC, including securities activities, were issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Government Office of Vietnam). [1][5]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the basis for establishing the TTTC and its operational framework, which underpins all securities and capital market activities conducted within it. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] specifically addresses [[Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|financial policies]] within the TTTC, which are directly relevant to securities activities and market participants. [1]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] governs the licensing and establishment of banks and foreign exchange management within the TTTC. It sets out permitted securities-related activities for member commercial banks, including: [6]
  - Buying and selling corporate bonds issued in Vietnamese territory (excluding bonds issued by Vietnamese credit institutions). [6]
  - Buying and selling Vietnamese Government bonds, government-guaranteed bonds, and local government bonds — including purchases on the primary market. [6]
  - Debt purchase ([[Mergers And Acquisitions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|mua nợ]]). [6]
  - Letter of credit operations and related business activities. [6]
  - Discounting ([[Debt Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|chiết khấu]]). [6]
  - Bank guarantees. [6]
  - Factoring and related services. [6]
- Foreign-owned banks (100% foreign capital) and branches of foreign banks that are TTTC members may conduct the above activities with organisations on Vietnamese territory that are not Vietnamese credit institutions. [6]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] governs the establishment and operation of a [[Financial Products And Instruments In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|commodities exchange]] (Sở giao dịch hàng hóa) within the TTTC — a distinct but related venue for structured financial trading, including commodity futures contracts with pre-agreed settlement terms. [1]
- Under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]], eligibility for [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|export and import duty incentives]] for commodities traded on the exchange is conditioned on documentary proof and compliance with financial policies in [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]. [2]
- Goods subject to specialised inspection (food safety, health, environment, agriculture, science and technology, national defence, and security) must comply with the regulations and oversight of the relevant specialised management authority. [2]
- The [[Regulatory Bodies Supervising The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Ministry of Industry and Trade (Bộ Công Thương)]] is responsible for coordinating with the supervisory authority to inspect the operations of the commodities exchange and commodity trading activities. [2]
- The [[Regulatory Bodies Supervising The Vietnam International Financial Centre|operating authority in each city]] (Cơ quan điều hành tại các thành phố) is responsible for: reviewing and approving applications for establishment of a commodities exchange by TTTC members; granting, reissuing, suspending, and revoking establishment licences; approving the list of Vietnam-produced commodities permitted for trading; and approving the list of foreign commodities exchange contracts eligible for linked trading. [2]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] imposes [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|professional conduct and governance standards]] on senior management of member commercial banks. Persons responsible for regulatory violations — including violations relating to share management, corporate bond purchases, credit extension, and prudential ratios — may be assessed as lacking professional ethics and disqualified from holding office. [4]
- Senior management of member banks (Deputy General Director, Chief Accountant, and equivalent positions) must meet qualification requirements including a university degree in finance, banking, economics, business administration, law, accounting, or auditing, or a minimum of three years of direct experience in the relevant field. [3]
- Additional decrees cover complementary areas that support securities activities, including [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] (land and environment), [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] (labour and social security), [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] (immigration for foreign participants), and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] (dispute resolution). [2][3][4][6]
- All decrees were issued by the same authority — VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ — indicating a coordinated, whole-of-government approach to regulating the TTTC and its securities activities. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Permitted Securities and Bond Activities for Foreign Banks

Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], 100% foreign-owned banks and branches of foreign banks that are TTTC members may conduct the following activities with non-credit-institution organisations on Vietnamese territory: [6]

| Activity | Notes |
|---|---|
| Lending (cho vay) | General lending activity |
| Discounting (chiết khấu) | [[Debt Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Debt instrument discounting]] |
| Bank guarantees (bảo lãnh ngân hàng) | Standard guarantee products |
| Factoring (bao thanh toán) | Including related services |
| Letter of credit (thư tín dụng) | And related business |
| Corporate bond trading | Bonds issued in Vietnam, excluding Vietnamese credit institution bonds |
| Vietnamese Government bond trading | Including government-guaranteed bonds and local government bonds; primary market purchases permitted |
| Debt purchase (mua nợ) | [[Cross Border Ma In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Debt acquisition]] |

## Commodities Exchange (Sở Giao Dịch Hàng Hóa)

[[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] establishes the legal framework for a [[Financial Products And Instruments In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|commodities exchange]] operating within the TTTC. Key features include: [1][2]

- Contracts traded on the commodities exchange include those with pre-agreed settlement terms (hợp đồng có thoả thuận trước), consistent with standardised futures-style instruments. [1]
- The exchange may trade both Vietnam-produced commodities (subject to approval by the city operating authority) and foreign commodities exchange contracts (subject to linked trading approval). [2]
- [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|Export and import duty incentives]] apply to eligible commodities, subject to documentary proof and compliance with [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] on financial policies. [2]
- The [[Regulatory Bodies Supervising The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Ministry of Industry and Trade]] and city-level operating authorities share oversight responsibilities. [2]
- [[Licenses And Approvals For Financial Institutions In Tttc|Establishment licences]] are granted, suspended, or revoked by the city operating authority. [2]

## Governance and Compliance Standards for Securities Market Participants

[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] establishes [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|governance and fitness standards]] for senior management of member banks operating in the TTTC: [3][4]

- **Qualification requirements:** Deputy General Directors, Chief Accountants, and equivalent roles must hold a university degree or higher in finance, banking, economics, business administration, law, accounting, or auditing — or a degree in another field combined with at least three years of direct experience in finance, banking, or the relevant specialty. [3]
- **Residency requirement:** Senior managers must reside in Vietnam during their term of office. [3]
- **Professional ethics disqualification:** Persons responsible — per inspection conclusions — for violations leading to administrative penalties against Vietnamese credit institutions or member banks in the monetary and banking sector are assessed as lacking professional ethics.
