# Securities Activities in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Securities activities within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]] are governed by a suite of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office ([[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], and related instruments). These decrees collectively establish the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] for capital market operations, financial services, and investment activities conducted within the TTTC, positioning it as a specialised hub for domestic and international securities business.

## Key Points

- The foundational legal documents governing activities in the TTTC, including securities activities, were issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Government Office of Vietnam). [1][5]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the basis for establishing the TTTC and its operational framework, which underpins all securities and capital market activities conducted within it. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] specifically addresses financial policies within the TTTC, which are directly relevant to securities activities and market participants. [1]
- Additional decrees cover complementary areas that support securities activities, including [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] (land and environment), [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] (labour and social security), [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] (immigration for foreign participants), and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] (dispute resolution). [2][3][4][6]
- All decrees were issued by the same authority — VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ — indicating a coordinated, whole-of-government approach to regulating the TTTC and its securities activities. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|TTTC]] — the Vietnam International Financial Centre, established under Decree 323, serving as the primary venue for regulated financial and securities activities in Vietnam. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Financial policies]] — the set of rules and incentives governing financial operations, including securities activities, within the TTTC as defined under Decree 324. [1]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — the Government Office of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all foundational decrees establishing and regulating the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
