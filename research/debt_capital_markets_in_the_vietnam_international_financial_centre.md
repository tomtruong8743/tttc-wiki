# Debt Capital Markets in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Debt Capital Markets (DCM) within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]] refers to the frameworks, instruments, and activities related to the issuance and trading of debt securities within the centre. The TTTC is governed by a suite of foundational decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office ([[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], and related instruments), which collectively establish the legal, financial, and operational environment in which debt capital market activities may take place. The specific contours of DCM activity within the TTTC are shaped by policies covering [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]], [[Currency And Capital Controls]], [[Foreign Investment Rules In Tttc]], and [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]].

---

## Key Points

- The TTTC is established and governed through a series of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ), which form the foundational legal framework for all financial market activities, including debt capital markets. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the overarching establishment framework within which debt capital market participants and instruments would operate. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] is the primary decree governing financial policies within the TTTC, and is expected to be the most directly relevant instrument for debt capital market regulation. [1]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] and [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] address ancillary operational conditions within the TTTC that may affect the operational environment for debt market participants. [3][6]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] governs entry and exit policies relevant to foreign professionals and investors participating in TTTC debt markets. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes dispute resolution infrastructure that would support the enforceability of debt instruments and contracts within the TTTC. [4]
- All six decrees are issued by the same issuing authority — the Vietnamese Government Office — indicating a coordinated, government-level approach to establishing the TTTC's financial ecosystem. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
