# Fund Management in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Fund management within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]] is governed by a suite of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office ([[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], and related instruments). These decrees collectively establish the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal and regulatory framework]] under which fund management activities, [[Financial Services In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|financial services]], and related operations are conducted within the TTTC. Additional decrees, including [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange]], further shape the operating environment for fund managers and related financial institutions within the centre.

## Key Points

- The Vietnam International Financial Centre is established and governed through a series of decrees issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Government Office of Vietnam) [1][5].
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out the financial policy framework applicable within the TTTC, which underpins fund management activities; entities operating under this decree are required to submit formal applications with supporting documentation signed by their legal representative [1].
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis for the establishment of the TTTC, within which fund management entities may operate [5].
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] governs land and environmental matters within the TTTC, relevant to the physical operations of fund management firms [3].
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] addresses labour, employment, and [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|social security policy]] within the TTTC, applicable to staff of fund management firms [6].
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] covers entry and exit (immigration) policies within the TTTC, relevant to foreign fund managers and experts working in the centre [2].
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the international arbitration framework within the TTTC, providing [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution]] options for fund management participants [4].
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] (Decree 329) sets out licensing, establishment, and [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]] rules for member banks operating within the TTTC. Key provisions relevant to fund managers and financial institutions include:
  - Senior management personnel (Deputy General Directors, Chief Accountants, and equivalent positions) at member banks must hold a university degree or higher in finance, banking, economics, business administration, law, accounting, auditing, or a relevant specialist field — or, alternatively, a university degree in another field combined with at least three years of direct working experience in finance, banking, or the relevant specialist area [3].
  - Member banks applying [[International Financial Reporting Standards|international accounting standards]] are exempt from complying with Vietnamese domestic regulations on the ratio for purchasing, holding, and investing in Vietnamese Government bonds or Government-guaranteed bonds [4].
  - Member banks are not required to comply with the maximum foreign currency position relative to equity capital as prescribed under Vietnamese law [4].
  - [[Risk Management Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Prudential ratios]] including the Leverage Ratio (LEV), [[Liquidity Coverage Ratio]] (LCR), and Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR) apply to member banks; the [[Supervision Mechanisms In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|supervisory authority]] may impose stricter ratios on individual member banks based on risk assessment [4].
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] (Decree 330) governs the establishment and operation of the [[Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Commodities Exchange]] within the TTTC. Key provisions include:
  - Conditions for enjoying preferential treatment (tax and customs) require documentary proof that goods qualify under the relevant article of Decree 330, with import/export tax incentives and customs procedure benefits implemented in accordance with [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|the financial policy decree]] applicable within the TTTC [2].
  - Goods subject to specialist inspection (food safety, health, environment, agriculture, science and technology, national defence, and security) must comply with the regulations and procedures of the relevant specialist regulatory authority [2].
  - The Ministry of Industry and Trade (Bộ Công Thương) is responsible for coordinating with the supervisory authority to inspect the operations of the Commodities Exchange and for implementing regulations on incentives and handling violations related to import/export and commodity trading activities [2].
  - The Operating Authority in each city is responsible for: reviewing applications for establishment of a Commodities Exchange by TTTC members; issuing, re-issuing, suspending, and revoking establishment licences; approving the list of domestically produced goods permitted for trading; approving the list of foreign Commodities Exchange contracts permitted for linked trading; and considering decisions on additions, amendments, suspension, or termination of trading activities [2].

## Definitions

- [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|TTTC]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre; the special financial zone established under Vietnamese government decree within which fund management and other financial activities are conducted [5].
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] — The foundational government decree establishing the legal basis and structure of the TTTC [5].
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] — The government decree setting out financial policies applicable within the TTTC, including those relevant to fund management [1].
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Government Office of Vietnam; the issuing authority for the decrees governing the TTTC [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]] — The government decree governing labour, employment, and social security within the TTTC [6].
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]] — The government decree governing [[Land Use Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|land use]] and [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|environmental regulations]] within the TTTC [3].
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] — The government decree covering [[Immigration And Residency Policies For Tttc Workers|immigration]] and entry/exit policies within the TTTC [2].
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] — The government decree establishing the [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|international arbitration centre]] within the TTTC [4].
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] — The government decree governing licensing, establishment, and foreign exchange management for banks and financial institutions within the TTTC [3][4].
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Decree 330]] — The government decree governing the establishment and operation of the Commodities Exchange within the TTTC [2][6].
- **Bộ Công Thương** — The Ministry of Industry and Trade; responsible for oversight and coordination of Commodities Exchange operations within the TTTC [2].
- **LCR (Liquidity Coverage Ratio)** — A [[Risk Management Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|prudential ratio]] measuring a bank's ability to meet short-term liquidity obligations; applicable to member banks within the TTTC [4].
- **NSFR (Net Stable Funding Ratio)** — A prudential ratio measuring the stability of a bank's funding over a longer time horizon; applicable to member banks within the TTTC [4].
- **LEV (Leverage Ratio)** — A prudential ratio measuring a bank's capital relative to its total exposures; applicable to member banks within the TTTC [4].
