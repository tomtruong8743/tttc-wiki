# Foreign Currency Transactions in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Foreign currency transactions within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]] are governed by a suite of government decrees issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. These regulations form part of the broader [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal framework]] established to govern financial activities within the TTTC, including rules relating to [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange]], [[Banking Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|banking activities]], and the movement of capital. The relevant policies are set out across multiple decrees, including [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324 on financial policies]] and [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329 on banking and foreign exchange]], which together shape how foreign currency operations function within the centre.

## Key Points

- The source documents related to foreign currency transactions in the TTTC were issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [1]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324 on financial policies within the TTTC]] is one of the key instruments governing financial operations, which may include foreign currency-related matters. [1]
