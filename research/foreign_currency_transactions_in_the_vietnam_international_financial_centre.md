# Foreign Currency Transactions in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Foreign currency transactions within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]] are governed by a suite of government decrees issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. These regulations form part of the broader [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal framework]] established to govern financial activities within the TTTC, including rules relating to [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange]], [[Banking Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|banking activities]], and the movement of capital. The relevant policies are set out across multiple decrees, including [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324 on financial policies]] and [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329 on banking and foreign exchange]], which together shape how foreign currency operations function within the centre. [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] contains a dedicated section (Section 4) on foreign exchange management rules for borrowing and lending activities conducted by TTTC members, providing specific rules on cross-border loans denominated in foreign currency.

## Key Points

- The source documents related to foreign currency transactions in the TTTC were issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [1]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324 on financial policies within the TTTC]] is one of the key instruments governing financial operations, which may include foreign currency-related matters. [1]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323 on the establishment of the TTTC]] provides the foundational legal basis upon which all subsequent financial and currency regulations operate, including mechanisms to handle complaints and disputes relating to financial centre operations. [2]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326 on land and environment]] and [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325 on labour and social security]] are among the broader regulatory instruments that accompany the financial decrees within the TTTC framework. [3][6]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328 on the International Arbitration Centre]] provides a [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution mechanism]] that may be relevant to resolving disagreements arising from foreign currency transactions. [4]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327 on immigration policy]] governs the entry and exit of foreign personnel, which is relevant to the participation of [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign financial institutions]] in currency operations within the TTTC. [2]
- Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]], TTTC members are permitted to borrow foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside the territory of Vietnam, subject to information declaration and reporting obligations. [1]
- Members wholly owned by foreign investors (100% foreign-owned) are permitted to lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnam, subject to information declaration and [[Reporting Obligations For Financial Institutions In Tttc|reporting obligations]]. [1]
- Members that are not 100% foreign-owned must comply with additional conditions set out in Article 89 of [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] before lending abroad. [1]
- Domestic commercial banks (100% domestically-owned) that are TTTC members may lend in foreign currency to: (a) organisations established and operating outside Vietnam in which Vietnamese enterprises or non-member Vietnamese entities hold more than 50% of shares or contributed capital; and (b) organisations outside Vietnam that are more than 50% owned by such entities. [1]
- Non-bank member enterprises may lend in foreign currency to organisations established and operating outside Vietnam in which the lending member or its parent company holds more than 50% of shares or contributed capital. [1]
- [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Fully foreign-owned banks and foreign bank branches]] that are TTTC members may conduct a range of activities with non-credit organisations on Vietnamese territory, including lending, discounting, bank guarantees, factoring, letters of credit, and the purchase and sale of corporate bonds and Vietnamese government bonds (including primary market purchases). [5]
- Members must prepare and submit financial statements — which may be prepared under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|international accounting standards or generally accepted accounting principles]] — to the Management Authority, the Supervisory Authority, and other competent Vietnamese authorities. [6]
- The governance structure under [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] grants the Management Authority powers to handle administrative violations and penalties, manage state budget allocations, and oversee assets and investment projects within the TTTC. [2]

## Definitions

- [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|**Foreign currency transaction**]] — an exchange, payment, or financial operation conducted in a currency other than the Vietnamese Dong within the TTTC framework. (uncertain — specific definition not provided in source documents)
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|**Decree 324**]] — a government decree issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam governing financial policies within the TTTC, including accounting standards and financial reporting requirements. [6]
- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|**TTTC**]] — the Vietnam International Financial Centre, established under [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]], subject to a range of specialised regulatory instruments issued by the Vietnamese government. [2]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|**Decree 329**]] — a government decree specifically addressing banking licensing, establishment, and [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|foreign exchange management]] (quản lý ngoại hối) within the TTTC, including detailed rules on cross-border borrowing and lending in foreign currency by TTTC members. [1][5]
- **Section 4 of Decree 329** — the dedicated section on foreign exchange management rules (Quy định quản lý ngoại hối) applicable to borrowing and lending activities of TTTC members, covering Articles 87 to 89 and beyond. [1]
- **100% foreign-owned member** (thành viên do nhà đầu tư nước ngoài sở hữu 100% vốn điều lệ) — a TTTC member enterprise wholly owned by foreign investors, which is subject to a more permissive regime for cross-border foreign currency lending under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]]. [1]
- **Domestic commercial bank member** (ngân hàng thương mại 100% vốn trong nước) — a TTTC member bank with entirely domestic ownership, which may lend in foreign currency abroad only to entities in which Vietnamese enterprises hold a majority stake. [1]
