# Equity Capital Markets in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Equity Capital Markets (ECM) within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) refers to the frameworks, activities, and opportunities relating to the issuance and trading of equity instruments within the Centre's jurisdiction. The TTTC is governed by a suite of foundational decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office ([[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange]]), which together establish the legal and operational environment in which [[Capital Raising In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital markets activity]], including equity capital markets, may take place.

## Key Points

- The foundational decrees governing the TTTC, including those related to [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]] and [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal frameworks]], were issued by the VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (Office of the Government of Vietnam). [1][2][3][4][5][6][7][8]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the overarching establishment framework within which equity capital markets activities would be situated. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] is the primary decree addressing financial policies within the TTTC, which would be expected to cover matters relevant to [[Capital Raising In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital raising]] and equity issuance. It also sets out priority payment rules applicable to insurance enterprises and other financial members upon dissolution, including wage obligations, tax debts, and other liabilities. [1][7]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] governs banking licensing, establishment, and [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]] within the TTTC. It contains detailed rules on [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|ngoại hối]] (foreign exchange) management applicable to member borrowing and lending activities, including cross-border lending and borrowing in foreign currency. [2][3][4][6]
- Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], members of the TTTC may borrow foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside Vietnam's territory, subject to information disclosure and reporting requirements. [6]
- Members wholly owned by foreign investors may lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnam, while members not wholly foreign-owned must satisfy additional conditions for cross-border lending set out in that decree. [6]
- Domestic commercial banks that are 100% domestically funded may lend foreign currency to foreign-established organisations in which Vietnamese enterprises or members hold more than 50% of equity or contributed capital. [6]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] sets out prudential safety ratios applicable to member banks, including capital adequacy (CAR), leverage ratio (LEV), liquidity coverage ratio (LCR), and net stable funding ratio (NSFR). Member banks applying international accounting standards are exempt from requirements to hold Vietnamese Government bonds and are not subject to maximum foreign currency position limits under Vietnamese domestic law. [4]
- Foreign-owned banks and branches of foreign banks that are TTTC members may buy and sell corporate bonds issued on Vietnamese territory (excluding bonds issued by Vietnamese credit institutions), as well as Vietnamese Government bonds, government-guaranteed bonds, and local government bonds, including purchases on the primary market. [3]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] sets professional ethics standards for key personnel of member banks, including disqualification of persons responsible for violations relating to licences, governance, shareholding, equity participation, credit extension, corporate bond purchases, or prudential safety ratios. [8]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] establishes the conditions and governance framework for commodities exchanges within the TTTC; eligibility for tax and customs incentives under this decree is linked to compliance with [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policy provisions]] and relevant sector-specific inspection requirements. [9]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration mechanism, relevant to [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution]] for equity market participants and investors. [5]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] addresses entry and exit policies, relevant to the participation of [[Foreign Investment Rules In Tttc|foreign investors]] and professionals in equity capital markets within the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] and [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] form part of the broader regulatory environment supporting operations within the TTTC. [1][2]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|TTTC]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre (Trung tâm Tài chính), established by government decree as a special financial zone in Vietnam. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Financial Policies Decree]] — The decree (Decree 324) issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam setting out financial policy rules applicable within the TTTC, including priority payment and dissolution rules for financial enterprises. [1][7]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|International Arbitration Centre]] — A dispute resolution body established under Decree 328 within the TTTC framework to handle international commercial and financial disputes. [5]
- [[Capital Raising In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Equity Capital Markets (ECM)]] — The segment of capital markets concerned with the issuance and trading of equity instruments (uncertain), operating within the regulatory environment defined by the TTTC decrees. [1][5]
- [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|Ngoại hối]] — Foreign exchange; regulated under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] with specific rules governing member borrowing and lending in foreign currency within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Prudential Safety Ratios]] — Regulatory capital and liquidity ratios (including CAR, LEV, LCR, NSFR) required of member banks under Decree 329; member banks applying international accounting standards receive certain exemptions from domestic Vietnamese requirements. [4]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Commodities Exchange (Sở giao dịch hàng hóa)]] — An exchange established within the TTTC under Decree 330, subject to licensing and oversight by the city-level executive body (Cơ quan điều hành), with eligibility for tax and customs incentives linked to compliance with the financial policy decree. [9]
