# Equity Capital Markets in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Equity Capital Markets (ECM) within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) refers to the frameworks, activities, and opportunities relating to the issuance and trading of equity instruments within the Centre's jurisdiction. The TTTC is governed by a suite of foundational decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office ([[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], and [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]]), which together establish the legal and operational environment in which [[Capital Raising In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital markets activity]], including equity capital markets, may take place.

## Key Points

- The foundational decrees governing the TTTC, including those related to [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]] and [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal frameworks]], were issued by the VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (Office of the Government of Vietnam). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the overarching establishment framework within which equity capital markets activities would be situated. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] is the primary decree addressing financial policies within the TTTC, which would be expected to cover matters relevant to [[Capital Raising In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital raising]] and equity issuance. [1]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration mechanism, relevant to [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution]] for equity market participants and investors. [4]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] addresses entry and exit policies, relevant to the participation of [[Foreign Investment Rules In Tttc|foreign investors]] and professionals in equity capital markets within the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] and [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] form part of the broader regulatory environment supporting operations within the TTTC. [3][6]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|TTTC]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre (Trung tâm Tài chính), established by government decree as a special financial zone in Vietnam. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Financial Policies Decree]] — The decree (Decree 324) issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam setting out financial policy rules applicable within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|International Arbitration Centre]] — A dispute resolution body established under Decree 328 within the TTTC framework to handle international commercial and financial disputes. [4]
- [[Capital Raising In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Equity Capital Markets (ECM)]] — The segment of capital markets concerned with the issuance and trading of equity instruments (uncertain), operating within the regulatory environment defined by the TTTC decrees. [1][5]
