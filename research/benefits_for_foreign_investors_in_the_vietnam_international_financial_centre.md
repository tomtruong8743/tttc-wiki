# Benefits for Foreign Investors in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) offers a range of benefits designed to attract [[Foreign Investment Rules In Tttc|foreign investors]], structured through a series of government decrees issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. These benefits span [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial and tax policies]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environmental arrangements]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration facilitation]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration access]], and [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security provisions]], all governed under the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal framework]] established by [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] and its associated regulations.

---

## Key Points

- The financial policies applicable within the TTTC are governed by [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]], issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam, which is expected to provide preferential financial conditions for participants and investors. [1]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] on immigration policy within the TTTC was issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam, suggesting that foreign investors and professionals may benefit from facilitated entry and residence arrangements. [2]
- Land and environmental policies within the TTTC are regulated by [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]], issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam, which may govern land access and usage rights for foreign investors operating in the centre. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] establishes an International Arbitration Centre within the TTTC, issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam, providing foreign investors with access to internationally recognised [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution mechanisms]]. [4]
- The overarching establishment of the TTTC is governed by [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]], issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam, forming the foundational [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal basis]] under which all investor benefits operate. [5]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]] on labour, employment, and social security within the TTTC was issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam, potentially offering foreign investors and their employees specific labour arrangements within the centre. [6]

---

## Definitions

- **[[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]]** — The Vietnam International Financial Centre; a specially designated financial zone established by the Vietnamese Government to attract domestic and foreign financial activity. [5]
- **[[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]]** — The government decree governing financial policies within the TTTC, issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. [1]
- **[[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]]** — The government decree governing immigration and entry/exit policies applicable within the TTTC. [2]
- **[[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]]** — The government decree regulating land use and environmental matters within the TTTC. [3]
- **[[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]]** — The government decree establishing the International Arbitration Centre within the TTTC, providing a formal [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution]] structure for investors. [4]
- **[[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]]** — The foundational government decree establishing the TTTC and its governing structure. [5]
- **[[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]]** — The government decree addressing labour, employment, and social security conditions within the TTTC. [6]
- **[[Financial Center Decrees|Office of the Government (Văn phòng Chính phủ)]]** — The issuing authority for all six decrees related to the TTTC referenced in these sources. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
