# Benefits for Foreign Investors in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) offers a range of benefits designed to attract [[Foreign Investment Rules In Tttc|foreign investors]], structured through a series of government decrees issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. These benefits span [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial and tax policies]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environmental arrangements]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration facilitation]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration access]], and [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security provisions]], all governed under the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal framework]] established by [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] and its associated regulations.

---

## Key Points

- The financial policies applicable within the TTTC are governed by [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]], issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam, which provides preferential financial conditions for participants and investors, including rules on insurance, lending, and foreign exchange. [1][3][4]
- Members (Thành viên) and their employees operating within the TTTC are permitted to purchase insurance from insurance enterprises established and operating within the TTTC, from insurance enterprises operating in the rest of Vietnam, and from foreign insurance enterprises overseas. [1]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] governs [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]] within the TTTC, including rules on cross-border borrowing and lending. Members are permitted to borrow foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to disclosure and reporting obligations. [3]
- Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]], members that are 100% foreign-investor-owned are permitted to lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to disclosure and reporting requirements. Members not wholly owned by foreign investors must comply with additional lending conditions set out in the decree. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] on immigration policy within the TTTC was issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam, suggesting that foreign investors and professionals may benefit from facilitated entry and residence arrangements. [2]
- Land and environmental policies within the TTTC are regulated by [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]], issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam, which may govern land access and usage rights for foreign investors operating in the centre. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] establishes an International Arbitration Centre within the TTTC, providing foreign investors with access to internationally recognised [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution mechanisms]] for investment and business disputes. The centre has jurisdiction to resolve disputes by agreement between parties related to investment and business activities within the TTTC, under rules and regulations issued by the centre itself. [5]
- The [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|International Arbitration Centre]] does not have jurisdiction over disputes concerning administrative decisions or acts of state management authorities, labour disputes, disputes related to personal rights of individuals, or matters already resolved by a competent state authority or court in Vietnam. [5]
- Courts in Vietnam may not refuse to hear applications to annul arbitral awards or decisions of arbitral tribunals, except in specific circumstances defined under the Law on Commercial Arbitration, including where a waiver agreement is void or was formed after a party had already filed an application to annul an award. [5]
- The overarching establishment of the TTTC is governed by [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]], issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam, forming the foundational [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal basis]] under which all investor benefits operate. The governing body of the TTTC is assigned additional operational responsibilities under the decree's operational charter. [6]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]] on labour, employment, and social security within the TTTC was issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam, potentially offering foreign investors and their employees specific labour arrangements within the centre. [2]
- In the event of dissolution of an insurance enterprise, reinsurance enterprise, or insurance brokerage enterprise operating within the TTTC, debt repayment follows a defined priority order: first, employee wages, severance allowances, social insurance, health insurance, unemployment insurance, and other entitlements under signed labour contracts; second, insurance claim payments and surrender values approved by the insurance enterprise; third, tax debts; and finally, other debts. [4]

## Definitions

- **[[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]]** — The Vietnam International Financial Centre; a specially designated financial zone established by the Vietnamese Government to attract domestic and foreign financial activity. [6]
- **[[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]]** — The government decree governing financial policies within the TTTC, including insurance, securities, and related financial sector rules. [1][4]
- **[[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]]** — The government decree governing immigration and entry/exit policies applicable within the TTTC. [2]
- **[[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]]** — The government decree regulating land use and environmental matters within the TTTC. [2]
- **[[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]]** — The government decree establishing the International Arbitration Centre within the TTTC, providing a formal [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution]] structure for investors, with defined jurisdiction over investment and business disputes. [5]
- **[[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]]** — The foundational government decree establishing the TTTC and its governing structure, including the operational responsibilities of the managing authority. [6]
- **[[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]]** — The government decree addressing labour, employment, and social security conditions within the TTTC. [2]
- **[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]]** — The government decree governing banking licensing, establishment, and [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|foreign exchange management]] (ngoại hối) within the TTTC, including rules on cross-border borrowing and lending by members. [3]
- **Thành viên (Member)** — A participant or member entity operating within the TTTC, subject to the rights, obligations, and regulations established under the TTTC decrees. [1][3]
- **[[Financial Center Decrees|Office of the Government (Văn phòng Chính phủ)]]** — The issuing authority for all decrees related to the TTTC referenced in these sources. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
