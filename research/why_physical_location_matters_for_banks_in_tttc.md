# Why Physical Location Matters for Banks in TTTC

## Summary

Within the [[Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC), a bank's physical location is a foundational legal and operational consideration. The [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] establishes the geographic and institutional boundaries of the centre, meaning that only entities physically situated within the designated zone are eligible to access the special legal, financial, and regulatory frameworks that apply there. This intersects with policies governed by [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], all of which confer rights, incentives, and protections that are geographically conditioned.

## Key Points

- The TTTC is established as a defined physical and legal zone; access to its special frameworks depends on an institution being located within that zone. [5]
- Financial policies applicable to banks — including [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]] and other benefits — are tied to the geographic boundaries of the TTTC as set out in [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]. [1]
- [[Land Use Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and environmental rules under [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] govern how banks can acquire, use, and occupy physical premises within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] under [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] apply specifically to workers employed at entities physically operating within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Immigration And Residency Policies For Tttc Workers]] under [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] grant special entry and residency rights to personnel of institutions physically based in the TTTC. [2]
- Access to the [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] and its [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] mechanisms is linked to institutions operating within the TTTC's geographic jurisdiction. [4]
- All six governing decrees were issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), indicating a unified regulatory framework anchored to the centre's physical perimeter. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview]] — **TTTC (Trung tâm tài chính)** — the Vietnam International Financial Centre, a specially designated geographic and legal zone established by [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] within which special financial, tax, labour, land, immigration, and arbitration rules apply. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] — **Financial policies decree** — the decree governing financial and tax arrangements for entities operating within the TTTC, the benefits of which are contingent on physical presence in the zone. [1]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] — **Land and environment decree** — the decree regulating land use, real estate occupancy, and environmental obligations for institutions physically located in the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] — **Labour and social security decree** — the decree setting employment, social insurance, and worker protection rules applicable to staff at TTTC-based institutions. [6]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] — **Immigration policy decree** — the decree providing special visa and residency arrangements for workers and experts employed by institutions physically present in the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] — **International arbitration centre decree** — the decree establishing and governing the international arbitration centre serving entities operating within the TTTC. [4]
