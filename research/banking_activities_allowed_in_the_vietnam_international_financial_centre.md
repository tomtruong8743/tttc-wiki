# Banking Activities Allowed in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) provides a regulated environment for a range of financial and banking activities, governed by a series of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office. The specific rules and permissions relating to banking and foreign exchange operations within the TTTC are addressed in detail under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], as part of the broader [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] established through the [[Financial Center Decrees]]. These decrees collectively define what banking activities are permitted, how foreign institutions may participate, and how the centre interfaces with Vietnam's existing financial regulatory architecture.

## Key Points

- The source documents are issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Government Office of Vietnam), confirming governmental authority over TTTC regulations. [1][5]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] serves as the foundational legal document establishing the TTTC and its operational scope. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] governs financial policies within the TTTC, which encompasses banking-related financial rules including infrastructure and systems requirements for institutions registering to operate within the centre. [6]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] provides detailed provisions on the licensing, establishment, and permitted activities of banks operating within the TTTC, including rules specific to [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|100% foreign-owned commercial banks]] and branches of foreign banks. [4][5]
- Supporting decrees covering areas such as [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] indicate the comprehensive, multi-sectoral legal architecture within which banking activities operate. [1][2][3]

## Permitted Activities for Foreign Banks and Branches

Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], 100% foreign-owned commercial banks and branches of foreign banks that are members of the TTTC are permitted to conduct the following activities with organisations on Vietnamese territory that are not Vietnamese credit institutions: [5]

1. **Lending** — provision of loans to eligible counterparties.
2. **Discounting** — discounting of financial instruments.
3. **Bank guarantees** — issuance of bank guarantee instruments.
4. **Factoring** — factoring services and other related activities.
5. **Letter of credit operations** — letter of credit business and other related activities.
6. **Corporate bond trading** — buying and selling corporate bonds issued on Vietnamese territory, excluding bonds issued by Vietnamese credit institutions.
7. **Government bond trading** — buying and selling Vietnamese Government bonds, government-guaranteed bonds, and local government bonds in accordance with applicable law, including purchases on the primary market. [5]
8. **Debt purchase** — purchasing debt obligations. [5]

These permitted activities reflect a defined scope of [[Capital Market Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] intersecting with banking operations, and are relevant to understanding [[Financial Services Investment Banks Can Provide In Tttc]].

## Personnel and Governance Requirements for Member Banks

[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] also specifies qualification requirements for senior management of member commercial banks within the TTTC: [4]

- **Deputy General Directors (Deputy Directors), Chief Accountants, and equivalent roles** must meet the following conditions:
  - Must not fall under the categories of persons prohibited from holding office as specified in the decree.
  - Must hold at least a university-level qualification in one of the following fields: finance, banking, economics, business administration, law, accounting, or auditing; **or** hold a university-level degree in another field combined with at least **three years of direct working experience** in finance, banking, or the relevant specialist field. [4]

- **General Directors (Directors)** must additionally:
  - Have direct experience in the fields of finance, banking, accounting, or auditing.
  - Be resident in Vietnam during their term of office. [4]

These requirements are relevant to [[Attracting International Financial Talent To Tttc]] and [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], and reflect broader standards under [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].

## Relationship to Arbitration and Dispute Resolution

Disputes arising from banking and investment activities within the TTTC may be submitted to the [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|International Arbitration Centre]] established under [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]]. The arbitration centre has jurisdiction to resolve disputes by agreement between parties relating to investment and business activities within the TTTC, subject to its own rules and regulations. [1]

However, the International Arbitration Centre does **not** have jurisdiction over the following categories of disputes: [1]

- Disputes involving administrative decisions or acts by state management agencies or authorised persons.
- Labour-related disputes.
- Disputes relating to personal rights of individuals.
- Matters already resolved by judgment or decision of a competent state management authority or a competent court in Vietnam. [1]

Courts also may not refuse to hear applications to set aside arbitral awards or decisions of the Arbitration Council in certain circumstances, including where the waiver agreement falls under categories of void agreements under the Law on Commercial Arbitration, or where the waiver was made after a party had already filed an application to annul an award. [1]

This framework is further described in [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[How Disputes Are Handled In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].

## Commodities Exchange and Related Activities

[[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] establishes a framework for a commodities exchange operating within the TTTC. Conditions for preferential treatment relating to import and export tariffs, and other customs procedure benefits, are to be implemented in accordance with [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] (the decree governing financial policies in the TTTC). [3]

Goods subject to specialised inspection requirements — including food safety, health, environment, agriculture, science and technology, national defence, and security — must comply with the relevant regulations, inspection procedures, and oversight authority of the corresponding specialised management agency. [3]

The managing authority at the city level (Cơ quan điều hành tại các thành phố) is responsible for: [3]

- Reviewing and appraising applications to establish a commodities exchange by TTTC members.
- Issuing, reissuing, suspending, and revoking establishment licences.
- Approving the list of goods produced in Vietnam permitted for trading.
- Approving the list of contracts of foreign commodities exchanges permitted for linked trading. [3]

## Physical Infrastructure Requirements

Organisations registering to operate within the TTTC under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] are required to submit a physical infrastructure declaration (Bản thuyết minh cơ sở vật chất), covering: [6]

- Total floor area and layout of the registered office premises.
- Office equipment and software systems.
- Systems supporting crowdfunding platform operations (nền tảng huy động vốn cộng đồng).
- Systems supporting client asset management activities (quản lý tài sản khách hàng). [6]

These requirements are relevant to [[Real Estate And Office Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Operational Due Diligence Questions For The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].

## Definitions

- **[[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]]** — Trung tâm Tài chính Quốc tế (Vietnam International Financial Centre), the special financial zone established by the Vietnamese government. [5]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Government Office of Vietnam; the issuing authority for the TTTC decrees referenced in the sources.
- **[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]** — The decree governing the licensing, establishment, and permitted activities of banks and management of foreign exchange within the TTTC. [4][5]
- **[[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|Ngoại hối]]** — Foreign exchange; the management of foreign exchange transactions within the TTTC is addressed under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]].
- **Ngân hàng thương mại thành viên** — Member commercial bank; a commercial bank that holds membership status within the TTTC framework. [4]
- **[[Legal Framework Of Tttc]]** — The collective body of decrees and regulations that define the legal structure and permitted activities within the TTTC.
