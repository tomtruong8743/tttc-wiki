# Banking Activities Allowed in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) provides a regulated environment for a range of financial and banking activities, governed by a series of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office. The specific rules and permissions relating to banking and foreign exchange operations within the TTTC are understood to fall under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], as part of the broader [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] established through the [[Financial Center Decrees]]. These decrees collectively define what banking activities are permitted, how foreign institutions may participate, and how the centre interfaces with Vietnam's existing financial regulatory architecture.

## Key Points

- The source documents provided are issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Government Office of Vietnam), confirming governmental authority over TTTC regulations. [1][5]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] serves as the foundational legal document establishing the TTTC and its operational scope. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] governs financial policies within the TTTC, which may encompass banking-related financial rules. [1]
- The regulatory framework for banking and foreign exchange activities in the TTTC is understood to be addressed within the suite of TTTC decrees. [1][5]
- Supporting decrees covering areas such as [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] indicate the comprehensive, multi-sectoral legal architecture within which banking activities operate.
