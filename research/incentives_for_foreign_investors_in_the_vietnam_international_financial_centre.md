# Incentives for Foreign Investors in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) was established through a series of government decrees issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam, collectively forming the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] that governs operations within the centre. These decrees address a range of policy areas relevant to foreign investors, including [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]], financial policies under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], land and environment regulations under [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], immigration policies under [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], labour and social security under [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], banking and [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], and international arbitration under [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]]. Together, these instruments form the foundation of the incentive framework designed to attract foreign participation in the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].

---

## Key Points

- The foundational legal framework for the TTTC is established through multiple decrees issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out the [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]] and broader financial policies applicable within the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview]], including licensing of insurance enterprises, reinsurance companies, and insurance brokers operating within the TTTC. [1][6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] governs land and environmental regulations that may affect foreign investors operating within the centre. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] provides immigration policy provisions relevant to foreign investors and foreign workers seeking to operate or work within the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] addresses labour, employment, and social security conditions applicable within the TTTC, which may affect the hiring of foreign and domestic workers. [6]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, providing [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] for foreign investors involved in investment and business activities at the centre. The arbitration centre has jurisdiction to resolve disputes by agreement between parties related to investment and business activities at the TTTC, subject to its own rules and regulations. [2]
- The [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] explicitly excludes certain categories of dispute from the arbitration centre's jurisdiction, including disputes involving administrative decisions or acts of state management bodies, labour disputes, disputes related to personal rights of individuals, and matters already resolved by competent state authorities or courts in Vietnam. [2]
- Courts may not refuse to hear applications to annul arbitral awards or decisions of arbitral tribunals, except in specified circumstances involving invalid waiver agreements or agreements made after a party has already filed for annulment. [2]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the overarching legal basis for the creation of the TTTC itself, including provisions for the management body to coordinate with relevant agencies in receiving and processing complaints, recommendations, and disputes within the TTTC. [4]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] governs [[Banking Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], including rules on borrowing and lending in foreign currency by TTTC members. [1][3][5]
- Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], TTTC members may borrow foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside Vietnam's territory, subject to information disclosure and reporting obligations. [1]
- Members wholly owned by foreign investors (100% foreign-owned) are permitted to lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnam's territory, subject to information disclosure and reporting requirements. [1]
- Members not wholly owned by foreign investors are subject to additional conditions on overseas lending set out in the decree, including requirements relating to the ownership structure of the borrowing entity. [1]
- Domestically-funded commercial banks (100% domestic capital) operating as TTTC members may lend in foreign currency to organisations established and operating outside Vietnam where Vietnamese enterprises or members hold more than 50% of shares or contributed capital, or to organisations controlled by such entities. [1]
- Member enterprises (non-bank) may lend to organisations established and operating outside Vietnam where the lender or its parent company holds more than 50% of shares or contributed capital. [1]
- Members and their employees are permitted to participate in insurance provided by insurance enterprises established and operating within the TTTC, insurance enterprises established and operating in the remainder of Vietnam, and insurance enterprises outside Vietnam. [6]
- The licensing authority within the TTTC's management body (Cơ quan điều hành) has the power to grant, amend, supplement, and revoke establishment and operation licences for insurance enterprises, reinsurance enterprises, and insurance brokerage enterprises within the TTTC. [6]
- Senior management appointments within TTTC member banks are subject to governance and professional ethics requirements, including residency requirements and restrictions on concurrent officeholding. Certain qualification requirements related to prior work experience in Vietnam do not apply to candidates without such experience. [3][5]

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview]] **TTTC (Trung tâm tài chính quốc tế)** — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, established by the Vietnamese Government as a special financial zone with dedicated regulatory policies. [4]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] **Financial Policy Decree** — The decree issued by the Office of the Government governing financial and tax policies within the TTTC, including insurance licensing. [1][6]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] **Immigration Policy Decree** — The decree governing entry, exit, and residency policies for foreign nationals operating within the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] **Land and Environment Decree** — The decree regulating land use and environmental obligations for entities within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] **International Arbitration Centre** — A dedicated arbitration body established within the TTTC to resolve disputes related to investment and business activities, particularly relevant to foreign investors, subject to defined jurisdictional limits. [2]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] **Labour and Social Security Decree** — The decree outlining employment conditions and social security obligations within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] **Establishment Decree** — The founding decree that legally created the TTTC framework and defines the powers and responsibilities of the TTTC management body. [4]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] **Banking and Foreign Exchange Decree** — The decree governing the licensing and establishment of banks, as well as [[Foreign Currency Transactions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|foreign exchange management]] for TTTC members, including rules on cross-border borrowing and lending. [1][3][5]
- **Thành viên (Member)** — An entity that is a recognised participant within the TTTC framework, subject to the rights and obligations set out in the relevant decrees, including foreign exchange reporting requirements and insurance participation rights. [1][6]
