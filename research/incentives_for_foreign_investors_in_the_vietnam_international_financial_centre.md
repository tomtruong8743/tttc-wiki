# Incentives for Foreign Investors in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) was established through a series of government decrees issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam, collectively forming the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] that governs operations within the centre. These decrees address a range of policy areas relevant to foreign investors, including [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]], financial policies under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], land and environment regulations under [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], immigration policies under [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], labour and social security under [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], and international arbitration under [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]]. Together, these instruments form the foundation of the incentive framework designed to attract foreign participation in the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].

---

## Key Points

- The foundational legal framework for the TTTC is established through multiple decrees issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out the [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]] and broader financial policies applicable within the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview]]. [1]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] governs land and environmental regulations that may affect foreign investors operating within the centre. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] provides immigration policy provisions relevant to foreign investors and foreign workers seeking to operate or work within the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] addresses labour, employment, and social security conditions applicable within the TTTC, which may affect the hiring of foreign and domestic workers. [6]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, providing [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] for foreign investors. [4]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the overarching legal basis for the creation of the TTTC itself. [5]

---

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview]] **TTTC (Trung tâm tài chính quốc tế)** — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, established by the Vietnamese Government as a special financial zone with dedicated regulatory policies. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] **Financial Policy Decree** — The decree issued by the Office of the Government governing financial and tax policies within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] **Immigration Policy Decree** — The decree governing entry, exit, and residency policies for foreign nationals operating within the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] **Land and Environment Decree** — The decree regulating land use and environmental obligations for entities within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] **International Arbitration Centre** — A dedicated arbitration body established within the TTTC to resolve disputes, particularly relevant to foreign investors. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] **Labour and Social Security Decree** — The decree outlining employment conditions and social security obligations within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] **Establishment Decree** — The founding decree that legally created the TTTC framework. [5]
