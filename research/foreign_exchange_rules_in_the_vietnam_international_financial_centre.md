# Foreign Exchange Rules in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The foreign exchange rules applicable within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) are governed by the suite of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office ([[Financial Center Decrees]]). The primary decree addressing banking and foreign exchange matters is [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], which sits alongside other foundational legal instruments such as [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], all issued by the Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) as part of the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]].

[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] contains a dedicated section (Section 4) on foreign exchange management rules (Quy Định Quản Lý Ngoại Hối) specifically governing borrowing and lending activities of TTTC members. These provisions form the core of the [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|ngoại hối]] (foreign exchange) regulatory framework within the Centre.

## Key Points

- The decrees establishing the TTTC framework, including those covering financial, banking, and foreign exchange policies, were all issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [1][5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] is one of the key instruments within the [[Legal Documents Establishing The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] that governs financial policy in the TTTC, which may include [[Currency And Capital Controls|currency and capital control]] provisions. [1]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis upon which all other TTTC regulations, including foreign exchange rules, operate. [5]
- The broader regulatory framework includes provisions on land, labour, immigration, and arbitration, suggesting a comprehensive special economic zone approach to [[Foreign Investment Rules In Tttc|foreign investment and exchange rules]]. [3][4][6][2]
- The specific content of foreign exchange rules within the TTTC is addressed under the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] as part of the [[Main Decrees Related To The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]. [1][5]
- Section 4 of [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] is specifically titled "Foreign Exchange Management Rules for Borrowing and Lending Activities of Members" (Quy Định Quản Lý Ngoại Hối Đối Với Hoạt Động Vay, Cho Vay Của Thành Viên). [1]

## Foreign Exchange Rules for Borrowing and Lending (Section 4, Decree 329)

### Borrowing from Overseas (Article 87)

Under Article 87 of [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], TTTC members are permitted to borrow foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside the territory of Vietnam. Members exercising this right must:

- Make information declarations (khai báo thông tin); and
- Comply with applicable reporting requirements (chế độ báo cáo). [1]

This provision supports [[International Capital Flows In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] by enabling TTTC members to access offshore funding in foreign currency.

### Lending Overseas (Articles 88–89)

The rules on overseas lending by TTTC members are set out in Articles 88 and 89 of [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] and depend on the ownership structure of the lending member. [1]

#### 100% Foreign-Owned Members (Article 88.1)

Members whose charter capital is 100% owned by foreign investors are permitted to lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnam, subject to:

- Information declaration requirements; and
- Compliance with the applicable reporting regime. [1]

#### Non-100% Foreign-Owned Members (Article 88.2)

Members that are not 100% foreign-investor owned must comply with the conditions for overseas lending specified in Article 89 of the Decree, and must also make the required information declarations and comply with reporting requirements. [1]

#### Conditions for Overseas Lending — Non-100% Foreign-Owned Members (Article 89)

Article 89 sets out specific eligibility conditions for overseas lending depending on the type of member: [1]

1. **Domestic commercial banks (100% domestic capital)** may lend in foreign currency to:
   - Organisations established and operating outside Vietnam in which Vietnamese members or non-member Vietnamese enterprises hold more than 50% of shares or contributed capital; and
   - Organisations established and operating outside Vietnam that are held more than 50% by entities falling within the above category. [1]

2. **Member enterprises** may lend to organisations established and operating outside Vietnam in which the lending member or its parent company holds more than 50% of shares or contributed capital. [1]

These conditions reflect the broader approach to [[Capital Movement Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Cross Border Payments In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], ensuring that overseas lending is channelled primarily through related-party or controlled structures.

## Reporting and Disclosure Obligations

Across all the borrowing and lending scenarios described above, TTTC members are consistently required to:

- Declare relevant information (khai báo thông tin); and
- Comply with the reporting regime (chế độ báo cáo).

These obligations are consistent with the broader [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Reporting Obligations For Financial Institutions In Tttc]] that apply to all participants in the TTTC framework. They also intersect with [[Anti Money Laundering Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Kyc Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] requirements applicable to cross-border transactions.

## Governance and Personnel Standards for Member Banks

[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] also addresses governance requirements for member commercial banks operating within the TTTC, including standards for key management personnel such as Deputy Directors (Phó Tổng Giám Đốc), Chief Accountants (Kế Toán Trưởng), and equivalent roles. Relevant requirements include: [5][6]

- Holding a university degree or above in finance, banking, economics, business administration, law, accounting, auditing, or another relevant field; or
- Holding a university degree in another field plus at least three years of direct working experience in finance, banking, or the relevant specialist area. [6]
- Residing in Vietnam during the period of appointment. [5][6]

In addition, Article 31 of Decree 329 sets out professional ethics standards, specifying that persons responsible for causing a Vietnamese credit institution or member bank to receive the highest-tier administrative penalty for violations — including those related to licensing, governance, shareholding, credit extension, corporate bond purchases, or safety ratios — will be assessed as not meeting professional ethics requirements. [5]

These governance standards are relevant to [[Rules For Banks Operating In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Licenses And Approvals For Financial Institutions In Tttc]].

## Definitions

- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] — The decree specifically governing banking activities and foreign exchange regulations within the TTTC, including a dedicated Section 4 on foreign exchange management rules for member borrowing and lending activities. [1]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — The Vietnamese Government Office; the issuing authority for all TTTC-related decrees. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Currency And Capital Controls]] — Regulatory measures governing the flow and conversion of currencies within and into/out of the TTTC. [1]
- [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc]] — Ngoại hối; the Vietnamese term for foreign exchange, which is the subject of Section 4 of Decree 329. [1]
- **Thành viên** — "Member"; refers to entities that are registered participants of the TTTC and subject to the foreign exchange rules under Decree 329. [1]
- **Khai báo thông tin** — Information declaration; a mandatory disclosure requirement accompanying all cross-border borrowing and lending by TTTC members. [1]
- **Chế độ báo cáo** — Reporting regime; the ongoing compliance reporting obligation applicable to TTTC members engaged in foreign currency borrowing and lending. [1]
- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] — The collective body of decrees and regulations establishing the legal structure of the Vietnam International Financial Centre. [5]
