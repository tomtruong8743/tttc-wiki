# Conditions for Tax Incentives in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The conditions for [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]] within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]] are governed by the financial policy framework established under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]. These incentives form part of a broader package of measures designed to attract participants to the TTTC, alongside related policies covered in [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]].

## Key Points

- The source documents are issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), indicating these are official government decrees. [1]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] is the primary decree covering financial policies within the TTTC, which would include conditions for [[Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]. Application forms and registration documentation requirements are embedded within this decree, including requirements for legal representatives to sign and stamp submissions. [6]
- The decrees covering conditions for tax incentives exist alongside a suite of related decrees addressing land and environment ([[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]]), labor and social security ([[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]]), immigration ([[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]]), international arbitration ([[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]]), banking and foreign exchange ([[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]), and commodities exchange ([[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]]). [3][6][2][4][5]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] establishes the foundational legal basis upon which the tax incentive conditions in subsequent decrees rest. [5]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] establishes conditions governing [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange]] management for TTTC members, including rules on borrowing and lending across borders. These conditions interact with the broader tax and financial incentive framework by defining which entities qualify as members and what activities they may undertake. [2][4]
- Members (**thành viên**) of the TTTC that are 100% foreign-investor-owned may lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnam's territory, subject to information declaration and reporting obligations. Members not 100% foreign-owned must satisfy additional lending conditions set out in Article 89 of [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. [2]
- Borrowing conditions imposed on domestic borrowers (including Vietnamese credit institutions) include prudential ratio requirements that must be satisfied during the three consecutive months prior to signing a loan agreement. Longer-term loans (over 12 months) must serve the domestic borrower's credit activities, with at least 50% of the loan value used to on-lend to investment projects in preferential investment sectors under applicable law. [4]
- Foreign investors transferring funds related to foreign investment into the TTTC must do so through a foreign investor capital account opened at a member bank, as stipulated under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. This requirement is a structural condition that intersects with [[Capital Movement Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Foreign Currency Transactions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]. [4]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] provides that the International Arbitration Centre has jurisdiction to resolve disputes related to investment and business activities at the TTTC by agreement between parties, except for disputes involving administrative decisions or acts of state management agencies, labour disputes, disputes involving personal rights, or matters already resolved by competent state authorities or courts. [5]

## Conditions Linked to Membership and Entity Type

A recurring theme across the TTTC decree framework is that the availability and scope of incentives — including tax-related benefits — depends on the nature and classification of the participating entity:

- **100% foreign-owned members** enjoy broader permissions, such as the right to lend abroad without the additional conditions applicable to other members. [2]
- **Domestic Vietnamese commercial banks** that are members may lend in foreign currency to overseas organisations where more than 50% of the equity is held by a Vietnamese enterprise (member or non-member), or where such an organisation holds more than 50% of the equity of another overseas entity. [2]
- **Non-100%-foreign-owned member enterprises** may lend to overseas organisations where more than 50% of the equity is held by the lending party or its parent company. [2]
- Conditions relating to [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|compliance]], reporting, and prudential ratios are prerequisites that must be satisfied before a member can access certain financial privileges, and by extension, any associated tax treatments. [4]

## Registration and Documentation Requirements

- Registration for activities covered under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] — including crowdfunding platform operations — requires a formal application file listing attached documents, signed and stamped by the legal representative of the applying organisation. [6]
- For Vietnamese citizens and persons of Vietnamese origin whose nationality has not yet been determined and who are residing in Vietnam, or for foreigners who have been issued a personal identification number, standard identification requirements apply. Foreigners not yet issued a personal identification number are subject to separate identification requirements. [6]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] — The government decree issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam governing financial policies within the TTTC, including conditions for tax incentives and registration requirements for financial activities. [1][6]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] — The decree governing licensing, establishment of banks, and [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|foreign exchange (ngoại hối)]] management within the TTTC, including borrowing and lending rules for members. [2][4]
- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, the subject of the suite of government decrees referenced in the source documents. [5]
- **Thành viên** — "Member"; an entity formally admitted to the TTTC, whose classification (e.g., 100% foreign-owned vs. partially domestic) determines the scope of available incentives and permissions. [2]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all decrees related to the TTTC. [1]
- **Ngoại hối** — Foreign exchange; subject to specific management rules under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] for TTTC members engaging in cross-border borrowing and lending. [2][4]
- **Người đại diện theo pháp luật** — Legal representative; the authorised signatory required on registration and application documents submitted under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]. [6]
