# Conditions for Tax Incentives in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The conditions for [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]] within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]] are governed by the financial policy framework established under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]. These incentives form part of a broader package of measures designed to attract participants to the TTTC, alongside related policies covered in [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]].

## Key Points

- The source documents are issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), indicating these are official government decrees. [1]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] is the primary decree covering financial policies within the TTTC, which would include conditions for [[Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]. [1]
- The decrees covering conditions for tax incentives exist alongside a suite of related decrees addressing land and environment ([[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]]), labor and social security ([[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]]), immigration ([[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]]), and international arbitration ([[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]]). [3][6][2][4]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] establishes the foundational legal basis upon which the tax incentive conditions in subsequent decrees rest. [5]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] — The government decree issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam governing financial policies within the TTTC, including conditions for tax incentives. [1]
- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, the subject of the suite of government decrees referenced in the source documents. [5]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all decrees related to the TTTC. [1]
