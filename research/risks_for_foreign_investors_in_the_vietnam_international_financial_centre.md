# Risks for Foreign Investors in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) is governed by a series of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office, including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], and [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. While these [[Financial Center Decrees]] establish the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] for the centre, foreign investors should be aware of specific regulatory constraints, compliance obligations, and operational restrictions embedded in the decrees — particularly those relating to [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]], [[Banking Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|banking activities]], personnel qualifications, and [[Capital Movement Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|cross-border lending]].

---

## Key Points

- The TTTC is established and governed by decrees issued by the VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (Government Office of Vietnam), indicating that its [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] is rooted in Vietnamese government regulation. Changes to these decrees carry inherent regulatory risk for foreign investors. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] forms the foundational legal instrument for the centre's creation, which may carry regulatory risks if its provisions are amended or reinterpreted. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] governs financial policies within the TTTC, suggesting that [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]] and other financial rules are subject to government policy decisions that may change over time. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] covers labour, employment, and social security matters within the TTTC, which may present compliance obligations for foreign employers. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] governs land and environmental regulations, which may affect foreign investors' ability to acquire or use property within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] regulates entry and exit policies, which could affect the mobility of foreign workers and executives within the TTTC. Forms such as Mẫu NT-01 must be submitted for foreign nationals seeking permanent residency while working at the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration centre, relevant to [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] for foreign investors in the event of legal disputes. [4]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] introduces detailed qualification requirements for senior bank personnel and significant restrictions on cross-border lending and [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange activities]], which represent operational and compliance risks for foreign financial institutions. [5][6]

## Specific Risks Identified

### Personnel and Qualification Requirements
Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], senior managers of member commercial banks — including Deputy General Directors, Chief Accountants, and equivalent roles — must meet strict qualification standards. These include holding at least a university degree in finance, banking, economics, business administration, law, accounting, or auditing, or a university degree in another field combined with at least three years of direct experience in finance or banking. Failure to meet these requirements constitutes a licensing and compliance risk for [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]. [5]

### Cross-Border Lending Restrictions
[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] imposes differentiated rules on cross-border lending depending on the ownership structure of the lending member:
- Members that are **100% foreign-owned** may lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnam but must register information and comply with reporting obligations. [5]
- Members that are **not 100% foreign-owned** face additional conditions: domestic commercial banks may only lend in foreign currency to overseas organisations in which a Vietnamese enterprise holds more than 50% of shares or contributed capital, or to organisations controlled by such entities. [5]
- Member enterprises (non-bank members) may lend to overseas organisations in which they or their parent companies hold more than 50% of shares or contributed capital. [5]

This means that partially foreign-owned or domestically invested members face more restrictive cross-border lending conditions, which may limit the scope of international financial operations for mixed-ownership vehicles in the TTTC. See also [[Capital Movement Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[International Capital Flows In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].

### Foreign Exchange Borrowing Obligations
Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], TTTC members are permitted to borrow foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside Vietnam, but must comply with information disclosure and reporting requirements. Non-compliance with these reporting obligations constitutes a regulatory risk. [5] See also [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc]] and [[Foreign Currency Transactions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].

### Restrictions on Bond Transactions
Foreign-currency-denominated Vietnamese government bonds, government-guaranteed bonds, and local government bonds **may not be sold** by domestic commercial banks operating within the TTTC. This restriction applies even where buying such instruments is permitted, creating an asymmetric market risk for investors holding or dealing in these instruments. [6] See also [[Debt Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Bond Issuance Opportunities In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].

### Scope-of-Activity Limitations for Foreign Bank Branches
Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], advisory and consulting services provided by 100% foreign-owned commercial banks or foreign bank branches must be implemented by the relevant licensed member institution itself, in accordance with the scope defined in the licence issued by the City Management Authority. This restricts the ability of foreign institutions to delegate or outsource advisory functions within the TTTC, creating operational constraints. [4] See also [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Licenses And Approvals For Financial Institutions In Tttc]].

### Regulatory and Policy Change Risk
All key rules — including [[Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|tax incentives]], [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|labour rules]], [[Land Use Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|land use]], and [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange]] — are set by government decree. This creates the risk that policy changes or reinterpretation of decrees could alter the operating environment for foreign investors without advance notice. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Legal Framework]] — The body of decrees and regulations issued by the Vietnamese Government Office that governs operations within the TTTC. [5]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Establishing Decree]] — The primary decree (Decree 323) that formally creates the TTTC and defines its foundational structure. [5]
- [[Financial Supervision And Governance|Financial Policy Risk]] — The risk that government-set financial policies, as defined in [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], may change in ways that affect investor returns or obligations. [1]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|International Arbitration Centre]] — A dispute resolution body established under Decree 328 to handle legal conflicts arising within the TTTC. [4]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] — A decree governing banking licensing, establishment, and foreign exchange management within the TTTC, including rules on cross-border lending, foreign currency borrowing, bond transactions, and personnel qualifications. [5][6]
- VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ — The Government Office of Vietnam, the issuing authority for all TTTC-related decrees. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- Mẫu NT-01 — The official form under [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] for requesting permanent residency for foreign nationals working at the TTTC. [2]
