# Licenses and Approvals for Financial Institutions in TTTC

## Summary

Licenses and approvals for financial institutions operating within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) are governed by a suite of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office ([[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]], and related instruments). These decrees collectively establish the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] under which financial institutions must obtain the necessary authorisations to operate, covering areas such as [[Banking Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], [[Capital Market Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], [[Foreign Investment Rules In Tttc]], and associated regulatory obligations. The licensing regime encompasses a wide range of institution types — from securities firms and insurance companies to member commercial banks and commodities exchanges — each requiring specific permits and approvals from the relevant managing authority (Cơ quan điều hành) within TTTC.

## Key Points

- The decrees governing TTTC, including those on licensing and approvals, were issued by the VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (Government Office of Vietnam). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] forms the foundational legal instrument establishing TTTC and its operational framework. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] directly addresses licensing for multiple institution types within TTTC, including securities businesses, crowdfunding platform providers, and insurance enterprises, through a set of prescribed application forms and licence templates. [3][6]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] governs the licensing, establishment, and governance of member commercial banks (ngân hàng thương mại thành viên) operating within TTTC, including requirements for internal governance committees such as the Risk Management Committee and Personnel Committee. [4]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] establishes the framework for the establishment and operation of the Commodities Exchange (Sở giao dịch hàng hóa) within TTTC, including a formal approval process for entities seeking to qualify as a Clearing Centre (Trung tâm thanh toán bù trừ). [1]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] covers entry and exit policies, which may be relevant to foreign financial institution personnel seeking to operate in TTTC; Decree 327 also provides a prescribed form (Mẫu NT-01) for applications for permanent residency by foreign nationals working in TTTC. [5]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] governs land and environmental matters, which may relate to the physical establishment of financial institution offices within TTTC. [3 — existing source]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the international arbitration framework applicable within TTTC, relevant to dispute resolution for licensed financial institutions. [4 — existing source]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] covers labour and social security matters applicable to employees of financial institutions operating in TTTC. [6 — existing source]

## Definitions

- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (Government Office)** — the issuing authority responsible for publishing all decrees governing TTTC, including those related to licenses and approvals for financial institutions. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- **Cơ quan điều hành (Managing Authority)** — the executive body within TTTC responsible for issuing approvals and licences to financial institutions, including approval of Clearing Centre status for the Commodities Exchange. [1][4]
- **Giấy phép thành lập và hoạt động (Establishment and Operating Licence)** — the primary licence required for financial institutions such as securities businesses, insurance companies, reinsurance companies, and insurance brokerage firms to operate within TTTC, issued under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]. [3][6]
- **Tổ chức kinh doanh chứng khoán là Thành viên (Member Securities Trading Organisation)** — a securities business that is a member of the TTTC framework, required to apply for an establishment and operating licence using the prescribed form under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]. [3]
- **Nền tảng huy động vốn cộng đồng (Crowdfunding Platform)** — an entity requiring a specific licence (Giấy phép cung cấp nền tảng huy động vốn cộng đồng) under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] to provide crowdfunding capital-raising services within TTTC. [3]
- **Ngân hàng thương mại thành viên (Member Commercial Bank)** — a commercial bank operating within TTTC under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], subject to governance requirements including the establishment of a Risk Management Committee (Ủy ban quản lý rủi ro) and Personnel Committee (Ủy ban nhân sự), each comprising at least three members with a majority of non-executive voting members. [4]
- **Trung tâm thanh toán bù trừ (Clearing Centre)** — an entity approved by the Managing Authority to perform clearing functions for the Commodities Exchange within TTTC, under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]]. [1]
- **Chấp thuận đủ điều kiện (Approval of Qualification)** — the formal decision issued by the Managing Authority confirming that an entity meets the conditions to act as a Clearing Centre for the Commodities Exchange within TTTC. [1]
- **Mẫu NT-01** — the prescribed application form under [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] for foreign nationals working in TTTC to apply for permanent residency (thường trú). [5]
- **[[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] — Establishment Decree** — the foundational decree creating TTTC and setting out its governing structure. [5 — existing source]
- **[[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] — Financial Policies Decree** — the decree addressing financial policies within TTTC, including provisions on licensing forms and templates for securities firms, crowdfunding platforms, and insurance enterprises. [3][6]

## Licensing Forms and Templates Under Decree 324

[[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] prescribes a series of standard forms (mẫu biểu) to be used in the licensing process for financial institutions within TTTC. These include: [3][6]

| Form | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Mẫu số 01 | Application for establishment and operating licence for member securities trading organisations (tổ chức kinh doanh chứng khoán là Thành viên) |
| Mẫu số 02 | Personnel list for securities trading organisations |
| Mẫu số 03 | Application for a licence to provide a crowdfunding platform (nền tảng huy động vốn cộng đồng) |
| Mẫu số 04 | Registration form for crowdfunding capital raising |
| Mẫu số 05 | Description of physical facilities |
| Mẫu số 06 | Licence for providing a crowdfunding platform |
| Mẫu số 07 | Application to revoke a crowdfunding platform licence |
| Mẫu số 08 | Application for establishment and operating licence for insurance enterprises, reinsurance enterprises, and insurance brokerage firms |
| Mẫu số 09 | Establishment and operating licence for insurance enterprises, reinsurance enterprises, and insurance brokerage firms |

Licences issued under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] take effect from the date specified on the face of the licence (Điều 4 of the licence template), and the licensed entity must comply with all relevant legal instruments and the company's charter. [6]
