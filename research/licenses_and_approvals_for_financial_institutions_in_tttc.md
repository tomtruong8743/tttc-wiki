# Licenses and Approvals for Financial Institutions in TTTC

## Summary

Licenses and approvals for financial institutions operating within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) are governed by a suite of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office ([[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], and related instruments). These decrees collectively establish the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] under which financial institutions must obtain the necessary authorisations to operate, covering areas such as [[Banking Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], [[Capital Market Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], [[Foreign Investment Rules In Tttc]], and associated regulatory obligations.

## Key Points

- The decrees governing TTTC, including those on licensing and approvals, were issued by the VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (Government Office of Vietnam). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] forms the foundational legal instrument establishing TTTC and its operational framework. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] addresses financial policies relevant to institutions seeking to operate within TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] covers entry and exit policies, which may be relevant to foreign financial institution personnel seeking to operate in TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] governs land and environmental matters, which may relate to the physical establishment of financial institution offices within TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the international arbitration framework applicable within TTTC, relevant to dispute resolution for licensed financial institutions. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] covers labour and social security matters applicable to employees of financial institutions operating in TTTC. [6]

## Definitions

- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (Government Office)** — the issuing authority responsible for publishing all decrees governing TTTC, including those related to licenses and approvals for financial institutions. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] **Establishment Decree** — the foundational decree creating TTTC and setting out its governing structure. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] **Financial Policies Decree** — the decree addressing financial policies within TTTC, likely including provisions relevant to licensing of financial institutions. [1]
