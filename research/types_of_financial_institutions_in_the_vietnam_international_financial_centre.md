# Types of Financial Institutions in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary
The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) is established under a series of government decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office, each governing different aspects of the centre's operation. The [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational framework for the centre, while related decrees such as [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] collectively define the regulatory environment within which financial institutions in the TTTC operate. The specific types of financial institutions permitted to operate within the TTTC are governed by this broader [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]].

## Key Points
- The TTTC is established and governed through a series of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] forms the foundational legal basis for the creation of the TTTC and the financial institutions that may operate within it. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] governs financial policies applicable to institutions within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] addresses labour, employment, and social security matters relevant to institutions and their staff operating within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] regulates land and environmental matters for institutions physically located within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] sets out immigration and entry/exit policies relevant to foreign staff and representatives of financial institutions in the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the framework for an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, relevant to [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] for financial institutions. [4]
- All decrees were issued by the same issuing authority, VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Government Office of Vietnam). [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- **[[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]]** — Vietnam International Financial Centre; the special financial zone established under Vietnamese government decrees. [5]
- **[[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]]** — The foundational decree establishing the TTTC, issued by the Vietnamese Government Office. [5]
- **[[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]** — The decree governing financial policies within the TTTC, including those applicable to financial institutions. [1]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Government Office of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all decrees establishing and governing the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- **[[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]]** — The decree establishing an international arbitration centre within the TTTC to handle disputes involving financial institutions and other participants. [4]
