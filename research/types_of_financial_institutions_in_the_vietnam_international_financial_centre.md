# Types of Financial Institutions in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary
The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) is established under a series of government decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office, each governing different aspects of the centre's operation. The [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational framework for the centre, while related decrees such as [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange]] collectively define the regulatory environment within which financial institutions in the TTTC operate. The specific types of financial institutions permitted to operate within the TTTC are governed by this broader [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]].

## Key Points
- The TTTC is established and governed through a series of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ). [1][2][3][4][5][6][7][8][9]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] forms the foundational legal basis for the creation of the TTTC and the financial institutions that may operate within it. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] governs financial policies applicable to institutions within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] addresses labour, employment, and social security matters relevant to institutions and their staff operating within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] regulates land and environmental matters for institutions physically located within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] sets out immigration and entry/exit policies relevant to foreign staff and representatives of financial institutions in the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the framework for an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, relevant to [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] for financial institutions. [4]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] governs banking licensing, establishment, and [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]] for member banks (ngân hàng thương mại thành viên) operating within the TTTC. [7][8][9]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange]] governs the establishment and operation of a commodities exchange (Sở giao dịch hàng hóa) within the TTTC, representing a distinct category of financial market infrastructure. [3a]
- All decrees were issued by the same issuing authority, VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Government Office of Vietnam). [1][2][3][4][5][6][7][8][9]

## Types of Financial Institutions

### Member Commercial Banks (Ngân hàng thương mại thành viên)
Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], commercial banks that are admitted as members of the TTTC are referred to as **ngân hàng thương mại thành viên** (member commercial banks). These institutions are subject to specific licensing, governance, and [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange]] rules within the TTTC framework. [7][8][9]

Key features of member commercial banks include:
- **Governance requirements**: Member commercial banks must have a Board of Members (Hội đồng thành viên), a Supervisory Board (Ban kiểm soát), and executive management including a General Director (Tổng giám đốc/Giám đốc), Deputy General Director(s) (Phó Tổng giám đốc/Phó giám đốc), and a Chief Accountant (Kế toán trưởng). [8][9]
- **Qualification requirements for executives**: Deputy General Directors, Chief Accountants, and equivalent roles must hold at minimum an undergraduate degree in finance, banking, economics, business administration, law, accounting, auditing, or a relevant specialist field — or alternatively, an undergraduate degree in another discipline combined with at least three years of direct experience in finance, banking, or the relevant specialist field. [8]
- **Residency requirement**: Key executives must reside in Vietnam during their term of office. [8][9]
- **Professional ethics standards**: Persons responsible for regulatory violations (e.g., licensing, governance, credit, bond purchases, or safety ratios) that led to the maximum administrative penalty being imposed on a Vietnamese credit institution or member bank may be assessed as failing professional ethics standards and disqualified from senior roles. [9]
- **Conflict of interest restrictions**: Members of the Board of Members of a member commercial bank may not simultaneously serve as: (i) an executive of that same member bank (except as General Director); (ii) a manager or executive of another non-member credit institution, another member commercial bank, or another enterprise in Vietnam (except as manager/executive of the bank's owner); or (iii) a supervisor or member of the supervisory board of another non-member credit institution, another member commercial bank, or another enterprise in Vietnam. [9]
- Members of the Supervisory Board of a member commercial bank may not simultaneously serve as a manager, executive, or employee of that member bank, another non-member credit institution, another member commercial bank, or another enterprise in Vietnam. [9]

### Foreign-Owned Member Institutions
Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], a distinction is drawn between TTTC members based on ownership structure, particularly in relation to [[Cross Border Payments In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|cross-border lending]] activities:
- **100% foreign-owned members**: Members whose charter capital is 100% owned by foreign investors are permitted to lend in foreign currency to individuals and organisations outside the territory of Vietnam, subject to information declaration and [[Reporting Obligations For Financial Institutions In Tttc|reporting obligations]]. [7]
- **Non-100%-foreign-owned members** (including 100% domestically funded commercial banks): These members are subject to additional conditions when lending abroad (see [[Rules For Banks Operating In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]). Specifically, a domestically funded commercial bank (ngân hàng thương mại 100% vốn trong nước) may lend in foreign currency to: (a) organisations established and operating outside Vietnam in which Vietnamese enterprises (whether members or non-members) hold more than 50% of shares or contributed capital; and (b) organisations established and operating outside Vietnam that are majority-owned by the entities described in (a). [7]
- **Non-100%-foreign-owned member enterprises**: May lend to organisations established and operating outside Vietnam in which the lending member or its parent company holds more than 50% of shares or contributed capital. [7]

### Member Enterprises (Doanh nghiệp thành viên)
In addition to commercial banks, [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] also refers to **doanh nghiệp thành viên** (member enterprises) as a distinct category of TTTC participant with certain cross-border lending rights under foreign exchange regulations, subject to the ownership conditions described above. [7]

### Commodities Exchange (Sở giao dịch hàng hóa)
Under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange]], a **Sở giao dịch hàng hóa** (commodities exchange) may be established and operated within the TTTC by members of the International Financial Centre. This represents a category of financial market infrastructure, distinct from banking institutions. Key regulatory features include:
- The operating authority (Cơ quan điều hành) in the relevant cities is responsible for reviewing and approving applications for establishment, issuing, re-issuing, suspending, and revoking establishment licences. [3a]
- The operating authority approves the list of domestically produced goods permitted for trading, as well as the list of contracts from foreign commodities exchanges permitted for linked trading. [3a]
- The Ministry of Industry and Trade (Bộ Công Thương) cooperates with the supervisory body (Cơ quan giám sát) to inspect the activities of the commodities exchange. [3a]
- Tax and customs incentives applicable to the commodities exchange are governed by [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]. [3a]
