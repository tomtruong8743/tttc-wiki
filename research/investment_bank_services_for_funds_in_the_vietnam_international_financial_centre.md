# Investment Bank Services for Funds in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Investment bank services for funds operating within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) are governed by a framework of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office. These decrees collectively establish the legal, financial, and operational environment in which [[Investment Funds In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|investment funds]] and [[How Investment Banks Can Participate In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|investment banks]] may interact and provide services. The regulatory framework spans areas including [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policies]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], and the [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment of the TTTC]] itself.

## Key Points

- The TTTC and its associated regulations, including those relevant to investment bank services for funds, were issued by the VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (Government Office of Vietnam). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis for the establishment of the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]], within which investment banks may provide services to funds. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] covers financial policies applicable within the TTTC, which are directly relevant to how investment banks structure and deliver services to [[Investment Funds In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|funds]]. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] addresses labour, employment, and social security matters within the TTTC, impacting the staffing and operations of investment banks serving funds. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] governs land and environmental regulations within the TTTC, relevant to the physical establishment of investment banking operations. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] sets out immigration and entry/exit policies, which affect the ability of foreign investment banking professionals to work within the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, providing [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution]] infrastructure relevant to investment bank–fund relationships. [4]

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, a special financial zone established by the Vietnamese government to attract international financial activity. [5]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] — The decree governing the establishment of the TTTC, issued by the VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] — The decree setting out financial policies within the TTTC, issued by the VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]] — The decree covering labour, employment, and social security within the TTTC, issued by the VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]] — The decree governing land use and environmental matters within the TTTC, issued by the VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] — The decree establishing immigration and entry/exit policies within the TTTC, issued by the VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] — The decree establishing the International Arbitration Centre within the TTTC, issued by the VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ. [4]
- [[VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ]] — The Government Office of Vietnam, the issuing authority for all TTTC-related decrees referenced in these sources. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
