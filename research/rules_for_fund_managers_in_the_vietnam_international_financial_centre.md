# Rules for Fund Managers in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

This page covers the rules applicable to fund managers operating within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC). The TTTC is governed by a series of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office, including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], and related instruments covering areas such as [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]], [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], and [[Fund Management In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].

## Key Points

- The source documents are a set of decrees issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Government Office of Vietnam) governing the TTTC framework. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] establishes the foundational legal basis for the TTTC, within which [[Fund Management In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|fund managers]] would operate. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] covers financial policies within the TTTC, which may include rules relevant to [[Asset Managers In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|asset and fund managers]]. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] addresses labour, employment, and social security matters, which would apply to fund management firms and their staff operating within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] governs land and environmental matters within the TTTC, relevant to the physical establishment of [[Fund Management In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|fund management offices]]. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] sets out immigration and entry/exit policies applicable to foreign professionals, including those working in [[Fund Management In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|fund management]]. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, providing [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] relevant to fund managers. [4]

## Definitions

- [[Fund Management In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Fund manager]] — an entity or individual responsible for managing investment funds within the TTTC framework; specific definitional criteria are not covered in the source documents.
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|TTTC]] — the Vietnam International Financial Centre, established under Decree 323 by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ. [5]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — the Government Office of Vietnam, the issuing authority for all decrees governing the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|International Arbitration Centre]] — a dispute resolution body established within the TTTC under Decree 328. [4]

> **Note:** The source excerpts provide only the issuing authority and document titles for each decree. Detailed definitional content specific to fund managers is not covered in the source documents.
