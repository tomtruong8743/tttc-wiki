# How Companies Raise Capital Through the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) provides a framework through which companies can access capital markets and financial services within a specially governed zone. The legal and financial architecture of the TTTC is established through a series of government decrees issued by the [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Office of the Government]], covering areas such as [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policy]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], all of which together form the enabling environment for [[Capital Raising In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital raising activities]] within the centre.

## Key Points

- The TTTC is established and governed through a set of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government's Office ([[Legal Framework Of Tttc|VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ]]). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis for the establishment of the TTTC. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] governs the financial policies applicable within the TTTC, which are directly relevant to how companies structure and execute capital raising. [1]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] addresses land and environmental regulations within the TTTC, relevant to companies seeking physical establishment in the centre. [3]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] covers labour, employment, and social security matters, forming part of the operating environment for companies raising and deploying capital. [6]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] governs immigration policies within the TTTC, facilitating the movement of international professionals involved in capital markets activities. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, providing [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution mechanisms]] relevant to capital raising transactions. [4]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|TTTC]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, established by government decree as a specialised financial zone. [5]
- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ]] — The Office of the Government of Vietnam, the issuing authority for the decrees governing the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Financial Policy Decree]] — The decree (Decree 324) setting out the financial policy framework applicable to entities operating within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|International Arbitration Centre]] — A dispute resolution body established within the TTTC under Decree 328 to handle commercial and financial disputes. [4]
