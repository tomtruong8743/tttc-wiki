# How Companies Raise Capital Through the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) provides a framework through which companies can access capital markets and financial services within a specially governed zone. The legal and financial architecture of the TTTC is established through a series of government decrees issued by the [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Office of the Government]], covering areas such as [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policy]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], and [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|banking licensing and foreign exchange management]], all of which together form the enabling environment for [[Capital Raising In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital raising activities]] within the centre. Additional instruments, including a [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|commodities exchange]], further extend the range of capital market activities available to member companies.

## Key Points

- The TTTC is established and governed through a set of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government's Office ([[Legal Framework Of Tttc|VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ]]). [1][2][3][4][5][6][7][8]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis for the establishment of the TTTC. [5 (existing)]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] governs the financial policies applicable within the TTTC, which are directly relevant to how companies structure and execute capital raising. It also sets out physical infrastructure requirements for organisations seeking to register, including for [[Fund Management In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|asset management]] platforms and [[Equity Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|crowdfunding]] or capital-raising platforms. [4][5 (existing)]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] addresses land and environmental regulations within the TTTC, relevant to companies seeking physical establishment in the centre. [existing]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] covers labour, employment, and social security matters, forming part of the operating environment for companies raising and deploying capital. [existing]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] governs immigration policies within the TTTC, facilitating the movement of international professionals involved in capital markets activities. [existing]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, providing [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution mechanisms]] relevant to capital raising transactions. [existing]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] regulates the licensing, establishment, and [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]] activities of banks operating within the TTTC, including rules governing how member companies may borrow in foreign currency, lend abroad, and conduct [[Foreign Currency Transactions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign currency transactions]]. [1][2][3][6]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] establishes and governs a [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange|commodities exchange]] within the TTTC, with the [[Regulatory Bodies Supervising The Vietnam International Financial Centre|managing authority in each host city]] responsible for licensing, approvals, and oversight of listed commodities and linked foreign exchange contracts. [7]

### Banking and Capital-Raising Activities Available to Member Companies

Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|100% foreign-owned commercial banks and branches of foreign banks]] that are TTTC members may conduct a range of activities relevant to [[Debt Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|debt capital markets]] and corporate finance, including: [1][6]

- Lending, discounting, and [[Bond Issuance Opportunities In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|bank guarantees]].
- Factoring and related services.
- Letters of credit and related business activities.
- Buying and selling of [[Debt Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|corporate bonds]] issued within Vietnam (excluding bonds issued by Vietnamese credit institutions).
- Buying and selling of Vietnamese Government bonds, government-guaranteed bonds, and local government bonds, including on the primary market.
- [[Foreign Currency Transactions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Foreign currency transactions]] (foreign currency-to-foreign currency), subject to State Bank of Vietnam governor regulations on foreign exchange activity scope. [6]

[[Domestic Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|100% domestically-owned commercial banks]] that are TTTC members may also access comparable activities, including: [2]

- Bank guarantees (subject to conditions relating to counterparty location and collateral requirements).
- Factoring and related services.
- Letters of credit and related activities.
- Opening payment accounts in Vietnamese dong and foreign currencies at non-member commercial banks or foreign bank branches, consistent with State Bank of Vietnam rules on accounts held by non-residents.
- Foreign currency transactions. [2]

### Cross-Border Borrowing and Lending

Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], [[Capital Movement Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|member entities]] are permitted to borrow foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside Vietnam, subject to information declaration and [[Reporting Obligations For Financial Institutions In Tttc|reporting obligations]]. [3]

Rules governing outbound lending differ depending on the ownership structure of the member: [3]

- Members that are [[Foreign Ownership Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|100% foreign-investor-owned]] may lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnam, subject to information declaration and reporting requirements.
- Members that are not 100% foreign-investor-owned must satisfy additional conditions under Decree 329 before lending abroad. Specifically, [[Domestic Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|100% domestically-owned commercial bank members]] may lend in foreign currency to: (a) organisations established and operating outside Vietnam in which a Vietnamese company (non-member) or a member holds more than 50% of shares or contributed capital; or (b) organisations outside Vietnam that are majority-owned by such entities. [3]
- Member enterprises (non-bank) may lend to organisations established and operating outside Vietnam in which the lender or its parent company holds more than 50% of shares or contributed capital. [3]

### Commodities Exchange and Export/Import Incentives

The [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|commodities exchange]] established under Decree 330 within the TTTC operates under a licensing and oversight framework administered by the [[Cities Involved In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|managing authority of the relevant host city]]. [7] Key features include:

- Export and import tax incentives and related customs procedure benefits are governed by reference to [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|the financial policy decree]] applicable to the TTTC. [7]
- Goods subject to specialised inspection (e.g. food safety, health, environment, agriculture, science and technology, defence, and security) must comply with the relevant regulatory regime. [7]
- The managing authority approves lists of domestically produced goods eligible for trading on the exchange, as well as lists of foreign commodities exchange contracts eligible for linked trading. [7]
- The [[Regulatory Bodies Supervising The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Ministry of Industry and Trade]] cooperates with the supervisory body in inspecting commodities exchange activities and enforcing rules on export/import and trading violations. [7]

### Infrastructure Requirements for Capital-Raising Platforms

[[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] includes registration forms specifying the physical infrastructure requirements for organisations seeking to operate within the TTTC. These requirements cover: [4]

- Office floor area and layout.
- Equipment and software systems, including those supporting [[Equity Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|crowdfunding capital-raising platforms]] (nền tảng huy động vốn cộng đồng) and [[Asset Managers In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|client asset management activities]]. [4]
