# Financial Products and Instruments in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) is governed by a series of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office ([[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], and related instruments) that collectively establish the legal and operational framework for financial products and instruments traded or issued within the centre. These decrees form part of the broader [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] and are intended to support the development of [[Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital markets]], attract [[Role Of Foreign Financial Institutions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign financial institutions]], and enable a wide range of financial activities within the TTTC's special regulatory environment.

---

## Key Points

- The decrees governing the TTTC, including those related to financial products and instruments, were issued by the **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** (Office of the Government of Vietnam). [1][5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] specifically addresses financial policies within the TTTC, suggesting it is the primary instrument regulating [[Capital Market Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|financial products and capital market activities]]. [1]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis upon which all financial instruments and products operating within the TTTC are premised. [5]
- Supporting decrees cover adjacent regulatory areas that affect how financial products operate, including [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]] [3], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]] [6], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]] [2], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]] [4].
- The existence of a dedicated [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|International Arbitration Centre]] decree suggests that [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] for financial instruments and products are formally provided for within the TTTC framework. [4]

---

## Definitions

- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|**Decree 324**]] — The decree issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam specifically governing financial policies within the TTTC, including (uncertain) rules relating to financial products and instruments. [1]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|**Decree 323**]] — The foundational decree establishing the Vietnam International Financial Centre, issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. [5]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all decrees establishing and regulating the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|**International Arbitration Centre**]] — A dedicated dispute resolution body established within the TTTC framework, relevant to the resolution of disputes arising from financial products and instruments. [4]
