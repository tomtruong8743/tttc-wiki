# Financial Products and Instruments in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) is governed by a series of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office ([[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]], and related instruments) that collectively establish the legal and operational framework for financial products and instruments traded or issued within the centre. These decrees form part of the broader [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] and are intended to support the development of [[Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital markets]], attract [[Role Of Foreign Financial Institutions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign financial institutions]], and enable a wide range of financial activities within the TTTC's special regulatory environment.

---

## Key Points

- The decrees governing the TTTC, including those related to financial products and instruments, were issued by the **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** (Office of the Government of Vietnam). [1][5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] specifically addresses financial policies within the TTTC, including the priority order of debt repayments for insurance enterprises — covering wage arrears, insurance compensation, tax debts, and other liabilities — upon dissolution. [4]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis upon which all financial instruments and products operating within the TTTC are premised. [5]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] sets out in detail which financial products and instruments [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign-owned banks]] and [[Domestic Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|domestic banks]] operating as TTTC members may engage in, including lending, discounting, bank guarantees, factoring, letters of credit, and bond transactions. [5][1][6]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] governs the establishment and operation of a commodities exchange within the TTTC, including rules on commodity trading contracts and eligibility for tax incentives under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]. [2][3]
- Supporting decrees cover adjacent regulatory areas that affect how financial products operate, including [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]].
- The existence of a dedicated [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|International Arbitration Centre]] decree suggests that [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] for financial instruments and products are formally provided for within the TTTC framework.

## Financial Products and Instruments by Category

### Banking Instruments ([[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]])

Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], TTTC member banks — including 100% foreign-owned commercial banks and branches of foreign banks — are permitted to engage in the following activities with organisations on Vietnamese territory that are not Vietnamese credit institutions:

- **Loans (Cho vay)** — Direct lending in foreign currency or domestic currency, subject to applicable rules. [5]
- **Discounting (Chiết khấu)** — Discounting of instruments, a core short-term financing product. [5]
- **Bank Guarantees (Bảo lãnh ngân hàng)** — Issuance of bank guarantees to support commercial and financial transactions. [5]
- **Factoring and Related Services (Bao thanh toán)** — Factoring services, including related activities. [5]
- **Letters of Credit (Thư tín dụng)** — Letters of credit and other related trade finance instruments. [5]
- **Corporate Bonds (Trái phiếu doanh nghiệp)** — Purchase and sale of corporate bonds issued on Vietnamese territory, excluding bonds issued by Vietnamese credit institutions. [5]
- **Government Bonds and Guaranteed Bonds (Trái phiếu Chính phủ, trái phiếu Chính phủ bảo lãnh)** — Purchase and sale of Vietnamese Government bonds, government-guaranteed bonds, and local government bonds, including primary market purchases. [5]
- **Debt Purchase (Mua nợ)** — Acquisition of debt, listed as a permitted activity for relevant TTTC member banks. [5]

For members that are [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|100% foreign-owned]], additional provisions govern:
- **Foreign Currency Borrowing (Vay vốn bằng ngoại tệ)** — TTTC members may borrow in foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to mandatory disclosure and reporting requirements. [1]
- **Lending Abroad (Cho vay ra nước ngoài)** — 100% foreign-owned TTTC members may lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to disclosure and reporting. Members not 100% foreign-owned face additional conditions, including ownership thresholds (greater than 50% shareholding) for eligible overseas borrowers. [1]

See also: [[Foreign Currency Transactions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc]], [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], [[Capital Movement Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].

### Commodities Exchange Products ([[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]])

[[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] provides for the establishment and operation of a **Commodities Exchange (Sở giao dịch hàng hóa)** within the TTTC. Key features include:

- Trading of commodities under pre-agreed settlement terms (uncertain — full details not available from excerpts). [2]
- An approval process for the **list of commodities produced in Vietnam** permitted to be traded on the exchange, subject to approval by the governing authority at each city. [3]
- A mechanism for approving **foreign commodities exchange contracts** eligible for linked (cross-border) trading. [3]
- Commodities subject to **specialist inspection** (food safety, medical, environmental, agricultural, science and technology, national defence, and security) must comply with relevant sectoral regulatory requirements. [3]
- Eligibility for **export/import tax incentives and customs procedure benefits** under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], subject to documentary proof requirements. [3]

Regulatory oversight responsibilities are shared between the **Ministry of Industry and Trade (Bộ Công Thương)** and the **city-level governing authority (Cơ quan điều hành tại các thành phố)**, which issues, suspends, and revokes establishment licences for the exchange. [3]

See also: [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]], [[Securities Activities In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], [[Licenses And Approvals For Financial Institutions In Tttc]].

### Insurance Products ([[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]])

[[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] addresses the treatment of insurance enterprises and reinsurance enterprises operating within the TTTC, particularly in the context of dissolution. The prescribed priority order for debt repayment upon dissolution of an insurance, reinsurance, or insurance brokerage enterprise is:

1. Wages, severance allowances, social insurance, health insurance, unemployment insurance, and other benefits for employees under signed labour contracts.
2. Insurance compensation and insurance payments for claims approved by the insurance or reinsurance enterprise, including surrender values, policy account values, or insurance premium refunds.
3. Tax debts.
4. Other debts.

Following completion of debt repayment, the enterprise proceeds to dissolution under the guidance of the governing authority. [4]
