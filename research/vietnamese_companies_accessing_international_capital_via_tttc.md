# Vietnamese Companies Accessing International Capital via TTTC

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) was established through a series of government decrees issued by [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ]] to create a framework enabling Vietnamese companies to access international capital markets. The TTTC operates under a comprehensive legal and regulatory structure, encompassing [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policies]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment rules]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security provisions]], and [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|banking and foreign exchange rules]], forming a coordinated ecosystem designed to attract international capital flows to and through Vietnam.

## Key Points

- The TTTC was established by a decree issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Government Office of Vietnam), providing the foundational [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal framework]] for international capital access. [1][5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] governs the [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]] applicable within the TTTC, which are directly relevant to Vietnamese companies seeking international capital. [1]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] provides the land and environment policy framework within the TTTC, which may affect the establishment of entities used for capital-raising activities. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] establishes immigration and entry/exit policies within the TTTC, facilitating the movement of international investors and financial professionals. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, providing [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution mechanisms]] to give confidence to international capital providers. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] governs labour, employment, and social security policies within the TTTC, relevant to companies establishing a presence to access capital. [6]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] sets out detailed [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management rules]] for TTTC members, including rules governing cross-border borrowing and lending that are directly relevant to Vietnamese companies accessing international capital. [7][8][9][10][11]
- All decrees were issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ, indicating a coordinated government-level effort to establish the TTTC's [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|regulatory framework]]. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Cross-Border Borrowing and Lending Rules

[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] (Section 4, Chapter VIII) establishes specific [[Capital Movement Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management rules]] governing borrowing and lending activities by TTTC members vis-à-vis parties outside Vietnam. These rules are particularly important for [[How Companies Raise Capital Through The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnamese companies seeking to raise international capital]]. [7]

### Borrowing from Overseas

Under Article 87 of [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], TTTC members are permitted to **borrow foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside the territory of Vietnam**, subject to information disclosure and reporting obligations. [7] This provision creates an explicit pathway for [[Capital Raising In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnamese companies operating as TTTC members]] to access foreign currency financing from international lenders.

### Lending Abroad

Article 88 establishes a two-tier framework for TTTC members wishing to lend to parties outside Vietnam: [7]

1. **100% foreign-owned TTTC members** may lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnam, subject to information disclosure and reporting obligations.
2. **Members not 100% foreign-owned** (including domestically-owned entities) must comply with the additional conditions set out in Article 89 before lending overseas.

Article 89 specifies that domestically-owned TTTC member commercial banks ([[Domestic Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|100% domestically-capitalised commercial banks]]) may lend in foreign currency to:
- Organisations established and operating outside Vietnam in which Vietnamese companies (whether TTTC members or not) hold more than 50% of shares or contributed capital; and
- Organisations established and operating outside Vietnam that are majority-owned by such entities. [7]

Non-bank TTTC member enterprises may lend to organisations established and operating outside Vietnam in which the lender or its parent company holds more than 50% of shares or contributed capital. [7]

### Activities of 100% Domestically-Owned Banks with Overseas Counterparties

Under Article 57 of [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], 100% domestically-owned commercial banks that are TTTC members may conduct the following activities with individuals and organisations outside Vietnam: [8]

- Accepting demand and term deposits
- Depositing with foreign credit institutions
- Borrowing
- Lending (subject to Articles 88 and 89)
- Bank guarantees
- Factoring and related services
- Letters of credit and related activities
- Issuing credit cards
- Opening payment accounts and providing payment services
- Opening and using payment accounts at foreign credit institutions
- Foreign currency-to-foreign currency transactions consistent with the scope of the member's or parent bank's licence (note: transactions between Vietnamese dong and foreign currency are **not** permitted) [8]
- Interest rate derivative products (excluding derivatives linked to Vietnamese dong) [8]

### Activities of 100% Foreign-Owned Banks and Foreign Bank Branches with Vietnamese Counterparties

Under Articles 55 and 56 of [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|100% foreign-owned commercial banks and foreign bank branches]] that are TTTC members may conduct the following activities with organisations on Vietnamese territory that are not Vietnamese credit institutions: [9][10]

- Lending and discounting
- Bank guarantees
- Factoring and related services
- Letters of credit
- Buying and selling corporate bonds issued in Vietnam (excluding bonds issued by Vietnamese credit institutions)
- Buying and selling Vietnamese government bonds, government-guaranteed bonds, and local government bonds (including primary market purchases — though not the sale of foreign-currency-denominated government or local government bonds) [9]

Foreign-owned banks and foreign bank branches may also participate in **international payment systems** of which their parent or owner is already a member. [10] This facilitates [[Cross Border Payments In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|cross-border payment]] connectivity for Vietnamese companies transacting through TTTC-member foreign banks.

## Insurance and Licensing Within the TTTC

[[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] also provides that TTTC members and their employees may participate in insurance with: (a) insurance enterprises established and operating within the TTTC; (b) insurance enterprises established and operating in the rest of Vietnam; or (c) insurance enterprises abroad. [3] This flexibility supports Vietnamese companies operating within the TTTC in managing their risk profiles for international capital transactions.

The decree further provides that the **governing authority (Cơ quan điều hành)** of the TTTC holds the power to grant, amend, supplement, and revoke operating licences for insurance enterprises, reinsurance enterprises, and insurance brokerage enterprises within the TTTC. [3] This is relevant to [[Business Licensing And Registration]] procedures for entities supporting capital-raising activities.

Additionally, [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] includes provisions on **crowdfunding platforms (nền tảng huy động vốn cộng đồng)** and **asset management systems (hệ thống quản lý tài sản khách hàng)**, with infrastructure and physical facilities requirements specified for entities registering to operate these services. [4] This is relevant to [[Capital Raising In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|alternative capital-raising mechanisms]] available to Vietnamese companies.
