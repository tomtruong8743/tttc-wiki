# Vietnamese Companies Accessing International Capital via TTTC

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) was established through a series of government decrees issued by [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ]] to create a framework enabling Vietnamese companies to access international capital markets. The TTTC operates under a comprehensive legal and regulatory structure, encompassing [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policies]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment rules]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security provisions]], forming a coordinated ecosystem designed to attract international capital flows to and through Vietnam.

## Key Points

- The TTTC was established by a decree issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Government Office of Vietnam), providing the foundational [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal framework]] for international capital access. [1][5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] governs the [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]] applicable within the TTTC, which are directly relevant to Vietnamese companies seeking international capital. [1]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] provides the land and environment policy framework within the TTTC, which may affect the establishment of entities used for capital-raising activities. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] establishes immigration and entry/exit policies within the TTTC, facilitating the movement of international investors and financial professionals. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, providing [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution mechanisms]] to give confidence to international capital providers. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] governs labour, employment, and social security policies within the TTTC, relevant to companies establishing a presence to access capital. [6]
- All decrees were issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ, indicating a coordinated government-level effort to establish the TTTC's [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|regulatory framework]]. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre; a specially established financial zone created by Vietnamese government decree to facilitate international financial activity. [5]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] — The foundational decree issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ establishing the TTTC. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] — The decree governing financial policies within the TTTC, including provisions relevant to capital markets and financial services. [1]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Trung tâm trọng tài quốc tế]] — The International Arbitration Centre operating within the TTTC, providing dispute resolution for international financial transactions. [4]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Government Office of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all foundational TTTC decrees. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
