# Location of the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The [[Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) is established under a framework of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office ([[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] and related instruments). The legal documents governing the TTTC — including those covering [[Financial Center Decrees]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] — are all issued by the same issuing authority, VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Government Office of Vietnam). The specific geographic location of the TTTC is not explicitly detailed in the available source excerpts.

---

## Key Points

- All decrees establishing and governing the TTTC were issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Government Office of Vietnam). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] is the foundational decree for the establishment of the [[Vietnam International Financial Centre]]. [5]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] addresses land and environmental matters related to the TTTC, suggesting a defined physical location exists. [3]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] all form part of the governing framework applicable to the TTTC's location. [1][2][4][6]
- The precise city or geographic coordinates of the TTTC are not covered in the source documents provided.

---

## Definitions

- [[Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (**TTTC**) — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, a special financial zone established by the Vietnamese government through a series of decrees. [5]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Government Office of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all decrees related to the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] — A government decree addressing land use and environmental regulations applicable within the TTTC. [3]
