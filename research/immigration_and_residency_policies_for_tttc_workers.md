# Immigration and Residency Policies for TTTC Workers

## Summary

Immigration and residency policies for workers in the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]] are governed primarily by [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], one of a suite of decrees issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) to regulate the establishment and operation of the TTTC. These policies sit alongside related frameworks including [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], and other [[Financial Center Decrees]] that together form the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]].

## Key Points

- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] is the primary legal instrument governing immigration and residency (entry/exit) policy within the TTTC, issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [1]
- The decree forms part of a broader set of government decrees establishing and regulating the TTTC, which also includes [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]]. [2]
- Immigration policy for TTTC workers is closely related to [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Policies For Foreign Experts In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], as entry and residency conditions affect the ability of foreign professionals to work within the centre. [2]
- The issuing authority for [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] is the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [2]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] — The specific government decree regulating immigration and residency (xuất nhập cảnh) policies applicable to workers and experts within the TTTC. [2]
- **Xuất nhập cảnh** — Vietnamese term for entry and exit (immigration), referring to the policies governing the movement of foreign nationals into and out of the TTTC zone. [2]
- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, established under [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], for which special immigration arrangements may apply. [2]
- [[Foreign Worker Regulations]] — The broader regulatory framework governing foreign nationals working in Vietnam, within which TTTC-specific immigration policies operate. [2]
