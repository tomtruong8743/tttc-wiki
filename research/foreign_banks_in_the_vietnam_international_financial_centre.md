# Foreign Banks in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Foreign banks are among the key target participants envisioned for the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]], which was established through a series of government decrees issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. Their participation is governed by a broader [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal framework]] spanning policies on [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policy]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], and the foundational [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment decree]], all of which collectively shape the operating environment for foreign financial institutions within the TTTC.

## Key Points

- The decrees governing the TTTC, including those relevant to foreign banks, were issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis for the establishment of the TTTC, within which [[Role Of Foreign Financial Institutions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign financial institutions]], including foreign banks, would operate. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out the financial policies applicable within the TTTC, which are relevant to the activities and obligations of foreign banks operating in the centre. [1]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] governs immigration policies within the TTTC, which may affect the ability of foreign banks to bring in international staff and personnel. [2]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] covers land and environmental regulations that could apply to foreign banks establishing physical premises within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, providing a [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution mechanism]] relevant to foreign banks engaged in cross-border transactions. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] governs labour, employment, and social security matters within the TTTC, applicable to foreign bank employees working in the centre. [6]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|**TTTC**]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre (Trung Tâm Tài Chính), established under Decree 323 by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|**Financial Policies**]] — The set of fiscal and financial regulations governing entities, including foreign banks, operating within the TTTC, as set out in Decree 324. [1]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|**International Arbitration Centre**]] — A dispute resolution body established within the TTTC under Decree 328, available to foreign banks and other financial participants. [4]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|**Immigration Policy**]] — Regulations governing the entry and stay of foreign nationals within the TTTC, as established under Decree 327, relevant to foreign bank personnel. [2]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|**Labour and Social Security Regulations**]] — Rules governing employment conditions and social security obligations for workers within the TTTC, including those employed by foreign banks. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|**Land and Environment Regulations**]] — Provisions under Decree 326 governing the use of land and environmental compliance within the TTTC, applicable to foreign banks establishing operations there. [3]
