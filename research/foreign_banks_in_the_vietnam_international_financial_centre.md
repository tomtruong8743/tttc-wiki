# Foreign Banks in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Foreign banks are among the key target participants envisioned for the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]], which was established through a series of government decrees issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. Their participation is governed by a broader [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal framework]] spanning policies on [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policy]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], and the foundational [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment decree]], all of which collectively shape the operating environment for foreign financial institutions within the TTTC. [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] provides the most detailed and operationally specific rules for foreign banks, covering licensing, permitted activities, foreign exchange management, and prudential restrictions.

## Key Points

- The decrees governing the TTTC, including those relevant to foreign banks, were issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis for the establishment of the TTTC, within which [[Role Of Foreign Financial Institutions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign financial institutions]], including foreign banks, would operate. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out the financial policies applicable within the TTTC, which are relevant to the activities and obligations of foreign banks operating in the centre. [1]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] governs immigration policies within the TTTC, which may affect the ability of foreign banks to bring in international staff and personnel. [2]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] covers land and environmental regulations that could apply to foreign banks establishing [[Why Physical Location Matters For Banks In Tttc|physical premises]] within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|international arbitration centre]] within the TTTC, providing a [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution mechanism]] relevant to foreign banks engaged in cross-border transactions. The arbitration centre has jurisdiction to resolve disputes arising from investment and business activities at the TTTC by agreement between parties, but expressly excludes disputes relating to administrative decisions or actions of state management authorities, labour disputes, disputes relating to personal rights of individuals, and matters already resolved by a competent state authority or court in Vietnam. [4][5]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] governs labour, employment, and social security matters within the TTTC, applicable to foreign bank employees working in the centre. [6]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] is the primary decree governing [[Banking Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|banking activities]], licensing, and [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]] for bank members of the TTTC, including fully foreign-owned commercial banks and branches of foreign banks. [1][2][3]

### Permitted Activities for 100% Foreign-Owned Commercial Banks and Foreign Bank Branches

Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], 100% foreign-owned commercial banks and foreign bank branches that are members of the TTTC are permitted to conduct the following activities with organisations located in Vietnam that are not Vietnamese credit institutions:

- Lending [2]
- Discounting [2]
- Bank guarantees [2]
- Factoring and related services [2]
- Letter of credit operations and related business activities [2]
- Buying and selling corporate bonds issued in Vietnam (excluding bonds issued by Vietnamese credit institutions) [2]
- Buying and selling Vietnamese Government bonds, Government-guaranteed bonds, and local government bonds, including purchases on the primary market; excluding foreign currency-denominated bonds issued by the Vietnamese Government, Government-guaranteed bonds in foreign currency, and local government bonds in foreign currency [2]
- Debt purchase [2]

### Permitted Activities for 100% Domestically Owned Commercial Banks

Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], 100% domestically owned commercial banks that are TTTC members may conduct the following activities with non-member organisations and individuals: [3][6]

- Bank guarantees, including counter-guarantees and confirmed guarantees, subject to conditions on the beneficiary being located in Vietnam or the bank depositing 100% of the guarantee value [6]
- Factoring and related services [6]
- Letter of credit operations and related business activities [6]
- Opening payment accounts denominated in Vietnamese dong or foreign currency at non-member commercial banks or non-member foreign bank branches, subject to applicable regulations on account opening and use [6]
- Foreign currency transactions [6]
- Participation in international payment systems of which the owner is already a member [3]

### Prudential Restrictions: Credit Prohibition

Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], banks that do not apply [[Financial Supervision And Governance|International Accounting Standards]] are prohibited from extending credit to certain categories of organisations and individuals. [3]

### Foreign Exchange Rules for Borrowing and Lending

Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], the following [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange rules]] apply to borrowing and lending activities of TTTC members:

- **Borrowing from abroad:** TTTC members are permitted to borrow foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to information disclosure and [[Reporting Obligations For Financial Institutions In Tttc|reporting obligations]]. [1]
- **Lending abroad — 100% foreign-owned members:** Members wholly owned by foreign investors are permitted to lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to information disclosure and reporting obligations. [1]
- **Lending abroad — non-100%-foreign-owned members:** Members not wholly owned by foreign investors must comply with the conditions for overseas lending set out in Article 89 of [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] and are subject to information disclosure and reporting requirements. [1]
- **Conditions for overseas lending by 100% domestically owned commercial banks:** A 100% domestically owned commercial bank member may lend in foreign currency to: (a) organisations established and operating outside Vietnamese territory in which Vietnamese enterprises (whether or not members) hold more than 50% of shares or contributed capital; and (b) organisations established and operating outside Vietnamese territory in which the organisations described in (a) hold more than 50% of shares or contributed capital. [1]
- **Conditions for overseas lending by non-bank member enterprises:** A member enterprise may lend to organisations established and operating outside Vietnamese territory in which the lending member or its parent company holds more than 50% of shares or contributed capital. [1]

### Management Personnel Requirements

Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], Deputy General Directors (Deputy Directors), Chief Accountants, and equivalent positions at member commercial banks must meet the following standards and conditions: [4]

- Must not fall within the categories of persons prohibited from holding positions as defined in the relevant articles of the decree; Deputy General Directors (Deputy Directors) must additionally not fall within the categories prohibited under a separate provision [4]
- Must hold at least one of the following qualifications: a university degree or above in finance, banking, economics, business administration, law, accounting, auditing, or another specialised field relevant to their responsibilities; or a university degree or above in another field with at least three years of direct working experience in finance, banking, or the relevant specialisation [4]
- General Directors (Directors) must additionally reside in Vietnam during their term of office [4]
