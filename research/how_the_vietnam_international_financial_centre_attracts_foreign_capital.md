# How the Vietnam International Financial Centre Attracts Foreign Capital

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) is established and governed through a suite of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office ([[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]]), with supporting regulations covering [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]], immigration, land, labour, and dispute resolution. Together, these legal instruments form the framework through which the TTTC seeks to attract foreign capital by creating a specialised, internationally oriented financial environment. Related policies address [[Currency And Capital Controls]], [[Foreign Investment Rules In Tttc]], and [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]], all designed to make the centre competitive with established regional financial hubs.

## Key Points

- The TTTC was formally established by a decree issued by the Vietnamese Government Office, providing the foundational [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] upon which all foreign capital attraction mechanisms rest. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out the financial policy framework within the centre, which is a core instrument for incentivising foreign investment. [1]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] governs entry and exit policies within the TTTC, suggesting that eased immigration conditions are part of the strategy to attract foreign financial professionals and capital. [2]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] addresses land and environmental regulations within the centre, providing a regulatory basis for foreign institutions seeking to establish a physical presence. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, indicating that [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] are a deliberate feature to build foreign investor confidence. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] covers labour and social security policies, which are relevant to [[Foreign Worker Regulations]] and the ability of foreign firms to staff operations within the centre. [6]
- All decrees were issued by the same authority — VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Government Office of Vietnam) — reflecting a coordinated, top-down policy approach to foreign capital attraction. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] — Vietnam International Financial Centre (Trung tâm Tài chính Quốc tế), the specialised financial zone established by the Vietnamese government to attract foreign capital and financial institutions. [5]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] — The foundational decree establishing the TTTC, issued by the Vietnamese Government Office. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] — The decree governing financial policies within the TTTC, a key instrument for defining incentives relevant to foreign capital. [1]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|International Arbitration Centre]] — A dedicated dispute resolution body established within the TTTC, intended to provide foreign investors with internationally recognised legal recourse. [4]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Government Office of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all TTTC-related decrees covered in these sources. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
