# Transferring Capital Into and Out of the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The transfer of capital into and out of the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) is governed by a suite of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office. These rules form part of the broader [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] established to regulate financial flows, investor rights, and operational conditions within the centre. Key instruments include [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], which addresses financial policies, and [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], which covers banking and foreign exchange matters relevant to [[Capital Movement Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital movement]]. Together, these decrees aim to facilitate [[How The Vietnam International Financial Centre Attracts Foreign Capital|foreign capital inflows]] while maintaining appropriate regulatory oversight.

## Key Points

- The decrees governing the TTTC were issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Government Office of Vietnam), indicating they carry the authority of the central government. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] is the primary instrument addressing financial policies within the TTTC, which would include matters related to capital transfer. [1]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] establishes the legal basis for the TTTC itself, forming the foundational framework within which capital transfer rules operate. [5]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] governs land and environmental policy within the TTTC, which may intersect with asset-based capital considerations. [3]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] covers labour and social security, which may relate to the transfer of income and remuneration for workers operating within the centre. [6]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] addresses entry and exit policies, which may be relevant to the movement of foreign investors and their associated capital activities. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the international arbitration centre, providing [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] relevant to disputes arising from capital transfers. [4]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Financial Policy Decree]] — The decree (Nghị định 324) issued by the Government Office governing financial policies within the TTTC, including those related to capital flows. [1]
- [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|TTTC]] — Trung tâm Tài chính Quốc tế (Vietnam International Financial Centre), the special financial zone established by the Vietnamese Government. [5]
- VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ — The Government Office of Vietnam, the issuing authority for all decrees governing the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Establishing Decree]] — Nghị định 323, the foundational decree establishing the legal existence and framework of the TTTC. [5]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|International Arbitration Centre]] — A body established under the TTTC framework to resolve international disputes, including those related to capital transfers. [4]
