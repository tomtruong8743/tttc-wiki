# Equity Issuance Opportunities in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The [[Vietnam International Financial Centre For Investment Banking Interns|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]] presents a range of opportunities for equity issuance as part of its broader mandate to develop [[Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital markets]] in Vietnam. The centre operates under a framework of government decrees issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], and related instruments, which together establish the legal and financial environment within which [[Equity Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|equity capital market]] activities may take place.

## Key Points

- The TTTC is established and governed through a series of decrees issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Office of the Government of Vietnam), providing the foundational [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal framework]] for all financial activities including equity issuance. [1][5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] governs financial policies within the TTTC, which covers equity issuance conditions, [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|tax and financial incentives]], and [[Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|accounting policy options]] for member institutions. [1][3]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] expressly permits TTTC members to elect to apply [[International Accounting Standards]] (Chuẩn mực quốc tế về kế toán) or [[Generally Accepted Accounting Principles]] (các nguyên tắc kế toán được chấp nhận chung), subject to full and strict compliance with those standards without modification, as authorised under Resolution 222/2025/QH15. [3]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] formally establishes the TTTC and sets out its structure, likely defining the scope for [[Capital Raising In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital raising]] activities including equity offerings (uncertain). [5]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] addresses land and environmental policy within the TTTC, which may affect the physical and operational conditions for institutions involved in equity issuance (uncertain). [3]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] governs labour and social security matters, relevant to staffing of [[Financial Services In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|financial institutions]] conducting equity issuance activities within the centre (uncertain). [6]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] covers immigration policies, relevant to attracting international talent and institutions to participate in [[Equity Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|equity capital markets]] within the TTTC (uncertain). [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, providing [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution]] infrastructure that supports investor confidence in equity markets (uncertain). [4]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] (Nghi_dinh_329) governs the licensing and establishment of member banks and foreign exchange management within the TTTC. Its provisions on prudential ratios — including the [[Capital Movement Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Leverage Ratio (LEV)]], [[Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR)]], and [[Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR)]] — set the capital adequacy environment within which institutions participating in equity issuance must operate. [1][6]
- Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], member banks applying [[International Accounting Standards]] are exempt from requirements to purchase, hold, or invest in Vietnamese Government bonds or government-guaranteed bonds under Vietnamese law. [1]
- Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], member banks are not required to comply with the maximum foreign currency position relative to equity capital as prescribed under Vietnamese law, reducing a constraint relevant to [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]] for institutions active in equity issuance. [1]
- The supervisory authority (Cơ quan giám sát) may, based on inspection and supervisory results, require member banks to comply with more stringent prudential ratios than the standard levels where necessary to ensure safety, with the degree of stringency determined by the nature and level of risk involved. [1]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out a priority order for debt repayment upon dissolution or winding-up of insurance enterprises within the TTTC: (a) wages, severance, [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|social insurance]], health insurance, unemployment insurance, and other labour entitlements under signed contracts; (b) insurance and reinsurance claim payments approved for payout; (c) tax debts; and (d) other debts. [2]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] includes provisions on import duty exemptions for raw materials, supplies, and components, with procedures governed by applicable Vietnamese export and import tax and customs law. [3]
- Governance standards for [[Banking Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|member commercial banks]] under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] include detailed fitness and propriety requirements for senior management, including members of the Members' Council, Supervisory Board, General Director, Deputy General Director, and Chief Accountant. Persons who have been responsible for causing a credit institution to be sanctioned at the highest penalty level for violations relating to licences, governance, shares (*cổ phần, cổ phiếu*), capital contributions, share purchases, credit extension, corporate bond purchases, or prudential ratios are deemed not to meet professional ethics standards. [5][6]
- Members of the Members' Council of a member commercial bank are prohibited from simultaneously holding certain positions, including: executive roles at another member bank or non-member credit institution (except as General Director of the same bank), management or executive roles at other enterprises in Vietnam (except as a manager of the bank's owner), or supervisory/audit roles at other credit institutions or enterprises in Vietnam. [6]

## Definitions

- [[Equity Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Equity issuance]] — the process by which companies raise capital by offering shares to investors, potentially facilitated within the TTTC under its governing decrees (uncertain). [5]
- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|TTTC legal framework]] — the collection of government decrees issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ that establish, regulate, and govern activities within the Vietnam International Financial Centre. [1][5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Financial policies]] — the rules and incentives governing financial activities within the TTTC, issued under Decree 324, including accounting policy options and dissolution priority rules. [1][3]
- [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|International arbitration]] — a mechanism for resolving disputes between parties, supported within the TTTC by a dedicated arbitration centre under [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] (uncertain). [4]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Prudential ratios]] — safety ratios required of member banks under Decree 329, including the Leverage Ratio (LEV), Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR), and Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR), which define the capital framework relevant to equity issuance activities. [1]
- [[International Accounting Standards]] (Chuẩn mực quốc tế về kế toán) — a set of accounting standards that TTTC members may elect to apply in full, as an alternative to Vietnamese accounting standards, under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] and Resolution 222/2025/QH15. [3]
- *Cổ phần / Cổ phiếu* — Vietnamese terms for shares and share certificates respectively, referenced in governance and compliance provisions under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. [5]
