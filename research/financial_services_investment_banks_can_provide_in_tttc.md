# Financial Services Investment Banks Can Provide in TTTC

## Summary

The Trung tâm Tài chính Quốc tế (TTTC), established under [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], is governed by a series of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (Văn phòng Chính phủ) covering areas such as [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]]. The specific [[Financial Services In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|financial services]] that investment banks are permitted to provide within the TTTC are subject to the broader [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] established by these decrees. New source material adds detail on banking prudential requirements, commodities exchange infrastructure, and credit rating standards applicable within the TTTC.

## Key Points

- The decrees governing the TTTC were issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (Văn phòng Chính phủ), indicating high-level governmental authority over the financial centre's [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|regulatory framework]]. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] is the primary instrument likely to govern [[Financial Products And Instruments In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|financial policies]] applicable to investment banks operating within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis for the TTTC, under which investment banking activities would be authorised. [1]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|dispute resolution mechanisms]] as part of the TTTC's institutional framework, which is relevant to investment banking operations. [1]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] contains detailed prudential requirements for member banks operating within the TTTC, including the Leverage Ratio (LEV), [[Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Liquidity Coverage Ratio]] (LCR), and Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR). These are key [[Risk Management Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|risk management]] metrics relevant to investment banks. [5]
- Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], member banks applying international accounting standards (Chuẩn mực quốc tế về kế toán) are exempt from requirements to purchase, hold, or invest in Vietnamese Government bonds or government-guaranteed bonds under Vietnamese law. [5]
- Member banks under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] are not required to comply with the maximum foreign currency position relative to equity capital as prescribed under Vietnamese law, which has significant implications for [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange]] management by investment banks. [5]
- The supervisory authority (Cơ quan giám sát) retains discretion to require member banks to apply stricter prudential ratios than the standard minimums, depending on the nature and level of risk identified through monitoring, inspection, or examination. [5]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] establishes the legal framework for the establishment and operation of a Commodities Exchange (Sở giao dịch hàng hóa) within the TTTC, including licensing (Giấy phép thành lập) and approval for clearing houses (Trung tâm thanh toán bù trừ). Investment banks may participate in or service commodities markets through these structures. [1][2]
- A clearing centre (Trung tâm thanh toán bù trừ) operating within the TTTC Commodities Exchange must obtain formal approval (Giấy chấp thuận) from the relevant governing authority, and must comply with all provisions of [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] and related legislation. [1][2]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] references internationally recognised credit rating agencies — Standard & Poor's, Moody's, and Fitch Ratings — suggesting that international credit ratings are a relevant factor in [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|compliance]] and eligibility assessments within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] and [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] address labour and immigration policies for the TTTC, affecting investment banks' ability to recruit [[Attracting International Financial Talent To Tttc|international talent]]. [1]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] covers land and environmental regulations, which may affect the [[Real Estate And Office Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|physical establishment]] of investment banking offices within the TTTC. [1]

## Definitions

- **[[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]]** — Trung tâm Tài chính Quốc tế, the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]], established by government decree as a specialised financial hub. [1]
- **[[Financial Center Decrees|Decree]]** — A legislative instrument issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (Văn phòng Chính phủ) setting out binding regulations for the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- **[[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|International Arbitration Center]]** — An institutional mechanism established within the TTTC framework for resolving financial and commercial disputes. [1]
- **[[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Financial Policies]]** — The regulatory rules governing financial activities, including those of investment banks, operating within the TTTC. [6]
- **Trung tâm thanh toán bù trừ** — Clearing house; an entity approved to operate as a central counterparty clearing centre within the TTTC's [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Commodities Exchange]]. [1][2]
- **Sở giao dịch hàng hóa** — Commodities Exchange; the organised marketplace for trading commodities and related services established within the TTTC under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]]. [1][2]
- **Chuẩn mực quốc tế về kế toán** — International accounting standards; banks within the TTTC applying these standards benefit from certain regulatory exemptions, including on Vietnamese government bond holdings and foreign currency position limits. [5]
- **Cơ quan giám sát** — Supervisory authority; the body responsible for monitoring, inspecting, and supervising [[Rules For Banks Operating In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|member banks]] within the TTTC. [5]
- **LCR (Liquidity Coverage Ratio)**, **LEV (Leverage Ratio)**, **NSFR (Net Stable Funding Ratio)** — Prudential ratios applicable to member banks under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], consistent with international Basel standards. [5]
