# Financial Services Investment Banks Can Provide in TTTC

## Summary

The Trung tâm Tài chính Quốc tế (TTTC), established under [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], is governed by a series of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (Văn phòng Chính phủ) covering areas such as [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]]. The specific financial services that investment banks are permitted to provide within the TTTC are subject to the broader [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] established by these decrees; however, the source documents available do not contain detailed content beyond their issuing authority.

## Key Points

- The decrees governing the TTTC were issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (Văn phòng Chính phủ), indicating high-level governmental authority over the financial centre's regulatory framework. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] is the primary instrument likely to govern financial policies applicable to investment banks operating within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis for the TTTC, under which investment banking activities would be authorised. [5]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] suggests that dispute resolution mechanisms are part of the TTTC's institutional framework, which may be relevant to investment banking operations. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] and [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] indicate that labour and immigration policies have been specifically addressed for the TTTC, which may affect investment banks' ability to recruit international talent. [2][6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] covers land and environmental regulations within the TTTC, which may affect the physical establishment of investment banking offices. [3]

## Definitions

- **[[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]]** — Trung tâm Tài chính Quốc tế, the Vietnam International Financial Centre, established by government decree as a specialised financial hub. [5]
- **[[Financial Center Decrees|Decree]]** — A legislative instrument issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (Văn phòng Chính phủ) setting out binding regulations for the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- **[[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|International Arbitration Center]]** — An institutional mechanism established within the TTTC framework for resolving financial and commercial disputes. [4]
- **[[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Financial Policies]]** — The regulatory rules governing financial activities, including those of investment banks, operating within the TTTC. [1]
