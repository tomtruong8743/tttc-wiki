# Policies for Foreign Experts in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) establishes a range of policies specifically designed to attract and support foreign experts working within its jurisdiction. These policies span multiple areas including [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]], immigration and entry procedures governed by [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], labour and employment conditions under [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security]], and broader financial incentives outlined in [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]. Together, these frameworks aim to make the TTTC a competitive destination for internationally qualified professionals, complementing the centre's broader goals of attracting global talent and capital as described in the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]].

## Key Points

- Policies for foreign experts in the TTTC are issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), reflecting the highest level of regulatory authority. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] specifically addresses immigration and entry/exit policies applicable within the TTTC, which is directly relevant to foreign experts seeking to live and work there. [2]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] governs labour, employment, and social security matters within the TTTC, covering conditions under which foreign experts may be engaged. [6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out financial policies within the TTTC, which may include provisions relevant to the remuneration and financial arrangements for foreign experts. [1]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] addresses land and environmental matters within the TTTC, which may affect the accommodation and operational conditions available to foreign experts. [3]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis for the TTTC itself, within which all expert-related policies operate. [5]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the international arbitration centre within the TTTC, relevant to dispute resolution for foreign experts and their employers. [4]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] **Immigration Policy Decree (Decree 327)** — The government decree issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam that regulates entry, exit, and immigration policies for participants in the TTTC, including foreign experts. [2]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] **Labour and Social Security Decree (Decree 325)** — The government decree governing labour relations, employment conditions, and social security entitlements within the TTTC, applicable to both domestic and foreign workers and experts. [6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] **Financial Policies Decree (Decree 324)** — The government decree that establishes the financial policy framework within the TTTC, potentially including provisions affecting the financial arrangements of foreign experts. [1]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ (Office of the Government)** — The issuing authority for all decrees establishing the TTTC's regulatory framework, indicating these are top-level government instruments. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] **Establishment Decree (Decree 323)** — The foundational decree creating the legal existence and structure of the TTTC, within which foreign expert policies are situated. [5]
