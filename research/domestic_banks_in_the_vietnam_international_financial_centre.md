# Domestic Banks in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Domestic banks are among the financial institutions operating within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]], established under the legal framework set out by the Vietnamese Government Office. Their roles, activities, and regulations within the TTTC are governed by a series of decrees issued by the [[Legal Documents Establishing The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ)]], including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], and [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], among others. These decrees collectively define the operating environment for all financial institutions, including domestic banks, within the centre.

---

## Key Points

- The source documents related to the TTTC, including those potentially governing domestic bank operations, were issued by the **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** (Government Office of Vietnam). [1]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] forms the foundational legal basis for the establishment of the TTTC, within which domestic banks may operate. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] relates to financial policies within the TTTC, which would likely govern aspects of domestic bank activity. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] addresses labour, employment, and social security matters within the TTTC, relevant to domestic bank staffing and employment obligations. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] covers land and environmental matters within the TTTC, relevant to the physical establishment of domestic bank branches or offices. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] sets out immigration policies within the TTTC, which may affect the hiring of foreign staff by domestic banks. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration framework within the TTTC, which could serve as a [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution mechanism]] for domestic banks. [4]

---

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, established by the Vietnamese Government as a special financial zone. [5]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — The Government Office of Vietnam; the issuing authority for the decrees governing the TTTC. [1]
- [[Domestic Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Domestic Banks]] — Vietnamese-incorporated banking institutions operating within the TTTC framework (uncertain). [5]
