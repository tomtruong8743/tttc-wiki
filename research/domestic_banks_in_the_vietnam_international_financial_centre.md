# Domestic Banks in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Domestic banks are among the financial institutions operating within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]], established under the legal framework set out by the Vietnamese Government Office. Their roles, activities, and regulations within the TTTC are governed by a series of decrees issued by the [[Legal Documents Establishing The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ)]], including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], and [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], among others. These decrees collectively define the operating environment for all financial institutions, including domestic banks, within the centre.

A key distinction exists in [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] between members that are 100% domestically owned (ngân hàng thương mại 100% vốn trong nước) and those that are 100% foreign-owned, with different rules applying to each category — particularly with respect to [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange activities]] and cross-border lending.

## Cross-Border Lending Rules for Domestic Banks

Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|foreign exchange]] rules for borrowing and lending activities differ significantly depending on the ownership structure of the member institution. [2]

### Borrowing from Overseas

- All TTTC members (thành viên), including domestically owned banks, are permitted to borrow foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside the territory of Vietnam. [2]
- Members must comply with information declaration and [[Reporting Obligations For Financial Institutions In Tttc|reporting obligations]] when undertaking such borrowings. [2]

### Lending Overseas

- Members that are **100% foreign-owned** may lend to individuals and organisations outside the territory of Vietnam, subject to information declaration and reporting requirements. [2]
- Members that are **not 100% foreign-owned** — which includes domestically owned banks (ngân hàng thương mại 100% vốn trong nước) — must satisfy additional conditions set out in Article 89 of [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] before lending overseas. [2]

### Conditions for Overseas Lending by Domestically Owned Banks (Article 89)

Domestically owned commercial banks (ngân hàng thương mại 100% vốn trong nước) that are TTTC members are permitted to lend foreign currency overseas to the following categories of borrowers: [2]

1. Organisations established and operating outside the territory of Vietnam in which Vietnamese enterprises (whether TTTC members or not) hold more than 50% of shares or contributed capital. [2]
2. Organisations established and operating outside the territory of Vietnam that are majority-owned (more than 50%) by entities falling within category (1) above. [2]

- Member enterprises (doanh nghiệp thành viên) that are not 100% foreign-owned may also lend to organisations established and operating outside Vietnam in which the lending member or its parent company holds more than 50% of shares or contributed capital. [2]

These rules reflect a [[Foreign Investment Rules In Tttc|graduated approach to foreign investment]] and [[Capital Movement Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital movement]], with domestically owned banks subject to tighter restrictions on outward lending than their fully foreign-owned counterparts.

## Management and Governance Requirements

[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] sets out qualification standards for senior management of domestic commercial bank members of the TTTC. [4]

- **Deputy General Directors (Phó Tổng giám đốc / Phó Giám đốc), Chief Accountants, and equivalent positions** must meet the following requirements: [4]
  - Must not fall within the categories of persons prohibited from holding office under Article 33 of [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. Deputy General Directors must additionally not fall within the prohibitions in Clause 1 of that Article. [4]
  - Must hold at least one of the following qualifications: [4]
    - A university degree or above in finance, banking, economics, business administration, law, accounting, auditing, or another field relevant to their specific role; or
    - A university degree or above in any other field, plus at least **three years of direct working experience** in finance, banking, or the relevant professional field. [4]

These [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|governance requirements]] are designed to ensure that domestic bank members of the TTTC maintain qualified and experienced leadership teams. Senior personnel are also required to reside in Vietnam during their tenure (uncertain — this condition is explicitly stated for certain senior roles and may apply more broadly). [4]

## Interaction with Foreign Banks and Credit Institutions

While domestic banks are subject to specific rules under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], the decree also governs the interaction between foreign-owned TTTC member banks and Vietnamese credit institutions (tổ chức tín dụng Việt Nam). This provides context for how domestic banks engage with foreign counterparts within the TTTC. [3][6-source-329]

For example, 100% foreign-owned commercial banks and foreign bank branches that are TTTC members may: [3][6-source-329]

- **Extend loans** to Vietnamese credit institutions.
- **Provide and receive bank guarantees** from Vietnamese credit institutions under specified conditions, including where the guaranteed party is an individual or organisation on Vietnamese territory, or where the foreign member provides full collateral (ký quỹ đủ 100% giá trị bảo lãnh). [6-source-329]
- **Provide counter-guarantees** (bảo lãnh đối ứng) to Vietnamese credit institutions in cases where the Vietnamese institution issues a guarantee to a member enterprise. [6-source-329]

Additionally, 100% foreign-owned banks and foreign branches that are TTTC members may conduct the following activities with **non-credit institution organisations on Vietnamese territory**: [3]

- Loans, discounting, and bank guarantees.
- Factoring and related services.
- Letter of credit operations and related activities.
- Purchase and sale of corporate bonds issued on Vietnamese territory (excluding bonds issued by Vietnamese credit institutions).
- Purchase and sale of Vietnamese Government bonds, government-guaranteed bonds, and local government bonds, including on the primary market. [3]

This framework shapes the competitive and cooperative landscape between [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign banks]] and domestic banks within the TTTC.
