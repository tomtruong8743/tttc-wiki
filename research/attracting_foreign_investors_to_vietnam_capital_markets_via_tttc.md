# Attracting Foreign Investors to Vietnam Capital Markets via TTTC

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) is supported by a suite of government decrees issued by the [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Office of the Government]], designed to create an attractive environment for foreign investors. These decrees address key areas including [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policies]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], and [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labor and social security]], collectively forming the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] intended to draw foreign capital into [[Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam's capital markets]]. [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] further strengthens the framework by regulating banking licensing, establishment, and [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]] for member institutions, including rules governing cross-border lending and borrowing that are directly relevant to [[Foreign Investor Participation In Vietnam Financial Markets Via Tttc|foreign investor participation]].

## Key Points

- The TTTC was formally established through a decree issued by the Office of the Government (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ), providing the foundational [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] for the financial centre. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] was issued by the Office of the Government to govern financial policies within the TTTC, a key mechanism for attracting foreign investors. [1]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] was issued to regulate entry and exit policies within the TTTC, facilitating the movement of foreign financial professionals and investors. [2]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] was issued to address land and environmental matters within the TTTC, providing regulatory certainty for investors. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] was issued to establish an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, supporting [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] for foreign investors. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] was issued to govern labor, employment, and social security within the TTTC, relevant to foreign institutions establishing operations. [6]
- All foundational decrees were issued by the same authority — the Office of the Government (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ) — indicating a coordinated, top-level policy commitment to the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], 100% foreign-owned commercial banks and foreign bank branches operating as TTTC members are permitted to conduct a broad range of [[Banking Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|banking activities]], including lending, discounting, bank guarantees, factoring, letters of credit, buying and selling corporate bonds issued in Vietnam (excluding those issued by Vietnamese credit institutions), and trading Vietnamese government bonds and government-guaranteed bonds. [S1][S4]
- 100% foreign-owned member institutions are permitted to lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnam's territory, subject to information declaration and reporting requirements, expanding the scope for [[International Capital Flows In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|international capital flows]] through the TTTC. [S2]
- Member institutions not wholly foreign-owned face additional conditions for cross-border lending, including requirements relating to the ownership structure of the borrowing entity, demonstrating a differentiated regulatory approach for domestic versus foreign-owned banks. [S2]
- [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Foreign bank branches and 100% foreign-owned banks]] operating as TTTC members may buy Vietnamese government bonds on the primary market, but are prohibited from selling Vietnamese government bonds, government-guaranteed bonds, or local government bonds issued in foreign currency. [S4]
- 100% domestically-owned Vietnamese commercial banks that are TTTC members may also buy and sell Vietnamese government bonds, including purchases on the primary market, but are similarly prohibited from selling foreign-currency-denominated government or local government bonds. [S3]
- Member institutions (including foreign-owned banks) may conduct foreign currency transactions in accordance with the scope permitted for Vietnamese credit institutions transacting with foreign financial organisations, as determined by the Governor of the State Bank of Vietnam. [S6]
- Foreign-owned bank members may open payment accounts in both Vietnamese dong and foreign currency at non-member commercial banks and foreign bank branches, subject to applicable regulations and the State Bank Governor's rules on Vietnamese dong account use by non-residents. [S6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] includes registration procedures for organisations seeking to operate within the TTTC, including requirements for physical infrastructure disclosure — such as office space, equipment, software, and systems supporting crowdfunding and asset management activities. [S5]

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, established by government decree as a special financial zone designed to attract domestic and foreign investment. [5]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] — The foundational decree issued by the Office of the Government formally establishing the TTTC. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] — A decree issued by the Office of the Government setting out the financial policies applicable within the TTTC, including registration and infrastructure requirements for member organisations. [1][S5]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] — A decree establishing an international arbitration centre within the TTTC to provide [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] for investors. [4]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] — A decree governing immigration and entry/exit policies within the TTTC to facilitate foreign investor and professional access. [2]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] — A decree governing the licensing, establishment, and [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|foreign exchange management]] (quản lý ngoại hối) of banks operating within the TTTC, including rules on cross-border lending and borrowing by member institutions. [S1][S2][S3][S4][S6]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all foundational TTTC decrees. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- **Thành viên** — "Member"; refers to institutions that are formally registered and licensed to operate within the TTTC framework, including both domestic and [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign banks]]. [S2][S3]
- **Ngoại hối** — Foreign exchange; regulated under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] for all TTTC member institutions. See also [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc]]. [S2][S6]
