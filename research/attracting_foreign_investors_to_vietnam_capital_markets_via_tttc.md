# Attracting Foreign Investors to Vietnam Capital Markets via TTTC

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) is supported by a suite of government decrees issued by the [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Office of the Government]], designed to create an attractive environment for foreign investors. These decrees address key areas including [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policies]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], and [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labor and social security]], collectively forming the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] intended to draw foreign capital into [[Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam's capital markets]].

## Key Points

- The TTTC was formally established through a decree issued by the Office of the Government (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ), providing the foundational [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] for the financial centre. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] was issued by the Office of the Government to govern financial policies within the TTTC, a key mechanism for attracting foreign investors. [1]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] was issued to regulate entry and exit policies within the TTTC, facilitating the movement of foreign financial professionals and investors. [2]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] was issued to address land and environmental matters within the TTTC, providing regulatory certainty for investors. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] was issued to establish an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, supporting [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] for foreign investors. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] was issued to govern labor, employment, and social security within the TTTC, relevant to foreign institutions establishing operations. [6]
- All foundational decrees were issued by the same authority — the Office of the Government (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ) — indicating a coordinated, top-level policy commitment to the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, established by government decree as a special financial zone designed to attract domestic and foreign investment. [5]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] — The foundational decree issued by the Office of the Government formally establishing the TTTC. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] — A decree issued by the Office of the Government setting out the financial policies applicable within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] — A decree establishing an international arbitration centre within the TTTC to provide [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] for investors. [4]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] — A decree governing immigration and entry/exit policies within the TTTC to facilitate foreign investor and professional access. [2]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all foundational TTTC decrees. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
