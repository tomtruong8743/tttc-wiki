# Role of Foreign Financial Institutions in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) was established through a series of government decrees issued by the [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Vietnamese Government]], creating a specialised legal and regulatory environment designed to attract both domestic and international financial participants. The role of foreign financial institutions within the TTTC is shaped by the broader [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]], including policies covering [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]], [[Currency And Capital Controls]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration and visa arrangements]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land use]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international dispute resolution]].

---

## Key Points

- The TTTC was established under a framework of decrees issued by the **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** (Government Office of Vietnam), providing the foundational legal basis within which foreign financial institutions may operate. [1][5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out financial policies applicable within the TTTC, which are relevant to the operating conditions for foreign financial institutions. [1]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] establishes immigration and entry/exit policies within the TTTC, which directly affects the ability of foreign institutions to deploy international personnel. [2]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] governs land and environmental regulations within the TTTC, affecting where and how foreign financial institutions may establish physical operations. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] provides for an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, offering foreign financial institutions access to internationally recognised [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]]. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] covers labour, employment, and social security policies applicable within the TTTC, governing how foreign institutions may hire and manage both local and foreign staff. [6]

---

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] — **Trung tâm Tài chính Quốc tế** (Vietnam International Financial Centre); the special financial zone established by the Vietnamese Government through a series of decrees. [5]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] — The foundational decree establishing the TTTC, issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] — The decree governing financial policies within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] — The decree governing entry, exit, and immigration policies within the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]] — The decree governing land use and environmental regulations within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] — The decree establishing the International Arbitration Centre within the TTTC. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]] — The decree covering labour, employment, and social security within the TTTC. [6]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Government Office of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all TTTC-related decrees referenced in the source documents. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
