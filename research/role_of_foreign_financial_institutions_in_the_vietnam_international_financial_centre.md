# Role of Foreign Financial Institutions in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) was established through a series of government decrees issued by the [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Vietnamese Government]], creating a specialised legal and regulatory environment designed to attract both domestic and international financial participants. The role of foreign financial institutions within the TTTC is shaped by the broader [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]], including policies covering [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]], [[Currency And Capital Controls]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration and visa arrangements]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land use]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international dispute resolution]]. [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] provides detailed rules on banking licensing, establishment, and [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]], directly shaping what foreign banks and financial institutions may do within the TTTC.

---

## Key Points

- The TTTC was established under a framework of decrees issued by the **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** (Government Office of Vietnam), providing the foundational legal basis within which foreign financial institutions may operate. [1][5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out financial policies applicable within the TTTC, which are relevant to the operating conditions for foreign financial institutions, including rules governing the dissolution and priority of debt repayment for insurance enterprises operating within the TTTC. [1][3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] establishes immigration and entry/exit policies within the TTTC, which directly affects the ability of foreign institutions to deploy international personnel. [2]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] governs land and environmental regulations within the TTTC, affecting where and how foreign financial institutions may establish physical operations. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] provides for an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, offering foreign financial institutions access to internationally recognised [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]]. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] covers labour, employment, and social security policies applicable within the TTTC, governing how foreign institutions may hire and manage both local and foreign staff. [6]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] sets out detailed rules for [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign banks]] and [[Banking Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|banking activities]] within the TTTC, including licensing, establishment, governance standards, and [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]] for member institutions. [1][2][4]

### Activities Permitted for 100% Foreign-Owned Banks and Foreign Bank Branches

Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], 100% foreign-owned commercial banks and foreign bank branches that are TTTC members are permitted to conduct the following activities with organisations on Vietnamese territory that are not Vietnamese credit institutions: [2]

- Lending (**cho vay**)
- Discounting (**chiết khấu**)
- Bank guarantees (**bảo lãnh ngân hàng**)
- Factoring and related services (**bao thanh toán**)
- Letter of credit operations and related business activities
- Buying and selling corporate bonds issued on Vietnamese territory (excluding bonds issued by Vietnamese credit institutions)
- Buying and selling Vietnamese Government bonds, government-guaranteed bonds, and local government bonds, including purchases on the primary market [2]
- Debt purchase (**mua nợ**) [2]

These permitted activities are relevant to [[Capital Market Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], [[Debt Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], and [[Bond Issuance Opportunities In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].

### Foreign Exchange Rules for Borrowing and Lending by Members

[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] establishes specific [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management rules]] governing the borrowing and lending activities of TTTC members: [1]

- **Borrowing from abroad:** TTTC members may borrow foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to information declaration and [[Reporting Obligations For Financial Institutions In Tttc|reporting obligations]]. [1]
- **Lending abroad — 100% foreign-owned members:** Members wholly owned by foreign investors (100% foreign capital) are permitted to lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to information declaration and reporting requirements. [1]
- **Lending abroad — non-100% foreign-owned members:** Members not wholly owned by foreign investors must comply with specific conditions for overseas lending as set out in Decree 329, and must also fulfil information declaration and reporting obligations. [1]
- **100% domestically-owned commercial banks lending abroad:** A 100% domestically-funded commercial bank member may lend in foreign currency to: (a) organisations established and operating outside Vietnamese territory in which Vietnamese enterprises (members or non-members) hold more than 50% of shares or contributed capital; and (b) organisations established and operating outside Vietnamese territory that are themselves majority-owned by such qualifying organisations. [1]
- **Non-bank member enterprises lending abroad:** Member enterprises (not banks) may lend to organisations established and operating outside Vietnamese territory in which the lending member or its parent company holds more than 50% of shares or contributed capital. [1]

These rules are relevant to [[Capital Movement Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], [[Cross Border Payments In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], [[International Capital Flows In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], and [[Transferring Capital Into And Out Of The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].

### Governance and Personnel Requirements for Foreign Bank Members

[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] sets qualification and residency requirements for senior management of member commercial banks, including foreign-owned banks: [4]

- Deputy General Directors (Deputy Directors), Chief Accountants, and equivalent positions must meet standards including: not being disqualified from holding office under relevant articles of Decree 329; holding a university degree or above in finance, banking, economics, business administration, law, accounting, auditing, or another relevant specialist field; or holding a university degree in another field combined with at least three years of direct working experience in finance, banking, or the relevant specialist area. [4]
- General Directors (Directors) of member commercial banks must reside in Vietnam during their tenure. [4]

These requirements are relevant to [[Attracting International Financial Talent To Tttc]], [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], and [[Policies For Foreign Experts In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].

### Commodities Exchange and Foreign Institution Participation

Under [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]], the Managing Authority (**Cơ quan điều hành**) in each city is responsible for approving the list of foreign commodities exchange contracts eligible for linked trading within the TTTC. [5] Tax and customs incentives applicable to commodities activities are governed by [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]. [5] These provisions are relevant to [[Financial Products And Instruments In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Financial Services In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].
