# Investment Banking Terms in TTTC Documents

## Summary

The TTTC (Trung tâm Tài chính) documents establish the legal and regulatory framework for the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]], covering a range of subject areas relevant to investment banking practice. These include [[Financial Center Decrees|foundational decrees]] on establishment, [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]], [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|labour and employment]], [[Land Use Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|land and environment]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration]], and [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|international arbitration]]. All six known decrees were issued by the same authority, the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn phòng Chính phủ), signalling a coordinated, top-down legislative approach to building the centre.

## Key Points

- All TTTC decrees were issued by **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** (the Office of the Government of Vietnam), indicating centralised government authority over the financial centre's establishment. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] governs the **establishment** (thành lập) of the TTTC. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] covers **financial policies** (chính sách tài chính) within the TTTC, likely including [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|tax incentives]] and related fiscal measures. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|Decree 325]] addresses **labour, employment, and social security** (lao động, việc làm và an sinh xã hội) within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]] covers **land and environment** (đất đai và môi trường) policy within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] sets out **immigration and entry/exit policies** (chính sách xuất nhập cảnh) for the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] establishes the **International Arbitration Centre** (Trung tâm Trọng tài Quốc tế) within the TTTC. [4]
- The six decrees together form the primary [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal framework]] of the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|**Thành lập TTTC**]] — Establishment of the Vietnam International Financial Centre; the foundational act creating the centre. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|**Chính sách tài chính**]] — Financial policies; the regulatory rules governing fiscal and financial matters within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|**Lao động, việc làm và an sinh xã hội**]] — Labour, employment, and social security; policies governing workers and social protections within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|**Đất đai và môi trường**]] — Land and environment; policies covering land use rights and environmental obligations within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|**Xuất nhập cảnh**]] — Immigration (entry and exit); policies controlling the movement of foreign nationals into and out of the TTTC zone. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|**Trung tâm Trọng tài Quốc tế**]] — International Arbitration Centre; the dedicated dispute resolution body established within the TTTC. [4]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all six TTTC decrees. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
