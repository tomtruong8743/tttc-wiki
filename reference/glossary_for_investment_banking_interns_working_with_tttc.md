# Glossary for Investment Banking Interns Working with TTTC

## Summary

This glossary provides investment banking interns with definitions of key terms encountered when working with the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC (Vietnam International Financial Centre)]]. The TTTC is governed by a suite of foundational decrees, including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], all issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn phòng Chính phủ). Understanding the terminology embedded in these [[Legal Documents Establishing The Vietnam International Financial Centre|legal documents]] is essential for interns supporting deal teams, compliance functions, and client advisory work within the TTTC framework.

## Key Points

- All six foundational decrees governing the TTTC were issued by the same authority: **Văn phòng Chính phủ** (the Office of the Government of Vietnam) [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] is the primary decree establishing the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|TTTC]] as a legal and institutional entity [5].
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] governs [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]] applicable within the TTTC, which are directly relevant to [[Investment Banking Opportunities In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|investment banking activities]] [1].
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] covers [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|labor, employment, and social security]] matters affecting staff working inside the TTTC [6].
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] addresses [[Land Use Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|land use and environmental regulations]] within the TTTC zone [3].
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] sets out [[Immigration And Residency Policies For Tttc Workers|immigration and entry/exit policies]] for personnel operating in the TTTC [2].
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the framework for [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|international arbitration]] within the TTTC [4].
- All decrees form part of the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]], which underpins the [[Financial Center Decrees|regulatory architecture]] of the centre [1][2][3][4][5][6].

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|**TTTC**]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre; the special-purpose financial zone established and governed by a series of government decrees [5].
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|**Decree 323**]] — The founding decree establishing the TTTC as an institution [5].
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|**Decree 324**]] — The decree governing financial policies within the TTTC, relevant to [[Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|tax incentives]], [[Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital markets]], and related matters [1].
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|**Decree 325**]] — The decree covering labor, employment, and social security regulations applicable to workers in the TTTC [6].
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|**Decree 326**]] — The decree addressing land use and environmental policy within the TTTC zone [3].
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|**Decree 327**]] — The decree setting immigration and entry/exit policies for individuals working in or visiting the TTTC [2].
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|**Decree 328**]] — The decree establishing the international arbitration centre associated with the TTTC, relevant to [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|dispute resolution]] [4].
- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|**Văn phòng Chính phủ**]] — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all foundational TTTC decrees [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- [[Financial Center Decrees|**Foundational Decrees**]] — The collective set of government decrees (323–328) that together establish and regulate the TTTC's legal, financial, labor, land, immigration, and arbitration frameworks [1][2][3][4][5][6].
