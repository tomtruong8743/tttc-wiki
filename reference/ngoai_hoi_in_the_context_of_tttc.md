# Ngoai Hoi in the Context of TTTC

## Summary

**Ngoai hoi** (foreign exchange) is a key operational and regulatory concept within the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]]. The governance framework of the TTTC is established through a series of decrees issued by the [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ)]], including [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], and related instruments. Foreign exchange rules and policies fall within this broader legislative framework governing the TTTC's financial operations.

## Key Points

- The decrees governing the TTTC, including those relevant to ngoai hoi (foreign exchange), were issued by the **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** (Office of the Government of Vietnam). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] is among the key instruments likely to cover [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|financial and foreign exchange policies]] within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis under which ngoai hoi-related activities are regulated within the TTTC. [5]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] and [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] form part of the broader regulatory environment that intersects with foreign exchange, particularly regarding remittances and cross-border payments for foreign workers. [2][6] (uncertain)
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] provides a dispute resolution framework that may be relevant to ngoai hoi-related disputes. [4] (uncertain)
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] is part of the overall decree package establishing the TTTC's operating environment. [3]

## Definitions

- [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|**Ngoai hoi**]] — Vietnamese term for "foreign exchange," referring to transactions, holdings, and regulations involving foreign currencies within the TTTC framework. [1]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|**Nghị định 324**]] — Decree issued by the Office of the Government covering financial policies within the TTTC, likely including foreign exchange provisions. [1]
- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|**TTTC**]] — Abbreviation for *Trung Tâm Tài Chính*, the Vietnam International Financial Centre, the special financial zone to which these decrees apply. [5]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all TTTC-related decrees referenced in the source documents. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
