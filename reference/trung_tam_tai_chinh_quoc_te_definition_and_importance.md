# Trung Tam Tai Chinh Quoc Te Definition and Importance

## Summary

The **Trung Tâm Tài Chính Quốc Tế** (TTTC), or Vietnam International Financial Centre, is a specialised financial zone established by the Government of Vietnam through a series of official decrees issued by the [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ)]]. The TTTC is governed by a comprehensive [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal framework]] comprising multiple decrees covering its establishment, [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policies]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration and entry/exit policies]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]]. These instruments collectively define the structure, scope, and regulatory environment of the TTTC.

---

## Key Points

- The TTTC was established through [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]], issued by the Văn Phòng Chính Phủ (Office of the Government of Vietnam). [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] sets out the financial policies applicable within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]] governs labour, employment, and [[Tax Benefits For Workers And Experts In Tttc|social security]] arrangements within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]] addresses land use and environmental regulations within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] establishes policies related to immigration and entry/exit (xuất nhập cảnh) for those associated with the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] provides for an international arbitration centre (Trung tâm trọng tài quốc tế) operating within the TTTC. [4]
- All six foundational decrees were issued by the same authority: the Văn Phòng Chính Phủ (Office of the Government). [1][2][3][4][5][6]

---

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|**Trung Tâm Tài Chính Quốc Tế (TTTC)**]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre; a specialised financial zone established by the Vietnamese government. [5]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|**Nghị định (Decree)**]] — An official legal instrument issued by the Government of Vietnam used to establish and regulate the TTTC and its component policies. [5]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority (cơ quan phát hành) for all foundational TTTC decrees. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|**Trung tâm trọng tài quốc tế**]] — International arbitration centre; a dispute resolution body established within the TTTC framework under Decree 328. [4]
- **Xuất nhập cảnh** — Immigration and entry/exit; the subject of [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]], governing movement of persons in relation to the TTTC. [2]
- **An sinh xã hội** — Social security; addressed alongside labour and employment matters in [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]]. [6]
