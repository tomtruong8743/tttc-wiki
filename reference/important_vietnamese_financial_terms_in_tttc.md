# Important Vietnamese Financial Terms in TTTC

## Summary

This page covers key Vietnamese financial and legal terminology found across the official decrees establishing and governing the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]]. The decrees, issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), address a range of subjects including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment of the TTTC]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policies]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]]. Understanding the Vietnamese terminology used in these foundational documents is essential for navigating the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal framework of the TTTC]].

---

## Key Points

- The decrees governing the TTTC are issued by **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** (the Office of the Government of Vietnam), the central authority responsible for publishing official government regulations [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- **Nghị định** (Decree) is the primary legal instrument used to establish and regulate the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]], with at least six separate decrees identified across the source documents [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- The decree on financial policies (**chính sách tài chính**) governs [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|tax incentives and financial rules]] within the TTTC [1].
- The decree on immigration (**xuất nhập cảnh**) addresses entry and exit policies for participants in the TTTC, relevant to [[Immigration And Residency Policies For Tttc Workers|immigration and residency policies]] [2].
- The decree on land and environment (**đất đai và môi trường**) covers [[Land Use Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|land use]] and [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|environmental regulations]] within the TTTC [3].
- The decree on the international arbitration centre (**Trung tâm trọng tài quốc tế**) establishes the framework for [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|arbitration]] and [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|dispute resolution]] in the TTTC [4].
- The founding decree (**thành lập TTTC**) formally establishes the financial centre itself [5].
- The decree on labour, employment, and social security (**lao động, việc làm và an sinh xã hội**) governs [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|labour and employment rules]] for those operating within the TTTC [6].

---

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|**TTTC**]] — Abbreviation for *Trung tâm Tài chính*, meaning "Financial Centre"; used to refer to the Vietnam International Financial Centre [5].
- **Nghị định** — "Decree"; the form of legal instrument issued by the Vietnamese Government to establish binding regulations. The primary [[Legal Documents Establishing The Vietnam International Financial Centre|legal documents]] of the TTTC take this form [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — "Office of the Government"; the issuing authority for all TTTC-related decrees [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|**Chính sách tài chính**]] — "Financial policies"; refers to the financial rules, [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|tax incentives]], and related measures applicable within the TTTC [1].
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|**Xuất nhập cảnh**]] — "Immigration" (literally "exit and entry"); used in the context of policies governing the movement of foreign workers and experts into and out of the TTTC [2].
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|**Đất đai và môi trường**]] — "Land and environment"; the subject matter of the decree covering [[Land Use Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|land use policies]] and [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|environmental regulations]] in the TTTC [3].
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|**Trung tâm trọng tài quốc tế**]] — "International Arbitration Centre"; the body established within the TTTC framework to handle [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|international arbitration]] and [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|dispute resolution]] [4].
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|**Thành lập**]] — "Establishment" or "founding"; as in *thành lập TTTC*, meaning the formal establishment of the financial centre [5].
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|**Lao động, việc làm và an sinh xã hội**]] — "Labour, employment, and social security"; the subject of the decree governing [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|workforce]] and social security matters within the TTTC [6].
