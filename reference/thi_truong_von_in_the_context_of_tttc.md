# Thi Truong Von in the Context of TTTC

## Summary

**Thi Truong Von** (capital market) is a foundational concept within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]], which was established through a series of government decrees issued by the [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (Office of the Government)]]. The TTTC framework encompasses a range of [[Financial Services In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|financial services]] and [[Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital market activities]], governed by interlocking policies covering [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial and tax policy]], [[Land Use Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|land and environment]], [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|labour and social security]], [[Immigration And Residency Policies For Tttc Workers|immigration]], and [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|international arbitration]].

---

## Key Points

- The TTTC was established through a decree issued by the VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (Office of the Government of Vietnam), providing the foundational legal basis for all capital market and financial centre activities. [1][5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] sets out the financial policies governing [[Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital market]] operations within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] addresses land and environmental policies relevant to the physical infrastructure supporting [[Thi Truong Von]] activities in the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] provides the immigration and entry/exit policy framework, relevant to attracting international capital market participants and talent to the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the international arbitration centre within the TTTC, providing [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|dispute resolution]] mechanisms for capital market participants. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] governs labour, employment, and social security conditions for workers operating within the TTTC's capital market ecosystem. [6]
- All decrees were issued by the same issuing authority — VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ — indicating a coordinated, whole-of-government approach to establishing the TTTC's [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|legal and regulatory framework]]. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

---

## Definitions

- [[Thi Truong Von]] — **Thi Truong Von** — Vietnamese term for "capital market"; refers to the market in which medium- and long-term financial instruments (such as equities and bonds) are issued and traded. In the TTTC context, it is the central subject of the centre's financial activities. [1][5]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] — **Nghị định 323** — The foundational decree establishing the TTTC (Trung Tâm Tài Chính), issued by the VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] — **Nghị định 324** — The decree setting out financial policies within the TTTC, directly relevant to capital market regulation. [1]
- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] — **Khung pháp lý TTTC** — The collective set of decrees and regulations issued by the Vietnamese Government to govern operations within the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all decrees establishing and governing the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
