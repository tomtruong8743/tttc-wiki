# Phong Chong Rua Tien in the Context of TTTC

## Summary

- [[Phong Chong Rua Tien]] — Vietnamese term for "anti-money laundering" (AML); refers to the set of laws, regulations, and procedures designed to prevent the practice of generating income through illegal actions. Not explicitly defined in the source documents. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
