# Bilingual Glossary of Key TTTC Terms

## Summary

This page provides a bilingual (Vietnamese–English) glossary of key terms used in the legal and regulatory framework of the Vietnam International Financial Centre ([[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|TTTC]]). The terms are drawn from the suite of founding decrees issued by the Government Office (Văn phòng Chính phủ), including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]]. Understanding the Vietnamese terminology used in these official documents is essential for foreign financial institutions, legal professionals, and investors seeking to operate within the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]].

---

## Key Points

- All six founding decrees of the TTTC were issued by **Văn phòng Chính phủ** (the Government Office of Vietnam), establishing the authoritative Vietnamese-language source for all key terms. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- The decree governing the establishment of the TTTC is formally titled using the term **TTTC** as an abbreviation in official Vietnamese government documents, as seen in [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]]. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] uses Vietnamese terminology related to financial policies (**chính sách tài chính**) within the centre. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] covers terminology relating to labour, employment, and social security (**lao động, việc làm và an sinh xã hội**) inside the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] introduces terms related to land and the environment (**đất đai và môi trường**) as they apply within the TTTC zone. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] governs terminology associated with immigration and entry/exit policies (**chính sách xuất nhập cảnh**) for those working in or visiting the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the terminology used for the international arbitration centre (**Trung tâm trọng tài quốc tế**) operating within the TTTC. [4]

---

## Definitions

- **[[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|TTTC]]** (Trung tâm Tài chính Thành phố / Trung tâm Tài chính Quốc tế) — The abbreviated Vietnamese name for the Vietnam International Financial Centre, as used across all founding decrees. [5]
- **[[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Nghị định 323]]** — Decree 323 on the establishment of the TTTC; the foundational legal instrument creating the centre. [5]
- **[[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Nghị định 324]]** — Decree 324 governing financial policies (**chính sách tài chính**) within the TTTC. [1]
- **[[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Nghị định 325]]** — Decree 325 on labour, employment, and social security (**lao động, việc làm và an sinh xã hội**) within the TTTC. [6]
- **[[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Nghị định 326]]** — Decree 326 on land and environmental policies (**đất đai và môi trường**) applicable within the TTTC. [3]
- **[[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Nghị định 327]]** — Decree 327 on immigration and entry/exit policies (**chính sách xuất nhập cảnh**) for the TTTC. [2]
- **[[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Nghị định 328]]** — Decree 328 establishing the international arbitration centre (**Trung tâm trọng tài quốc tế**) within the TTTC. [4]
- **[[Financial Center Decrees|Văn phòng Chính phủ]]** — The Government Office of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all TTTC founding decrees. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- **Chính sách tài chính** — Financial policies; the subject of [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]. [1]
- **Lao động, việc làm và an sinh xã hội** — Labour, employment, and social security; the subject of [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]]. [6]
- **Đất đai và môi trường** — Land and environment; the subject of [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]]. [3]
- **Chính sách xuất nhập cảnh** — Immigration and entry/exit policies; the subject of [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]]. [2]
- **Trung tâm trọng tài quốc tế** — International Arbitration Centre; the institution established under [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]]. [4]
