# Vietnamese Legal and Financial Terms in TTTC Defined in English

## Summary

The decrees establishing and governing the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) are issued in Vietnamese and contain a range of specialised legal and financial terminology. Understanding these terms is essential for foreign investors, financial institutions, and professionals seeking to operate within the TTTC framework. The key source documents — [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] — were all issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn phòng Chính phủ) and collectively form the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]].

## Key Points

- All six core TTTC decrees were issued by **Văn phòng Chính phủ** (the Office of the Government of Vietnam), the body responsible for publishing official government legislation [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- The decrees collectively cover the establishment, [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]], [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], [[Land Use Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], [[Immigration And Residency Policies For Tttc Workers]], and [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] within the TTTC [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- Each decree is a primary [[Legal Documents Establishing The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and forms part of the [[Main Decrees Related To The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- The official issuing authority — **Văn phòng Chính phủ** — is Vietnam's central government office responsible for coordinating and publishing legislative instruments [1][2][3][4][5][6].

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] **Nghị định 323** — The decree concerning the establishment of the TTTC (Trung tâm tài chính), issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam [5].
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] **Nghị định 324** — The decree governing financial policies within the TTTC, covering matters such as [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]] and related fiscal arrangements [1].
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security]] **Nghị định 325** — The decree addressing labour, employment, and social security (an sinh xã hội) within the TTTC [6].
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] **Nghị định 326** — The decree covering land (đất đai) and environmental (môi trường) policies applicable within the TTTC [3].
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] **Nghị định 327** — The decree setting out immigration and entry/exit (xuất nhập cảnh) policies for persons working or operating within the TTTC [2].
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] **Nghị định 328** — The decree establishing and regulating the International Arbitration Centre (Trung tâm trọng tài quốc tế) associated with the TTTC [4].
- **Văn phòng Chính phủ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the official body responsible for issuing and publishing government decrees and legislative instruments [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- **TTTC** — Abbreviation for *Trung tâm tài chính*, meaning "Financial Centre"; refers to the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] established under these decrees [5].
- **Nghị định** — Vietnamese term for "Decree"; a binding legal instrument issued by the Government of Vietnam to implement laws and policies [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- **An sinh xã hội** — Social security; referenced in the context of [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] governing worker welfare within the TTTC [6].
- **Xuất nhập cảnh** — Immigration; entry and exit; the subject matter of [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] governing movement of foreign nationals into and out of the TTTC [2].
- **Trọng tài quốc tế** — International arbitration; the mechanism for resolving cross-border commercial disputes, governed by [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] within the TTTC [4].
- **Đất đai** — Land; refers to land use and land rights policies regulated under [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] [3].
- **Môi trường** — Environment; refers to environmental regulations applicable within the TTTC under [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] [3].
