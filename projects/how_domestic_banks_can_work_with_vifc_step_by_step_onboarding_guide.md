# How Domestic Banks Can Work With VIFC — Step by Step Onboarding Guide

## Summary

This guide covers how Vietnamese domestically-owned commercial banks ([[Domestic Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|ngân hàng thương mại 100% vốn trong nước]]) can become member institutions of the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|Vietnam International Financial Centre (VIFC/TTTC)]]. The relevant legal framework is established primarily under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], which governs licensing, establishment, permitted activities, foreign exchange management, and prudential requirements for member banks operating within the Centre. Domestic banks that join VIFC gain access to a specialised operating environment with distinct rules on [[Banking Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|permitted banking activities]], [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange transactions]], and [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|compliance obligations]] that differ in important ways from standard Vietnamese banking law.

---

## 1. Overview

**Type of organisation:** Vietnamese commercial banks with 100% domestic capital (ngân hàng thương mại 100% vốn trong nước) seeking to establish a member presence within VIFC.

**Why VIFC is relevant:** VIFC operates as a special financial zone within Vietnam where member banks can conduct a broader range of activities — including certain cross-border foreign currency transactions, bond trading, interest rate derivatives, and advisory services — than would typically be available under standard Vietnamese banking regulation. [[Domestic Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Domestic member banks]] operate under the supervision of the City-level Regulatory Authority (Cơ quan điều hành tại thành phố) and the Supervisory Authority (Cơ quan giám sát), rather than solely under the State Bank of Vietnam's standard framework. See also [[Role Of Domestic Banks And Securities Firms In Tttc]] and [[Domestic Vs Foreign Institutions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].

## 2. Requirements from the Organisation

### Licensing Authority
- All licences (Giấy phép) are issued by the **City-level Regulatory Authority** (Cơ quan điều hành tại thành phố), not directly by the State Bank of Vietnam, per [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. [7]

### Licence Application Documents and Procedures
- The specific documentation (hồ sơ) and procedures for the first-time licence application are to be carried out **in accordance with the guidance of the Regulatory Authority** (Cơ quan điều hành). [7]
- Exact document checklists are not enumerated in the source excerpts; applicants must follow the Regulatory Authority's published guidance (uncertain). [7]

### Licence Issuance Timeline
- Within **120 days** from the date of receipt of a complete and valid application dossier, the City-level Regulatory Authority shall either grant or refuse the establishment and operation licence for the member bank. [7]

### Key Personnel Requirements
- **Deputy General Director (Phó Tổng giám đốc / Phó Giám đốc), Chief Accountant, and equivalent positions** must satisfy the following conditions: [4]
  - Must not fall within the categories of persons prohibited from holding such positions under Article 33 of [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]; [4]
  - Must hold a **university degree or higher** in finance, banking, economics, business administration, law, accounting, auditing, or another relevant field; **or** hold a university degree in any other field plus at least **3 years of direct working experience** in finance, banking, or the relevant specialised field. [4]
  - Must be **resident in Vietnam** during their term of office. [4]

### Capital Requirements
- Specific minimum charter capital (vốn điều lệ) amounts for domestic member banks are **not covered in the source documents**. See [[Rules For Banks Operating In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] for further detail.

### Prudential / Safety Ratios
- Member banks must maintain the **liquidity coverage ratio** (tỷ lệ khả năng chi trả) on a daily basis. [8]
- Banks that do **not** apply International Accounting Standards (IAS/IFRS) are subject to existing State Bank of Vietnam Governor regulations on safety limits for credit extended to invest in or trade equities and corporate bonds. [8]
- Banks that **do** apply International Accounting Standards must comply with conditions and limits set by the owner/parent bank's policies for credit extended to invest in or trade equities and corporate bonds. [8]

## 3. Step-by-Step Process

> **Note:** The source documents provide partial procedural information. Steps below are derived directly from [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]; certain intermediate steps are not covered in full detail in the source excerpts.

**Step 1 — Confirm Eligibility**
Confirm that the bank qualifies as a Vietnamese commercial bank with 100% domestic capital (ngân hàng thương mại 100% vốn trong nước) and that its proposed activities fall within the scope permitted for domestic member banks under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. [5][6]

**Step 2 — Obtain Guidance from the Regulatory Authority**
Contact the City-level Regulatory Authority (Cơ quan điều hành tại thành phố) to obtain the official guidance on the required application dossier (hồ sơ) and procedures for a first-time licence. Per Article 15 of [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], procedures for first-time licence issuance are conducted in accordance with the Regulatory Authority's guidance. [7]

**Step 3 — Prepare and Submit Application Dossier**
Prepare the complete application dossier as specified by the Regulatory Authority's guidance. Submit the complete and valid dossier to the Regulatory Authority. [7]

**Step 4 — Await Licensing Decision**
The Regulatory Authority has **120 days** from receipt of a complete and valid dossier to issue or refuse the Licence (Giấy phép). [7]

**Step 5 — Appoint and Obtain Approval for Key Personnel**
Appoint qualified individuals to senior management positions. Ensure Deputy General Directors, Chief Accountant, and equivalent roles satisfy the qualifications under Article 30 and related articles of [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], including academic qualifications and residency requirements. [4]

**Step 6 — Commence Operations Within Licensed Scope**
Once the Licence is granted, the bank may commence the permitted activities as specified in the Licence issued by the City-level Regulatory Authority. Activities must remain strictly within the scope set out in the Licence. [6]

**Step 7 — Establish Ongoing Compliance and Reporting Mechanisms**
Implement daily liquidity reporting, maintain prudential ratios, and ensure all transactions comply with applicable [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange rules]] and [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|compliance requirements]] under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. [8]

## 4. Key Decrees and Legal Basis

- **[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]** — The primary decree governing the licensing, establishment, permitted activities, foreign exchange management, personnel requirements, and prudential obligations for all member banks (including domestic commercial banks) within VIFC. Key provisions referenced include: [7]
  - **Article 15** — Documentation and procedures for licence issuance [7]
  - **Article 16** — 120-day timeline for licence decision [7]
  - **Article 30** — Standards and conditions for General Director/Director and equivalent senior managers [4]
  - **Article 33** — Prohibited persons for senior management positions [4]
  - **Articles 43–54** — Permitted activities for domestic member banks and their interactions with foreign banks, Vietnamese credit institutions, and member enterprises [5][6][9]
  - **Article 66** — Liquidity coverage ratio compliance [8]
  - **Article 68** — Conditions and limits on credit for equity and bond investment/trading [8]

- **[[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]]** — Establishes the legal basis for the VIFC itself.
- **[[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]** — Sets out financial policies applicable within TTTC, including [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]].
