# Regulatory Due Diligence Questions for the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Regulatory due diligence for the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) involves systematically examining the legal and policy framework established by the Vietnamese government to understand the obligations, rights, and risks facing financial institutions operating within the centre. The TTTC is governed by a suite of decrees issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), covering areas such as [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policy]], [[Land And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|land and environment]], [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|labour and employment]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], and the [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment of the centre itself]]. Conducting thorough regulatory due diligence across these decree areas is essential for any institution evaluating entry into the TTTC.

## Key Points

- The TTTC is established and governed by decrees issued by **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** (the Office of the Government of Vietnam), indicating that the regulatory framework originates at the highest levels of Vietnamese executive authority. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] is the foundational legal instrument establishing the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]], and should be the starting point for any regulatory due diligence exercise. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] governs [[Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|financial policies]] within the TTTC, making it a primary document for institutions assessing [[Tax Benefits For Companies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|tax incentives]], [[Capital Movement Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital movement rules]], and related financial regulatory questions. [1]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] covers [[Immigration And Residency Policies For Tttc Workers|immigration and entry/exit policies]] relevant to foreign experts and workers in the TTTC, a key area for firms planning to deploy international staff. [2]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] addresses [[Land Use Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|land and environmental regulations]], which are relevant for institutions considering [[Real Estate And Office Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|establishing a physical presence]] in the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the framework for [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|international arbitration]] within the TTTC, a critical consideration for [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|dispute resolution due diligence]]. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] governs [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|labour, employment, and social security]] matters within the TTTC, relevant to hiring practices and workforce compliance. [6]
- All six decrees identified in the source documents were issued by the same government authority (**Văn Phòng Chính Phủ**), suggesting a coordinated and unified regulatory regime across the TTTC's operational areas. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|**Decree 323**]] — The foundational decree establishing the Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC), issued by Văn Phòng Chính Phủ. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|**Decree 324**]] — The decree governing financial policies within the TTTC, including tax, capital, and financial regulatory matters. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|**Decree 325**]] — The decree covering labour, employment, and social security obligations for entities operating in the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|**Decree 326**]] — The decree addressing land use and environmental regulations applicable within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|**Decree 327**]] — The decree establishing immigration and entry/exit policies for workers and experts in the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|**Decree 328**]] — The decree establishing the international arbitration centre associated with the TTTC. [4]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all six TTTC foundational decrees identified in the source documents. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|**TTTC**]] — Trung Tâm Tài Chính, the Vietnam International Financial Centre; the subject of the regulatory framework established by these decrees. [5]
