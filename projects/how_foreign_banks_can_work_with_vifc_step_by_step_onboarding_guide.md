# How Foreign Banks Can Work With VIFC — Step by Step Onboarding Guide

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre ([[Tttc Financial Center Overview]]) establishes a dedicated legal framework under which foreign banks may operate as member institutions within the Centre. [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] is the primary instrument governing the licensing, establishment, and permitted activities of foreign banks — whether as 100% foreign-owned commercial banks or as foreign bank branches — operating within the VIFC. This guide outlines what foreign banks need to know about entry requirements, permitted operations, and ongoing obligations based on the source decrees, particularly in relation to [[Banking Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], and [[Licenses And Approvals For Financial Institutions In Tttc]].

---

## 1. Overview

Foreign banks seeking to operate within the VIFC may do so through two principal legal forms recognised under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]:

- **100% foreign-owned commercial banks** (*ngân hàng thương mại 100% vốn nước ngoài*) — independently incorporated legal entities operating within the Centre under Vietnamese law as adapted by the VIFC framework [3][9].
- **Foreign bank branches** (*chi nhánh ngân hàng nước ngoài thành viên*) — economic entities without separate legal personality, established as dependent units of their overseas parent bank. The parent bank is fully liable for all obligations and commitments of the branch within the Centre [9].

Foreign bank branches that are **not** VIFC members (*chi nhánh ngân hàng nước ngoài không là thành viên*) remain subject to the general Law on Credit Institutions and do not enjoy the special VIFC framework [9].

The VIFC framework is relevant to foreign banks because it grants access to a dedicated regulatory environment, including permissions to conduct cross-border banking activities, deal in foreign currencies, and operate under parent-bank accounting and policy standards — privileges not generally available under standard Vietnamese banking law. See also [[Role Of Foreign Financial Institutions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Rules For Banks Operating In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]].

## 2. Requirements from the Organisation

### Legal Form and Accountability

- A foreign bank branch member (*chi nhánh ngân hàng nước ngoài thành viên*) must be a dependent unit of a foreign bank, with the parent bank guaranteeing responsibility for **all obligations and commitments** of the branch within the Centre [9].
- Operations must comply with Resolution No. 22/2025/QH15 and [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] [9].

### Accounting Standards

- Where the parent bank or owner applies **International Accounting Standards (IAS/IFRS)** as issued by the International Accounting Standards Board, or generally accepted accounting principles as specified in Article 11(1)(e) of Resolution No. 222/2025/QH15, the foreign bank member may apply those same standards internally. In such cases, "related persons" are defined according to the parent bank's or owner's rules rather than Vietnamese domestic rules [3].

### Governance Positions

Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], the following governance roles are defined for member commercial banks:

- **Management personnel** (*người quản lý*): Chairman, other members of the Members' Council, General Director (Director), and other management titles as defined in the bank's charter [3].
- **Executive personnel** (*người điều hành*): General Director (Director), Deputy General Director (Deputy Director), Chief Accountant, and equivalent titles as defined in the charter [3].

### Licensing

- A foreign bank member must obtain a **Licence** (*Giấy phép*) issued by the **City Management Authority** (*Cơ quan điều hành tại thành phố*) [1][6].
- All permitted activities — including advisory, lending, guarantees, and derivatives — must fall within the scope of this Licence [1][6].

*Note: The source documents do not specify exact minimum capital amounts or a detailed list of application documents for this wiki. See [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Business Licensing And Registration]] for further detail.*

## 3. Step-by-Step Process

> **Note:** The source excerpts do not provide a fully enumerated step-by-step application procedure. The following steps are reconstructed from references to licensing, activity scope, and governance requirements in [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. Steps should be verified against the full text of the decree.

**Step 1 — Choose Legal Form**
Determine whether to establish as a 100% foreign-owned commercial bank or as a foreign bank branch member (*chi nhánh ngân hàng nước ngoài thành viên*). The branch form does not have separate legal personality; the parent bank bears full liability [9].

**Step 2 — Confirm Applicable Legal Framework**
Ensure the entity will operate under Resolution No. 22/2025/QH15 and [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], not solely under the general Law on Credit Institutions [9].

**Step 3 — Confirm Accounting Standards Alignment**
If the parent bank applies IAS/IFRS or equivalent generally accepted accounting principles under Article 11(1)(e) of Resolution No. 222/2025/QH15, confirm this will be applied to the member entity, and that related-party definitions will follow the parent bank's standards [3].

**Step 4 — Apply for a Licence from the City Management Authority**
Submit an application for a Licence (*Giấy phép*) to the City Management Authority (*Cơ quan điều hành tại thành phố*). The Licence defines the scope of all permitted activities [1][6].

**Step 5 — Appoint Governance and Executive Personnel**
Appoint the required management and executive personnel — including General Director, Deputy Directors, Chief Accountant, and Members' Council as applicable — consistent with the bank's charter and the definitions in [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] [3].

**Step 6 — Establish Operational Scope Per the Licence**
Once licensed, the bank may commence the activities authorised in its Licence. These include, depending on the Licence scope: deposit taking, lending, bank guarantees, factoring, letters of credit, credit card issuance, foreign exchange transactions, derivatives, debt purchase and sale, trust banking, advisory services, safe custody, and acting as collateral management agent [1][2][4][6].

**Step 7 — Begin Member-to-Member and Cross-Sector Activity**
Upon operation, the bank may engage in permitted transactions with other member banks, Vietnamese credit institutions, and member enterprises, as governed by Articles 52–54 of [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] [1][2][4][6].

*For immigration and work permit steps for foreign staff, see [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] and [[Immigration And Residency Policies For Tttc Workers]].*

## 4. Key Decrees and Legal Basis

The primary legal instruments governing foreign banks in the VIFC are:

| Decree | Subject Matter | Relevance to Foreign Banks |
|---|---|---|
| [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] | Licensing, establishment, permitted activities, foreign exchange management | Primary governing decree for all foreign bank members [1][2][3][4][6][9] |
| Resolution No. 22/2025/QH15 | National Assembly resolution establishing the VIFC legal framework | Superior legal basis referenced throughout Decree 329 [9][3] |
| [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] | Immigration and visa policy for VIFC workers | Applies to foreign staff of foreign banks [11] |
| [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] | Establishment of the Centre | Foundational decree for VIFC existence |
| [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] | Financial policies of the Centre | May affect tax and financial incentives available |

### Key Articles in Decree 329

- **Article 52** — Activities permitted between foreign bank members and *member enterprises* (*doanh nghiệp thành viên*), including deposit-taking, lending, and other services [1].
- **Article 53** — Activities permitted between foreign bank members and *other member banks*, including lending, deposits, forward purchases/sales of foreign-currency securities, guarantees, factoring, letters of credit, and credit card issuance [6].
- **Article 54** — Activities permitted between foreign bank members and *Vietnamese credit institutions*, including lending, bank guarantees (with specific conditions on deposit of 100% of guarantee value), factoring, and letters of credit [4].
