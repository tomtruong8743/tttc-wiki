# How Foreign Companies Can Establish Operations at VIFC — Step by Step

## Summary

The [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre (VIFC)]], also known as TTTC (Trung tâm tài chính quốc tế), was established under a framework of decrees issued in December 2025. Foreign companies — including 100% foreign-owned commercial banks, foreign bank branches, foreign securities firms, and commodities exchange participants — may establish operations within VIFC subject to licensing, capital, and compliance requirements set out primarily in [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]]. Each type of foreign institution must register with the relevant city-level management authority (Cơ quan điều hành), satisfy minimum capital thresholds, and operate only within the geographic boundaries of the financial centre. [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]] and [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] provide additional incentives and constraints that foreign operators must understand before commencing operations.

---

## 1. Overview

**What type of organisation this covers:**
This page covers foreign companies seeking to establish a legal presence and conduct regulated financial or commodities activities inside VIFC. This includes:
- 100% foreign-owned commercial banks (ngân hàng thương mại 100% vốn nước ngoài)
- Foreign bank branches (chi nhánh ngân hàng nước ngoài)
- Foreign securities firms (tổ chức kinh doanh chứng khoán nước ngoài)
- Foreign traders and members of the [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Commodities Exchange]] operating within VIFC

**Why VIFC is relevant:**
VIFC offers foreign companies access to Vietnamese and international capital markets, a dedicated regulatory regime with bespoke licensing, [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|tax incentives]], and the ability to conduct foreign currency transactions and cross-border financial services under conditions not generally available elsewhere in Vietnam. The centre is physically located within designated geographic zones in Ho Chi Minh City and Da Nang (see [[Cities Involved In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]).

## 2. Requirements from the Organisation

### For Foreign Banks (100% Foreign-Owned Commercial Banks and Foreign Bank Branches)

- Each investor (foreign bank or parent institution) is permitted to establish **only one (01) presence** within VIFC, in one of the forms permitted under Article 17 of Resolution 222/2025/QH15. [8]
- Member banks (**ngân hàng thành viên**) may **not** expand their branch network and may **not** relocate their registered office outside the geographic boundaries of VIFC. [8]
- Chartered capital (**vốn điều lệ**) of a member bank is defined as the total amount contributed by the owner to the member commercial bank, or the total amount allocated by the parent bank to the foreign bank branch. [8]
- Own capital (**vốn tự có**) is calculated in accordance with current regulations of the Governor of the State Bank of Vietnam on capital adequacy ratios for commercial banks and foreign bank branches. [8]
- Management personnel of member banks must act in good faith, with due care and prudence, and in the interests of the member bank and its owner; they must not use the bank's information, trade secrets, or business opportunities for personal gain or to benefit other parties at the bank's expense. [4]

### For Foreign Securities Firms (Tổ chức kinh doanh chứng khoán nước ngoài)

A foreign securities firm wishing to become a member/investor in a VIFC securities trading organisation must satisfy all of the following conditions [7]:

- **Minimum equity**: At least **USD 200 million** (equivalent), per the most recently audited annual financial statements. [7]
- **Licensing**: Must be licensed or registered with a competent authority in the field of securities in its home country. [7]
- **IOSCO membership**: The competent securities authority of the firm's home country must be a **full member of IOSCO** (International Organization of Securities Commissions). [7]
- **Operational continuity**: Must have operated continuously for at least **two (02) years** immediately prior to application, with no suspension, cessation, warning, supervision, or special control status imposed during that period; and must show no accumulated losses — must be profitable — in each of the two most recent consecutive audited annual financial statements. [7]
- **Capital and personnel allocation**: The investor must arrange sufficient capital and a projected staffing plan for the member securities trading organisation. [7]

*(Note: For domestic Vietnamese securities firms investing in VIFC member organisations, the minimum audited equity is VND 5,000 billion, and the monthly liquid capital ratio must reach at least 300% in each of the three most recent consecutive months. [7])*

### For Commodities Exchange Members

- Members of the [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Commodities Exchange]] (Sở giao dịch hàng hóa) must be enterprises meeting conditions set by the Exchange and approved by it. [9]
- Members must comply with the Government's regulations on commodity trading through a commodities exchange and the Exchange's operating charter. [9]
- Foreign individuals and traders who are **not** trading members of the Commodities Exchange may participate by **delegating (ủy thác)** to a trading member to conduct buying and selling activities on their behalf. [9]

## 3. Step-by-Step Process

> **Note:** The source documents describe the regulatory framework and conditions but do not provide a single consolidated step-by-step application procedure covering all foreign company types. The steps below are reconstructed from the procedural requirements described across the relevant decrees. Steps marked *(uncertain)* reflect reasonable inference from the framework described.

**For Foreign Banks:**

1. **Confirm eligibility**: Verify that the parent institution is not already present in VIFC in any form, as only one presence per investor group is permitted under Article 4 of [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. [8]
2. **Prepare application dossier**: Compile required documents including proof of parent bank licensing, capital adequacy documentation, proposed charter, and projected personnel plan *(uncertain — specific document list not detailed in source excerpts)*. [8]
3. **Submit to the City-Level Management Authority (Cơ quan điều hành)**: Applications are filed with the Cơ quan điều hành at the relevant city (Ho Chi Minh City or Da Nang). The licence (Giấy phép) is issued by this authority. [1]
4. **Obtain the Operating Licence (Giấy phép)**: The scope of permitted activities — including advisory services, foreign exchange operations, and other banking activities — is defined by and limited to what is stated in the Giấy phép issued by the Cơ quan điều hành. [1]
5. **Establish registered office within VIFC geographic boundaries**: The bank must be physically located within the designated zone and cannot relocate outside it. [8]
6. **Commence operations within permitted scope**: Activities such as foreign currency transactions, interest rate derivatives, corporate bond trading, government bond trading, debt purchase/sale, trustee activities, and safe custody services may only be conducted as expressly permitted in the licence. [1][3]
7. **Register with supervisory authorities**: The supervisory body (Cơ quan giám sát) oversees ongoing compliance; the bank must be prepared for periodic and ad hoc supervision, including audits by auditing firms designated by the supervisory authority. [2]

**For Foreign Securities Firms:**

1. **Verify minimum capital**: Confirm audited equity of at least USD 200 million. [7]
2. **Confirm IOSCO membership of home-country regulator**: Obtain documentation from the home-country securities regulator confirming IOSCO full membership. [7]
3. **Confirm two-year continuous operation and profitability**: Prepare two years of audited financial statements showing profitability and uninterrupted operation. [7]
4. **Prepare capital and staffing plan**: Develop a plan for capital allocation and personnel for the VIFC member securities organisation. [7]

6. **Obtain membership/approval**: Upon approval, the firm operates as a member organisation within the VIFC framework. [7]
