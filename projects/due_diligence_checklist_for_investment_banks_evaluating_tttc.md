# Due Diligence Checklist for Investment Banks Evaluating TTTC

## Summary

This page provides a structured due diligence checklist for investment banks evaluating opportunities within the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] (Trung tâm Tài chính Quốc tế — Vietnam International Financial Centre). The TTTC is governed by a series of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (Văn phòng Chính phủ), covering areas including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policies]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]]. Investment banks conducting due diligence should systematically review each of these regulatory instruments as part of their entry assessment.

---

## Key Points

- The TTTC is established and governed by a suite of decrees all issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (Văn phòng Chính phủ), making that office the primary regulatory authority to engage with during due diligence [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] is the foundational legal instrument establishing the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] and should be the first document reviewed in any due diligence process [5].
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] governs financial policies within the TTTC and is directly relevant to understanding the [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|tax incentives]], capital rules, and financial operating conditions applicable to investment banks [1].
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] covers labour, employment, and social security matters within the TTTC, which is essential for assessing [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|staffing]] and [[Attracting International Financial Talent To Tttc|talent]] strategies [6].
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] addresses land use and environmental regulations, relevant to investment banks planning [[Why Physical Location Matters For Banks In Tttc|physical office or operational presence]] within the TTTC [3].
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] governs immigration and entry/exit policies for personnel working within the TTTC, a key consideration for [[Policies For Foreign Experts In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign expert deployment]] [2].
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the international arbitration framework within the TTTC, which is critical for assessing [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|dispute resolution]] rights and [[Legal Protections For Foreign Financial Institutions In Tttc|legal protections]] [4].
- All six decrees share a common issuing authority — Văn phòng Chính phủ (the Government Office of Vietnam) — indicating a centralised regulatory structure that investment banks must engage with comprehensively [1][2][3][4][5][6].

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] — Abbreviation for *Trung tâm Tài chính Quốc tế*, the Vietnam International Financial Centre; the special financial zone subject to the decrees reviewed in this checklist [5].
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] — The foundational decree establishing the TTTC, issued by Văn phòng Chính phủ [5].
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] — The decree governing financial policies applicable within the TTTC [1].
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]] — The decree covering labour, employment, and social security within the TTTC [6].
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]] — The decree addressing land use and environmental matters within the TTTC [3].
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] — The decree governing immigration and entry/exit policies for TTTC personnel [2].
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] — The decree establishing the international arbitration centre within the TTTC [4].
- **Văn phòng Chính phủ** — The Government Office of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all six core TTTC decrees [1][2][3][4][5][6].
