# How Fintech Companies Can Work With VIFC — Step by Step Onboarding Guide

## Summary

The [[Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (VIFC, also referred to as TTTC — Trung tâm tài chính quốc tế) establishes a dedicated framework for [[Financial Services In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|financial technology (fintech) companies]] to operate within a regulated but innovation-friendly environment. Governed primarily by [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] and [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], VIFC offers fintech companies access to a [[Co Che Thu Nghiem Co Kiem Soat In The Context Of Tttc|controlled sandbox testing mechanism]], digital asset infrastructure rights, and a range of capital market technology services. Fintech firms seeking to participate must navigate licensing, capital, and compliance requirements administered by the VIFC's managing authority (Cơ quan điều hành).

---

## Key Points

- Fintech companies at VIFC are permitted to provide infrastructure and commercial products and services related to **digital assets and decentralised finance (DeFi)** [1].
- Fintech firms may provide **trading infrastructure for small and medium enterprises (SMEs) and innovative startups** [1].
- A **blockchain-based financial product sandbox** (thí điểm sandbox) is available for fintech companies operating at VIFC, allowing pilot testing of commercial financial products built on blockchain technology [1].
- [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|RegTech solutions]] — including [[Anti Money Laundering Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|anti-money laundering and counter-terrorism financing]], [[Kyc Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|customer and business verification]], data management, personal data protection, cybersecurity, and fraud management — are explicitly listed as permitted fintech activities [1].
- Fintech companies may operate **capital market and fund management platforms**, including crowdfunding (huy động vốn cộng đồng), personal finance tools, asset management technology, robo-advisory, and alternative trading platforms [1].
- Research and development of **new financial products** such as asset tokenisation and stablecoins is a permitted activity at VIFC [1].
- Fintech firms may develop and provide **capital financing platforms**, including supply chain finance, receivables finance, and commodity-based finance [1].
- **Non-deposit-taking lending institutions** — including factoring companies (công ty bao thanh toán), finance companies, and financial leasing companies — may develop corporate financing activities within VIFC [1].
- Crowdfunding platform operators must apply for a **Giấy phép cung cấp nền tảng huy động vốn** (licence to provide a capital-raising platform); upon dissolution, merger, or division, the managing authority (Cơ quan điều hành) will revoke this licence within **05 working days** of receiving notification [7].
- At least **one legal representative** of an entity operating at VIFC must be resident at the VIFC or elsewhere in Vietnam [8].

## Definitions

- **[[Trung Tam Tai Chinh Quoc Te Definition And Importance|TTTC / VIFC]]** — Vietnam International Financial Centre; the special-purpose international financial hub established by the Vietnamese Government [1].
- **[[Co Che Thu Nghiem Co Kiem Soat In The Context Of Tttc|Sandbox (thí điểm sandbox)]]** — A controlled regulatory pilot mechanism allowing fintech companies to test new financial products, such as blockchain-based commercial financial products, within defined boundaries before full regulatory authorisation [1].
- **Tài sản số (Digital Assets)** — Digital assets that fintech companies may provide infrastructure and services for within VIFC [1].
- **Tài chính phi tập trung (DeFi — Decentralised Finance)** — Decentralised financial services that fintech firms are permitted to provide infrastructure and products for within VIFC [1].
- **RegTech (Công nghệ tuân thủ pháp luật)** — Technology solutions for legal and regulatory compliance, including [[Anti Money Laundering Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|AML/CFT]], [[Kyc Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|KYC]], data governance, cybersecurity, and fraud management [1].
- **Huy động vốn cộng đồng (Crowdfunding)** — A capital-raising method permitted for fintech platforms at VIFC, targeting SMEs and startups [1].
- **Giấy phép cung cấp nền tảng huy động vốn** — Licence to provide a capital-raising (crowdfunding) platform, issued and revoked by the Cơ quan điều hành (managing authority) [7].
- **[[Cơ quan điều hành (Managing Authority)]]** — The VIFC's executive body responsible for issuing licences, approving governance appointments, and overseeing regulatory compliance [7][8].
- **Token hóa tài sản (Asset Tokenisation)** — The process of representing real-world assets as blockchain tokens; listed as a permitted area of fintech research and product development at VIFC [1].
- **Stablecoin** — A type of digital currency pegged to a stable asset; listed as a permitted area of new financial product research within VIFC [1].
- **[[Thi Truong Von In The Context Of Tttc|Thị trường vốn (Capital Market)]]** — The market for equity and debt instruments; fintech firms may provide platforms and infrastructure supporting capital market activities at VIFC [1].

## 1. Overview

Fintech companies represent one of the four principal financial activity categories explicitly defined under [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]]. The VIFC framework recognises fintech as a distinct sector with its own permitted activities, licensing pathway, and regulatory environment. The rationale for including fintech within VIFC is to stimulate financial innovation, support Vietnam's capital market development, provide technology infrastructure for SMEs and startups, and develop a regulatory ecosystem for digital assets and decentralised finance.

Unlike traditional banking or securities firms, fintech companies at VIFC operate across a broader range of technology-driven activities — from blockchain product pilots and RegTech solutions to alternative trading platforms and asset tokenisation research — subject to oversight by the Cơ quan điều hành (managing authority) and the Cơ quan giám sát (supervisory authority).

## 2. Requirements from the Organisation

The source documents provide the following specific requirements relevant to fintech companies and related entities at VIFC:

**Licensing:**
- Fintech companies providing crowdfunding platforms must obtain a **Giấy phép cung cấp nền tảng huy động vốn** from the Cơ quan điều hành [7].
- Entities wishing to operate in insurance agency or auxiliary insurance services (potentially relevant for insurtech) must hold appropriate **insurance certificates** issued by a legally established domestic or foreign training institution, matching the type of insurance product or service [3].

**Governance / Personnel:**
- At least **one legal representative** must be resident at the VIFC or elsewhere in Vietnam at all times [8].
- Where a Director General or actuarial expert leaves their position, the entity must submit a valid application for approval of a replacement to the Cơ quan điều hành within **75 days** of the departure (note: this requirement is specifically stated for insurance companies, and may apply to insurtech firms operating in this space) [8].

**Capital and Financial Requirements:**
- Not covered in source documents for fintech companies specifically.

**Operational / Activity Scope:**
- Fintech companies must operate within the explicitly permitted activity categories listed under Article 4, Section 4 of [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], which include digital asset infrastructure, RegTech, capital market platforms, asset tokenisation research, capital financing platforms, and DeFi services [1].
- Blockchain sandbox participants must comply with the specific conditions of the **thí điểm sandbox** mechanism [1].
