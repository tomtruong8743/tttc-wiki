# Market Opportunity Due Diligence Questions for TTTC

## Summary

This page addresses the key due diligence questions that investors, financial institutions, and other stakeholders should consider when evaluating the market opportunity presented by the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]]. The TTTC is governed by a suite of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ), including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]]. Understanding the scope, structure, and policies embedded in these decrees is essential for any party conducting serious market opportunity analysis of the TTTC.

## Key Points

- The TTTC is established and governed through a series of decrees issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Government Office of Vietnam), forming the core [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] that due diligence must address. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] is the foundational decree establishing the TTTC, making it a primary document for any [[Due Diligence Checklist For Investment Banks Evaluating Tttc|market opportunity due diligence checklist]]. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] governs the financial policies within the TTTC and is a critical reference for understanding [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]] and other financial incentives relevant to market entry. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] covers labor, employment, and social security policies, which are directly relevant to [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|workforce planning]] and operational due diligence. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] addresses land use and environmental regulations within the TTTC, relevant to [[Real Estate And Office Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|physical establishment]] and infrastructure considerations. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] sets out immigration and entry/exit policies applicable to the TTTC, a key factor for [[Attracting International Financial Talent To Tttc|attracting and retaining international talent]]. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the international arbitration framework within the TTTC, informing due diligence on [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|dispute resolution mechanisms]]. [4]
- All source decrees originate from the same issuing authority (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ), indicating a coordinated and unified regulatory framework for the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] — Abbreviation for *Trung Tâm Tài Chính Quốc Tế* (Vietnam International Financial Centre), a specially established financial zone governed by a series of government decrees. [5]
- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ]] — The Government Office of Vietnam; the issuing authority responsible for publishing the decrees that establish and regulate the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Nghị định 323]] — The foundational decree establishing the TTTC, setting out its legal basis and structure. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Nghị định 324]] — The decree governing financial policies within the TTTC, including fiscal and tax-related incentives. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Nghị định 325]] — The decree covering labor, employment, and social security within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Nghị định 326]] — The decree addressing land use and environmental regulations within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Nghị định 327]] — The decree governing immigration and entry/exit policies for persons operating within the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Nghị định 328]] — The decree establishing the international arbitration centre within the TTTC. [4]
