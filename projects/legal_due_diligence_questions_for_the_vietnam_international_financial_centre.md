# Legal Due Diligence Questions for the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

This page addresses key legal due diligence questions relevant to the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC), drawing on the suite of government decrees issued by the [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Office of the Government of Vietnam]]. Due diligence for the TTTC spans multiple regulatory domains, including [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]], [[Land Use Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], and [[Immigration And Residency Policies For Tttc Workers]], each governed by a dedicated decree. Understanding these areas is essential for any financial institution, investor, or legal team evaluating participation in the TTTC.

---

## Key Points

- The TTTC is established and governed by a series of decrees issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Office of the Government of Vietnam), forming the core [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]]. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] is the foundational legal instrument establishing the TTTC and is the starting point for any [[Due Diligence Checklist For Investment Banks Evaluating Tttc|due diligence review]]. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] governs financial policies within the TTTC and is critical for understanding [[Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and related financial regulations. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] covers [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and social security obligations, which are essential for workforce planning and compliance. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] addresses [[Land Use Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and environmental regulations, relevant to any institution planning a physical presence in the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] sets out immigration and entry/exit policies, directly affecting [[Policies For Foreign Experts In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and the ability to bring in international talent. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the framework for [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|international arbitration]] within the TTTC, which is a key consideration for [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]. [4]
- All six decrees were issued by the same authority — VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ — indicating a unified, centrally coordinated [[Legal Documents Establishing The Vietnam International Financial Centre|legal framework]]. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

---

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|**Decree 323**]] — The foundational decree establishing the Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC), issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|**Decree 324**]] — The decree governing financial policies within the TTTC, including [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|tax incentives and financial regulations]]. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|**Decree 325**]] — The decree covering labour, employment, and social security policies applicable within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|**Decree 326**]] — The decree governing land use and environmental policies within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|**Decree 327**]] — The decree setting out entry, exit, and immigration policies for individuals working within the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|**Decree 328**]] — The decree establishing the international arbitration centre within the TTTC, providing a framework for [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]]. [4]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all six TTTC-related decrees identified in source documents. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|**TTTC Legal Framework**]] — The collective body of decrees and regulations governing the establishment, operation, and supervision of the Vietnam International Financial Centre. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
