# Customs and Import Export Regulations at VIFC

## Summary

The [[Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (VIFC, also referred to as TTTC — Trung tâm tài chính quốc tế) establishes a distinct customs and import-export regulatory framework designed to facilitate trade, attract foreign investment, and support the development of financial and technological infrastructure. Enterprises and individuals operating within VIFC benefit from preferential [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]] on import and export duties, governed primarily by [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] and aligned with Vietnam's international treaty obligations. The [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] also addresses logistics and import-export infrastructure specific to the commodities exchange operating within the centre. These customs policies intersect with broader [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Capital Movement Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] to create an integrated trade and financial environment.

---

## Key Points

- Goods and services exported from VIFC to foreign countries, or imported from abroad into VIFC, are subject to preferential export and import duty rates and procedures under Vietnam's international treaties, the [[Export and Import Tax Law No. 107/2016/QH13]], and the [[Customs Law No. 54/2014/QH13]], and their implementing regulations. [1]
- Enterprises and individuals at VIFC are entitled to a **priority administrative regime** (chế độ ưu tiên về thủ tục hành chính) in tax management, including registration, declaration, payment, and finalisation of taxes. [1]
- **Import tax exemptions** apply to technical equipment, technology, and software solutions not yet domestically produced that are imported to serve construction of information technology infrastructure, management and operations systems, and large data centres within VIFC. [1]
- The determination of when [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]] begin, and the duration of tax holidays or reductions, follows the laws on [[Corporate Income Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and [[Personal Income Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]. [1]
- The People's Committees of Ho Chi Minh City and Da Nang are responsible for ensuring the availability of technical infrastructure, warehousing, [[logistics]] services, and security to support import-export activities and trading through the [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Commodities Exchange]]. [2]
- The list of priority industries and trades at VIFC that qualify for [[Corporate Income Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] is determined by the government decree establishing VIFC — i.e., [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]]. [1]
- The [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] took effect on **18 December 2025**, governing commodity trading and related import-export operations at the exchange. [2]
- Administrative procedures related to [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]] are handled through a **one-stop administrative system** (Hệ thống hành chính một cửa), coordinated with the State Bank of Vietnam. [5]

## Definitions

- **[[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|Thuế xuất khẩu, thuế nhập khẩu ưu đãi]]** — Preferential export and import tax rates and procedures applied to goods and services entering or leaving VIFC, pursuant to international treaties, the Export-Import Tax Law, and the Customs Law. [1]
- **Chế độ ưu tiên về thủ tục hành chính** — The priority administrative regime in tax management available to enterprises and individuals registered and operating within VIFC, covering tax registration, declaration, payment, and settlement. [1]
- **Miễn thuế nhập khẩu** — Import tax exemption, specifically applicable to technical equipment, technology, and software not domestically produced, imported for the purpose of building IT infrastructure, operational management systems, and large data centres within VIFC. [1]
- **[[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Sở giao dịch hàng hóa]]** — The Commodities Exchange established within VIFC, whose import-export operations are supported by logistics, warehousing, and technical infrastructure maintained by the local People's Committees. [2]
- **Hệ thống hành chính một cửa** — The one-stop administrative system used for processing administrative procedures and receiving declarations related to foreign exchange management within VIFC. [5]
- **[[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Danh mục ngành, nghề ưu tiên phát triển]]** — The list of priority sectors and industries designated for development at VIFC, which determines eligibility for corporate income tax incentives; this list is established by [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]]. [1]

## Overview

VIFC applies a specialised customs and import-export regulatory framework that differs from standard Vietnamese customs rules. The framework is primarily relevant to:

- Enterprises operating within VIFC (financial institutions, fintech firms, commodities traders, IT infrastructure developers)
- Importers of technology and equipment for VIFC's data and operational infrastructure
- Participants in the [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Commodities Exchange]] located within VIFC

The customs regime is designed to reduce barriers to trade, incentivise investment in infrastructure, and align VIFC with international standards through the application of Vietnam's treaty obligations. The [[Cities Involved In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|two cities involved]] — Ho Chi Minh City and Da Nang — each bear local responsibility for supporting import-export infrastructure.

---

## Requirements from the Organization

- Enterprises and individuals at VIFC must **register, declare, pay, and finalise** all applicable taxes (including import-export duties) in accordance with Vietnamese tax administration law. [1]
- To qualify for import tax exemptions on equipment and technology, the imported items must meet the condition that they are **not yet domestically produced** in Vietnam and must be destined for IT infrastructure, management systems, or large data centres within VIFC. [1]
- Preferential duty rates apply only to goods and services moving **between VIFC and foreign countries** (i.e., exports from VIFC to abroad, or imports from abroad into VIFC); the source documents do not specify rules for goods moving between VIFC and the domestic Vietnamese market (uncertain).
- Applications and declarations related to [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange]] connected to import-export activity must be processed through the **one-stop administrative system**, in coordination with the State Bank of Vietnam. [5]

## Step-by-Step Process

Not covered in source documents in full procedural detail. The following partial steps are indicated by the sources:

1. **Register with VIFC authorities**: Enterprises must register with the relevant management body at VIFC (the Supervisory Authority — Cơ quan giám sát) and with local People's Committees, as applicable. [7]
2. **Declare applicable taxes**: Submit import-export tax declarations in accordance with the priority administrative regime (chế độ ưu tiên về thủ tục hành chính) under tax administration law. [1]
3. **Apply for import tax exemption** (if applicable): Demonstrate that imported goods are technical equipment, technology, or software not domestically produced, and that they are intended for VIFC's IT infrastructure, management systems, or data centres. [1]
4. **Submit foreign exchange-related declarations** through the one-stop administrative system (Hệ thống hành chính một cửa), coordinated with the State Bank of Vietnam. [5]
5. **Coordinate with local People's Committees**: For commodities exchange-related import-export activities, liaise with the Ho Chi Minh City or Da Nang People's Committee for logistics, warehousing, and security support. [2]

Full procedural timelines, form numbers, and specific filing deadlines are **not covered in source documents**.
