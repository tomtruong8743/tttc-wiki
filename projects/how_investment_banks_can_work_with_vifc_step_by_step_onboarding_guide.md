# How Investment Banks Can Work With VIFC — Step by Step Onboarding Guide

## Summary

This guide outlines the process by which investment banks — operating as securities business organisations ([[Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]) — can establish and operate within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre (VIFC/TTTC)]]. The VIFC is governed by a suite of foundational decrees, principally [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], and [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], which together define [[Membership Requirements For The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], permitted activities, [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]], and [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]. Investment banks seeking to participate must obtain a licence as a Member securities business organisation (*tổ chức kinh doanh chứng khoán là Thành viên*), comply with ongoing supervisory requirements, and restrict their client base to permitted counterparties within the VIFC's geographic boundaries.

---

## Key Points

- Investment banks wishing to operate in the VIFC must be established as a **Member securities business organisation** (*tổ chức kinh doanh chứng khoán là Thành viên*), structured as a single-member limited liability company. [5]
- Permitted activities include: securities brokerage, securities investment advisory, proprietary trading, securities underwriting, investment fund management, derivatives securities business, services related to those activities, and provision of crowdfunding platforms for innovative start-ups within the VIFC. [5]
- The [[Licenses And Approvals For Financial Institutions In Tttc|licence application]] uses Form 01 (*Giấy đề nghị cấp giấy phép thành lập và hoạt động tổ chức kinh doanh chứng khoán là Thành viên*) and must be accompanied by a personnel list (Form 02). [3]
- Member securities business organisations **must have their registered office within the geographic boundaries** of the VIFC and are **prohibited from expanding their network or relocating outside** those boundaries. [5]
- [[Financial Services In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Services]] may only be provided to other Members, employees of Members, and foreign individuals/organisations; direct or indirect provision of services to Vietnamese nationals or foreign nationals resident in Vietnam **outside** the VIFC boundary is prohibited. [5]
- The [[Regulatory Bodies Supervising The Vietnam International Financial Centre|supervisory authority]] (*Cơ quan giám sát*) conducts regular and ad hoc inspections, may require member organisations to engage an auditor of the authority's choosing, and coordinates with the State Bank of Vietnam (*Ngân hàng Nhà nước*) quarterly or as needed. [4] [7]
- The supervisory authority operates a **one-stop administrative system** (*Hệ thống hành chính một cửa*) for handling administrative procedures, including those related to foreign exchange management. [8]
- [[Financial Products And Instruments In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Innovative or untested]] technologies, products, services, and business models may be licensed under a **controlled sandbox** (*chính sách thử nghiệm có kiểm soát*) for a maximum of **five years**. [8]
- [[Accounting And Financial Reporting]] policies for Member securities business organisations are governed by the provisions referenced at Point (e), Clause 1, Article 11 of [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]. [5]
- Insurance, reinsurance, and insurance brokerage entities operating as Members are also subject to [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] and must use Form 08 (application) and Form 09 (licence) for establishment. [3]

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|VIFC / TTTC]] (*Trung tâm tài chính quốc tế*) — The Vietnam International Financial Centre; a special economic zone with its own legal, tax, and regulatory framework established by a suite of government decrees. [7]
- **Member** (*Thành viên*) — An organisation or institution that has been licensed and registered to operate within the VIFC, subject to the VIFC's specific rules and entitled to its incentives. [5]
- **Member securities business organisation** (*tổ chức kinh doanh chứng khoán là Thành viên*) — The legal form through which investment banks, broker-dealers, and similar capital market intermediaries operate within the VIFC; must be incorporated as a single-member limited liability company. [5]
- [[Regulatory Bodies Supervising The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Supervisory authority]] (*Cơ quan giám sát*) — The dedicated inspection, examination, and supervisory body of the VIFC, responsible for monitoring compliance, handling violations, and coordinating with domestic and foreign regulators. [7]
- **One-stop administrative system** (*Hệ thống hành chính một cửa*) — The unified administrative platform through which licence applications and regulatory notifications (including foreign exchange declarations) are processed within the VIFC. [8]
- [[Co Che Thu Nghiem Co Kiem Soat In The Context Of Tttc|Controlled sandbox]] (*chính sách thử nghiệm có kiểm soát*) — A time-limited regulatory framework (maximum five years) permitting the operation of financial technologies, products, services, or business models not yet addressed by existing Vietnamese law. [8]
- **Registered capital** (*vốn điều lệ*) — The minimum capital that must be declared and registered in the establishment licence; the exact amount for investment banks/securities firms is to be specified in the licence document pursuant to [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]. [1]
- [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Foreign exchange management]] (*quản lý ngoại hối*) — Rules governing cross-border and domestic foreign currency transactions within the VIFC, administered by the supervisory authority in coordination with the State Bank of Vietnam under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. [8]

## Step-by-Step Process

> **Note:** The source documents provide the regulatory framework and template forms but do not set out a single consolidated numbered procedure for investment banks. The steps below are reconstructed from the available provisions; steps for which detail is absent are noted accordingly.

**Step 1 — Confirm Eligibility**
Confirm that the applicant is a foreign securities business organisation (*tổ chức kinh doanh chứng khoán nước ngoài*) or qualifies under the relevant eligibility provisions of [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]. The entity will be established as a **single-member limited liability company** within the VIFC. [5]

**Step 2 — Prepare the Application Package**
Assemble the required documents, including:
- **Form 01**: *Giấy đề nghị cấp giấy phép thành lập và hoạt động tổ chức kinh doanh chứng khoán là Thành viên* (Application for a licence to establish and operate a Member securities business organisation). [3]
- **Form 02**: *Danh sách nhân sự tại tổ chức kinh doanh chứng khoán* (Personnel list for the securities business organisation). [3]
- **Form 05**: *Bản thuyết minh cơ sở vật chất* (Physical infrastructure statement). [3]
- Supporting legal documents identifying the investor/founder: full name, transaction address, date of establishment, licence/decision number, and the name and title of the authorised representative. [1]
- Declaration of proposed registered capital (*vốn điều lệ*), including each investor's contribution in monetary terms and percentage. [1]

**Step 3 — Submit to the Licensing Authority via the One-Stop System**
Submit the completed application package to the relevant licensing authority (*cơ quan cấp phép*) through the **one-stop administrative system** (*Hệ thống hành chính một cửa*). [8] The licensing authority reviews the application against the legal basis set out in [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] and other applicable instruments. [1]

The licensing authority's assessment unit (*bộ phận thẩm định cấp phép*) reviews the application. The timeline for this review is **not specified in the source documents**.
