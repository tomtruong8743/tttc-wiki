# Due Diligence Topics for Investment Banks in TTTC

## Summary

Investment banks conducting due diligence on opportunities within the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview]] must navigate a framework established by multiple government decrees issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). These decrees collectively govern the establishment, financial policies, land and environment regulations, labor and social security, immigration, and international arbitration within TTTC. A thorough due diligence process requires engagement with each of these regulatory instruments, including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], to assess the full legal and operational landscape.

## Key Points

- The foundational legal instrument for TTTC is issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), establishing the regulatory basis for all operations within the financial centre [5].
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] is a key document for investment banks seeking to understand the [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]] and financial policy framework applicable within TTTC [1].
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] is relevant to due diligence on physical asset ownership, land use rights, and [[Environmental Regulations Tttc]] within the centre [3].
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] governs labor, employment, and social security matters, which are critical considerations when assessing workforce-related obligations and [[Foreign Worker Regulations]] [6].
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] is relevant to due diligence on the mobility of foreign personnel and experts operating within TTTC [2].
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms]] framework, which investment banks should review when assessing legal risk and contract enforceability [4].
- All six identified decrees are issued by the same authority — the Office of the Government of Vietnam — indicating a unified and centrally coordinated [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]] [1][2][3][4][5][6].

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] — The foundational decree establishing TTTC, issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam [5].
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] — The decree governing financial policies within TTTC, relevant to tax, investment, and capital market considerations [1].
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] — The decree covering labor, employment, and social security obligations within TTTC [6].
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] — The decree addressing land use and environmental regulations within TTTC [3].
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] — The decree governing immigration and entry/exit policies for personnel operating within TTTC [2].
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] — The decree establishing the international arbitration centre within TTTC, relevant to dispute resolution and legal risk assessment [4].
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all identified TTTC decrees [1][2][3][4][5][6].
