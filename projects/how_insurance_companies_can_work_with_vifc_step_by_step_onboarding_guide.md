# How Insurance Companies Can Work With VIFC — Step by Step Onboarding Guide

---

## Summary

The [[Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (VIFC, also referred to as [[Trung Tam Tai Chinh Quoc Te Definition And Importance|Trung tâm tài chính quốc tế]]) provides a dedicated framework for insurance companies — including direct insurers, reinsurers, insurance brokers, insurance agents, and ancillary insurance service providers — to establish and operate within its jurisdiction. Governed primarily by [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], the VIFC framework sets out specific licensing procedures, organizational requirements, scope-of-service rules, and ongoing compliance obligations for insurance entities. Insurance companies wishing to operate in the VIFC must obtain a [[Business Licensing And Registration|Giấy phép thành lập và hoạt động]] (Establishment and Operating Licence) and adhere to Vietnamese insurance law as adapted for the international financial centre context.

---

## Key Points

- Insurance companies, reinsurance companies, insurance brokers, insurance agents, and ancillary insurance service providers are all recognised as eligible [[Types Of Financial Institutions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Member types]] within VIFC. [1][8]
- Insurance entities operating as Members may only provide services to other Members, employees of Members, and organisations or individuals abroad; providing services to entities in the rest of Vietnam must follow Vietnam's cross-border insurance rules. [8]
- Reinsurance companies that are Members may provide reinsurance to other Member insurers/reinsurers and to overseas insurance entities; providing services to insurers established in the rest of Vietnam must follow Vietnamese reinsurance law. [8]
- Insurance brokerage activities permitted include [[Financial Products And Instruments In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|reinsurance brokerage]] and ancillary insurance services. [13]
- All insurance enterprises, reinsurance enterprises, and insurance brokerage enterprises must operate in compliance with Vietnamese law on insurance business. [13]
- The governance structure of an insurance company, reinsurance company, or insurance brokerage company must include a Members' Council, a Director or General Director, and a Supervisory Board (if applicable). [13]
- Staff of insurance agents and ancillary insurance service provider organisations must hold full civil legal capacity and possess insurance certificates issued by a legally established domestic or foreign insurance training institution, relevant to the type of insurance, product, or ancillary service involved. [1]
- The Establishment and Operating Licence (Giấy phép thành lập và hoạt động) is issued by the [[Regulatory Bodies Supervising The Vietnam International Financial Centre|licensing authority]], with copies distributed to the investor, the supervisory authority, and relevant agencies. [2][3]
- The licence specifies the company's full name, abbreviated name, English-language trading name, registered address, legal status, charter capital, investors and their capital contributions, and permitted business scope. [2]
- Vietnamese employees at VIFC participate in social insurance under the Law on Social Insurance No. 41/2024/QH15; foreign employees who fall within mandatory social insurance scope are also covered under that law. [6]
- The supervisory authority coordinates with the State Bank of Vietnam, relevant ministries, and the People's Committees of Ho Chi Minh City and Da Nang on a quarterly or ad hoc basis regarding supervision of Member entities. [4]
- Commercial international insurance, reinsurance, international commercial insurance brokerage, transport insurance, and maritime finance insurance are among the recognised activities at VIFC. [10]

## Definitions

- [[Types Of Financial Institutions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|**Doanh nghiệp bảo hiểm**]] — Insurance enterprise; an entity licensed to conduct direct insurance business within VIFC. [2][8]
- [[Types Of Financial Institutions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|**Doanh nghiệp tái bảo hiểm**]] — Reinsurance enterprise; an entity licensed to conduct reinsurance business within VIFC. [2][8]
- [[Types Of Financial Institutions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|**Doanh nghiệp môi giới bảo hiểm**]] — Insurance brokerage enterprise; an entity licensed to act as an intermediary in insurance transactions within VIFC. [2][13]
- **Tổ chức đại lý bảo hiểm** — Insurance agent organisation; an organisation acting as agent for an insurer within VIFC, whose staff must hold relevant insurance certificates. [1]
- **Tổ chức cung cấp dịch vụ phụ trợ bảo hiểm** — Ancillary insurance service provider; an organisation providing support services to the insurance industry within VIFC, whose staff must also hold relevant insurance certificates. [1]
- [[Business Licensing And Registration|**Giấy phép thành lập và hoạt động (GP/KDBH)**]] — Establishment and Operating Licence for insurance business; the primary licence issued by the licensing authority permitting an entity to conduct insurance, reinsurance, or brokerage activities within VIFC. [2]
- **Vốn điều lệ** — Charter capital; the registered capital of the insurance entity as specified in its licence, broken down by investor name, monetary contribution, and percentage. [2]
- **Cơ quan giám sát** — Supervisory authority; the body responsible for monitoring the activities of VIFC Member entities, coordinating with the State Bank of Vietnam and other agencies. [4]
- **Cơ quan điều hành** — Operating/Management authority; the authority responsible for issuing and revoking licences within VIFC. [11]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|**Bảo hiểm xã hội**]] — Social insurance; mandatory or voluntary social security contributions applicable to both Vietnamese and foreign workers at VIFC under Law No. 41/2024/QH15. [6]

Steps below are reconstructed from available source material; steps marked *(uncertain)* lack explicit procedural detail in the excerpts.

**Step 1 — Determine Eligible Entity Type**
Confirm which category of insurance entity you intend to establish: insurance enterprise (*doanh nghiệp bảo hiểm*), reinsurance enterprise (*doanh nghiệp tái bảo hiểm*), insurance brokerage enterprise (*doanh nghiệp môi giới bảo hiểm*), insurance agent organisation, or ancillary insurance service provider. Each type has distinct permitted activities and staffing requirements. [8][13]

**Step 2 — Confirm Permitted Scope of Service**
Verify that your intended client base is eligible under VIFC rules: services may only be provided to other VIFC Members, employees of Members, and overseas organisations/individuals. Any intention to serve clients in the rest of Vietnam requires compliance with Vietnam's cross-border insurance and reinsurance rules. [8]

**Step 3 — Prepare Application Dossier**
Compile the required application dossier (*hồ sơ*) and submit an application (*đơn*) to the licensing authority (*cơ quan cấp phép*). The dossier must identify the investor/founder(s), including their name, address, date/number of establishment, and the name and title of their representative. [2]

**Step 4 — Specify Charter Capital**
The application must state the charter capital (*vốn điều lệ*) of the entity, specifying each investor's contribution in monetary terms and as a percentage. The exact minimum capital amount is not specified in the source excerpts. *(uncertain — minimum capital threshold not stated in source documents)* [2]

**Step 5 — Define Business Scope**
The licence application must set out the full scope of intended activities, which may include: direct insurance business, reinsurance, cession of reinsurance, insurance brokerage, reinsurance brokerage, and/or ancillary insurance services. [2][13]

**Step 6 — Establish Governance Structure**
Prior to licensing, the entity must confirm its management and governance structure, comprising at minimum: a Members' Council (*Hội đồng thành viên*), a Director or General Director (*Giám đốc hoặc Tổng giám đốc*), and a Supervisory Board (*Ban kiểm soát*) if required. [13]
