# Operational Due Diligence Questions for the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Operational due diligence for the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) involves examining the practical, day-to-day functioning of the centre as governed by a suite of decrees issued by the [[Legal Documents Establishing The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnamese Government Office]]. Key operational areas include [[Land And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|land and environment policies]], [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|labour and employment rules]], [[Immigration And Residency Policies For Tttc Workers|immigration and entry/exit policies]], and the operations of the [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|International Arbitration Centre]] — all of which are critical for financial institutions assessing whether the TTTC can support their day-to-day business needs. Operational due diligence sits alongside [[Legal Due Diligence Questions For The Vietnam International Financial Centre|legal]], [[Regulatory Due Diligence Questions For The Vietnam International Financial Centre|regulatory]], [[Tax Due Diligence Questions For The Vietnam International Financial Centre|tax]], and [[Compliance Due Diligence Questions For The Vietnam International Financial Centre|compliance]] due diligence as a core pillar of the [[Due Diligence Checklist For Investment Banks Evaluating Tttc|overall due diligence framework]] for the TTTC.

## Key Points

- The foundational operational framework for the TTTC was established by the Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), which issued all key decrees governing the centre's operations. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] establishes the TTTC itself, making it the starting point for any operational due diligence review of the centre's legal and institutional foundation. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] governs [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]] within the TTTC, a key area of operational concern for institutions planning to deploy capital or structure products inside the centre. [1]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]] covers [[Land Use Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|land and environmental policies]], which are operationally relevant for institutions seeking to secure office space or physical infrastructure within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]] addresses [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|labour, employment, and social security]] within the TTTC, which has direct operational implications for staffing, hiring, and workforce management. [6]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] sets out [[Immigration And Residency Policies For Tttc Workers|immigration and entry/exit policies]], which are operationally significant for institutions seeking to relocate or deploy [[Attracting International Financial Talent To Tttc|international financial talent]] to the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] establishes the [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|International Arbitration Centre]] within the TTTC, which is operationally relevant for institutions needing reliable [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|dispute resolution mechanisms]] for contracts and transactions. [4]
- All decrees were issued by the same issuing authority — the Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) — suggesting a coordinated, top-down operational framework for the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|**Decree 323**]] — The foundational decree establishing the Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC), setting out its institutional structure and mandate. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|**Decree 324**]] — The decree governing financial policies within the TTTC, covering matters relevant to [[Capital Markets In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|capital markets]] and financial operations. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|**Decree 325**]] — The decree governing labour, employment, and social security conditions applicable to workers and employers operating within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|**Decree 326**]] — The decree covering land use and environmental regulations within the TTTC, relevant to physical operations and infrastructure. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|**Decree 327**]] — The decree setting out immigration and entry/exit policies for personnel working within the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|**Decree 328**]] — The decree establishing the International Arbitration Centre within the TTTC, providing an operational dispute resolution mechanism. [4]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — The Government Office of Vietnam, the issuing authority for all major TTTC decrees. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|**TTTC**]] — Abbreviation for Trung Tâm Tài Chính Quốc Tế, the Vietnam International Financial Centre. [5]
