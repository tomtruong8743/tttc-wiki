# How Import and Export Companies Can Work With VIFC — Step by Step Onboarding Guide

## Summary

The [[Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (VIFC / TTTC) includes a dedicated [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] framework that governs a [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange]] (Sở giao dịch hàng hóa) operating within the centre. Import and export companies — referred to in the decrees as *thương nhân* (traders/merchants) — may participate in commodity trading through this exchange, including using it to execute physical delivery contracts for goods. Such participation may be subject to [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]] under the [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]. This guide sets out what is known from the source documents about how import and export companies can engage with VIFC's commodities exchange infrastructure.

---

## Key Points

- Import and export companies (*thương nhân*) and individuals are permitted to participate in transactions on the [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] within VIFC. [1]
- Foreign traders (*thương nhân nước ngoài*) who are not trading members of the Commodities Exchange may delegate (ủy thác) to a trading member to conduct commodity trading on their behalf. [7]
- Members of VIFC, individuals, and foreign traders who are not trading members may mandate a trading member to execute commodity buying and selling through the exchange. [7]
- Where a contract is executed via physical delivery of goods (*giao nhận hàng hóa vật chất*) and those goods fall under the category of [[Financial Products And Instruments In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] requiring conditional business licences, the participating organisation or individual must satisfy the relevant business conditions under law. [1]
- Physical delivery of goods through the [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] is entitled to preferential treatment (*ưu đãi*) under the decree on [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]. [1]
- All participants in the Commodities Exchange — including import and export companies — must comply with the Government's regulations on commodity trading through the exchange, as well as the exchange's own charter and operating rules (*Điều lệ, quy chế hoạt động*). [1][7]
- The Commodities Exchange is responsible for monitoring transactions to detect and prevent violations including price manipulation, fraud, and [[Anti Money Laundering Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]; it applies early-warning mechanisms, position limits, and transaction limits to ensure market safety. [3]
- The Commodities Exchange must implement anti-money laundering ([[Anti Money Laundering Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]), counter-terrorism financing, and counter-proliferation financing measures as a reporting financial institution under VIFC law. [7]
- Members of the Commodities Exchange are enterprises (*doanh nghiệp*) that meet the conditions set by the exchange and are approved for membership by the exchange. [7]
- The Commodities Exchange is required to list goods and commodity contracts, ensuring that the origin and quality of goods meet the standards of the relevant contract. [3]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] (**Sở giao dịch hàng hóa**) — The commodities exchange established and operating within VIFC, through which commodity trading contracts are executed. [7]
- **Thương nhân** (Trader/Merchant) — An individual or enterprise engaging in commercial activities; in the VIFC context, refers to participants — domestic or foreign — who buy and sell commodities through the exchange. [1][7]
- **Thành viên kinh doanh** (Trading Member) — An enterprise approved by the Commodities Exchange to conduct commodity trading on the exchange; foreign traders who are not trading members must delegate to a trading member. [7]
- **Ủy thác** (Mandate/Delegation) — The arrangement whereby a non-member (including a foreign trader) authorises a trading member to conduct transactions on their behalf on the Commodities Exchange. [7]
- **Giao nhận hàng hóa vật chất** (Physical Delivery of Goods) — Execution of a commodity trading contract through the actual physical handover of goods, as opposed to cash settlement; eligible for financial incentives under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]. [1]
- **Hàng hóa kinh doanh có điều kiện** (Conditionally Licensed Goods) — Goods whose import, export, or trade requires fulfilment of specific legal business conditions; participants dealing in such goods via physical delivery must satisfy these conditions. [1]
- **Điều lệ, quy chế hoạt động** (Charter and Operating Rules) — The internal governance documents of the Commodities Exchange that all members and participants must comply with. [1][7]
- [[Anti Money Laundering Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (**Phòng chống rửa tiền**) — Obligations applicable to the Commodities Exchange as a reporting financial institution, including anti-money laundering, counter-terrorism financing, and counter-proliferation financing requirements. [7]

## Step-by-Step Process

> **Note:** The source documents do not provide a complete, step-by-step onboarding procedure for import and export companies specifically. The steps below are drawn directly from the available decree excerpts and should not be supplemented with outside knowledge. Steps marked *(uncertain)* reflect reasonable inference from the text that is not explicitly stated.

1. **Determine Participation Route**
   Identify whether your company wishes to become a formal *trading member* (thành viên kinh doanh) of the Commodities Exchange, or whether you will participate as a non-member by delegating to an existing trading member via *ủy thác* (mandate). Foreign traders who are not trading members must use the delegation route. [7]

2. **Assess Membership Eligibility (if seeking trading membership)**
   Membership of the Commodities Exchange is open to enterprises (*doanh nghiệp*) that satisfy conditions set by the exchange itself. The exchange has the authority to approve or terminate membership. Your company must meet those conditions and apply for approval from the exchange. [7]

3. **Check Commodity Classification**
   If your company intends to execute contracts through physical delivery of goods (*giao nhận hàng hóa vật chất*), confirm whether those goods are classified as *hàng hóa kinh doanh có điều kiện* (conditionally licensed goods). If so, ensure all applicable business conditions under Vietnamese law are satisfied before proceeding. [1]

4. **Comply With Exchange Rules**
   All participants — whether members or non-members — must comply with the Government's regulations on commodity trading through the exchange, as well as the exchange's own *Điều lệ* (charter) and *quy chế hoạt động* (operating rules). Obtain and review these documents from the exchange. [1][7]

5. **Delegation Arrangement (for non-members, including foreign traders)**
   If participating via delegation, enter into a formal *ủy thác* (mandate) arrangement with an approved trading member of the Commodities Exchange. The trading member will execute commodity buying and selling on your behalf. [7]

6. **Execute Transactions and Physical Delivery (if applicable)**
   Once membership or a delegation arrangement is in place, conduct commodity trading through the exchange. If physical delivery is used to settle contracts, this qualifies for financial incentives under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]. [1]

7. **Ongoing Compliance**
   Maintain ongoing compliance with exchange rules, government regulations, and applicable [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] including [[Anti Money Laundering Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] obligations. The exchange will monitor all transactions for violations including price manipulation, fraud, and money laundering. [3][7]

## Key Decrees and Legal Basis

- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]] — The primary decree governing the establishment and operation of the Commodities Exchange within VIFC, including provisions on membership, participant rights and responsibilities, export/import for physical delivery of contracts, and compliance obligations. Articles 15, 16, and 17 are directly relevant. [1][3][7]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] — Provides the financial incentives (*ưu đãi*) applicable to physical delivery of commodity trading contracts executed through the exchange. Referenced in Article 17 of Decree 330. [1]
