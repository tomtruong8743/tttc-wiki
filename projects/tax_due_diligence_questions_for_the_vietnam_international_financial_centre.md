# Tax Due Diligence Questions for the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

Tax due diligence is a critical step for any financial institution or investor considering participation in the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC). The TTTC is governed by a suite of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office, including [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], which sets out the core [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|tax incentives and financial policies]] applicable within the centre. Understanding the tax framework — including [[Corporate Income Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|corporate income tax incentives]], [[Personal Income Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|personal income tax incentives]], and the [[Conditions For Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|conditions attached to those incentives]] — is essential for institutions conducting [[Due Diligence Checklist For Investment Banks Evaluating Tttc|due diligence]] before entering the TTTC.

## Key Points

- The primary decree governing financial and tax policy within the TTTC is [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ). [1]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational legal basis for the establishment of the TTTC and is a key reference document for understanding the overall framework within which tax policies operate. [5]
- Tax due diligence questions should be considered alongside [[Legal Due Diligence Questions For The Vietnam International Financial Centre|legal]], [[Regulatory Due Diligence Questions For The Vietnam International Financial Centre|regulatory]], and [[Due Diligence Topics For Investment Banks In Tttc|other due diligence topics]] relevant to the TTTC. [1][5]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] may have implications for [[Tax Benefits For Workers And Experts In Tttc|payroll tax, social insurance, and employment-related tax obligations]] within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] may affect land-related tax and fee obligations for institutions operating within the TTTC. [3]
- All decrees related to the TTTC are issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ), indicating a centralised legislative framework governing tax and financial matters. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] — The primary government decree setting out financial and tax policies applicable within the Vietnam International Financial Centre. [1]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] — The foundational government decree establishing the legal and institutional basis of the TTTC. [5]
- [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]] — Preferential tax treatments and exemptions available to qualifying entities operating within the TTTC, governed primarily by [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]. [1]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Vietnamese Government Office; the issuing authority for all TTTC-related decrees. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] — A government decree addressing labour, employment, and social security matters within the TTTC, with potential implications for employment-related tax obligations. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] — A government decree governing land use and environmental matters within the TTTC, which may affect land-related taxes and fees. [3]
