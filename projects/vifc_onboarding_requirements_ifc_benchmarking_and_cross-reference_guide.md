# VIFC Onboarding Requirements — IFC Benchmarking and Cross-Reference Guide

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre ([[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|VIFC]]) operates across two locations — Ho Chi Minh City (VIFC-HCMC) and Da Nang City (VIFC-DN) — and is governed by a suite of founding decrees, most notably [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]], and [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]]. This guide benchmarks the onboarding requirements for Member institutions ([[Membership Requirements For The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]) entering the VIFC, with reference to the applicable [[Legal Framework Of Tttc]], [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]], [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]], and [[Licenses And Approvals For Financial Institutions In Tttc]]. It is intended as a cross-reference tool for institutions comparing the VIFC against other international financial centres such as Hong Kong, Singapore, and Dubai.

---

## Key Points

- The VIFC operates simultaneously across two cities — Ho Chi Minh City and Da Nang — under a unified legal framework where all standards, regulations, procedures, rules, templates, and licensing criteria are issued uniformly and applied simultaneously at both locations. [1][8]
- [[Securities Activities In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Securities business organisations]] that are Members must operate strictly within the scope of activities specified in their [[Licenses And Approvals For Financial Institutions In Tttc|Establishment and Operating Licence]] and must at all times maintain at least one legally authorised representative residing in Vietnam. [1]
- Member [[Types Of Financial Institutions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|securities business organisations]] must maintain equity capital no lower than the minimum charter capital as a continuing licence condition. [1]
- [[Asset Managers In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Customer assets]] managed or entrusted to a Member securities business organisation are the property of the customer, not the company, and must be protected accordingly — including in the event of dissolution or bankruptcy. [1]
- [[How Insurance Companies Can Work With Vifc Step By Step Onboarding Guide|Insurance enterprises]] that are Members may only provide services to other Members, employees of Members, and foreign organisations/individuals. To serve customers in the rest of Vietnam, they must comply with Vietnamese cross-border insurance regulations. [4]
- The [[Regulatory Bodies Supervising The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Supervisory Authority]] monitors compliance with licence conditions and relevant regulations on an ongoing basis after a licence is issued. [1]
- Suspension of operations by a Member securities business organisation is limited to a maximum of **90 days**; if this period is exceeded, the [[Business Licensing And Registration|Establishment and Operating Licence]] is revoked by the Management Authority. [10]
- A Member resuming operations after a suspension must report to both the Management Authority and the Supervisory Authority **within 24 hours** of resuming. [10]
- Suspension requires advance written approval from the Management Authority, which must decide within **07 working days** of receiving the application. [10]
- The Management Authority at each city location has authority to issue, renew, extend, and revoke [[Foreign Worker Regulations|work permits]] and related confirmations for foreign workers employed by Members. [2]
- [[Attracting International Financial Talent To Tttc|Employers within the VIFC]] may recruit foreign workers freely based on operational need, without being subject to foreign labour ratio limits that apply elsewhere in Vietnam. [2]
- The VIFC's development objectives include forming a synchronised, transparent legal framework aligned with international standards designed to attract international financial institutions and investors. [8]

## Definitions

- **[[Membership Requirements For The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Thành viên (Member)]]** — An organisation that has been granted an Establishment and Operating Licence to operate within the VIFC under the applicable decrees. [1]
- **[[Licenses And Approvals For Financial Institutions In Tttc|Giấy phép thành lập và hoạt động (Establishment and Operating Licence)]]** — The formal licence granted by the Management Authority permitting a financial institution to establish and conduct activities within the VIFC; specifies the permitted scope of activity. [1]
- **[[Regulatory Bodies Supervising The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Cơ quan điều hành (Management Authority)]]** — The governing body at each VIFC city location (Ho Chi Minh City and Da Nang) responsible for issuing licences, approving suspensions, and administering the VIFC. [1][2][6]
- **[[Supervision Mechanisms In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Cơ quan giám sát (Supervisory Authority)]]** — The body responsible for post-licence monitoring of Members' ongoing compliance with licence conditions and applicable regulations. [1]
- **[[Securities Activities In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Tổ chức kinh doanh chứng khoán (Securities Business Organisation)]]** — A Member entity licensed to conduct securities-related activities within the VIFC. [1]
- **[[Financial Products And Instruments In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vốn điều lệ tối thiểu (Minimum Charter Capital)]]** — The minimum capital amount prescribed as a condition for licensing; Member equity must not fall below this threshold at any time during operation. [1]
- **[[How Insurance Companies Can Work With Vifc Step By Step Onboarding Guide|Doanh nghiệp bảo hiểm (Insurance Enterprise)]]** — A Member entity licensed to provide insurance services within the VIFC, subject to restrictions on service delivery scope. [4][6]
- **[[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Trung tâm trọng tài quốc tế (International Arbitration Centre)]]** — A dispute resolution body established within the VIFC, governed by commercial arbitration law. [9]
- **[[Foreign Worker Regulations|Giấy phép lao động (Work Permit)]]** — A permit issued by the Management Authority at each city to foreign workers employed by VIFC Members. [2]

## Overview

### What Type of Organisation This Covers and Why VIFC Is Relevant

This guide covers all categories of [[Types Of Financial Institutions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|financial institutions]] seeking to onboard as Members of the VIFC, including [[Securities Activities In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|securities business organisations]], [[How Insurance Companies Can Work With Vifc Step By Step Onboarding Guide|insurance enterprises]], [[Asset Managers In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|asset managers]], and other [[Role Of Foreign Financial Institutions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign and domestic financial institutions]]. The VIFC is relevant because it operates as a unified, internationally-benchmarked financial centre with a distinct legal and regulatory framework, designed to attract global capital and financial institutions. It offers a single, consistent licensing and standards regime applied simultaneously at both the Ho Chi Minh City and Da Nang locations. [8]

## Requirements from the Organisation

### Documents, Capital Minimums, Licences, and Qualifications

- Member securities business organisations must obtain an **Establishment and Operating Licence** from the Management Authority before commencing activities. [1]
- Insurance enterprises, reinsurance enterprises, and insurance brokerage enterprises must also obtain an Establishment and Operating Licence from the Management Authority. [6]
- Member securities business organisations must at all times maintain **equity capital no lower than the minimum charter capital** prescribed under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]]. [1]
- At least **one legally authorised representative** of a Member securities business organisation must reside in Vietnam at all times during operation. [1]
- Foreign employers within the VIFC must obtain **work permits** for foreign workers through the Management Authority; there is no cap on the ratio of foreign workers. [2]

> **Note:** Specific minimum capital amounts, document checklists, and qualification criteria for each institution type are **not fully specified in the source documents provided**. Refer to [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] and guidance issued by the Management Authority for complete requirements.
