# Compliance Due Diligence Questions for the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

This page outlines key compliance due diligence questions relevant to entities evaluating or operating within the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC). The TTTC is governed by a suite of government decrees issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Government Office), covering areas such as [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policies]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|labour and social security]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]]. Compliance due diligence for the TTTC requires careful review of each of these decree areas to assess obligations, restrictions, and risks for financial institutions considering entry or ongoing operations.

## Key Points

- The TTTC is established and governed by a framework of decrees issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Government Office of Vietnam). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] forms the foundational legal basis for the TTTC's existence and structure, making it a primary reference point for any compliance due diligence exercise. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] governs financial policies within the TTTC and is central to understanding [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]], capital rules, and related compliance obligations. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] covers labour, employment, and social security matters, which are relevant to [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] for institutions hiring staff within the centre. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] addresses land use and environmental regulations, relevant to due diligence on physical operations and [[Real Estate And Office Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]]. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] sets out immigration and entry/exit policies, relevant to compliance obligations around [[Foreign Worker Regulations]] and the hiring of international staff. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes the framework for international arbitration within the TTTC, relevant to [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre]] and risk assessment. [4]
- All six identified decrees originate from the same issuing authority, VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ, suggesting a unified regulatory framework for compliance purposes. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] — The decree establishing the Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC), issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] — The decree governing financial policies applicable within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]] — The decree covering labour, employment, and social security within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]] — The decree addressing land use and environmental matters within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] — The decree setting immigration and entry/exit policies for persons operating within the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] — The decree establishing the international arbitration centre associated with the TTTC. [4]
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Government Office of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all six foundational TTTC decrees. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|TTTC]] — Trung tâm tài chính quốc tế; the Vietnam International Financial Centre. [5]
