# Main Goals of the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) is established and governed through a series of decrees issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). These decrees collectively define the legal, financial, labour, land, immigration, banking, foreign exchange, commodities exchange, and dispute resolution frameworks that underpin the centre, reflecting a comprehensive effort to create a structured and internationally oriented financial hub. Key instruments include [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], alongside supporting regulations covering areas such as [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]], [[Foreign Worker Regulations]], [[Environmental Regulations Tttc]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|banking licensing and foreign exchange management]], [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange|commodities exchange operations]], and [[Currency And Capital Controls|capital controls]].

## Key Points

- The legal framework for the TTTC is established through a suite of government decrees issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational establishment of the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Financial policy within the TTTC]] is addressed through a dedicated decree (Decree 324), and its provisions also govern the conditions under which export and import tax incentives and customs procedure benefits are granted to commodities exchange participants. [1][2]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment|Land and environmental regulations]] within the TTTC are governed by a separate decree (Decree 326). [4]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Immigration and entry/exit policies]] for the TTTC are covered under Decree 327. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|An international arbitration centre]] forms part of the TTTC's [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|dispute resolution]] framework under Decree 328. [5]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|Labour, employment, and social security]] within the TTTC are regulated under Decree 325. [1]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Banking licensing and foreign exchange management]] within the TTTC are governed by Decree 329. This decree sets out detailed qualification and fitness standards for senior management of member commercial banks, including requirements for professional ethics, academic qualifications in finance, banking, economics, law, accounting or auditing, and residency in Vietnam during tenure. [2][3][5][6]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Decree 330]] governs the establishment and operation of the Commodities Exchange (Sở Giao Dịch Hàng Hóa) within the TTTC. The managing authority in each city is responsible for reviewing applications, issuing and revoking establishment licences, approving lists of domestically produced commodities permitted for trading, and approving the linked contract lists of foreign commodities exchanges. [1]
- [[Banking Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Banking activities]] permitted under Decree 329 include a range of operations for both domestically funded and fully foreign-funded commercial banks, such as lending, discounting, bank guarantees, factoring, letters of credit, foreign currency transactions, and the purchase and sale of government bonds, government-guaranteed bonds, local government bonds, and corporate bonds issued in Vietnam. [3][5]
- [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Fully foreign-funded commercial banks and foreign bank branches]] that are TTTC members may conduct lending and other credit operations with non-credit organisations on Vietnamese territory, including the purchase of corporate bonds issued in Vietnam (excluding bonds issued by Vietnamese credit institutions) and government-related bonds on both the primary and secondary markets. [5]
- [[Domestic Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Domestically funded commercial banks]] (100% domestic capital) operating within the TTTC may issue bank guarantees, counter-guarantees, and confirmed guarantees in conjunction with Vietnamese credit institutions, subject to specific conditions including full collateralisation or deposit of the guaranteed value. [3]
- The Ministry of Industry and Trade (Bộ Công Thương) is responsible for coordinating with the supervisory authority to inspect the operations of the Commodities Exchange and to implement regulations on incentives and handling of violations related to import/export and commodity trading activities. [1]
- Conditions for receiving [[Incentives For Foreign Investors In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|preferential treatment]] in the Commodities Exchange include holding documentary proof that the goods qualify under the relevant article of Decree 330, and goods subject to specialised inspection (food safety, health, environment, agriculture, science and technology, national defence, and security) must comply with the regulations and procedures of the relevant specialised management authority. [1]
- Professional ethics standards for senior bank personnel are strictly defined: individuals found responsible — through inspection or audit conclusions — for administrative violations in the monetary and banking sector at the highest penalty bracket, or within a specified period, are deemed to fail the professional ethics requirement. [6]

> **Note:** The source excerpts provide substantive provisions from Decree 329 and Decree 330. Some provisions of other decrees referenced in this page are not covered in the provided excerpts and are described based on cover-page metadata only.

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] — Abbreviation for *Trung Tâm Tài Chính* (International Financial Centre of Vietnam), the subject of the series of government decrees. [6]
- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Văn Phòng Chính Phủ]] — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all decrees governing the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] — The foundational decree concerning the establishment of the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] — A decree addressing financial policies within the TTTC; its provisions also govern the granting of export/import tax incentives and customs procedure benefits to commodities exchange participants. [1][2]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|Decree 325]] — A decree governing labour, employment, and social security within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment|Decree 326]] — A decree covering land and environmental matters within the TTTC. [4]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] — A decree regulating immigration and entry/exit policies within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] — A decree establishing and governing the international arbitration centre within the TTTC. [5]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] — A decree governing bank licensing, establishment, and foreign exchange management ([[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|ngoại hối]]) within the TTTC, including detailed qualification standards for senior bank management. [2][3][5][6]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Decree 330]] — A decree governing the establishment and operation of the Commodities Exchange (Sở Giao Dịch Hàng Hóa) within the TTTC, including licensing, supervision, approved commodity lists, and conditions for preferential treatment. [1]
- **Sở Giao Dịch Hàng Hóa** — The Commodities Exchange operating within the TTTC, subject to licensing and supervision by the managing authority in each city. See [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc]]. [1]
- **Bộ Công Thương** — The Ministry of Industry and Trade; responsible for coordinating inspection of the Commodities Exchange and implementing regulations on incentives and violation handling. [1]
- **Ngân hàng thương mại 100% vốn nước ngoài** — A fully foreign-funded commercial bank; a category of [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign bank]] permitted to operate as a TTTC member under Decree 329. [5]
