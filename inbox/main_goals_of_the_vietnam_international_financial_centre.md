# Main Goals of the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) is established and governed through a series of decrees issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). These decrees collectively define the legal, financial, labour, land, immigration, banking, foreign exchange, and dispute resolution frameworks that underpin the centre, reflecting a comprehensive effort to create a structured and internationally oriented financial hub. Key instruments include [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]], alongside supporting regulations covering areas such as [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions]], [[Foreign Worker Regulations]], [[Environmental Regulations Tttc]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|banking licensing and foreign exchange management]], and [[Currency And Capital Controls|capital controls]].

## Key Points

- The legal framework for the TTTC is established through a suite of government decrees issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] provides the foundational establishment of the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Financial policy within the TTTC]] is addressed through a dedicated decree (Decree 324). [2]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment|Land and environmental regulations]] within the TTTC are governed by a separate decree (Decree 326). [4]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Immigration and entry/exit policies]] for the TTTC are covered under Decree 327. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|An international arbitration centre]] forms part of the TTTC's dispute resolution framework under Decree 328. [5]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|Labour, employment, and social security]] within the TTTC are regulated under Decree 325. [1]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Banking licensing and foreign exchange management]] within the TTTC are governed by Decree 329, also issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. [1]

> **Note:** The source excerpts provide only cover-page metadata (issuing authority and decree titles). Detailed goals and provisions within each decree are not covered in the provided excerpts.

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] — Abbreviation for *Trung Tâm Tài Chính* (International Financial Centre of Vietnam), the subject of the series of government decrees. [6]
- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Văn Phòng Chính Phủ]] — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all decrees governing the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]] — The foundational decree concerning the establishment of the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] — A decree addressing financial policies within the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|Decree 325]] — A decree governing labour, employment, and social security within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment|Decree 326]] — A decree covering land and environmental matters within the TTTC. [4]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] — A decree regulating immigration and entry/exit policies within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] — A decree establishing and governing the international arbitration centre within the TTTC. [5]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] — A decree governing bank licensing, establishment, and foreign exchange management ([[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|ngoại hối]]) within the TTTC. [1]
