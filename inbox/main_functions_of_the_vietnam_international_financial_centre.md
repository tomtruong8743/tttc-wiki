# Main Functions of the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) is governed by a series of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office ([[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ]]), covering key operational areas including [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policy]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment|land and environment]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|labour and social security]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|banking and foreign exchange]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange|commodities exchange]]. Together, these decrees define the main functions and regulatory framework under which the TTTC operates.

## Key Points

- The TTTC was established under a dedicated decree issued by the Vietnamese Government Office, forming the legal foundation for its functions. [5]
- [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|Financial policy]] within the TTTC is regulated by a specific decree, suggesting that one of the centre's core functions is to provide a distinct financial and tax environment. [1]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Immigration policy]] is governed by a separate decree, indicating that facilitating the entry and residence of foreign workers and investors is a key function of the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment|Land use and environmental management]] form a dedicated area of regulation, suggesting the TTTC has specific functions related to physical infrastructure and environmental compliance. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|International arbitration]] is established as a distinct function within the TTTC, providing [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|dispute resolution mechanisms]] for participants in the centre. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|Labour, employment, and social security]] are regulated separately within the TTTC, indicating that workforce management is a recognised operational function. [6]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange|Commodities exchange]] activities are a defined function of the TTTC, with specific rules governing the establishment and operation of a commodities exchange (Sở giao dịch hàng hóa) for members of the centre. [1][3]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Banking and foreign exchange]] activities are regulated within the TTTC, including licensing requirements and qualification standards for senior bank officers operating within the centre. [2]
- The [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange|Commodities Exchange]] within the TTTC operates under state management oversight, with the Ministry of Industry and Trade (Bộ Công Thương) and the local Operating Authority (Cơ quan điều hành) sharing supervisory and licensing responsibilities. [1]
- [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|Export and import tax incentives]] applicable to goods traded through the TTTC Commodities Exchange are governed by [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324 on financial policy]], reinforcing that financial policy is a cross-cutting function spanning multiple activity areas. [1]
- Dissolution and winding-up procedures for [[Financial Services In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|insurance enterprises]] operating within the TTTC follow a defined priority order for settling liabilities — including wages, social insurance, tax debts, and other obligations — before formal dissolution under guidance from the Operating Authority. [6]
- [[Infrastructure Planned For The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Physical infrastructure requirements]] are a formal element of the TTTC's regulatory framework, with registered organisations required to document office space, equipment, and technology systems as part of their registration process. [5]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|**TTTC**]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, established by government decree as a specialised financial zone with its own regulatory framework. [5]
- [[Financial Center Decrees|**Decree (Nghị định)**]] — A legal instrument issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ) used to regulate specific areas of the TTTC's operations. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|**International Arbitration Centre**]] — A dedicated body established within the TTTC framework to handle [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|dispute resolution]] at an international level. [4]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment|**Land and Environment Policy**]] — Regulations governing the use of land and environmental responsibilities within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|**Labour and Social Security Policy**]] — Rules governing employment conditions and social security entitlements for workers within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange|**Sở giao dịch hàng hóa (Commodities Exchange)**]] — A regulated exchange established within the TTTC for the buying and selling of commodities, including through linkages with foreign commodities exchanges. [1][3]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|**Cơ quan điều hành (Operating Authority)**]] — The local administrative body in each city responsible for licensing, approving, and supervising activities within the TTTC, including issuing and revoking establishment licences for the Commodities Exchange. [1][2]
- **Bộ Công Thương (Ministry of Industry and Trade)** — The central government ministry responsible for coordinating inspection of commodities exchange activities and overseeing import/export incentive rules within the TTTC. [1]
