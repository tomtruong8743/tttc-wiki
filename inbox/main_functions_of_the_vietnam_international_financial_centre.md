# Main Functions of the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) is governed by a series of decrees issued by the Vietnamese Government Office ([[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ]]), covering key operational areas including [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policy]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment|land and environment]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|labour and social security]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]]. Together, these decrees define the main functions and regulatory framework under which the TTTC operates.

## Key Points

- The TTTC was established under a dedicated decree issued by the Vietnamese Government Office, forming the legal foundation for its functions. [5]
- [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|Financial policy]] within the TTTC is regulated by a specific decree, suggesting that one of the centre's core functions is to provide a distinct financial and tax environment. [1]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Immigration policy]] is governed by a separate decree, indicating that facilitating the entry and residence of foreign workers and investors is a key function of the TTTC. [2]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment|Land use and environmental management]] form a dedicated area of regulation, suggesting the TTTC has specific functions related to physical infrastructure and environmental compliance. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|International arbitration]] is established as a distinct function within the TTTC, providing dispute resolution mechanisms for participants in the centre. [4]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|Labour, employment, and social security]] are regulated separately within the TTTC, indicating that workforce management is a recognised operational function. [6]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|**TTTC**]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, established by government decree as a specialised financial zone with its own regulatory framework. [5]
- [[Financial Center Decrees|**Decree (Nghị định)**]] — A legal instrument issued by the Vietnamese Government Office (VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ) used to regulate specific areas of the TTTC's operations. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|**International Arbitration Centre**]] — A dedicated body established within the TTTC framework to handle dispute resolution at an international level. [4]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment|**Land and Environment Policy**]] — Regulations governing the use of land and environmental responsibilities within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|**Labour and Social Security Policy**]] — Rules governing employment conditions and social security entitlements for workers within the TTTC. [6]
