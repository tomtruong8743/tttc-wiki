# Vietnam International Financial Centre for Investment Banking Interns

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) is governed by a series of decrees issued by [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|the Vietnamese Government Office]], establishing its legal and operational framework. These decrees cover a broad range of topics relevant to investment banking interns, including [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]], [[Foreign Worker Regulations|immigration and labor policy]], [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|land and environment]], [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|international arbitration]], [[Banking Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|banking and foreign exchange]], [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange|commodities exchange]], and the foundational establishment of the centre itself under [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]].

---

## Key Points

- The TTTC is established and governed by a set of decrees issued by **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** (the Government Office of Vietnam). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] covers the formal establishment of the TTTC. [5]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] addresses [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]] within the TTTC, including conditions for [[Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|tax incentives]] and export/import duties. [6]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security]] covers labor, employment, and social security within the TTTC. [original]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment]] governs land and environmental matters within the TTTC. [original]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] sets out immigration and entry/exit policies applicable within the TTTC, relevant to [[Foreign Worker Regulations|foreign workers and interns]]. [original]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration centre as part of the TTTC's [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution framework]]. [original]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] governs [[Banking Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|banking licensing and establishment]], as well as [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]] within the TTTC. [1][2][3][5]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Decree 330]] establishes and governs the [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange|Commodities Exchange]] (Sở giao dịch hàng hóa) within the TTTC. [4]
- Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], senior management of member commercial banks — including the CEO, Deputy CEO, and Chief Accountant — must meet strict qualification and residency requirements. [1][3]
- [[Rules For Banks Operating In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Member banks]] applying international accounting standards are exempt from certain Vietnamese regulations, including mandatory holdings of Vietnamese government bonds and maximum foreign currency position limits relative to equity. [2]
- [[Capital Movement Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|TTTC members]] may borrow foreign currency from individuals and organizations outside Vietnam's territory and must comply with information declaration and reporting obligations. [5]
- Fully foreign-owned TTTC members may lend to individuals and organizations outside Vietnam's territory, subject to [[Reporting Obligations For Financial Institutions In Tttc|reporting requirements]]. [5]
- Members not 100% foreign-owned must meet additional conditions under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] before lending abroad. [5]
- The [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Commodities Exchange]] licensing process — including issuance, re-issuance, suspension, and revocation of establishment licenses — is managed by the city-level operating authority (Cơ quan điều hành tại các thành phố). [4]
- Tax and customs incentives for commodities traded through the Exchange are governed by [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]], which sets out the relevant [[Conditions For Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|conditions for tax incentives]]. [4]

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|**TTTC**]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, established by government decree as a special financial zone. [original]
- [[Financial Center Decrees|**Decree**]] — A legal instrument issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Government Office) setting binding rules and policies for the TTTC. [original]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|**Chính sách xuất nhập cảnh**]] — Immigration and entry/exit policy applicable within the TTTC, governing the movement of foreign nationals including interns. [original]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|**Lao động, việc làm và an sinh xã hội**]] — Labor, employment, and social security regulations governing workers within the TTTC. [original]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment|**Đất đai và môi trường**]] — Land and environmental regulations applicable to operations within the TTTC. [original]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|**Trung tâm trọng tài quốc tế**]] — The International Arbitration Centre established within the TTTC to handle [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution]]. [original]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|**Ngân hàng thương mại thành viên**]] — Member commercial bank operating within the TTTC, subject to licensing, governance, and [[Risk Management Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|prudential requirements]] under Decree 329. [1][2][3]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|**Quản lý ngoại hối**]] — [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|Foreign exchange management]] rules applicable to TTTC members, including rules on borrowing from and lending to parties outside Vietnam. [5]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|**Sở giao dịch hàng hóa**]] — The Commodities Exchange established within the TTTC under Decree 330, subject to government oversight by the [[Regulatory Bodies Supervising The Vietnam International Financial Centre|city-level operating authority]] and the Ministry of Industry and Trade (Bộ Công Thương). [4]
- **LEV / LCR / NSFR** — International prudential ratios (Leverage Ratio, [[Risk Management Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Liquidity Coverage Ratio]], Net Stable Funding Ratio) applied to member banks within the TTTC under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]]. [2]

## Banking Governance and Prudential Standards (Decree 329)

Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], [[Rules For Banks Operating In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|member commercial banks]] within the TTTC are subject to detailed governance and prudential requirements: [1][2][3]

- **Senior management qualifications:** The CEO (Tổng giám đốc), Deputy CEO (Phó Tổng giám đốc), and Chief Accountant (Kế toán trưởng) of member banks must hold at least a university-level degree in finance, banking, economics, business administration, law, accounting, or auditing — or a degree in another field combined with at least three years of direct experience in finance or banking. [1]
- **Residency requirement:** Senior executives must reside in Vietnam during their term of office. [1][3]
- **Professional ethics:** Persons responsible for violations leading to administrative penalties against Vietnamese credit institutions or member banks — particularly in areas of licensing, governance, capital, credit, or prudential ratios — may be deemed to lack professional ethics and disqualified from senior roles. [3]
- **Prudential ratios:** Member banks must comply with international prudential ratios including the Leverage Ratio (LEV), Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR), and Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR). The [[Supervision Mechanisms In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|supervisory authority]] may require stricter ratios where warranted by risk. [2]
- **Exemptions for banks applying international accounting standards:** Such banks are exempt from requirements to hold Vietnamese government bonds or government-guaranteed bonds, and are not subject to the maximum foreign currency position limit relative to equity under Vietnamese law. [2]
