# Vietnam International Financial Centre for Investment Banking Interns

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) is governed by a series of decrees issued by [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|the Vietnamese Government Office]], establishing its legal and operational framework. These decrees cover a broad range of topics relevant to investment banking interns, including [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]], [[Foreign Worker Regulations|immigration and labor policy]], [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|land and environment]], [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|international arbitration]], and the foundational establishment of the centre itself under [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]].

---

## Key Points

- The TTTC is established and governed by a set of decrees issued by **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** (the Government Office of Vietnam). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] covers the formal establishment of the TTTC. [5]
- [[Financial Center Decrees|Decree 324]] addresses [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]] within the TTTC. [1]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security]] covers labor, employment, and social security within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment]] governs land and environmental matters within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] sets out immigration and entry/exit policies applicable within the TTTC, relevant to [[Foreign Worker Regulations|foreign workers and interns]]. [2]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration centre as part of the TTTC's [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution framework]]. [4]

---

## Definitions

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|**TTTC**]] — The Vietnam International Financial Centre, established by government decree as a special financial zone. [5]
- [[Financial Center Decrees|**Decree**]] — A legal instrument issued by VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ (the Government Office) setting binding rules and policies for the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|**Chính sách xuất nhập cảnh**]] — Immigration and entry/exit policy applicable within the TTTC, governing the movement of foreign nationals including interns. [2]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|**Lao động, việc làm và an sinh xã hội**]] — Labor, employment, and social security regulations governing workers within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment|**Đất đai và môi trường**]] — Land and environmental regulations applicable to operations within the TTTC. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|**Trung tâm trọng tài quốc tế**]] — The International Arbitration Centre established within the TTTC to handle [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|dispute resolution]]. [4]
