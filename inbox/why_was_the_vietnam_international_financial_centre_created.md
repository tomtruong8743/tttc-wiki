# Why Was the Vietnam International Financial Centre Created

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) was established through a series of decrees issued by the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Vietnamese Government Office]], with [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] serving as the foundational legal instrument for its creation. Supporting decrees address a broad range of regulatory domains — including [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policy]], [[Foreign Worker Regulations|labour and social security]], [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]], [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|international arbitration]], [[Currency And Capital Controls|banking and foreign exchange]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange|commodities exchange]] — suggesting that the TTTC was designed as a comprehensive, specially governed financial zone intended to attract international business and investment to Vietnam.

The detailed regulatory content of these decrees reveals the depth of the TTTC's institutional design. [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] includes granular rules on [[Foreign Currency Transactions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign currency borrowing and lending]], the [[Licensing And Approvals For Financial Institutions In Tttc|standards for bank management appointees]], and the specific activities permitted for [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|100% foreign-owned commercial banks and foreign bank branches]] operating within the centre. [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] extends to operational documentation requirements for registered organisations, including infrastructure declarations covering office premises, equipment, and technology platforms — indicating that the TTTC framework governs institutions at a granular operational level, not merely at the level of broad policy.

## Key Points

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] was issued by the Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) as the primary decree establishing the TTTC. [6]
- A dedicated financial policy decree ([[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]]) was issued alongside the establishment decree, indicating that specialised financial regulations were considered central to the centre's purpose. [2]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]] was created to govern labour, employment, and social security within the TTTC, suggesting the centre was intended to host a significant workforce. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]] addresses land and environmental matters specific to the TTTC, implying the centre occupies a defined physical territory with its own [[Land Use Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|land governance framework]]. [4]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] establishes [[Immigration And Residency Policies For Tttc Workers|immigration and entry/exit policies]] for the TTTC, indicating an intent to facilitate the movement of foreign professionals and investors. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] establishes an [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|international arbitration centre]] within the TTTC, suggesting the centre was designed to support international commercial [[Dispute Resolution In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|dispute resolution]]. [5]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] governs [[Banking Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|banking licensing, establishment]], and [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]] within the TTTC, reflecting the centre's ambition to operate as a full-service international financial hub. [1]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] establishes that TTTC members may borrow foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to disclosure and reporting obligations. [4]
- Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]], 100% foreign-owned member entities are permitted to lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnamese territory, subject to disclosure and reporting requirements; members not 100% foreign-owned must satisfy additional lending conditions. [4]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] sets out the activities that 100% foreign-owned commercial banks and foreign bank branches operating as TTTC members may conduct with non-credit-institution organisations on Vietnamese territory, including lending, discounting, bank guarantees, factoring, letters of credit, and trading in corporate bonds and government bonds. [5]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] includes detailed standards for the professional ethics and qualifications of individuals appointed to senior management positions — including members of supervisory boards and chief accountants — at member banks within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] includes standardised application forms requiring registered organisations to declare their physical infrastructure, including total office floor area, office equipment, and technology systems supporting [[Capital Raising In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|crowdfunding platforms]] and [[Asset Managers In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|client asset management operations]]. [1]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Decree 330]] governs the establishment and operation of a commodities exchange within the TTTC, with provisions referencing pre-agreed settlement arrangements. [2]
- All decrees were issued by the same authority — Văn Phòng Chính Phủ (the Government Office) — reflecting a coordinated, top-down policy initiative. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- **[[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]]** — Abbreviation for Trung Tâm Tài Chính Quốc Tế, the Vietnam International Financial Centre, established by government decree. [6]
- **[[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]]** — The foundational government decree formally establishing the TTTC. [6]
- **[[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]]** — A decree issued by the Government Office governing financial policies within the TTTC, including operational and infrastructure documentation requirements for registered institutions. [2][1]
- **[[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]]** — A decree governing labour, employment, and social security matters within the TTTC. [6]
- **[[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]]** — A decree governing [[Land Use Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|land use]] and [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|environmental regulations]] within the TTTC. [4]
- **[[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]]** — A decree establishing [[Immigration And Residency Policies For Tttc Workers|immigration and entry/exit policies]] applicable to the TTTC. [3]
- **[[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]]** — A decree establishing an [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|international arbitration centre]] as part of the TTTC framework. [5]
- **[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]]** — A decree governing [[Banking Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|banking licensing and establishment]] and [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]] within the TTTC, including detailed rules on cross-border lending and borrowing and management qualification standards. [1][4][5][6]
- **[[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Decree 330]]** — A decree governing the establishment and operation of a [[Financial Products And Instruments In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|commodities exchange]] within the TTTC. [2]
- **[[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Văn Phòng Chính Phủ]]** — The Vietnamese Government Office; the issuing authority for all TTTC-related decrees. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- **Bản thuyết minh cơ sở vật chất** — A standardised infrastructure declaration form required under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] for organisations registering to operate in the TTTC, covering office space, equipment, and technology systems. [1]
- **[[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|Ngoại hối]]** — Foreign exchange; governed in the TTTC context by [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]], which sets out rules on foreign currency borrowing, lending, and reporting obligations for TTTC members. [4]
