# Why Was the Vietnam International Financial Centre Created

## Summary

The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) was established through a series of decrees issued by the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Vietnamese Government Office]], with [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] serving as the foundational legal instrument for its creation. Supporting decrees address a broad range of regulatory domains — including [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policy]], [[Foreign Worker Regulations|labour and social security]], [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]], [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|international arbitration]], and [[Currency And Capital Controls|banking and foreign exchange]] — suggesting that the TTTC was designed as a comprehensive, specially governed financial zone intended to attract international business and investment to Vietnam.

## Key Points

- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] was issued by the Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) as the primary decree establishing the TTTC. [6]
- A dedicated financial policy decree ([[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]]) was issued alongside the establishment decree, indicating that specialised financial regulations were considered central to the centre's purpose. [2]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security]] was created to govern labour, employment, and social security within the TTTC, suggesting the centre was intended to host a significant workforce. [6]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment]] addresses land and environmental matters specific to the TTTC, implying the centre occupies a defined physical territory with its own land governance framework. [4]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] establishes immigration and entry/exit policies for the TTTC, indicating an intent to facilitate the movement of foreign professionals and investors. [3]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an international arbitration centre within the TTTC, suggesting the centre was designed to support international commercial dispute resolution. [5]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] governs banking licensing, establishment, and foreign exchange management within the TTTC, reflecting the centre's ambition to operate as a full-service international financial hub. [1]
- All decrees were issued by the same authority — Văn Phòng Chính Phủ (the Government Office) — reflecting a coordinated, top-down policy initiative. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions

- **[[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]]** — Abbreviation for Trung Tâm Tài Chính Quốc Tế, the Vietnam International Financial Centre, established by government decree. [6]
- **[[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323]]** — The foundational government decree formally establishing the TTTC. [6]
- **[[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]]** — A decree issued by the Government Office governing financial policies within the TTTC. [2]
- **[[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|Decree 325]]** — A decree governing labour, employment, and social security matters within the TTTC. [6]
- **[[Decree 326 Land And Environment|Decree 326]]** — A decree governing land use and environmental regulations within the TTTC. [4]
- **[[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]]** — A decree establishing immigration and entry/exit policies applicable to the TTTC. [3]
- **[[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]]** — A decree establishing an international arbitration centre as part of the TTTC framework. [5]
- **[[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]]** — A decree governing banking licensing, establishment, and foreign exchange management within the TTTC. [1]
- **[[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Văn Phòng Chính Phủ]]** — The Vietnamese Government Office; the issuing authority for all TTTC-related decrees. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
