The source documents available relate to a series of government decrees issued by the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Vietnamese Government Office]] concerning the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]], covering areas such as [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment]], [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policy]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment|land and environment]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|labour and social security]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]], and [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]]. ## Key Points

- All six source documents were issued by the **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** (Office of the Government of Vietnam), indicating these are official national-level decrees related to the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- The decrees cover a broad range of policy areas associated with the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]], including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment]], [[Financial Center Decrees|financial regulations]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment|land and environment]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|labour]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration]], and [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|international arbitration]] [1][2][3][4][5][6].

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|**TTTC**]] — Abbreviation for *Trung tâm Tài chính Quốc tế* (Vietnam International Financial Centre), the subject of the series of government decrees referenced in the source documents [5].
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all decrees referenced in these sources [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|**Nghị định 323**]] — Decree on the establishment of the TTTC [5].
- [[Financial Center Decrees|**Nghị định**]] — Vietnamese term for a government decree, the legislative instrument used to regulate the TTTC [1][2][3][4][5][6].
