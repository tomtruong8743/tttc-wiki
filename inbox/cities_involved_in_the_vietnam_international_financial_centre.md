# Cities Involved in the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary

The source documents available relate to a series of government decrees issued by the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Vietnamese Government Office]] concerning the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC)]], covering areas such as [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment]], [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policy]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|banking and foreign exchange]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|commodities exchange]]. However, the specific cities involved in or designated to host the TTTC are not detailed in the provided source excerpts.

---

## Key Points

- All source documents were issued by the **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** (Office of the Government of Vietnam), indicating these are official national-level decrees related to the [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|TTTC]] [1][2][3][4][5][6][7][8].
- The decrees cover a broad range of policy areas associated with the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]], including [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment]], [[Financial Center Decrees|financial regulations]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration]], [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|international arbitration]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|banking and foreign exchange management]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|commodities exchange]] [1][2][3][4][5][6][7][8].
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] addresses the licensing and establishment of banks, as well as [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management]], within the TTTC. It references activities by member banks (**ngân hàng thương mại thành viên**), [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|100% foreign-owned commercial banks]], and [[Domestic Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|100% domestically-owned commercial banks]] operating within the TTTC framework [1][2][3][4][6].
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] includes provisions on [[Cross Border Payments In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|cross-border lending and borrowing]], [[Capital Movement Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign currency borrowing from overseas individuals and organisations]], and lending abroad by TTTC member entities [2].
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Decree 330]] addresses the establishment and operation of a [[Financial Products And Instruments In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|commodities exchange]] within the TTTC [5].
- No specific cities or geographic locations are named within the provided excerpts as hosts or participants of the TTTC (uncertain) [1][2][3][4][5][6][7][8].

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|**TTTC**]] — Abbreviation for *Trung tâm Tài chính Quốc tế* (Vietnam International Financial Centre), the subject of the series of government decrees referenced in the source documents [7].
- **VĂN PHÒNG CHÍNH PHỦ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all decrees referenced in these sources [1][2][3][4][5][6][7][8].
- [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|**Nghị định 323**]] — Decree on the establishment of the TTTC [7].
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|**Nghị định 329**]] — Decree on the licensing and establishment of banks, and [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|foreign exchange management]] (*quản lý ngoại hối*) at the TTTC [1][2][3][4][6].
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|**Nghị định 330**]] — Decree on the establishment and operation of the commodities exchange within the TTTC [5].
- [[Financial Center Decrees|**Nghị định**]] — Vietnamese term for a government decree, the legislative instrument used to regulate the TTTC [1][2][3][4][5][6][7][8].
- **Ngân hàng thương mại thành viên** — Member commercial bank; a bank operating as a member within the [[Types Of Financial Institutions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|TTTC framework]] [6].
- **Ngân hàng thương mại 100% vốn nước ngoài** — 100% foreign-owned commercial bank; a category of [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign bank]] permitted to operate within the TTTC [1][4].
- **Ngân hàng thương mại 100% vốn trong nước** — 100% domestically-owned commercial bank; a category of [[Domestic Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|domestic bank]] operating within the TTTC [3].
- [[Ngoai Hoi In The Context Of Tttc|**Ngoại hối**]] — Foreign exchange; the subject of [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign exchange management regulations]] under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] [2].
