# What documents discuss financial centers?

## Summary
Several official decrees issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) address the establishment and regulation of financial centers (TTTC — Trung Tâm Tài Chính). These documents cover a range of topics related to the organization and operation of financial centers, including land and environment, immigration policy, labor and social welfare, international arbitration, and the establishment of the centers themselves.

## Key Points
- Decree 323 concerns the establishment of financial centers (TTTC), issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. [4]
- Decree 325 addresses labor, employment, and social welfare within financial centers, issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. [5]
- Decree 326 covers land and environmental policies related to financial centers, issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. [2]
- Decree 327 addresses immigration entry and exit policies within financial centers, issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. [1]
- Decree 328 concerns the International Arbitration Center belonging to the financial centers, issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam. [3]

## Definitions
- **TTTC (Trung Tâm Tài Chính)** — Vietnamese term for "Financial Center," the subject of the regulatory decrees listed in the sources. [4]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — The Office of the Government of Vietnam, the issuing authority for all decrees referenced in the sources. [1]
- **Trung tâm trọng tài quốc tế** — International Arbitration Center, an institution operating within the financial center framework. [3]
