# Problems the Vietnam International Financial Centre Is Solving

## Summary

The [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) was established through a suite of government decrees issued by the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Office of the Government of Vietnam]], each targeting a specific structural or regulatory challenge facing Vietnam's financial and economic landscape. These decrees — covering areas such as financial policy, land and environment, labour and social security, immigration, and international arbitration — collectively address longstanding barriers to foreign investment, institutional development, and international integration. By creating a dedicated regulatory framework, the TTTC aims to resolve problems that have historically limited Vietnam's competitiveness as a global financial hub.

---

## Key Points

- The establishment of the TTTC through [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] signals a government-led effort to address the absence of a dedicated, internationally competitive financial centre in Vietnam [5].
- [[Financial Center Decrees|A dedicated set of decrees]] was issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) to solve specific regulatory gaps across multiple domains simultaneously [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|Financial policy challenges]] are addressed through a specific decree (Decree 324) targeting fiscal and financial regulatory barriers within the TTTC [1].
- Problems related to [[Foreign Worker Regulations|immigration and the movement of skilled foreign workers]] are addressed through Decree 327, which establishes tailored entry and exit policies for the TTTC [2].
- [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|Land use and environmental regulatory challenges]] specific to the TTTC zone are addressed through Decree 326 [3].
- [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|The lack of internationally recognised dispute resolution infrastructure]] is addressed through Decree 328, which establishes an [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|International Arbitration Centre]] within the TTTC [4].
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|Labour, employment, and social security challenges]] for workers within the TTTC are addressed through a dedicated decree (Decree 325) [6].

---

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|**TTTC**]] — Abbreviation for *Trung Tâm Tài Chính*, the Vietnam International Financial Centre; a specially regulated zone designed to address structural gaps in Vietnam's financial and investment environment [5].
- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|**Văn Phòng Chính Phủ**]] — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all decrees establishing and governing the TTTC [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|**International Arbitration Centre**]] — A dispute resolution body established within the TTTC to address the problem of inadequate international-standard arbitration infrastructure in Vietnam [4].
- [[Financial Center Decrees|**Regulatory decrees**]] — A set of numbered government decrees (323–328) each solving a distinct category of problems within the TTTC framework [1][2][3][4][5][6].
