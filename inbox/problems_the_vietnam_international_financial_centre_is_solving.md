# Problems the Vietnam International Financial Centre Is Solving

## Summary

The [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) was established through a suite of government decrees issued by the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Office of the Government of Vietnam]], each targeting a specific structural or regulatory challenge facing Vietnam's financial and economic landscape. These decrees — covering areas such as financial policy, land and environment, labour and social security, immigration, international arbitration, banking and foreign exchange, and commodities exchange — collectively address longstanding barriers to foreign investment, institutional development, and international integration. By creating a dedicated regulatory framework, the TTTC aims to resolve problems that have historically limited Vietnam's competitiveness as a global financial hub.

---

## Key Points

- The establishment of the TTTC through [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] signals a government-led effort to address the absence of a dedicated, internationally competitive financial centre in Vietnam [5].
- [[Financial Center Decrees|A dedicated set of decrees]] was issued by the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) to solve specific regulatory gaps across multiple domains simultaneously [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|Financial policy challenges]] are addressed through a specific decree ([[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]]) targeting fiscal and financial regulatory barriers within the TTTC [1].
- Problems related to [[Foreign Worker Regulations|immigration and the movement of skilled foreign workers]] are addressed through [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], which establishes tailored entry and exit policies for the TTTC, including differentiated criteria for strategic investors, senior managers, executives, experts, and highly skilled workers [6].
- [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|Land use and environmental regulatory challenges]] specific to the TTTC zone are addressed through [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]] [3].
- [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|The lack of internationally recognised dispute resolution infrastructure]] is addressed through [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]], which establishes an International Arbitration Centre within the TTTC with defined jurisdiction over investment and business disputes — while explicitly excluding administrative, labour, and personal status disputes from its scope [2].
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|Labour, employment, and social security challenges]] for workers within the TTTC are addressed through a dedicated decree ([[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]]) [6].
- The absence of a regulated [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|commodities exchange]] within Vietnam's financial system is addressed through [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange]], which governs the establishment and operation of a commodities exchange (Sở giao dịch hàng hóa) within the TTTC, including conditions for preferential treatment and state management responsibilities [1][5].
- [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Foreign exchange management challenges]], including rules for cross-border borrowing and lending by TTTC members, are addressed through [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]], which provides a tailored framework distinguishing between fully foreign-owned and domestically owned member institutions [3][4].
- The problem of restricted market access for [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign banks]] is addressed through Decree 329, which specifies the range of activities that fully foreign-owned commercial banks and branches of foreign banks may conduct with non-credit organisations operating within Vietnam [4].

## Definitions

- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|**TTTC**]] — Abbreviation for *Trung Tâm Tài Chính*, the Vietnam International Financial Centre; a specially regulated zone designed to address structural gaps in Vietnam's financial and investment environment [5].
- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|**Văn Phòng Chính Phủ**]] — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all decrees establishing and governing the TTTC [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|**International Arbitration Centre**]] — A dispute resolution body established within the TTTC to address the problem of inadequate international-standard arbitration infrastructure in Vietnam; has jurisdiction over investment and business disputes by agreement of the parties, but not over administrative, labour, or personal rights disputes [2].
- [[Financial Center Decrees|**Regulatory decrees**]] — A set of numbered government decrees (323–330) each solving a distinct category of problems within the TTTC framework [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|**Sở giao dịch hàng hóa**]] — Commodities exchange established within the TTTC under Decree 330; addresses the absence of a regulated commodities trading venue in Vietnam's financial system [1][5].
- [[Immigration And Residency Policies For Tttc Workers|**Nhà đầu tư chiến lược**]] — Strategic investor; a category of foreign national eligible for preferential visa and residency treatment under [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]], with criteria set by the TTTC's governing authority (Cơ quan điều hành) [6].

## Problems Addressed by Each Decree

### Decree 323 — Absence of a Dedicated Financial Centre
Vietnam lacked a purpose-built, internationally competitive financial centre. [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc]] creates the legal basis for the TTTC as a distinct zone with its own governance and regulatory framework [5].

### Decree 324 — Financial and Fiscal Policy Gaps
Vietnam's standard financial and tax regime was not calibrated to attract international financial institutions. [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc]] addresses this by establishing dedicated [[Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|tax incentives]] and financial policies within the TTTC, including preferential treatment for export and import taxes applicable to the [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|commodities exchange]] [1][5].

### Decree 325 — Labour and Social Security Barriers
Standard Vietnamese labour and social security rules presented obstacles to deploying international workforces. [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc]] creates tailored rules for [[Labor And Employment Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|labour, employment, and social security]] within the TTTC [6].

### Decree 326 — Land Use and Environmental Complexity
The absence of streamlined land and environmental rules for a financial zone created uncertainty for developers and occupiers. [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc]] provides dedicated [[Land Use Policies In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|land and environment policies]] for the TTTC [3].

### Decree 327 — Immigration and Talent Mobility Constraints
Vietnam's standard immigration framework did not provide sufficient flexibility to attract [[Attracting International Financial Talent To Tttc|international financial talent]]. [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy]] introduces differentiated visa, temporary residence, and permanent residence criteria for strategic investors, senior managers, executives, experts, scientists, and highly skilled workers, with criteria cross-referenced to Decree 219/2025/NĐ-CP on foreign workers in Vietnam [6].

### Decree 328 — Lack of International-Standard Dispute Resolution
The absence of a recognised international arbitration body within Vietnam was a deterrent to foreign financial institutions. [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center]] establishes an International Arbitration Centre with jurisdiction over investment and business disputes arising from TTTC activities, subject to arbitration agreements between parties. Courts may not refuse to hear applications to annul arbitral awards or decisions except in defined circumstances. The Centre does not have jurisdiction over administrative acts, labour disputes, personal rights matters, or matters already resolved by a competent Vietnamese authority [2].

### Decree 329 — Banking Licensing and Foreign Exchange Management
Vietnam's existing banking and foreign exchange rules were not designed for a hub with significant cross-border capital flows. [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange]] addresses this by:
