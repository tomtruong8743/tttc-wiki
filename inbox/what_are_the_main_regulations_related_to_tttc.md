# What are the main regulations related to TTTC?

## Summary
The main regulations related to TTTC (Trung tâm Tài chính - Financial Center) consist of a series of government decrees issued by the Office of the Government (Văn phòng Chính phủ), covering key aspects such as the establishment of the financial center, financial policies, entry and exit policies, international arbitration, and the operation of commodity exchanges within the TTTC framework.

## Key Points
- Decree 323 governs the establishment of the TTTC (Trung tâm Tài chính), issued by the Office of the Government. [5]
- Decree 324 addresses financial policies within the TTTC, issued by the Office of the Government. [2]
- Decree 327 sets out entry and exit (immigration) policies applicable within the TTTC, issued by the Office of the Government. [3]
- Decree 328 regulates the International Arbitration Center (Trung tâm Trọng tài Quốc tế) belonging to the TTTC, issued by the Office of the Government. [4]
- Decree 330 governs the establishment and operation of commodity exchanges (Sở Giao dịch Hàng hóa) within the TTTC, issued by the Office of the Government. [1]

## Definitions
- **TTTC** — Trung tâm Tài chính, meaning "Financial Center," the subject entity governed by the series of decrees listed above [5]
- **Sở Giao dịch Hàng hóa** — Commodity Exchange, an institution established and operating within the TTTC framework [1]
- **Trung tâm Trọng tài Quốc tế** — International Arbitration Center, an entity belonging to the TTTC [4]
- **Văn phòng Chính phủ** — Office of the Government, the issuing authority for all the decrees related to TTTC [1][2][3][4][5]
