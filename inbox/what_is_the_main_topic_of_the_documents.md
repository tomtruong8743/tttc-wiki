# What is the main topic of the documents?

## Summary
The documents are a collection of decrees (Nghị định) issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), all relating to the establishment and regulation of a Financial Centre (Trung tâm Tài chính - TTTC). The decrees cover various aspects of the centre's operations, including its founding, banking and foreign exchange management, financial policy, land and environment, immigration policy, international arbitration, labour and social welfare matters, and the establishment and operation of a commodity exchange.

## Key Points
- The documents are official decrees issued by the **Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ)**. [1][2][3][4][5][6][7][8]
- Decree 323 concerns the **establishment of the Financial Centre (TTTC)**. [4]
- Decree 324 covers **financial policy** within the Financial Centre. [2]
- Decree 325 covers **labour, employment, and social welfare** within the Financial Centre. [5]
- Decree 326 addresses **land and environment** matters within the Financial Centre. [3]
- Decree 327 addresses **entry and exit (immigration) policies** applicable within the Financial Centre. [6]
- Decree 328 relates to the **International Arbitration Centre** operating under the Financial Centre. [7]
- Decree 329 covers **licensing and establishment of banks, and foreign exchange management** within the Financial Centre. [8]
- Decree 330 covers the **establishment and operation of a Commodity Exchange (Sở giao dịch hàng hóa)** within the Financial Centre. [1]

## Definitions
- **Nghị định (Decree)** — an official regulatory document issued by the Government of Vietnam to implement laws and policies. [1][2][3][4][5][6][7][8]
- **TTTC (Trung tâm Tài chính)** — the Financial Centre, a designated zone subject to special regulations covered by these decrees. [4]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — the Office of the Government of Vietnam, the issuing authority of these decrees. [1][2][3][4][5][6][7][8]
- **Trung tâm Trọng tài Quốc tế** — the International Arbitration Centre, an institution established under the Financial Centre. [7]
- **Quản lý ngoại hối** — foreign exchange management, regulated within the Financial Centre under Decree 329. [8]
- **Sở giao dịch hàng hóa** — the Commodity Exchange, an institution whose establishment and operation within the Financial Centre is regulated under Decree 330. [1]
