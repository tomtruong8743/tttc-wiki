# Target Participants of the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary
The target participants of the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) are defined within the legal framework established by a series of government decrees issued by the [[Financial Center Decrees|Office of the Government of Vietnam]]. These decrees — covering areas such as [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policy]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment|land and environment]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment]], and [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|labour and social security]] — collectively shape who may participate in and benefit from the TTTC's special operating environment.

## Key Points
- The decrees governing the TTTC were all issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), indicating a high-level, centralised framework for regulating participants. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- The [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment decree (Decree 323)]] provides the foundational legal basis that would define eligible participants within the TTTC. [5]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|Decree 325]] on labour, employment, and social security suggests that both domestic and foreign workers are among the target participants operating within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] on immigration policy indicates that foreign individuals — including investors, professionals, and workers — are targeted participants, with specific entry and residency provisions likely applying to them. [2]
- [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|Financial policy under Decree 324]] implies that financial institutions and investors, both domestic and foreign, are targeted as key participants through incentive structures. [1]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] on the International Arbitration Centre suggests that legal and dispute resolution service providers are also among the participants expected to operate within the TTTC. [4]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment|Decree 326]] on land and environment implies that businesses and organisations requiring physical premises — such as financial firms and enterprises — are targeted participants with defined land-use rights. [3]

## Definitions

- [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|**TTTC**]] — Abbreviation for *Trung Tâm Tài Chính*, referring to the Vietnam International Financial Centre, the special financial zone established by the Vietnamese Government. [5]
- [[Financial Center Decrees|**Văn Phòng Chính Phủ**]] — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all decrees governing the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Foreign Worker Regulations|**Target Participants**]] — Entities and individuals eligible to operate within the TTTC, including (uncertain) financial institutions, foreign and domestic investors, workers, legal service providers, and enterprises, as implied across the various governing decrees. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
