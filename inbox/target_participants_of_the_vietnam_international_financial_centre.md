# Target Participants of the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary
The target participants of the [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Vietnam International Financial Centre]] (TTTC) are defined within the legal framework established by a series of government decrees issued by the [[Financial Center Decrees|Office of the Government of Vietnam]]. These decrees — covering areas such as [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|financial policy]], [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration]], [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|land and environment]], [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|international arbitration]], [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment]], [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|labour and social security]], [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|banking and foreign exchange]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|commodities exchange]] — collectively shape who may participate in and benefit from the TTTC's special operating environment.

## Key Points
- The decrees governing the TTTC were all issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), indicating a high-level, centralised framework for regulating participants. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- The [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment decree (Decree 323)]] provides the foundational legal basis that would define eligible participants within the TTTC. [5]
- [[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security In Tttc|Decree 325]] on labour, employment, and social security suggests that both domestic and foreign workers are among the target participants operating within the TTTC. [6]
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Decree 327]] on immigration policy indicates that foreign individuals — including investors, professionals, and workers — are targeted participants, with specific entry and residency provisions likely applying to them. [2]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Financial policy under Decree 324]] implies that financial institutions and investors, both domestic and foreign, are targeted as key participants through incentive structures. [1]
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] on the International Arbitration Centre suggests that legal and dispute resolution service providers are also among the participants expected to operate within the TTTC. [4]
- [[Decree 326 Land And Environment In Tttc|Decree 326]] on land and environment implies that businesses and organisations requiring physical premises — such as financial firms and enterprises — are targeted participants with defined land-use rights. [3]
- Under [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]], **Members** (*Thành viên*) of the TTTC include both domestically-owned and fully foreign-owned entities. Fully foreign-owned member banks are permitted to lend to individuals and organisations outside Vietnam's territory, while members not wholly foreign-owned must meet additional conditions before doing so. [Src2]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] also specifies that 100% foreign-owned commercial banks and branches of foreign banks that are TTTC members may conduct a range of activities with non-credit-institution organisations in Vietnam, including lending, discounting, bank guarantees, factoring, letters of credit, and buying and selling corporate and government bonds. This confirms that [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|foreign banks]] are explicitly targeted participants. [Src4]
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Decree 329]] sets qualifications for senior management of member commercial banks, requiring that Deputy Directors, Chief Accountants, and equivalent roles hold at minimum a university degree in finance, banking, economics, business administration, law, accounting, auditing, or a related field, or at least three years of direct experience in finance, banking, or the relevant professional field. This indicates that qualified financial professionals are a targeted participant category. [Src5]
- Under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]], **Members and their employees** (*Thành viên, người lao động của các Thành viên*) are explicitly listed as participants entitled to access insurance services — from insurers operating within the TTTC, from insurers operating in the rest of Vietnam, and from foreign insurers. This confirms that both institutional members and their individual employees are recognised target participants. [Src6]
- [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] also includes a standardised form (*Mẫu số 05 — Bản thuyết minh cơ sở vật chất*) for organisations registering to operate within the TTTC, covering office space, equipment, software infrastructure, crowd-funding platforms, and asset management systems. This suggests that [[Asset Managers In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|asset managers]], platform operators, and other institutionally organised financial service providers are target participants. [Src3]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Decree 330]] on the commodities exchange establishes that TTTC Members may establish a [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Commodities Exchange]] (*Sở giao dịch hàng hóa*) within the TTTC, and that goods traded must comply with conditions relating to export/import tax incentives and sector-specific inspection requirements (food safety, health, environment, agriculture, science and technology, defence, and security). This broadens the target participant base to include commodities trading entities. [Src1]

## Categories of Target Participants

Based on the governing decrees, target participants of the TTTC can be grouped as follows:

### 1. Financial Institutions
- [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Foreign commercial banks]] and branches of foreign banks (100% foreign-owned) [Src2][Src4]
- [[Domestic Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Domestic commercial banks]] (100% domestically-owned) [Src2]
- Mixed-ownership member banks [Src2]
- [[Asset Managers In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Asset management companies]] [Src3]
- Insurance companies, reinsurance companies, and insurance brokers [Src6]
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|Commodities exchange operators]] [Src1]

### 2. Investors
- Foreign investors (individuals and organisations outside Vietnam's territory) [Src2]
- Domestic investors, including Vietnamese enterprises with majority stakes in overseas entities [Src2]

### 3. Workers and Professionals
- Employees of TTTC member organisations, both domestic and foreign [Src6]
- Qualified financial professionals serving in senior management roles (requiring relevant university qualifications or experience in finance, banking, law, accounting, or auditing) [Src5]
- Foreign experts and skilled workers covered by [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|immigration policy]] [2]

### 4. Legal and Dispute Resolution Service Providers
- [[Arbitration In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|International arbitration]] and dispute resolution service providers under [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|Decree 328]] [4]

### 5. Enterprises Requiring Physical Premises
- Companies requiring dedicated office infrastructure and technology systems as evidenced by registration requirements under [[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]] [Src3]

## Definitions

- [[What Is The Vietnam International Financial Centre|**TTTC**]] — Abbreviation for *Trung Tâm Tài Chính*, referring to the Vietnam International Financial Centre, the special financial zone established by the Vietnamese Government. [5]
- [[Financial Center Decrees|**Văn Phòng Chính Phủ**]] — The Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all decrees governing the TTTC. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- [[Types Of Financial Institutions In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|**Thành viên (Member)**]] — An entity formally admitted to operate within the TTTC framework. Members may be financial institutions, enterprises, or other eligible organisations, and are subject to specific rights and obligations under the governing decrees. [Src2][Src6]
- [[Foreign Worker Regulations|**Target Participants**]] — Entities and individuals eligible to operate within the TTTC, including financial institutions, foreign and domestic investors, workers, qualified financial professionals, legal service providers, insurance entities, commodities exchange operators, and enterprises, as defined across the various governing decrees.
