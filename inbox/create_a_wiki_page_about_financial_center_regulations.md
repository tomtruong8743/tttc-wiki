# Create a wiki page about financial center regulations.

## Summary
A series of decrees issued by the Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ) of Vietnam establishes the regulatory framework governing financial centers. These decrees cover a range of areas including banking licensing and establishment, foreign exchange management, commodity exchange operations, immigration policies, land and environment management, and international arbitration within financial centers.

## Key Points
- A decree has been issued concerning the licensing, establishment of banks, and foreign exchange management within financial centers. [1]
- A separate decree governs the establishment and operation of commodity exchanges (Sở giao dịch hàng hóa) within financial centers. [2]
- Immigration (entry and exit) policies specific to financial centers are regulated under a dedicated decree. [3]
- Land use and environmental matters within financial centers are addressed by a distinct decree. [4]
- An international arbitration center (Trung tâm trọng tài quốc tế) operating within financial centers is governed by its own decree. [5]
- All identified decrees were issued by the same issuing authority: the Government Office of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [1][2][3][4][5]

## Definitions
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — The Government Office of Vietnam, the issuing authority for these financial center decrees. [1][2][3][4][5]
- **Sở giao dịch hàng hóa** — Commodity exchange; an institution for trading commodities, regulated within financial centers. [2]
- **Trung tâm trọng tài quốc tế** — International arbitration center; a dispute resolution body operating within financial centers. [5]
- **TTTC** — Abbreviation used to refer to the financial center (Trung tâm tài chính) in the context of these decrees. [1][2][3][4][5]
