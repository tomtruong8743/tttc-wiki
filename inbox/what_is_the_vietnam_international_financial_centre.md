# What Is the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary
The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) is a government-established financial zone in Vietnam, created and governed through a series of decrees issued by the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Vietnamese Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ)]]. The TTTC is underpinned by a comprehensive regulatory framework covering its [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment]], [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]], [[Foreign Worker Regulations|labour and social security]], [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|land and environment]], [[Foreign Investment Rules In Tttc|immigration policy]], [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|international arbitration]], and [[Banking Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|banking licensing and foreign exchange management]], reflecting a broad and coordinated legislative effort to stand up a specialised international financial hub within Vietnam.

## Key Points
- The TTTC was established through a formal government decree, with its founding legal basis originating from the [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323 on the establishment of the TTTC]] [6].
- [[Financial Center Decrees|A suite of dedicated decrees]] governs the TTTC, each addressing a distinct policy area including financial policy, labour, land, immigration, arbitration, and banking and foreign exchange [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|Financial policies within the TTTC]] are regulated under a specific decree (Decree 324) issued by the Government Office [2].
- [[Foreign Worker Regulations|Labour, employment, and social security]] arrangements within the TTTC are governed by a dedicated decree ([[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|Decree 325]]) [6].
- [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|Land use and environmental matters]] within the TTTC are addressed through [[Decree 326 Land And Environment|Decree 326]] [4].
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Immigration and entry/exit policies]] for the TTTC are set out in Decree 327 [3].
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|An International Arbitration Centre]] forms part of the TTTC's institutional structure, governed by Decree 328 [5].
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Banking licensing and foreign exchange management]] within the TTTC are governed by Decree 329, also issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam [1].
- All decrees related to the TTTC were issued by the **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** (Office of the Government of Vietnam) [1][2][3][4][5][6].

## Definitions
- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|**TTTC**]] — Abbreviation for *Trung Tâm Tài Chính* (International Financial Centre), the specialised financial zone established by the Vietnamese government [6].
- [[Financial Center Decrees|**Decree (Nghị định)**]] — A form of subordinate legislation issued by the Vietnamese Government, used here to establish and regulate the TTTC across multiple policy domains [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|**Trung tâm trọng tài quốc tế**]] — International Arbitration Centre, an institutional body situated within the TTTC to handle dispute resolution [5].
- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|**Văn Phòng Chính Phủ**]] — Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all TTTC-related decrees [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|**Cấp phép và thành lập ngân hàng, quản lý ngoại hối**]] — Banking licensing, establishment, and foreign exchange management within the TTTC, as regulated under Decree 329 [1].
