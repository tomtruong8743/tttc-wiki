# What Is the Vietnam International Financial Centre

## Summary
The Vietnam International Financial Centre (TTTC) is a government-established financial zone in Vietnam, created and governed through a series of decrees issued by the [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|Vietnamese Government Office (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ)]]. The TTTC is underpinned by a comprehensive regulatory framework covering its [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|establishment]], [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|financial policies]], [[Foreign Worker Regulations|labour and social security]], [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|land and environment]], [[Foreign Investment Rules In Tttc|immigration policy]], [[Dispute Resolution Mechanisms|international arbitration]], [[Banking Activities Allowed In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|banking licensing and foreign exchange management]], and [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|commodities exchange operations]], reflecting a broad and coordinated legislative effort to stand up a specialised international financial hub within Vietnam.

## Key Points
- The TTTC was established through a formal government decree, with its founding legal basis originating from the [[Decree 323 Establishment Of Tttc|Decree 323 on the establishment of the TTTC]] [6].
- [[Financial Center Decrees|A suite of dedicated decrees]] governs the TTTC, each addressing a distinct policy area including financial policy, labour, land, immigration, arbitration, banking and foreign exchange, and commodities exchange [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- [[Tax Incentives And Exemptions|Financial policies within the TTTC]] are regulated under a specific decree ([[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]]) issued by the Government Office [2].
- [[Foreign Worker Regulations|Labour, employment, and social security]] arrangements within the TTTC are governed by a dedicated decree ([[Decree 325 Labor And Social Security|Decree 325]]) [6].
- [[Environmental Regulations Tttc|Land use and environmental matters]] within the TTTC are addressed through [[Decree 326 Land And Environment|Decree 326]] [4].
- [[Decree 327 Immigration Policy|Immigration and entry/exit policies]] for the TTTC are set out in Decree 327 [3].
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|An International Arbitration Centre]] forms part of the TTTC's institutional structure, governed by Decree 328 [5].
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|Banking licensing and foreign exchange management]] within the TTTC are governed by Decree 329, also issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam [1].
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|A Commodities Exchange (Sở giao dịch hàng hóa)]] may be established and operated within the TTTC under Decree 330, with licences issued, suspended, or revoked by the local management authority in each TTTC city [6].
- [[Foreign Banks In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|100% foreign-owned commercial banks and foreign bank branches]] that are TTTC members are permitted to conduct a defined range of activities with non-credit organisations on Vietnamese territory, including lending, discounting, bank guarantees, factoring, letters of credit, and buying and selling corporate bonds and government bonds [1].
- [[Foreign Exchange Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Foreign exchange rules for borrowing and lending]] apply to TTTC members: members may borrow foreign currency from individuals and organisations outside Vietnam subject to reporting obligations, while the right to lend abroad depends on whether a member is 100% foreign-owned or has mixed ownership [1].
- [[Compliance Requirements In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Senior management of member commercial banks]] must meet prescribed qualification and residency requirements, including relevant tertiary-level education or at least three years of direct experience in finance, banking, or the relevant professional field, and must reside in Vietnam during their term [5].
- [[Tax Incentives In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Export and import tax incentives]] available to the TTTC's Commodities Exchange are governed by the decree on financial policies within the TTTC ([[Decree 324 Financial Policies Of Tttc|Decree 324]]), and goods subject to specialised inspection (food safety, health, environment, agriculture, science and technology, national defence, and security) must comply with the relevant regulatory authority's inspection procedures [6].
- [[Infrastructure Planned For The Vietnam International Financial Centre|Organisations registering to operate within the TTTC]] must provide a physical infrastructure statement (Bản thuyết minh cơ sở vật chất) as part of their application, detailing office floor area, layout, equipment, software, systems for crowdfunding platforms, and systems for asset management activities [4].
- All decrees related to the TTTC were issued by the **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** (Office of the Government of Vietnam) [1][2][3][4][5][6].

## Definitions
- [[Tttc Financial Center Overview|**TTTC**]] — Abbreviation for *Trung Tâm Tài Chính* (International Financial Centre), the specialised financial zone established by the Vietnamese government [6].
- [[Financial Center Decrees|**Decree (Nghị định)**]] — A form of subordinate legislation issued by the Vietnamese Government, used here to establish and regulate the TTTC across multiple policy domains [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- [[Decree 328 International Arbitration Center|**Trung tâm trọng tài quốc tế**]] — International Arbitration Centre, an institutional body situated within the TTTC to handle dispute resolution [5].
- [[Legal Framework Of Tttc|**Văn Phòng Chính Phủ**]] — Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all TTTC-related decrees [1][2][3][4][5][6].
- [[Decree 329 Banking And Foreign Exchange|**Cấp phép và thành lập ngân hàng, quản lý ngoại hối**]] — Banking licensing, establishment, and foreign exchange management within the TTTC, as regulated under Decree 329 [1].
- [[Decree 330 Commodities Exchange In Tttc|**Sở giao dịch hàng hóa**]] — Commodities Exchange; an exchange for the trading of commodities that may be established and operated within the TTTC under Decree 330 [6].
- [[Capital Movement Rules In The Vietnam International Financial Centre|**Quản lý ngoại hối đối với hoạt động vay, cho vay**]] — Foreign exchange management rules applicable to borrowing and lending activities of TTTC members, including cross-border lending and borrowing in foreign currency [1].
- [[Infrastructure Planned For The Vietnam International Financial Centre|**Bản thuyết minh cơ sở vật chất**]] — Physical infrastructure statement; a formal document that applicant organisations must submit when registering to operate within the TTTC, detailing office space, equipment, and IT systems [4].
