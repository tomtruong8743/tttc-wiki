# Financial Center Decrees

## Summary
The Financial Center Decrees are a series of legal instruments issued by the Office of the Government of Vietnam (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ), covering the establishment and operations of a Financial Center (TTTC). The decrees address a range of regulatory areas including the center's founding framework, financial policies, immigration policies, international arbitration, labor and social welfare, and commodity exchange operations.

## Key Points
- Decree 323 concerns the establishment of the Financial Center (TTTC), issued by the Office of the Government. [5]
- Decree 324 addresses financial policies within the Financial Center. [2]
- Decree 325 covers labor, employment, and social welfare matters within the Financial Center. [6]
- Decree 327 sets out immigration and entry/exit policies applicable within the Financial Center. [3]
- Decree 328 relates to the International Arbitration Center operating within the Financial Center. [4]
- Decree 330 governs the establishment and operations of a Commodity Exchange within the Financial Center. [1]
- All six decrees were issued by the same authority: the Office of the Government (Văn Phòng Chính Phủ). [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## Definitions
- **TTTC** — abbreviation used throughout the decrees referring to the Financial Center (Trung Tâm Tài Chính). [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- **Văn Phòng Chính Phủ** — the Office of the Government of Vietnam; the issuing authority for all decrees in this series. [1][2][3][4][5][6]
- **Sở giao dịch hàng hóa** — Commodity Exchange; the subject of Decree 330, concerning its establishment and operations within the Financial Center. [1]
- **Trung tâm trọng tài quốc tế** — International Arbitration Center; the subject of Decree 328, operating within the Financial Center. [4]
